# Evaluation method

> Proposed method only. [#2](https://github.com/Homes-That-Behave-Well-LLC/home_steward/issues/2)
> must complete and obtain Owner acceptance. No qualification tests executed.

## Evidence labels

- **Official product claim:** vendor marketing/FAQ; attributed and dated, not verified operation.
- **Official technical documentation:** published API/specification/source/configuration; not proof of a tested deployment.
- **Independent observation:** an explicitly cited external test/review with scope, version and limitations.
- **Direct vendor response:** attributable technical response, distinct from public documentation;
  publish only separately authorized non-private evidence. None is published in bootstrap.
- **Reference Home observation:** explicitly authorized, versioned household validation evidence.
  None was collected in bootstrap.
- **Reasonable inference:** stated reasoning from evidence, not a fact or capability guarantee.
- **Unverified claim:** a positive assertion lacking adequate supporting evidence; not a verified capability.
- **Unknown:** missing, inaccessible, ambiguous, conflicting or untested evidence.

Future evidence ratings must state confidence, reproducibility, age and exact evaluated configuration.
This baseline does not invent independent observation where none was collected.

## Candidate lifecycle and entry

Inventory -> public evidence/unknowns -> accepted common method -> authorized safe test plan ->
per-gate results -> post-gate comparison -> explicit outcome -> separately authorized Reference Home
validation. No procurement or live testing is authorized by moving a document between stages.

Unknown mandatory evidence means RESEARCH INCOMPLETE. Proven failure means NOT QUALIFIED.
Qualified outcomes require all hard gates PASS. Conditional qualification cannot waive a gate.
Market deferral is a reasoned conclusion, not a selection or substitute for missing evidence.

## Proposed tests, not executed

Use synthetic accounts/documents with mixed Person permissions. Inventory DNS/network endpoints,
credentials, update/support behavior and claimed retention before tests. Observe default and explicit
local configuration, block internet, interrupt providers, check auth/interface exposure and tool denial.
Verify overbroad source queries do not transfer or retrieve restricted data.

Track OCR/chunks/embeddings/memory/caches through revocation, correction, source removal, export,
erase and backup restore. Test malicious source instructions and model-proposed unauthorized actions.
Record failures, unavailable behavior, logs/redaction, versions and scope, not just favorable demos.
No production tokens, personal data or live household actions.

## Weighted criteria only after gates

No weights are approved or scores computed. #2 must publish normalized weights/rationale **before**
candidate scoring: household answer quality, performance/context/concurrency, extensibility,
portability/derived lifecycle, operability/security, 24x7 noise/power and five-year TCO/Owner time.
Development-inference quality and throughput are separate workload evidence, not the household score.
Mandatory privacy/locality/authority gates cannot be traded for performance or lower cost.

## Five-year TCO control

Record cash cost plus Owner hours at an explicitly chosen rate: acquisition/upgrades, energy,
storage/backups, licenses/services, installation, integration, patching/security, restore, monitoring,
support and exit/migration. Show assumptions/ranges and workload; do not treat Owner time as free.
No actual price forecast, measured performance or cost total is claimed in bootstrap.

## Reference Home entry

Requires accepted #2 method, completed mandatory evidence, approved scope/configuration, synthetic
data/credentials, network safeguards, rollback/export/deletion plan, named risks and separately
authorized Owner runtime validation. Documentation or vendor demo is not Reference Home acceptance.
