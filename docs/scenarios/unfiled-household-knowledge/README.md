# Unfiled Household Knowledge

Navigation and scenario-capture guidance only; not architecture, a roadmap or delivered functionality. Browse the [scenario-first map](../README.md).

## Purpose

Understand source, date, privacy, classification and association gaps in separately authorized household information before any admission or durable relationship. Finding a file is not authority to ingest or classify it.

## Relationship to HTBW

Foundation describes structure, not current Truth; Stewardship owns household-declared significance, care, obligations and accepted Custody; Identity supplies Person/context, not authentication or authorization; Truth owns current/historical Facts; Continuity retains accepted preference/session/experience responsibilities; Operational Trust determines permission/disclosure for this request/context; Concierge retains governed orchestration and Trace, not centralized ownership or acceptance. Native Explainability already includes historical/time/cross-Room retrieval. Home Assistant/devices retain execution.

See the accepted-owner [HTBW boundary](../../architecture/htbw-boundary.md), [source authorization](../../architecture/source-authorization.md) and [derived lifecycle](../../architecture/derived-knowledge-lifecycle.md).

## Boundaries

NOTICE != DIAGNOSE; NOTICE != DISCLOSE; NOTICE != RETAIN; NOTICE != RECOMMEND; NOTICE != ACT. Noticing is not authority or automatic Truth, household significance, notification or action.

Potential evidence is not admitted access. Person/source/purpose/namespace/audience gates remain independent; one Person's grant is not everyone's access and cross-person grants do not merge. The most restrictive applicable source constraints remain binding. Summaries can disclose their sources; conclusions are not automatically safe to share. USE != INGEST != DERIVE != RETAIN. Derived information remains source-bound through correction, retention, expiry, deletion and revocation within the household-controlled network boundary. Household-configured ordinary retention may be zero/disabled; no numeric minimum or maximum is imposed. Confirmation alone does not grant retention, disclosure, policy or action authority. No shadow history or silent cloud/source fallback.

Observation, source report, comparison and inference are not automatic Truth. Correlation is not causation; unknown is valid. Issued command != observed physical result. People and Pets are not Assets; Pet/device location does not flow through human Identity. No diagnosis, prognosis, treatment or medical causation follows from changed observations. No medical, life-safety or emergency path depends on an LLM; native/deterministic/manual safety remains independent.

All future source eligibility, meaningful-change review, retention, correction, disclosure, notification and action questions remain OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTIONS. No runtime, scheduler, camera pipeline, learning system, hook, consent engine, platform qualification or acceptance mechanism is designed here.

## Primary scenarios

Primary files: **0 developed + 15 Vision = 15**. Existing coverage and aliases do not add primary files or change target maturity.

- [Which household documents appear important but are not associated with anything?](which-household-documents-appear-important-but-are-not-associated-with-anything.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A household can possess useful documents without having associated them with an Asset or obligation. The household wants eligible gaps considered against declared significance, not an automatic scan of every file or a model deciding which private information is important.
- [Which files appear to describe the same Asset?](which-files-appear-to-describe-the-same-asset.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Several files may refer to one Asset under different names or dates. The household wants candidate equivalence explored without merging sources or creating an authoritative association, because similar titles, addresses or model descriptions can also refer to different objects.
- [Which duplicate records disagree?](which-duplicate-records-disagree.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Two records may look like duplicates yet differ in a value, date or description. The household wants the disagreement and versions exposed rather than a model choosing a convenient winner, deleting evidence or assuming the newer record is correct.
- [Which records lack dates?](which-records-lack-dates.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Undated records can mislead a household about current care, ownership or coverage. The household wants the missing date visible without treating a download timestamp as the document's effective date or inventing chronology from where a file was stored.
- [Which records lack source information?](which-records-lack-source-information.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A useful-looking record may lack an attributable origin. The household wants that limitation made clear before relying on the information, because a filename, confident wording or repeated copy does not establish who supplied the claim or whether it is authoritative.
- [Which records lack Asset relationships?](which-records-lack-asset-relationships.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A document can be authorized for a purpose without being connected to an Asset. The household wants potential associations considered against specific identifiers and ambiguity, so finding a manual or invoice does not silently update Foundation's authoritative relationships.
- [Which information should remain person-private?](which-information-should-remain-person-private.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Information found during household review may belong to one Person even when it seems useful to everyone. The household wants privacy disposition considered before classification or sharing, so a convenient location or new review context does not erase source restrictions.
- [What important household information exists only in screenshots?](what-important-household-information-exists-only-in-screenshots.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Screenshots may be the only authorized record of an important household detail. The household wants their limitations and possible relevance understood without automatically extracting everything, enrolling visible people or treating a cropped display as a complete authoritative document.
- [Which household notes require human review?](which-household-notes-require-human-review.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Household notes may mix factual claims, private recollection and unfinished questions. The household wants eligible items that need human clarification distinguished from accepted knowledge, rather than a model smoothing ambiguity into a complete narrative or attributing the notes to an unverified person.
- [Which information can be safely classified?](which-information-can-be-safely-classified.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Classification can make information easier to find but also reveal sensitive context. The household wants potential categories examined under the authorized purpose and audience, without an apparently harmless label becoming a shared inference about a person's finances, health or relationships.
- [Which classifications require household confirmation?](which-classifications-require-household-confirmation.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Some classifications affect the meaning or audience of a record beyond simple retrieval. The household wants ambiguity and responsibility visible before any candidate is accepted, so casual agreement does not become a permission change, authoritative relationship or durable private label.
- [Which records have no authoritative source?](which-records-have-no-authoritative-source.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A copied record can be useful while lacking an authoritative source relationship. The household wants that distinction visible because a locally held document or repeated statement does not establish governance ownership, authenticity or qualification as an HTBW Fact.
- [What information should not be ingested at all?](what-information-should-not-be-ingested-at-all.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Some information should remain outside a household knowledge corpus despite being accessible. The household wants source, purpose and sensitivity exclusions considered before use, rather than treating local availability or potential usefulness as permission for ingestion and future cross-source reasoning.
- [Which downloaded manuals should be associated with existing Assets?](which-downloaded-manuals-should-be-associated-with-existing-assets.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Downloaded manuals may be generic, outdated or intended for another appliance variant. The household wants exact-model candidates compared with eligible Asset descriptions before association, rather than a folder of manuals becoming authoritative care or overriding instructions for the actual equipment.
- [Which invoices appear relevant but have not been connected to Stewardship or Asset evidence?](which-invoices-appear-relevant-but-have-not-been-connected-to-stewardship-or-asset-evidence.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. An invoice may contain useful Asset or service evidence without proving accepted maintenance. The household wants candidate connections and date limits understood while keeping financial access separate, because payment descriptions can differ from completed work and native Stewardship obligations.
