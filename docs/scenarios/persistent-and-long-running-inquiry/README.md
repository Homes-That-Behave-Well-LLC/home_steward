# Persistent and Long-Running Inquiry

Navigation and scenario-capture guidance only; not architecture, a roadmap or delivered functionality. Browse the [scenario-first map](../README.md).

## Purpose

Capture an unresolved household inquiry that remains relevant across later, independently authorized evidence. A continuing interest is not a perpetual source grant, background job or promise that anything is being watched.

## Relationship to HTBW

Foundation describes structure, not current Truth; Stewardship owns household-declared significance, care, obligations and accepted Custody; Identity supplies Person/context, not authentication or authorization; Truth owns current/historical Facts; Continuity retains accepted preference/session/experience responsibilities; Operational Trust determines permission/disclosure for this request/context; Concierge retains governed orchestration and Trace, not centralized ownership or acceptance. Native Explainability already includes historical/time/cross-Room retrieval. Home Assistant/devices retain execution.

See the accepted-owner [HTBW boundary](../../architecture/htbw-boundary.md), [source authorization](../../architecture/source-authorization.md) and [derived lifecycle](../../architecture/derived-knowledge-lifecycle.md).

## Boundaries

NOTICE != DIAGNOSE; NOTICE != DISCLOSE; NOTICE != RETAIN; NOTICE != RECOMMEND; NOTICE != ACT. Noticing is not authority or automatic Truth, household significance, notification or action.

Potential evidence is not admitted access. Person/source/purpose/namespace/audience gates remain independent; one Person's grant is not everyone's access and cross-person grants do not merge. The most restrictive applicable source constraints remain binding. Summaries can disclose their sources; conclusions are not automatically safe to share. USE != INGEST != DERIVE != RETAIN. Derived information remains source-bound through correction, retention, expiry, deletion and revocation within the household-controlled network boundary. Household-configured ordinary retention may be zero/disabled; no numeric minimum or maximum is imposed. Confirmation alone does not grant retention, disclosure, policy or action authority. No shadow history or silent cloud/source fallback.

Observation, source report, comparison and inference are not automatic Truth. Correlation is not causation; unknown is valid. Issued command != observed physical result. People and Pets are not Assets; Pet/device location does not flow through human Identity. No diagnosis, prognosis, treatment or medical causation follows from changed observations. No medical, life-safety or emergency path depends on an LLM; native/deterministic/manual safety remains independent.

All future source eligibility, meaningful-change review, retention, correction, disclosure, notification and action questions remain OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTIONS. No runtime, scheduler, camera pipeline, learning system, hook, consent engine, platform qualification or acceptance mechanism is designed here.

## Primary scenarios

Primary files: **0 developed + 10 Vision = 10**. Existing coverage and aliases do not add primary files or change target maturity.

- [Keep researching replacement products until something actually meets our requirements.](keep-researching-replacement-products-until-something-actually-meets-our-requirements.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A replacement search may remain unresolved because no product meets the actual requirements. The household wants the question preserved as an intention for later evidence, not relaxed criteria, perpetual procurement monitoring or a promised eventual recommendation.
- [Watch Home Assistant documentation for support for this deferred requirement.](watch-home-assistant-documentation-for-support-for-this-deferred-requirement.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A deferred requirement may become relevant when official Home Assistant evidence changes. The household wants its original blocker and governance objections preserved so technical support can be reconsidered, without a watch request starting an engineering issue or reopening acceptance.
- [Track whether this recurring problem improves after the next software update.](track-whether-this-recurring-problem-improves-after-the-next-software-update.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A promised software fix may need later lived evidence rather than installation success. The household wants the unresolved outcome question carried forward with comparable exposure, so quieter reporting or changed usage does not become evidence that recurrence improved.
- [Monitor whether conditions around this Asset return to the accepted pattern.](monitor-whether-conditions-around-this-asset-return-to-the-accepted-pattern.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Conditions may take time to return toward an Asset's accepted pattern. The household wants that unresolved comparison interpreted against governed care, sampling gaps and actual exposure, without inventing a conservation threshold or continuous monitoring obligation.
- [Keep looking for the missing provenance evidence across newly authorized sources.](keep-looking-for-the-missing-provenance-evidence-across-newly-authorized-sources.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Provenance gaps may remain after the currently permitted documents are explored. The household wants later authorized sources considered against the same unresolved question, without treating a request to keep looking as permission to search every person's records.
- [Tell us if the utility changes the rate plan.](tell-us-if-the-utility-changes-the-rate-plan.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A utility plan change can affect interpretation of bills even when consumption stays similar. The household wants later rate evidence associated with its unresolved concern, but an interest in rates must not authorize plan enrollment or guaranteed continuous notice.
- [Watch for product end-of-life announcements affecting household technology.](watch-for-product-end-of-life-announcements-affecting-household-technology.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. End-of-life announcements may matter to a household long after technology was installed. The household wants relevant later official evidence understood against actual use and dependencies, not a product announcement automatically becoming a replacement deadline or purchase recommendation.
- [Track an unresolved warranty or service matter and tell us when a response requires attention.](track-an-unresolved-warranty-or-service-matter-and-tell-us-when-a-response-requires-attention.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A warranty or service matter may stay unresolved while different responses arrive. The household wants the original question and stated commitments available for eligible human review, rather than Home Steward contacting providers, accepting terms or deciding that silence means agreement.
- [Maintain the unresolved questions we want answered during the next service visit.](maintain-the-unresolved-questions-we-want-answered-during-the-next-service-visit.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Useful questions for a service visit can be forgotten while waiting for the appointment. The household wants its unresolved concerns retrievable in the authorized visit context, with obsolete or answered matters distinguishable from current questions and no invented obligations.
- [Tell us when sufficient new evidence exists to reconsider this question.](tell-us-when-sufficient-new-evidence-exists-to-reconsider-this-question.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A prior answer may remain uncertain until genuinely relevant evidence becomes available. The household wants reconsideration to depend on the unresolved gap rather than mere novelty, while preserving the earlier uncertainty and avoiding a model deciding its own review trigger.
