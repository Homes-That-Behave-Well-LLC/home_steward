# What changed before the fall

> Aging in Place and Care Awareness: synthetic retrospective evidence review, not prediction or diagnosis.

## 1. Household request

"What changed in the week before the fall?" Prepare authorized retrospective observations.

## 2. Existing HTBW knowledge

Truth current/historical Home Facts, Foundation Person/Room context, eligible Identity, Stewardship
care and existing Explainability. A reported fall's circumstances are not assumed known.

## 3. Authorized private retrieval

Potential Person-authorized activity, sleep, movement, environmental evidence, device-originated
wearable reports and household observations. Person movement history requires existing separate governance.

## 4. Optional public research

Device/environmental measurement descriptions may clarify source limits, not diagnose a fall's cause.
Private event/care/Person context must not enter public queries.

## 5. Home Steward synthesis

Clarify the event/date and selected prior week/personal comparison period. Describe observed changes,
unchanged evidence, source gaps and known context; correlation is not cause.
Do not claim the fall could have been predicted or prevented by Home Steward.

## 6. Provenance

Retain device alert/report wording, Home observation and household event report as separate sources,
with times/precision, baseline/recent coverage and inference limits.

## 7. Candidate relationships

Temporal associations are tentative, not actual causal recurrence, medical findings or durable care records.

## 8. Household confirmation

Clarify reported event/dates/context; confirmation does not grant retrospective surveillance/disclosure.
Future retention/review is an OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION.

## 9. Governed durable relationship

OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION: no fall event model, risk object, care proposal or diagnostic schema.

## 10. Proposed actions

The household may prepare observations/questions for independently authorized human review.
Disclosure, communication or handoff remains an OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION.

## 11. Prohibited autonomous actions

No diagnosis, disease/fall prediction, inferred medical cause/medication effect, treatment, clinical
monitoring, emergency decision, automatic caregiver notification or alert/care/policy alteration.

## 12. HTBW authority

Truth current Home state/history; Stewardship care obligations; Identity context; Foundation definitions;
Operational Trust action/disclosure; Continuity where applicable; Concierge and existing Explainability/Trace.

## 13. Privacy and locality

Person-scoped event/history/care evidence stays separately governed; ingestion does not authorize
disclosure. Local derivatives inherit sensitivity/lifecycle; no private query leakage or shadow archive.

## 14. Unavailable / degraded behavior

"I don't know what changed" is valid; unavailable evidence does not establish no change or cause.
Established emergency mechanisms/native HTBW stay independent; no LLM critical path or cloud fallback.

## 15. Acceptance expectations

Six potential sources and retrospective personal comparison captured; no correlation-as-cause,
fall prediction, clinical/emergency judgment or implementation architecture.

## 16. Open questions

Eligible event/baseline evidence, comparison/retention and human disclosure/handoff remain OPEN
ARCHITECTURAL QUESTIONS; no predictive or diagnostic logic.
