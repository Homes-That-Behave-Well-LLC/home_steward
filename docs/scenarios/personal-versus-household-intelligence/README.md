# Personal versus Household Intelligence

Navigation and scenario-capture guidance only; not architecture, a roadmap or delivered functionality. Browse the [scenario-first map](../README.md).

## Purpose

Distinguish Person-private evidence and preferences from eligible shared knowledge. Home Steward is not simply single-user personal AI; household participation does not merge sources, consent, namespaces or audiences.

## Relationship to HTBW

Foundation describes structure, not current Truth; Stewardship owns household-declared significance, care, obligations and accepted Custody; Identity supplies Person/context, not authentication or authorization; Truth owns current/historical Facts; Continuity retains accepted preference/session/experience responsibilities; Operational Trust determines permission/disclosure for this request/context; Concierge retains governed orchestration and Trace, not centralized ownership or acceptance. Native Explainability already includes historical/time/cross-Room retrieval. Home Assistant/devices retain execution.

See the accepted-owner [HTBW boundary](../../architecture/htbw-boundary.md), [source authorization](../../architecture/source-authorization.md) and [derived lifecycle](../../architecture/derived-knowledge-lifecycle.md).

## Boundaries

NOTICE != DIAGNOSE; NOTICE != DISCLOSE; NOTICE != RETAIN; NOTICE != RECOMMEND; NOTICE != ACT. Noticing is not authority or automatic Truth, household significance, notification or action.

Potential evidence is not admitted access. Person/source/purpose/namespace/audience gates remain independent; one Person's grant is not everyone's access and cross-person grants do not merge. The most restrictive applicable source constraints remain binding. Summaries can disclose their sources; conclusions are not automatically safe to share. USE != INGEST != DERIVE != RETAIN. Derived information remains source-bound through correction, retention, expiry, deletion and revocation within the household-controlled network boundary. Household-configured ordinary retention may be zero/disabled; no numeric minimum or maximum is imposed. Confirmation alone does not grant retention, disclosure, policy or action authority. No shadow history or silent cloud/source fallback.

Observation, source report, comparison and inference are not automatic Truth. Correlation is not causation; unknown is valid. Issued command != observed physical result. People and Pets are not Assets; Pet/device location does not flow through human Identity. No diagnosis, prognosis, treatment or medical causation follows from changed observations. No medical, life-safety or emergency path depends on an LLM; native/deterministic/manual safety remains independent.

All future source eligibility, meaningful-change review, retention, correction, disclosure, notification and action questions remain OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTIONS. No runtime, scheduler, camera pipeline, learning system, hook, consent engine, platform qualification or acceptance mechanism is designed here.

## Primary scenarios

Primary files: **0 developed + 10 Vision = 10**. Existing coverage and aliases do not add primary files or change target maturity.

- [One person authorized a source. May it answer another person's question?](one-person-authorized-a-source-may-it-answer-another-person-s-question.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. One Person may authorize a source that would be useful to another person's question. The household wants that boundary respected rather than assuming shared residence merges grants, because answering can reveal private information even when no raw document is shown.
- [Is this a personal preference or household preference?](is-this-a-personal-preference-or-household-preference.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A preference expressed by one Person may conflict with household intent or be meant only for that context. The household wants ownership and scope understood, without a model silently promoting private wishes into shared preferences or replacing native Continuity's accepted responsibilities.
- [What happens when two people remember an event differently?](what-happens-when-two-people-remember-an-event-differently.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Two people may remember the same event differently while each account remains valuable. The household wants attributable recollections and uncertainty preserved, rather than a model choosing a more fluent story, treating agreement as Truth or exposing one person's private account to another.
- [Who can correct shared household memory?](who-can-correct-shared-household-memory.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Shared memory may contain contributions from several people and sources. The household wants responsibility for a proposed correction understood, because editing a shared conclusion can affect private contributors and does not by itself authorize changes to authoritative Facts or other people's records.
- [When can personal calendar evidence inform a shared Stewardship concern?](when-can-personal-calendar-evidence-inform-a-shared-stewardship-concern.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A private calendar entry may be relevant to a shared care obligation without being shareable itself. The household wants that distinction examined before synthesis, so a useful scheduling implication does not expose personal plans or automatically alter native Stewardship.
- [Can a household conclusion depend on evidence owned by multiple people?](can-a-household-conclusion-depend-on-evidence-owned-by-multiple-people.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A household conclusion may require information from more than one Person. The household wants the combination itself governed, not merely each source opened separately, because together the claims can reveal relationships or sensitive context neither Person authorized for the shared audience.
- [What happens if one person revokes a source involved in a shared conclusion?](what-happens-if-one-person-revokes-a-source-involved-in-a-shared-conclusion.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Revoking one contributing source can affect a conclusion that other people still use. The household wants that dependency and uncertainty acknowledged, rather than a shared summary retaining the revoked information as a hidden substitute or silently pretending its original support remains available.
- [Which conclusions are shared?](which-conclusions-are-shared.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A conclusion may be relevant to the household but still not permitted for a shared audience. The household wants declared sharing scope distinguished from usefulness, because summaries can expose private source facts and source owners may authorize different audiences for different purposes.
- [Which conclusions remain person-private?](which-conclusions-remain-person-private.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Derived conclusions can disclose personal facts even when they omit names or raw evidence. The household wants Person-private conclusions distinguished from eligible shared knowledge, rather than assuming a concise or de-identified answer escapes the restrictions of its underlying sources.
- [What happens when a person leaves the household?](what-happens-when-a-person-leaves-the-household.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A person's departure can affect private sources, shared conclusions and personalized vocabulary differently. The household wants those scopes preserved without blanket deletion of others' records, continued private access or assumptions that former membership and earlier consent remain current authority.
