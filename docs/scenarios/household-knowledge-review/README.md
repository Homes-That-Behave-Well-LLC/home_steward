# Household Knowledge Review

Navigation and scenario-capture guidance only; not architecture, a roadmap or delivered functionality. Browse the [scenario-first map](../README.md).

## Purpose

Let eligible people inspect new evidence and candidate associations, corrections, exclusions and conclusions. Review is an experience to capture, not a named inbox, central approval mechanism or acceptance authority.

## Relationship to HTBW

Foundation describes structure, not current Truth; Stewardship owns household-declared significance, care, obligations and accepted Custody; Identity supplies Person/context, not authentication or authorization; Truth owns current/historical Facts; Continuity retains accepted preference/session/experience responsibilities; Operational Trust determines permission/disclosure for this request/context; Concierge retains governed orchestration and Trace, not centralized ownership or acceptance. Native Explainability already includes historical/time/cross-Room retrieval. Home Assistant/devices retain execution.

See the accepted-owner [HTBW boundary](../../architecture/htbw-boundary.md), [source authorization](../../architecture/source-authorization.md) and [derived lifecycle](../../architecture/derived-knowledge-lifecycle.md).

## Boundaries

NOTICE != DIAGNOSE; NOTICE != DISCLOSE; NOTICE != RETAIN; NOTICE != RECOMMEND; NOTICE != ACT. Noticing is not authority or automatic Truth, household significance, notification or action.

Potential evidence is not admitted access. Person/source/purpose/namespace/audience gates remain independent; one Person's grant is not everyone's access and cross-person grants do not merge. The most restrictive applicable source constraints remain binding. Summaries can disclose their sources; conclusions are not automatically safe to share. USE != INGEST != DERIVE != RETAIN. Derived information remains source-bound through correction, retention, expiry, deletion and revocation within the household-controlled network boundary. Household-configured ordinary retention may be zero/disabled; no numeric minimum or maximum is imposed. Confirmation alone does not grant retention, disclosure, policy or action authority. No shadow history or silent cloud/source fallback.

Observation, source report, comparison and inference are not automatic Truth. Correlation is not causation; unknown is valid. Issued command != observed physical result. People and Pets are not Assets; Pet/device location does not flow through human Identity. No diagnosis, prognosis, treatment or medical causation follows from changed observations. No medical, life-safety or emergency path depends on an LLM; native/deterministic/manual safety remains independent.

All future source eligibility, meaningful-change review, retention, correction, disclosure, notification and action questions remain OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTIONS. No runtime, scheduler, camera pipeline, learning system, hook, consent engine, platform qualification or acceptance mechanism is designed here.

## Primary scenarios

Primary files: **0 developed + 10 Vision = 10**. Existing coverage and aliases do not add primary files or change target maturity.

- [What newly authorized household information needs review?](what-newly-authorized-household-information-needs-review.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Newly authorized information may deserve human attention without needing immediate ingestion. The household wants the purpose and review scope preserved, so granting access to one source does not automatically turn all its new material into shared knowledge or a persistent review obligation.
- [Which new documents appear relevant to existing Assets?](which-new-documents-appear-relevant-to-existing-assets.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A new document may seem relevant to an existing Asset while its identifiers remain ambiguous. The household wants the candidate explained before any authoritative association, including records that are useful but belong to another Person or a different version of the equipment.
- [What evidence conflicts with existing records?](what-evidence-conflicts-with-existing-records.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Newly authorized evidence can disagree with an existing Asset record or other household claim. The household wants competing dates, versions and source limits exposed, rather than a model deciding that the new claim replaces authoritative Truth or hiding the disagreement in a summary.
- [Which candidate relationships require household confirmation?](which-candidate-relationships-require-household-confirmation.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A candidate relationship may be plausible without being appropriate to retain. The household wants the supporting and conflicting evidence considered by eligible people, while distinguishing confirmation of a description from permission to create an authoritative Asset, care or provenance relationship.
- [Which information cannot safely be classified?](which-information-cannot-safely-be-classified.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Some information remains ambiguous or too sensitive for a proposed category. The household wants a legitimate unclassified result rather than a model forcing every record into a label, especially when the label itself would disclose protected personal context to the review audience.
- [Which derived conclusions need review?](which-derived-conclusions-need-review.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A derived conclusion may depend on weak, stale or contradictory evidence. The household wants those specific limitations available for human review, rather than a polished answer becoming durable knowledge merely because it was generated successfully or previously repeated without challenge.
- [What changed in an authorized source?](what-changed-in-an-authorized-source.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. An authorized source can change its records while earlier answers remain in circulation. The household wants attributable differences and applicable dates understood, because a new version may correct, supersede or merely add context without making every earlier interpretation wrong.
- [Which records should be associated?](which-records-should-be-associated.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Association can connect a record to useful household context but may also disclose ownership or significance. The household wants candidate links considered under independent source and audience grants, not a general review question silently accepting every plausible relationship.
- [Which records should be corrected?](which-records-should-be-corrected.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Records can contain questionable dates, identifiers or claims without a model knowing the correct replacement. The household wants potential correction needs distinguished from verified errors, so review does not overwrite originals, erase disagreement or fabricate a more coherent record.
- [Which should be rejected?](which-should-be-rejected.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Some candidates may be irrelevant, unreliable or outside the permitted purpose. The household wants rejection considered without deleting authoritative originals or suppressing inconvenient evidence, because rejecting a proposed use is different from declaring the source false or revoking another person's access.
