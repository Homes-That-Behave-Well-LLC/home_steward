# Which previously deferred ideas are now possible

> Proposed synthetic scenario; no actual wish list, reopened requirement or accepted proposal.
> Applies [Possibility and Improvement Discovery](../architecture/possibility-and-improvement-discovery.md).

## 1. Household request

"Which things we wanted before are now possible?" Resolve eligible desired outcomes and why they
were deferred, distinguishing technical limitation from rejection on governance/value grounds.

## 2. Existing HTBW knowledge

Owner-held requirements/rejected designs and Foundation configuration; Continuity eligible context;
Stewardship declared needs; Truth Historical Facts and authoritative Decision Traces.

## 3. Authorized private retrieval

Minimum approved wish/deferred requirement, historical blocker and unfulfilled-request references.
Voice/log/history sources need separate grants; no unrestricted historical-interest profile.

## 4. Optional public research

Match current [official HA release evidence](https://www.home-assistant.io/blog/categories/core/)
and feature-specific developer/integration documentation. No private rejected design/context uploaded.

## 5. Home Steward synthesis

For each candidate give original desired outcome, historical blocker, what changed, whether the blocker
is actually removed, remaining constraints and recommended next step. Preserve evidence of partial
removal, unchanged support and uncertainty rather than treating newer technology as sufficient.

**Technical blocker removed does not imply governance objection removed.** Do not reopen privacy,
authority or household-value rejections or deliberately retired experiences merely because a feature
exists. Identify expected benefit/new obligation; current authorized review may still choose no action.

## 6. Provenance

Original date/reason/version, official publication/review/effective version, installed applicability,
GA/Labs/preview/deprecation/removal and missing coverage. Inference is not Truth or accepted intent.

## 7. Candidate relationships

Possible blocker-to-capability association remains a candidate, not an reopened issue/decision or
accepted requirement. Temporal coincidence is not proof the original limitation disappeared.

## 8. Household confirmation

An eligible participant confirms present interest and constraints, not blanket permission to implement.
Previous rejection/consent records remain provenance and are never silently erased.

## 9. Governed durable relationship

Use responsible owner-held requirement/document references; final wish-list schema/capture remains open.
No runtime model, acceptance queue, persisted Pattern store or automatic requirement state change.

## 10. Proposed actions

Propose a bounded review through HTBW/Operational Trust with outcome, obligations, exceptions,
manual control, reversible experiment/removal path and acceptance criteria.

## 11. Prohibited autonomous actions

No requirement/decision reopening, issue edits, automation/scripts, configuration/policy/preference/
obligation change, HA update/rollback, Labs enablement or autonomous upstream work.

## 12. HTBW authority

Foundation records; Stewardship care; Truth Facts; Identity requester; Continuity context;
Operational Trust permissions; Concierge governed formulation/orchestration, not acceptance ownership.

## 13. Privacy and locality

Minimized approved requirement/Person/purpose/audience scope; local derivations inherit lifecycle.
Denied or aged-out history does not create consent to retrieve alternate private sources.

## 14. Unavailable / degraded behavior

Missing reason for deferral or stale platform evidence yields "I don't know whether reconsideration
is justified". Preserve native operation and existing governance choices.

## 15. Acceptance expectations

All six per-candidate fields; blocker removal evidenced and governance objections intact.
Confirmed interest is evidence only; no reopening, automatic adoption or forced recommendation.

## 16. Open questions

Governed wish/deferred evidence capture, freshness and contextual review remain future requirements;
no ingestion, storage schema or implementation mechanism.
