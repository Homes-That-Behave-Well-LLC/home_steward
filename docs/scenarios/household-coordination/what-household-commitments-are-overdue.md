Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

What household commitments are overdue?

## Why it matters

People can struggle to distinguish an overdue household obligation from an old note or an appointment that has simply passed. A clear explanation can reduce uncertainty without assigning blame, inventing a deadline, or converting another person's private commitment into a shared household demand.
