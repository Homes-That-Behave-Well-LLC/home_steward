# Which capabilities have not been exercised recently enough to know whether they still work?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

Which capabilities have not been exercised recently enough to know whether they still work?

## Why it matters

A capability that has not been used may look healthy without any recent outcome evidence. The household wants lack of exercise separated from successful operation and from deliberate nonparticipation. Recent enough depends on the declared concern and evidence context, not a new model-authored testing interval.

## Important boundary

No exercise is not proof of failure, and silence is not proof of success. No testing schedule, capability probe or new obligation is created.

Evidence categories are not source admission. Person/source/purpose/namespace/audience authorization remains independent before retrieval, combination and disclosure. One Person's grant is not another's; summaries retain the most restrictive applicable source constraints. USE != INGEST != DERIVE != RETAIN. Source dates, uncertainty, correction, retention, expiry, deletion and revocation remain binding within the household-controlled network boundary; ordinary retention may be zero/disabled. Confirmation does not grant authority to retain, disclose or act. No shadow history or silent cloud/source fallback.

Observation, report and inference are not automatic Truth. Correlation is not causation; unknown is valid. Issued command != observed physical result. People and Pets are not Assets. No medical, life-safety or emergency path depends on Home Steward or an LLM.
