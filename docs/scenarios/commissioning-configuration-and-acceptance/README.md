# Commissioning, Configuration and Acceptance

Navigation and scenario-capture guidance only; not architecture, a roadmap or delivered functionality. Browse the [scenario-first map](../README.md).

## Purpose

Compare declared configuration, physical observations, original acceptance and current lived outcomes without a drift, commissioning or remediation engine.

## Relationship to HTBW

Foundation owns declared definitions and configured relationships; Stewardship owns household-declared significance, care, obligations and Custody; Identity supplies purpose-specific Person context, not authentication or authorization; Truth owns qualified current and historical Facts; Continuity owns accepted preferences and experience intent; Operational Trust determines permission and disclosure for this request, in this context; Concierge retains governed orchestration and authoritative Decision Trace composition. Native Explainability remains HTBW-owned. Home Assistant and devices retain execution. Home Steward does not require the standalone predecessor Asset Intelligence integration.

See the [HTBW boundary](../../architecture/htbw-boundary.md), [source authorization](../../architecture/source-authorization.md) and [derived lifecycle](../../architecture/derived-knowledge-lifecycle.md).

## Boundaries

Evidence categories are not source admission. Person/source/purpose/namespace/audience authorization remains independent before retrieval, combination and disclosure. One Person's grant is not another's; summaries retain the most restrictive applicable source constraints. USE != INGEST != DERIVE != RETAIN. Source dates, uncertainty, correction, retention, expiry, deletion and revocation remain binding within the household-controlled network boundary; ordinary retention may be zero/disabled. Confirmation does not grant authority to retain, disclose or act. No shadow history or silent cloud/source fallback.

Observation, report and inference are not automatic Truth. Correlation is not causation; unknown is valid. Issued command != observed physical result. People and Pets are not Assets. No medical, life-safety or emergency path depends on Home Steward or an LLM. Scenario capture only: no runtime, hook, interface, schema, handler, pipeline, scheduling, access decision or configuration change is specified or authorized.

## Primary scenarios

Primary files: **0 developed + 19 Vision = 19**. Existing coverage and aliases do not add primary files or change target maturity.

Configured, physically observed, accepted and currently lived are different claims. Drift is a candidate finding, not an automatic Foundation edit or acceptance revocation. Input/microphone and output/speaker functions are separately configured and governed. No fixed role coupling, commissioning engine, network change or AI-generated configuration occurs.

New primary files in this increment: **19**. Each is catalogued only in this primary family. All earlier bodies and maturity remain unchanged.

- [Does the configured Home still match the physical Home?](does-the-configured-home-still-match-the-physical-home.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. An accepted configuration can remain unchanged while equipment, placement or household use changes physically. The household wants declared structure compared with dated observations without treating either as an infallible current inventory. A mismatch may be temporary, a stale record or a genuine matter for owner review.
- [Which devices appear to have moved rooms?](which-devices-appear-to-have-moved-rooms.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A device appearing in another Room may have been temporarily carried, replaced or misidentified. The household wants the apparent change and dates understood before anyone assumes a permanent move or reassigns its interaction role. Its physical placement and configured Room relationship may legitimately differ.
- [Does every configured room still have the correct voice-input and audio-output relationships?](does-every-configured-room-still-have-the-correct-voice-input-and-audio-output-relationships.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A microphone associated with the wrong Room can misframe a request even when speech capture works. A speaker associated with the wrong Room can disclose an answer to a different audience even when audio playback works. The household wants both assignments reviewed independently across configured Rooms; co-location or a working input must not be taken as proof that output is correct.
- [Which Assets are not where HTBW expects them to be?](which-assets-are-not-where-htbw-expects-them-to-be.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. An Asset may not be where its descriptive record places it, while a dated observation still cannot establish its current location. The household wants expected, last observed and qualified current placement distinguished. A borrowed or temporarily displayed object should not become a permanent Foundation relocation because the comparison appears persuasive.
- [Which configured capabilities no longer appear to exist?](which-configured-capabilities-no-longer-appear-to-exist.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A capability can disappear from an interface while its device remains installed, or remain configured after equipment is removed. The household wants existence, participation, exposure and availability distinguished before calling it missing. Loss of discoverability alone does not establish retirement or permission to delete its relationships.
- [Which new devices have appeared but are not yet governed?](which-new-devices-have-appeared-but-are-not-yet-governed.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A newly observed device can be useful, irrelevant or intentionally outside household participation. The household wants an apparent gap in declared governance understood without automatic enrollment. Network visibility does not establish ownership, consent, a Room assignment or a valued household outcome.
- [Which entity names no longer match their physical purpose?](which-entity-names-no-longer-match-their-physical-purpose.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. An entity name can preserve an old installation purpose after the underlying device or use changes. The household wants naming, configured meaning and physical function compared without treating a label as authoritative semantics. Renaming alone might hide rather than resolve an incorrect relationship.
- [Did a device replacement preserve the correct relationships?](did-a-device-replacement-preserve-the-correct-relationships.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Replacing a device may leave its name intact while changing the Room, Asset, input, output or service relationships that matter. The household wants the actual replacement compared with accepted relationships rather than accepting a responding entity as a complete replacement. Missing evidence must not be filled by assumptions about a similar model.
- [Which wall kiosk appears to be presenting the wrong room experience?](which-wall-kiosk-appears-to-be-presenting-the-wrong-room-experience.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A wall kiosk can load successfully yet present another Room's controls, help or private context. The household wants the configured surface relationship distinguished from what is currently shown and who can see it. A confusing display is not permission to inspect excluded content or automatically switch experiences.
- [Are cameras, sensors, remotes, voice endpoints, televisions or speakers assigned inconsistently?](are-cameras-sensors-remotes-voice-endpoints-televisions-or-speakers-assigned-inconsistently.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Cameras, sensors, remotes, voice endpoints, televisions and speakers may each have different configured functions. A household can notice apparent inconsistencies without knowing whether the assignments are intentional. The useful outcome is a role-specific comparison, not a rule that every device in a Room must share the same input, output or audience relationship.
- [Which documentation describes equipment that is no longer installed?](which-documentation-describes-equipment-that-is-no-longer-installed.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A useful manual or operating note can describe equipment that has been retired, replaced or kept elsewhere. The household wants its historical value separated from current applicability. Deleting it as stale could erase legitimate service evidence, while presenting it as current could mislead a person preparing work.
- [Which accepted capabilities now produce materially different outcomes?](which-accepted-capabilities-now-produce-materially-different-outcomes.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. An accepted capability may still respond technically while producing a materially different lived experience. The household wants the original accepted outcome compared with current evidence, including a single affected capability, repeated workarounds or reasons for a lived-outcome review. A changed experience can deserve understanding without revoking acceptance or blaming a particular update.
- [Which acceptance assumptions are no longer true?](which-acceptance-assumptions-are-no-longer-true.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Acceptance may have relied on a particular Room arrangement, audience, provider support or household use. The household wants those recorded assumptions compared with current evidence rather than treating them as permanently true. A challenged assumption is not itself a failed capability or authority to rewrite the original acceptance.
- [Did a software or firmware update invalidate an acceptance test?](did-a-software-or-firmware-update-invalidate-an-acceptance-test.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. An update can change what a test exercises without visibly breaking the Home. The household wants exact version and test applicability understood separately from whether the capability currently works. A later software date alone cannot prove that the prior acceptance test became invalid or that acceptance should be withdrawn.
- [Which capabilities have not been exercised recently enough to know whether they still work?](which-capabilities-have-not-been-exercised-recently-enough-to-know-whether-they-still-work.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A capability that has not been used may look healthy without any recent outcome evidence. The household wants lack of exercise separated from successful operation and from deliberate nonparticipation. Recent enough depends on the declared concern and evidence context, not a new model-authored testing interval.
- [Which manual fallbacks have never been tested?](which-manual-fallbacks-have-never-been-tested.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A fallback may be documented, actually exercised or still unproven under the relevant outage conditions. The household wants tested manual alternatives distinguished from document-only and never-exercised procedures, including which experiences lack qualifying evidence. A procedure tested in another context must not become an assurance that anyone can safely use it today.
- [Does the current room configuration still satisfy the original acceptance criteria?](does-the-current-room-configuration-still-satisfy-the-original-acceptance-criteria.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Room configuration may satisfy individual assignments while no longer satisfying the experience the household accepted. The household wants original criteria, current exposure and actual Room outcomes compared without confusing physical placement with acceptance. New household needs remain questions for review, not silent additions to the criteria.
- [Which capabilities passed in HA-DEV but behave differently in the Reference Home?](which-capabilities-passed-in-ha-dev-but-behave-differently-in-the-reference-home.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A capability can pass in HA-DEV yet encounter different people, Rooms, devices or dependencies in a lived Home. The household wants those conditions and outcomes compared without treating a development result as production acceptance. Neither environment supplies private evidence or authorization for the other by default.
- [What evidence would be required before declaring that configuration drift exists?](what-evidence-would-be-required-before-declaring-that-configuration-drift-exists.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. An apparent mismatch can arise from stale documentation, temporary placement, incomplete observation or a wrong object match. The household wants the evidence needed to distinguish these possibilities before calling the condition configuration drift. A confident label must not silently change Foundation or imply that accepted behavior has been rejected.
