## Why it matters

An accepted capability may still respond technically while producing a materially different lived experience. The household wants the original accepted outcome compared with current evidence, including a single affected capability, repeated workarounds or reasons for a lived-outcome review. A changed experience can deserve understanding without revoking acceptance or blaming a particular update.
