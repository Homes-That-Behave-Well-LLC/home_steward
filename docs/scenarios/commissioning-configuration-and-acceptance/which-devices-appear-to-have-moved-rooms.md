# Which devices appear to have moved rooms?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

Which devices appear to have moved rooms?

## Why it matters

A device appearing in another Room may have been temporarily carried, replaced or misidentified. The household wants the apparent change and dates understood before anyone assumes a permanent move or reassigns its interaction role. Its physical placement and configured Room relationship may legitimately differ.
