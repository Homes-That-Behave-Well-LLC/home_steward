## Why it matters

A provider's mold-risk indicator can increase without establishing mold or a health condition. The household wants to understand what the source actually derives and what changed nearby in time, while unsupported causes and missing history remain unresolved.

Household-specific governed care outranks generic advice; no HVAC, window or filter-control action is authorized by explanation. Source reports and inference are not automatic Truth; unknown is a valid result. No silent cloud or source fallback.
