## Household question

Did the air-filter change improve particulate levels?

## Why it matters

After a filter change, particulate readings may differ because of source changes, outdoor air or household activity. The household wants an honest lived-outcome comparison, not automatic proof that maintenance was completed correctly or that the intervention caused the observed result.
