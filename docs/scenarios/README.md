# Scenario-family map

Home Steward is being developed **scenario first**. This library preserves household questions,
experiences, problems, opportunities and future possibilities before deciding how any solution
should be architected.

**SCENARIO -> RECURRING REQUIREMENT -> ARCHITECTURE -> GOVERNED IMPLEMENTATION -> ACCEPTANCE -> LIVED EVIDENCE**

Not: technology -> invent use case -> implement.

Inclusion is **not a product roadmap, ownership decision, platform choice or acceptance**. A scenario
may ultimately be satisfied by HTBW, Home Assistant, Home Steward, a private-AI platform, a specialist
system, a professional, deterministic automation, another governed capability, or **no technology**.
Recurring questions may reveal requirements; they do not authorize a new runtime mechanism.

## Scenario maturity

Maturity belongs **inside the scenario file**, not only in its directory. Developed and Vision
scenarios share the same family folders; there is no separate canonical-versus-Vision tree.

| Status | Meaning and limit |
|---|---|
| CANONICAL SCENARIO | Developed household scenario retained at its prior content maturity; not proof of accepted solution architecture, Owner PASS or implemented/runtime behavior. |
| VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED | Short-form desired experience and potential evidence; solution ownership and mechanisms remain open. |
| ARCHITECTURE RESEARCH REQUIRED | A separately reviewed requirement needs architecture evidence; no implementation authority follows. |
| SUPERSEDED | Explicitly replaced with a named successor and retained historical context; never silently deleted. |
| OUT OF SCOPE | Explicitly dispositioned outside the current scope; exclusion is recorded, not inferred. |
| DUPLICATE / COVERED ELSEWHERE | Explicit coverage with a primary link; not a second canonical copy or a downgrade of the target. |

These are scenario-document statuses, **not** the separate seven-level opportunity maturity model,
Operational Trust autonomy levels, platform qualification or implementation acceptance. This increment
preserves 78 prior developed scenarios and creates Vision content only. No scenario is newly promoted
to accepted architecture or runtime.

## Formats

The unchanged numbered prose of the [sixteen-section canonical template](scenario-template.md)
remains authoritative for fully developed scenarios. Its Catalog links were repaired after migration.

Short-form Vision files deliberately do **not** duplicate that full template. They have a title,
`Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED`, and exactly these content sections:

1. Household question — natural household wording.
2. Why it matters — one to three substantive paragraphs about the desired outcome.
3. Existing HTBW relationship — already-owned capabilities and knowledge, with no responsibility transfer.
4. Possible Home Steward extension — retrieval, synthesis, comparison, context or exploration only.
5. Potential evidence — categories, not admitted sources or access grants.
6. Important boundary — authority, privacy, safety, uncertainty and professional limits.
7. Future questions — unresolved evidence, ownership and applicability.

Any future representation, source admission, handoff, handler, catcher, hook, notification or
implementation remains an **OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION**. Mentioning a need does not name or design
its mechanism. Do not add technical schemas, services or contracts merely because several questions
look similar.

## Promotion

1. Preserve and clarify the desired household outcome, beneficiary, scope and evidence gaps.
2. Compare existing developed coverage and native HTBW/HA, specialist, professional or nontechnical options.
3. Identify recurring requirements without inferring consent, importance, ownership or permission.
4. Seek separately authorized architecture research; explicitly record disposition and the responsible context.
5. Develop the full canonical scenario when warranted, preserving boundaries, alternatives and evidence requirements.
6. Govern any implementation separately, then require exact-version acceptance and later lived evidence.

There is no automatic transition, roadmap deadline, central approval inbox or architecture contract
in this catalog. Confirmation of an idea is not action authorization or Owner acceptance. Native
HTBW responsibilities stay native; contextual acceptance stays with its accepted owning lifecycle.

NOTICE != DIAGNOSE; NOTICE != DISCLOSE; NOTICE != RETAIN; NOTICE != RECOMMEND; NOTICE != ACT. Noticing is not authority or automatic Truth, household significance, notification or action.

Useful awareness need not begin with a known household question. Ordinary activity, changed evidence
and a candidate matter worth understanding are distinct. An ambient origin does not require a
second file where the existing bounded inquiry already covers the requested experience; cross-links
do not add monitoring, notifications or authority. New entries do not borrow earlier Owner acceptance.

Visual LAST OBSERVED is not automatic CURRENT LOCATION. Person/source/purpose/namespace/audience
gates remain independent; USE != INGEST != DERIVE != RETAIN. Derived lineage, source deletion,
revocation and corrections stay binding. **GOVERNED HOUSEHOLD KNOWLEDGE SHOULD NOT BE TRAPPED IN MODEL WEIGHTS.**
No runtime, scheduler/agent, camera ingestion, personalization/fine-tuning, routing or HTBW hook is designed.

## Shared boundaries and navigation

HTBW Foundation, Truth, Identity, Stewardship, Continuity, Operational Trust and Concierge retain
their accepted responsibilities. Native Explainability includes historical/time/cross-Room retrieval;
Home Steward is not required for those native questions. Custody stays HTBW-owned, including its
lifecycle, history, expected-return obligations, reminders and insurance responsibility.

Conservation advisory never replaces HTBW Risk or Asset-specific governed care. Professionals retain
conservation, appraisal, authentication, legal, veterinary and clinical judgment. People and Pets are
not Assets; Pet/device location does not flow through human Identity. No medical or life-safety path
depends on Home Steward or an LLM.

Person/source/purpose/namespace/audience scope, household-controlled locality, source sensitivity and
derived-data revocation/retention remain binding. Unknown is valid; observation, source report and
inference are not automatic Truth; correlation is not causation. Knowledge is not action or
disclosure authority. There is no silent cloud/source fallback, continuous recording, shadow history,
new memory responsibility or notification mechanism.
