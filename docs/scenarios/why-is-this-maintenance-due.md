# Why is this maintenance due

> Proposed synthetic scenario; no real care cycle or runtime result.

## 1. Household request

"Why is this due?", "Is this inspect or replace?", "Can we move the care cycle?"

## 2. Existing HTBW knowledge

Stewardship supplies the obligation, trigger, cycle, last eligible service evidence and applicable
requirements. Foundation supplies Asset/manual associations. Truth owns observed condition.

## 3. Authorized private retrieval

Approved exact-model manual, maintenance records, technician recommendations and known warranty
terms. No unapproved source joins or person correspondence.

## 4. Optional public research

Permitted manufacturer updates/general care context, clearly external and model-bounded.
No private service history sent to public research.

## 5. Home Steward synthesis

HTBW can explain a configured due date natively. Home Steward adds document interpretation only
where helpful: compare inspect versus replace guidance and identify differences/gaps. It cannot
infer that a previous service satisfied this obligation or silently replace the configured cycle.

## 6. Provenance

Obligation/trigger reference, evidence date, manual revision and interpretation versus HTBW decision.
Distinguish required care from optional general advice.

## 7. Candidate relationships

A service record may relate to an obligation, but possible relevance is not satisfaction evidence.

## 8. Household confirmation

A Person's report of completed work is evidence subject to the responsible owner's process.
Agreeing to review an interval is not automatic authorization to change it.

## 9. Governed durable relationship

Foundation associates documents; Stewardship evaluates obligation/schedule/satisfaction changes.
Do not invent state/write semantics.

## 10. Proposed actions

Propose reviewing discrepant guidance or scheduling authorized care. HTBW/Operational Trust must
evaluate modifications and contact/booking before execution.

## 11. Prohibited autonomous actions

No dismissing/replacing/satisfying obligations, changing cycles, assuming condition, contacting
service or paying because a model recommendation exists.

## 12. HTBW authority

Stewardship care; Truth condition; Foundation structure; Identity/Continuity request context;
Operational Trust permission/disclosure; Concierge orchestration.

## 13. Privacy and locality

Approved records only; local synthesis/derived indexes remain scoped. Spoken output must not disclose
private correspondence/account details to an ineligible audience.

## 14. Unavailable / degraded behavior

Native due explanation and obligations persist. Explain missing manual/context; no model/cloud
substitute for an authoritative schedule.

## 15. Acceptance expectations

Due reason remains HTBW-owned; inspect/replace interpretation cites evidence; uncertainty is visible;
no care cycle or completion changes without the accepted owner process.

## 16. Open questions

Care-evidence eligibility, review/change workflow, requester authority and conflict resolution remain
in their governing HTBW work, not settled by this scenario.
