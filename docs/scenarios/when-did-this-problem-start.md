# When did this problem start

> Proposed synthetic scenario; no actual latency measurements or update history.
> Applies the [investigation evidence rules](../architecture/knowledge-architecture.md#evidence-and-epistemic-rules).

## 1. Household request

"The Living Room lights seem slower than they used to be. When did that start?"
Define which lights, which timing measure and the comparison interval.

## 2. Existing HTBW knowledge

Foundation identifies targets/configuration; authoritative Decision Traces explain evaluations.
Truth Historical Facts describe observations; exact Change Records distinguish old/new configuration.
Native historical reconstruction remains HTBW's, not replaced by Home Steward.

## 3. Authorized private retrieval

Minimum matched before/after traces, command/acknowledgement/state timing where retained, eligible HA
integration/log history through a separate future relationship, configuration/update records and
network evidence only under its own authorization.

## 4. Optional public research

Permitted version-specific release notes may identify documented timing behavior; no private logs
or network configuration uploaded. Applicability remains uncertain until supported.

## 5. Home Steward synthesis

Compare equivalent targets, operations and observation opportunities before and after. Distinguish
evaluation time, command latency, acknowledgement and observed physical outcome. Hypothetically,
two comparable windows show rising acknowledgement latency; that does not prove slower physical
lighting when outcome timing is missing. Bound onset between last adequate baseline and first
adequate changed window; first retained evidence is not necessarily the actual start.

## 6. Provenance

Record sample size, window/exclusions, units/distribution, clock precision and gaps. Cite trace
timing, Historical Facts, HA operational logs, exact configuration versions and update source separately.
A nearby change is correlation, not a causal root-cause finding.

## 7. Candidate relationships

Update, availability or network changes are competing hypotheses. Temporal coincidence does not
create a causation reference or diagnose the integration/device.

## 8. Household confirmation

Confirm the intended operation and perceived latency, as attributed feedback, not proof of cause
or permission to modify timeout/network settings.

## 9. Governed durable relationship

No inferred onset is published as Truth or written over historical records. Foundation/responsible
configuration owners govern eligible associations; no new latency-history authority.

## 10. Proposed actions

Propose collecting a discriminating, authorized timing observation or reviewing a relevant change
through HTBW/Operational Trust; Concierge orchestrates permitted work.

## 11. Prohibited autonomous actions

No policy/preference edits, integration disablement, automation removal, obligation changes,
rollback, restart, device replacement, scheduling or vendor contact without HTBW authority.

## 12. HTBW authority

Truth Facts; Foundation configuration; Concierge Trace/explanation; Continuity context; Stewardship
care; Identity requester; Operational Trust source/disclosure/action. Timing inference owns none.

## 13. Privacy and locality

Retrieve minimum Person-safe technical evidence; logs/network history are separately scoped.
Local derived timelines inherit lifecycle; no automatic indefinite raw-log memory or invented expired-content reconstruction.

## 14. Unavailable / degraded behavior

If older timing aged out or was never measured, say "I don't know when it started." Do not compare
today's configuration with fabricated past versions; native behavior continues without enrichment.

## 15. Acceptance expectations

Before/after windows are comparable or explicitly limited; onset is bounded to evidence precision;
acknowledgement is not physical outcome; alternative explanations and missing evidence remain visible.

## 16. Open questions

Timing source semantics, minimum comparable observations, clock alignment and future query/retention
controls require separate design; no instrumentation or ingestion is implemented.
