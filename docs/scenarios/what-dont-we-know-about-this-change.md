# What don't we know about this change

> Aging in Place and Care Awareness: synthetic, person-scoped, non-diagnostic experience capture.

## 1. Household request

"What don't we know about what has changed?" Make evidence limits as visible as observations.

## 2. Existing HTBW knowledge

Truth current/historical Home Facts, Foundation Person context, eligible Identity, Stewardship care
and existing Explainability. Unknown, unavailable and never observed are not interchangeable.

## 3. Authorized private retrieval

Potential independently approved Person observations/history and source quality/coverage information.
An evidence gap does not authorize extra wearable/history collection or access to another Person.

## 4. Optional public research

Source/device documentation may explain measurement limitations, not diagnose missing evidence.
Protected Person/care data never become public query context.

## 5. Home Steward synthesis

Separate known observations, missing measurements, unavailable history, insufficient personal baseline,
uncertainty, competing explanations and evidence needed before stronger conclusions.
"I don't know" is valid; first retained evidence is not necessarily actual onset, and correlation is not cause.

## 6. Provenance

Identify source/Person/time/quality/coverage and selected baseline/recent period; distinguish Home/
wearable reports, household narrative, comparison, inference and unknown.

## 7. Candidate relationships

Competing possible explanations remain tentative, not diagnostic conclusions or durable care records.

## 8. Household confirmation

Clarify context/gaps with an eligible participant; clarification does not authorize collecting/
disclosing new evidence. Any retention/review remains an OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION.

## 9. Governed durable relationship

OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION: no evidence-gap object, diagnostic model or care proposal schema.

## 10. Proposed actions

The household may consider human review or separately authorize relevant information later.
Collection, disclosure, notification and handoff mechanisms remain OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTIONS.

## 11. Prohibited autonomous actions

No diagnosis, medical-causation/medication-effect inference, disease/fall prediction, treatment,
clinical monitoring, emergency decision, automatic caregiver notification, ingestion or care/policy change.

## 12. HTBW authority

Truth current Home state/history; Stewardship care obligations; Identity context; Foundation definitions;
Operational Trust action/disclosure; Continuity where applicable; Concierge and existing Explainability/Trace.

## 13. Privacy and locality

Person-scoped observations/gaps stay independently governed; ingestion is not disclosure.
Local derivatives inherit sensitivity/lifecycle; expired or unauthorized evidence is not reconstructed by invention.

## 14. Unavailable / degraded behavior

Unavailable extension/source yields an explicit gap, not a reassuring clinical result or no-change claim.
Native HTBW/emergency mechanisms remain independent; no LLM critical path or silent cloud fallback.

## 15. Acceptance expectations

All seven uncertainty considerations captured; no compelled data collection, fabricated evidence,
diagnosis, new evidence model or runtime acceptance.

## 16. Open questions

Future authorized evidence admission, representation/retention and any human review/disclosure/handoff
remain OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTIONS; no diagnostic or ingestion design.
