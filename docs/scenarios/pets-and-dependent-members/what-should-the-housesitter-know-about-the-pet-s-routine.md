# What should the housesitter know about the pet's routine?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

What should the housesitter know about the pet's routine?

## Why it matters

A housesitter needs enough current information to honor a pet's already-approved routine, without receiving unrelated household or personal history. The household wants a scoped explanation of care expectations and uncertainty rather than an assistant inventing feeding, medication or access instructions.

## Existing HTBW relationship

A Pet is a non-human household member, not an Asset or a Person. HTBW Stewardship owns declared Pet care obligations; Truth owns supported current and historical Facts. Pet or device location must not be routed through human Identity.

## Possible Home Steward extension

Summarize independently permitted existing Pet care and visit information for the explicitly authorized temporary audience. Any new source admission, representation, handoff or implementation is an OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION; no mechanism is specified.

## Potential evidence

- Approved Pet care obligations
- Authorized routine and manual references
- Explicit visit scope and permitted contacts

These are possible categories only, not admitted sources. Each requires independent Person/source/purpose/audience scope; derived information inherits sensitivity, household-controlled locality and source lifecycle.

## Important boundary

This is consented household care awareness, not veterinary diagnosis, disease prediction, treatment or emergency judgment. Caretaker assignment alone does not grant source or disclosure access. No medical or life-safety path depends on Home Steward, and timing or correlation is not causation. Source reports and inference are not automatic Truth; unknown is a valid result. No silent cloud or source fallback.
