# Pets and Dependent Members

Navigation and scope guidance only; not an architecture contract or roadmap. Browse the [scenario-family map](../README.md).

## Purpose

Preserve careful Pet routine, environmental and temporary-care questions.

## Relationship to HTBW

Pets are not Assets or People. Stewardship owns declared Pet care, Truth owns supported Facts, and Pet/device location does not use human Identity. See the existing [HTBW boundary](../../architecture/htbw-boundary.md).

## Possible Home Steward extension

Compare permitted source observations with the pet's own observed pattern and approved care context. Any new source admission, representation, handoff or implementation is an OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION.

## Boundaries

No veterinary diagnosis, treatment, prediction or emergency judgment. Dispensed food is not proof of eating; an alert is not strengthened or dismissed. Caretaker assignment alone is not data access.

Person/source/purpose/namespace/audience scope, household-controlled locality and source-derived lifecycle remain independent requirements. Potential evidence is not admitted access; knowledge is not action or disclosure authority. Source reports and inference are not automatic Truth; unknown is valid and correlation is not causation.

### Vision — Not Yet Architected

- [Has the pet's routine changed?](has-the-pet-s-routine-changed.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A change in a pet's routine can concern the household, but incomplete device evidence or a changed household schedule may explain the apparent difference.
- [Did the pet eat while we were away?](did-the-pet-eat-while-we-were-away.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A feeder dispensing food or a bowl changing weight does not prove that a particular pet ate.
- [Is the Home getting too warm for the pet?](is-the-home-getting-too-warm-for-the-pet.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. The household may have declared environmental requirements for a pet that differ from a generic comfort target.
- [Has the pet door been used unusually often?](has-the-pet-door-been-used-unusually-often.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A pet-door activity change may reflect device noise, another animal or a different household routine.
- [What should the housesitter know about the pet's routine?](what-should-the-housesitter-know-about-the-pet-s-routine.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A housesitter needs enough current information to honor a pet's already-approved routine, without receiving unrelated household or personal history.
- [Has activity returned to normal after a veterinary visit?](has-activity-returned-to-normal-after-a-veterinary-visit.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. After a veterinary visit, people may want to understand how observed activity compares with the pet's prior pattern.
- [What happened around the time the pet monitor generated an alert?](what-happened-around-the-time-the-pet-monitor-generated-an-alert.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A pet-monitor alert can prompt questions about surrounding observations, but the original device report has its own scope and limits.
- [Are there rooms or environmental conditions that should be avoided?](are-there-rooms-or-environmental-conditions-that-should-be-avoided.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Some rooms or conditions may conflict with already-declared Pet care requirements or professional instructions.
