## Household question

What failed to recover after the restart?

## Why it matters

A restart can leave one function unavailable while other services appear healthy. Understanding what did not recover requires a bounded comparison and honest coverage, rather than assuming the restart caused the problem or prescribing another restart based only on temporal proximity.
