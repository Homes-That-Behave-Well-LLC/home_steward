## Household question

## Why it matters

The household wants to understand which intelligence is optional without making safety depend on a reasoning service. This is a boundary review of documented dependencies, not a declaration that the current Home is safe or that a model can validate a life-safety installation.

A dependency narrative does not authorize a restart, restore, reconfiguration or recovery order. Source reports and inference are not automatic Truth; unknown is a valid result. No silent cloud or source fallback.
