## Why it matters

Resetting a seemingly isolated system can interrupt downstream services, erase useful evidence or make a care task harder. The household wants existing restrictions and documented consequences understood before anyone attempts a reset. Unknown downstream effects are a reason for qualified review, not confidence that restarting is harmless.
