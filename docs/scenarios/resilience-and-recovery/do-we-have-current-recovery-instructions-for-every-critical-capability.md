# Do we have current recovery instructions for every critical capability?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

Do we have current recovery instructions for every critical capability?

## Why it matters

Recovery instructions can exist but be stale, inaccessible during an outage or untested. The household wants to understand documentation gaps for capabilities it has explicitly identified as important, without letting the assistant decide criticality or promise that a written plan works.

## Existing HTBW relationship

HTBW retains native capability ownership, configured dependencies, Truth availability evidence, Stewardship care and Operational Trust. Home Assistant and devices retain their own execution and manual-operation behavior; Home Steward is not required for native HTBW outcomes.

## Possible Home Steward extension

Compare authorized instruction coverage, applicability and evidence of review against household-declared capability importance. Any new source admission, representation, handoff or implementation is an OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION; no mechanism is specified.

## Potential evidence

- Household-declared significance
- Authorized recovery documents and versions
- Attributable review or restoration records

These are possible categories only, not admitted sources. Each requires independent Person/source/purpose/audience scope; derived information inherits sensitivity, household-controlled locality and source lifecycle.

## Important boundary

Resilience includes deliberate continuation, safe stopping, degradation and recovery, not keeping everything running forever. No life-safety or emergency path may depend on Home Steward or an LLM. A dependency narrative does not authorize a restart, restore, reconfiguration or recovery order. Source reports and inference are not automatic Truth; unknown is a valid result. No silent cloud or source fallback.
