# What should someone else know if I am unavailable

Status: CANONICAL SCENARIO

Developed-content maturity only; not Owner, runtime or architecture acceptance.

> Proposed synthetic scenario; no personal contingency, health or estate information.

## 1. Household request

"What should someone else know if I am unavailable?"

## 2. Existing HTBW knowledge

Foundation eligible Assets/capabilities/documents; Stewardship care; Identity/Operational Trust
authorized beneficiaries; Continuity applicable context. Private AI memory is not Continuity.

## 3. Authorized private retrieval

Explicitly approved contingency/service/manual fields for a named eligible beneficiary.
No automatic health, finance, credentials or mailbox inclusion.

## 4. Optional public research

Usually unnecessary; generic manufacturer care context if approved.

## 5. Home Steward synthesis

Draft a minimized, audience-specific Home-care briefing, gaps and source dates. Do not infer
incapacity, death, consent, succession, identity delegation or future authority from the request.

## 6. Provenance

Approved source/beneficiary/purpose and draft freshness; distinguish native duties from suggestions.

## 7. Candidate relationships

Possible document-to-care relevance remains candidate; no automatic Person delegation.

## 8. Household confirmation

Confirm contents and beneficiary separately from delivery and ongoing permissions.

## 9. Governed durable relationship

Any record/beneficiary relationship returns to Foundation/Identity/Operational Trust's existing
process. No new delegation schema or Home Steward authority is created.

## 10. Proposed actions

Propose review and separately authorized sharing of the minimized briefing through HTBW.

## 11. Prohibited autonomous actions

No credential handoff, consent/delegation inference, financial/health disclosure, automatic delivery,
payments or role changes.

## 12. HTBW authority

Identity consent lifecycle; Foundation records; Operational Trust policy; Continuity context;
Truth Facts; Stewardship care; Concierge orchestration.

## 13. Privacy and locality

Each beneficiary/source/question purpose is independently scoped. Local draft/memory inherits
source constraints; a possible future emergency does not waive current privacy.

## 14. Unavailable / degraded behavior

Eligible native Home information remains usable; unavailable synthesis never creates broad disclosure.

## 15. Acceptance expectations

Named audience/minimization, no delegation inferred, delivery separately governed and revocation
applied to derived drafts.

## 16. Open questions

Beneficiary authorization, revocation/expiry, contingency activation, identity assurance and delivery
workflow remain explicit governance questions.
