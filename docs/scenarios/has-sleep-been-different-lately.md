# Has sleep been different lately

> Aging in Place and Care Awareness: synthetic, person-scoped, non-diagnostic experience capture.

## 1. Household request

"Has sleep been different lately?" Understand reported sleep changes, not obtain a diagnosis.

## 2. Existing HTBW knowledge

Foundation Person/Room definitions, eligible Identity context, Truth current/historical Home Facts
and Stewardship care. Home inactivity is not proof of sleep.

## 3. Authorized private retrieval

Potential Person-authorized device-reported sleep, household observations and retained history.
Wearable ownership, family/caretaker role or available data does not grant access/disclosure.

## 4. Optional public research

Device documentation may clarify reported duration/fragmentation and limitations; no clinical inference.
Protected sleep/care details do not enter public queries.

## 5. Home Steward synthesis

Clarify "lately" and personal comparison periods. Describe trend, personal baseline, duration,
fragmentation, evidence quality and missing evidence; separate device estimates from actual observations.
A sleep change is not a diagnosis or proof of its medical cause.

## 6. Provenance

Preserve device/provider-qualified reports, source/version/time, chosen baseline/recent period,
quality/coverage and uncertainty; label household narrative and inference separately.

## 7. Candidate relationships

Possible sleep/context overlaps remain candidates, not medical causation or accepted care records.

## 8. Household confirmation

Clarify dates/context and the person's report; agreement is not processing/disclosure authority.
Future retention/review remains an OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION.

## 9. Governed durable relationship

OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION: no sleep/clinical model, care proposal or baseline object.

## 10. Proposed actions

The household may review observations or prepare independently authorized questions for a human.
Any caregiver/clinician disclosure or handoff is an OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION.

## 11. Prohibited autonomous actions

No diagnosis, disease/fall prediction, medication-effect inference, treatment, clinical monitoring,
emergency decision, automatic caregiver notification or care/policy alteration.

## 12. HTBW authority

Truth current Home state/history; Stewardship care obligations; Identity context; Foundation definitions;
Operational Trust action/disclosure; Continuity where applicable; Concierge and existing Explainability/Trace.

## 13. Privacy and locality

Person-scoped sleep data remains independently governed; ingestion is not disclosure permission.
Local derivations inherit sensitivity/lifecycle; no indefinite sleep corpus or public private-data queries.

## 14. Unavailable / degraded behavior

"I don't know whether sleep changed" is valid for missing/denied/poor-quality evidence, not a reassuring
clinical finding. Native HTBW/emergency paths stay independent; no LLM critical path or cloud fallback.

## 15. Acceptance expectations

All six sleep considerations, personal comparison and source estimates captured; no medical normality,
diagnostic capability, sleep algorithm or runtime acceptance.

## 16. Open questions

Device evidence eligibility, comparison/retention and any human review/disclosure/handoff remain
OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTIONS; no clinical or ingestion mechanism.
