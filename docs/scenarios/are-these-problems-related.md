# Are these problems related

> Proposed synthetic scenario; no actual Sonos, TV or network incident evidence.
> Applies the [investigation evidence rules](../architecture/knowledge-architecture.md#evidence-and-epistemic-rules).

## 1. Household request

"The Sonos disappeared twice and the TVs went unavailable yesterday. Were those related?"
Resolve the technical systems and observation interval without assuming a common cause.

## 2. Existing HTBW knowledge

Foundation declared system/dependency relationships; Truth Historical availability Facts;
authoritative Decision Traces for relevant HTBW evaluations. Native historical/correlation
reconstruction remains HTBW's, not Home Steward-owned.

## 3. Authorized private retrieval

Minimum Sonos/TV availability evidence, relevant integration/HA logs, reload/restart records and
declared dependencies. HA log access is a separately governed future source. Network evidence
requires its own explicit source/account/field/time authorization, not inferred LAN entitlement.

## 4. Optional public research

Permitted version-specific technical documentation may contextualize a reported condition.
Do not disclose household topology, device identifiers or private logs in public queries.

## 5. Home Steward synthesis

Distinguish four steps: timestamp overlap; independently evidenced shared dependency; correlation;
causal root-cause evidence. Hypothetically, events overlap within supported clock precision and
systems have a documented shared dependency: "This is evidence consistent with a common
infrastructure event, but it does not establish the root cause." A reload may also affect reported
availability without proving a physical outage. If dependency evidence is absent, do not invent it.

## 6. Provenance

Cite source/version, timestamps/clock uncertainty, episode deduplication, availability semantics,
reload records and topology evidence separately. Label Historical Fact, trace, operational log,
configuration evidence, correlation and hypothesis; timing alone is not causation.

## 7. Candidate relationships

Possible common infrastructure event is a hypothesis, not a diagnosis, new Truth Fact or automatic
causation/dependency relationship.

## 8. Household confirmation

Ask about a known reload/intervention as scoped feedback, not proof of cause or authority to restart.

## 9. Governed durable relationship

Foundation governs any approved dependency correction; Truth owns qualified Facts. No overwritten
trace, invented historical relationship or competing incident/history store.

## 10. Proposed actions

Propose retrieving specific discriminating network/device evidence through its owner and HTBW/
Operational Trust. Concierge coordinates only authorized work.

## 11. Prohibited autonomous actions

No policy/preference edits, integration disablement, automation removal, obligation changes,
rollback, network/HA restart, replacement, scheduling or vendor contact without HTBW authority.

## 12. HTBW authority

Foundation dependencies; Truth Facts; Concierge Trace/explanation; Stewardship care; Identity
requester; Continuity context; Operational Trust sources/audience/actions.

## 13. Privacy and locality

Minimum authorized HA/network evidence only; network and Person privacy remain scoped before
assembly/disclosure. Local derivations inherit lifecycle; no automatic indefinite raw-log memory.

## 14. Unavailable / degraded behavior

Aged-out/never-retained topology/logs and uncertain clocks limit correlation. Say "I don't know
whether they had the same cause." Keep native HTBW operation; no guessed or cloud investigation.

## 15. Acceptance expectations

Temporal overlap, shared dependency, correlation and root cause are separately stated. Missing
topology is not filled from assumptions; no model-generated diagnosis becomes Truth or action authority.

## 16. Open questions

Network source scope, clock comparability, supported availability semantics and future query/
redaction/retention controls remain open; no network capture or log ingestion is implemented.
