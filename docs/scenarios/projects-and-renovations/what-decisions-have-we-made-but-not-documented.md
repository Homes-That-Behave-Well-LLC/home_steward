# What decisions have we made but not documented?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

What decisions have we made but not documented?

## Why it matters

Project conversations can leave people with a shared impression of a decision but no approved record. The household wants to identify voluntarily recalled topics that need clarification without reconstructing intent from activity, purchases or overrides. Absence of documentation is a gap, not proof of an agreement or permission for an AI to create one.

## Existing HTBW relationship

Foundation retains definitions/configuration; Truth, current/historical Facts; Identity, Person resolution, not authentication/authorization; Stewardship, household-declared care/obligations; Continuity, preference/experience eligibility; Operational Trust, request-specific permissions; Concierge, orchestration/Trace. Home Steward acquires no ownership.

## Important boundary

Do not infer agreement from execution or silence; no automatic decision record or commitment is created.

Approved scope and recorded commitments matter; observations/manual overrides are not proof of intent or contract performance. No procurement, execution, control or policy changes.

Knowledge/interpretation never grants action or disclosure authority. Unclear evidence stays unknown; correlation/timing is not causation. No life-safety or medical/emergency path depends on Home Steward/LLM.
