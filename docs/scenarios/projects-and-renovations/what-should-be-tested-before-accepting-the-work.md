Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

What should be tested before accepting the work?

## Why it matters

Accepting work should reflect approved household requirements and recorded commitments, not just whether an interface responds. The household wants to understand relevant documented criteria and evidence gaps before qualified humans decide what verification is necessary. Observed behavior, manual usability, accessibility and maintenance context may differ from a contractor's completion statement.
