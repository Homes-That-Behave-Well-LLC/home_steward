# What should we ask before approving this change order?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

What should we ask before approving this change order?

## Why it matters

A change order can alter cost, scope, ongoing obligations and the intended household experience. The household wants to understand unanswered documentary questions before a human approval decision, especially where the proposed change affects care or accessibility. A clear list of uncertainties should not imply that the change is acceptable, affordable or already authorized.

## Existing HTBW relationship

Foundation retains definitions/configuration; Truth, current/historical Facts; Identity, Person resolution, not authentication/authorization; Stewardship, household-declared care/obligations; Continuity, preference/experience eligibility; Operational Trust, request-specific permissions; Concierge, orchestration/Trace. Home Steward acquires no ownership.

## Potential evidence

Change-order versions; approved scope; recorded cost/commitment categories; care/accessibility requirements; documented dependency descriptions.

Categories only, each independently authorized for purpose, Person, source, audience and namespace. Local processing and derived retention, correction, expiry and revocation remain source-bound.

## Important boundary

No approval, negotiation, payment, vendor contact or contractual decision; readable terms grant no procurement authority.

Approved scope and recorded commitments matter; observations/manual overrides are not proof of intent or contract performance. No procurement, execution, control or policy changes.

Knowledge/interpretation never grants action or disclosure authority. Unclear evidence stays unknown; correlation/timing is not causation. No life-safety or medical/emergency path depends on Home Steward/LLM.
