Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

What changed between proposal and final installation?

## Why it matters

The final installation may differ from an early proposal because of approved changes, substitutions or incomplete documentation. The household wants a careful comparison of versions and observed conditions before deciding what requires human clarification. A visible difference is not proof of unauthorized work, and a working installation is not proof that every recorded commitment was met.
