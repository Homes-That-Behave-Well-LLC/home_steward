# Projects and Renovations

Navigation and scope guidance only; not an architecture contract or roadmap. Browse the [scenario-family map](../README.md).

## Purpose

Understand approved scope, unresolved choices, project evidence and household experience without managing the project autonomously.

## Relationship to HTBW

Foundation retains Asset/service relationships; Stewardship retains obligations and care. Approved agreements and native records remain source-owned. See the existing [HTBW boundary](../../architecture/htbw-boundary.md).

## Possible Home Steward extension

Reconcile authorized specifications, correspondence and observed outcomes for human discussion. Any new source admission, representation, handoff or implementation is an OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION.

## Boundaries

No generated procurement, control, scheduling or contract-performance judgment. A manual override, invoice or unconfirmed note is not proof of intent, agreement or satisfactory completion.

Person/source/purpose/namespace/audience scope, household-controlled locality and source-derived lifecycle remain independent requirements. Potential evidence is not admitted access; knowledge is not action or disclosure authority. Source reports and inference are not automatic Truth; unknown is valid and correlation is not causation.

- [What changed between proposal and final installation?](what-changed-between-proposal-and-final-installation.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. The final installation may differ from an early proposal because of approved changes, substitutions or incomplete documentation.
- [Which punch-list items remain unresolved?](which-punch-list-items-remain-unresolved.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A punch list can contain reported issues, agreed tasks and items whose completion has not been documented.

A new proposal may appear attractive while conflicting with an earlier household requirement about privacy, accessibility, care or maintenance.
- [What should we ask before approving this change order?](what-should-we-ask-before-approving-this-change-order.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A change order can alter cost, scope, ongoing obligations and the intended household experience.
- [Which systems will this renovation affect?](which-systems-will-this-renovation-affect.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A renovation can affect systems outside the immediate work area through documented power, network, environmental or care dependencies.
