# Why did we choose this lighting system?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

Why did we choose this lighting system?

## Why it matters

The reasons behind a lighting choice may include household needs, design preferences and accepted tradeoffs that are no longer obvious. The household wants to understand recorded rationale without inferring motives from current use or repeated overrides. An old choice may explain the project without proving that today's preferences or authority to change the system remain the same.

## Existing HTBW relationship

Home Steward acquires no ownership.
