# Does this proposal contradict an earlier household requirement?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

Does this proposal contradict an earlier household requirement?

## Why it matters

A new proposal may appear attractive while conflicting with an earlier household requirement about privacy, accessibility, care or maintenance. The household wants to understand the specific documented tension and whether the earlier requirement is still applicable. A newer proposal should not silently replace the accepted scope, and remembered preferences should not be promoted into binding requirements.
