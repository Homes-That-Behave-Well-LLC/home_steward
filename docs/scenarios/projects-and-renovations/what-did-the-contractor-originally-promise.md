# What did the contractor originally promise?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

What did the contractor originally promise?

## Why it matters

A contractor's original commitments may be distributed across an approved proposal, scope revisions and recorded clarifications. The household wants to understand which version and wording are relevant before comparing expectations with later evidence. A plausible summary must not turn ambiguous language, informal recollection or a subsequent change into a definitive legal interpretation of the promise.
