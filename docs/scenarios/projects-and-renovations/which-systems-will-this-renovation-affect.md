# Which systems will this renovation affect?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

Which systems will this renovation affect?

## Why it matters

A renovation can affect systems outside the immediate work area through documented power, network, environmental or care dependencies. The household wants to understand those relationships and unknowns before qualified people plan the work. A dependency description can support questions, but does not prove that every impact is known or authorize technical changes.
