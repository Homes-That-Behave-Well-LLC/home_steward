# Did the completed project produce the intended outcome?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

Did the completed project produce the intended outcome?

## Why it matters

A project can meet a visible specification while still failing to deliver the household experience that motivated it. The household wants to compare approved outcomes with observations and voluntarily stated experience, preserving competing needs and missing coverage. A successful-looking installation, fewer overrides or silence after completion should not be mistaken for satisfaction or proof of contractual performance.
