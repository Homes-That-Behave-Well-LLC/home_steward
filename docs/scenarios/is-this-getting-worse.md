# Is this getting worse

> Proposed synthetic scenario; no actual pool equipment measurements or diagnosis.
> Applies the [investigation evidence rules](../architecture/knowledge-architecture.md#evidence-and-epistemic-rules).

## 1. Household request

"Is the pool equipment getting less reliable?" Define the equipment/capability and comparable windows.

## 2. Existing HTBW knowledge

Foundation equipment/dependencies; authoritative Decision Traces and Truth Historical Facts;
Stewardship service/intervention history. Native historical evidence remains owner-governed.

## 3. Authorized private retrieval

Minimum unavailable duration, disconnects, failed commands, retry frequency, degraded traces and
service records for comparable operation. HA log/availability history is a separately governed
future source; technical network records need their own scope.

## 4. Optional public research

Permitted exact-model manufacturer troubleshooting can contextualize a supported observation;
no private logs, schedules or household location in external queries.

## 5. Home Steward synthesis

Compare unavailable time per observed operating time, disconnects per coverage, failures per
command opportunity and interventions per equivalent use. Preserve missing/unknown outcomes;
changes in monitoring, season/use, integration versions and maintenance can alter apparent rates.
Evidence may show deteriorating operational reliability. It does not diagnose a failing pump,
controller, interface or power/network path without independent supporting evidence.

## 6. Provenance

Cite traces, Historical Facts, operational logs, service documents, windows/units/denominators and
coverage. Deduplicate repeated warnings/retries. A statistical trend is not a Truth diagnosis;
correlation or temporal association does not establish causation.

## 7. Candidate relationships

Possible shared failure mode or service-related improvement is a hypothesis, not a physical cause
or new authoritative dependency.

## 8. Household confirmation

Confirm relevant operation/service context as evidence, not authorization to repair/replace equipment.

## 9. Governed durable relationship

No rewritten trace, re-derived Fact history or autonomous care record. Foundation associations and
Stewardship service/obligation history stay with their owners.

## 10. Proposed actions

Propose reviewing an evidenced trend or gathering authorized diagnostics through HTBW/Operational
Trust. Any qualified service request is a separately governed action coordinated by Concierge.

## 11. Prohibited autonomous actions

No policy/preference change, disabling integration, removing automation, obligation change,
rollback/restart, replacing equipment, booking service or vendor contact without HTBW authority.

## 12. HTBW authority

Truth current/historical Facts; Stewardship care; Foundation equipment; Identity requester;
Continuity context; Operational Trust source/action; Concierge Trace/explanation/orchestration.

## 13. Privacy and locality

Separately scoped HA logs and service evidence are minimally retrieved. Local derived metrics
inherit source privacy/retention; no automatic indefinite raw-log memory or shadow copy of expired traces.

## 14. Unavailable / degraded behavior

If prior data aged out, was never captured or lacks exposure, say "I don't know whether reliability
is deteriorating." Current native HTBW operation/obligations persist.

## 15. Acceptance expectations

Comparable exposure/gaps and alternatives are explicit; trend claims cannot become unsupported
hardware diagnoses, forecasts or permission to change equipment.

## 16. Open questions

Availability semantics, independent failure evidence, exposure/cycle comparability and future
query/redaction/derived retention remain open; no monitoring runtime is created.
