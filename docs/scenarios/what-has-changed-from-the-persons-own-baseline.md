# What has changed from the person's own baseline

> Aging in Place and Care Awareness: synthetic personal comparison, not generic or clinical normality.

## 1. Household request

"What has changed from this person's normal pattern?" Use personal observed baseline, not generic normal person.

## 2. Existing HTBW knowledge

Foundation Person/context, eligible Identity, Truth current/historical Home Facts, Stewardship care
and existing Explainability. No universal normality, human ranking or medical state is supplied.

## 3. Authorized private retrieval

Potential separately approved Person observations/history and household-provided lifestyle/context.
One Person's baseline does not grant access to others; movement histories remain separately governed.

## 4. Optional public research

Device definitions may clarify units/limits, not substitute population-level medical normality.
Private Person/baseline/care data never become public query context.

## 5. Home Steward synthesis

Document baseline period, evidence quality, recent comparison period, known lifestyle/context changes,
missing evidence and confidence limits. Compare actual eligible personal observations, not an invented
"normal person". Context may limit comparability; no clinical thresholds or causes are derived.

## 6. Provenance

Wearable/Home/household sources carry dates, units, coverage and selected personal periods.
Historical comparison, inference and unknown remain distinct; no new confidence/scoring model.

## 7. Candidate relationships

Personal differences/context associations remain tentative, not a clinical norm or durable care relationship.

## 8. Household confirmation

Clarify periods/context with an eligible participant; confirmation does not authorize new sources/
disclosure. Future retention/review remains an OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION.

## 9. Governed durable relationship

OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION: no baseline/normality object, model, care proposal or schema.

## 10. Proposed actions

The household may review observations and independently authorized questions for a human.
Any disclosure, notification or handoff mechanism remains an OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION.

## 11. Prohibited autonomous actions

No diagnosis, disease/fall prediction, medication-effect/medical-causation inference, treatment,
clinical monitoring, emergency decision, automatic caregiver notification, Person profiling or care/policy change.

## 12. HTBW authority

Truth current Home state/history; Stewardship care obligations; Identity context; Foundation definitions;
Operational Trust action/disclosure; Continuity where applicable; Concierge and existing Explainability/Trace.

## 13. Privacy and locality

Person-scoped baseline/context remains separately governed; ingestion does not authorize disclosure.
Local derivatives inherit sensitivity/lifecycle; no indefinite personal baseline archive or public leakage.

## 14. Unavailable / degraded behavior

"I don't know this person's prior pattern" is valid with insufficient/denied evidence; do not substitute
population normality. Native HTBW/emergency paths stay independent; no LLM critical path or cloud fallback.

## 15. Acceptance expectations

All six baseline considerations captured; personal observation is not medical normality.
No baseline algorithm, wearable ingestion, care workflow or runtime acceptance.

## 16. Open questions

Baseline/source eligibility, comparison representation/retention and human disclosure/handoff remain
OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTIONS; no new data model or medical algorithm.
