# Why did electricity use increase this month?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

Why did electricity use increase this month?

## Why it matters

A higher electricity total may reflect weather, billing coverage, changed equipment use or a different household pattern. The household wants to understand which comparisons are valid before blaming a device or changing routines. Increased consumption can also support needed comfort, care or maintenance, so the explanation must preserve the intended outcome.
