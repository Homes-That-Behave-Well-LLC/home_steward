# How much of our water use is landscaping?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

How much of our water use is landscaping?

## Why it matters

Landscaping may contribute materially to water use, but irrigation duration is not a measured volume. Zone coverage, known flow rates, rainfall, seasonal schedules and missing submetering can limit how confidently the household can describe that contribution.

Understanding quantity and separately authorized cost can help the household review resource use while preserving accepted landscape care. A less-watered landscape that loses its declared outcome is not automatically an improvement.

## Important boundary

Without direct submetering, contribution estimates remain estimates with explicit uncertainty. Do not allocate unexplained household water to landscaping or infer a leak from a remainder.

HTBW Truth remains authoritative for governed Home Facts; retrieved utility bills are not automatically Truth.
