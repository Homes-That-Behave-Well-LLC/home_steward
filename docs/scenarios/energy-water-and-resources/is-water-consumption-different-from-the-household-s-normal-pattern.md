# Is water consumption different from the household's normal pattern?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

Is water consumption different from the household's normal pattern?

## Why it matters

Water use can vary with visitors, seasonal care, irrigation or the length of a measurement period. The household wants a qualified comparison with its own declared context rather than a universal normality judgment. An unexplained difference is useful to recognize, but should not become a leak diagnosis or an accusation about someone's habits.
