# How much does charging the vehicle at home cost us?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

How much does charging the vehicle at home cost us?

## Why it matters

Energy reported at the charger, energy reaching the vehicle and utility purchases are not necessarily the same quantity. Charge timing, solar/storage contributions and known charging losses can matter to cost, while missing measurements can prevent a reliable allocation.

The household wants to understand charging cost without sacrificing the vehicle availability it relies on. A cheaper charging period is not automatically usable given declared travel needs, equipment constraints and resilience.

Home Steward could compare authorized charging-energy and timing evidence with applicable tariffs and documented source contributions/losses. It could explain MEASURED, ESTIMATED and UNKNOWN cost interpretations, without assuming all charging came from the grid or that solar/stored energy was free.

## Potential evidence

Eligible charger/vehicle energy reports and their measurement boundaries; charge times; tariff effective periods; solar/storage contribution evidence where supported; known charging losses; utility coverage; household-declared availability needs.
