# Did the irrigation change actually help?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

Did the irrigation change actually help?

## Why it matters

An irrigation change may save water while harming plant health or increasing later maintenance. The household wants to understand the intended landscape outcome alongside consumption and relevant weather. A favorable comparison over a short period should not hide delayed effects, missing observations or a change in the area being watered.
