# What changed in our energy and water use this month that is actually worth understanding?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

What changed in our energy and water use this month that is actually worth understanding?

## Why it matters

A monthly review can become noisy if every small variation is presented as a household problem. The household wants supported material changes in consumption, cost, rates or equipment behavior interpreted against its declared needs and suitable comparison periods.

There may be no meaningful change to pursue. Missing coverage is different from evidence of stability, and an unusual difference is not automatically a fault, saving opportunity or reason to interrupt the household.

Home Steward could compare authorized monthly resource/cost periods and context, explaining material consumption or rate changes, unusual equipment behavior and candidate questions. This bounded change inquiry is distinct from the broader [Monthly Household Resource Review](prepare-the-monthly-household-resource-review.md).

## Potential evidence

Eligible electricity/water periods; separately authorized bills/rates and corrections; equipment behavior summaries; weather/season; authorized occupancy/absence context; changed care/service requirements; household-declared relevance; source coverage and comparison limitations.

Categories only, not admitted sources. Person/source/purpose/namespace/audience authorization is independent. Household-controlled network locality and sensitivity persist; derivations inherit correction, retention, expiry, deletion and revocation. No new monitoring or shadow history.

## Important boundary

"No material resource change requiring attention" is valid with adequate authorized coverage; denied, missing or stale evidence is instead a gap. No universal materiality threshold, automatic diagnosis, notification or monthly action requirement.

HTBW Truth remains authoritative for governed Home Facts; retrieved utility bills are not automatically Truth. Financial/billing access is separately authorized; billing access grants neither banking access nor payment authority. Knowledge/recommendations grant no action or disclosure authority. No bill payment, utility-plan change, demand-response enrollment, procurement or autonomous HVAC, battery dispatch, EV/pool schedule, irrigation or equipment change.

Measurements, source reports, estimates and unknown remain distinct. Cost is not consumption; usage change, rate change, context change and unknown remain distinct. Show cost evidence and assumptions. Weather, occupancy/travel, billing-cycle length, tariffs, equipment/setpoint changes, season/extreme events and overlapping interventions may confound comparisons.

LOWER CONSUMPTION != BETTER HOUSEHOLD OUTCOME; LOWER COST != BETTER HOUSEHOLD OUTCOME. Preserve comfort, health, accessibility, Asset care, security, resilience, Stewardship, preference/intent and manual control. No medical or life-safety path depends on an LLM. Insufficient evidence and no action are valid.
