# Energy, Water and Resources

Navigation and scope guidance only; not an architecture contract or roadmap. Browse the [scenario-family map](../README.md).

## Purpose

**LOWER CONSUMPTION != BETTER HOUSEHOLD OUTCOME**

**LOWER COST != BETTER HOUSEHOLD OUTCOME**

This family explores three related questions:

1. WHAT ARE WE USING?
2. WHAT ARE WE PAYING?
3. IS THERE A BETTER WAY TO ACHIEVE THE SAME OR BETTER HOUSEHOLD OUTCOME?

The family is not about minimizing utility consumption at all costs. It explores whether authorized operational and cost evidence may someday help a household make better resource decisions without sacrificing the outcomes the Home was designed to create.

Given how this Home actually operates, what are we paying for energy and water, why are we consuming it, and where might we economize without making the Home behave worse? Comfort, health, accessibility, Asset care, security, resilience, Stewardship, preference and household intent are legitimate outcomes. A lower bill or meter total that compromises them is not automatically an improvement.

## Relationship to HTBW

Foundation owns descriptions and relationships, not current Truth. Stewardship represents household-declared significance, care and obligations. Identity resolves Persons, not access authority; Truth owns authoritative current/historical Facts. Continuity retains accepted preference/session/experience responsibilities; Operational Trust governs permission/disclosure for this request in this context; Concierge retains orchestration and Trace, not every owner's acceptance.

Native Explainability, including historical/time/cross-Room retrieval, remains HTBW-owned and does not require Home Steward. Ordinary retention remains household-configured, including zero/disabled; missing history is not reconstructed. See the existing [HTBW boundary](../../architecture/htbw-boundary.md).

## Possible Home Steward extension

Compare authorized consumption, utility cost, rates, weather, occupancy and lived outcomes. Distinguish measured use, separately retrieved billing reports, qualified estimates and unknown; distinguish usage change, rate change and context change. Financial saving is not established by reduced consumption or solar generation alone.

Potential operational categories include electricity/circuit/equipment consumption where available, HVAC runtime and thermostat modes, indoor temperature/humidity, authorized occupancy and Room use, solar production, battery charge/discharge, EV charging, pool/pump/appliance operation, water/irrigation, independently reported leak evidence, weather, absence and eligible HTBW history.

Separately authorized financial categories may include electricity/water/gas bills, tariffs and time-of-use periods, peak pricing, applicable demand charges, taxes/fees, solar credits, historical invoices and resource service costs. These are categories, not source admission; no household is assumed to expose every source.

Any new source admission, representation, handoff, comparison mechanism or implementation is an OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION, not a solved interface or runtime requirement.

## Boundaries

HTBW Truth remains authoritative for governed Home Facts. Retrieved utility bills, reports and estimates are not automatically Truth. Financial/billing source access requires separate authorization and grants neither banking access nor payment authority. Knowledge or a recommendation is not action or disclosure authority. No bill payment, utility-plan change, demand-response enrollment, procurement or autonomous HVAC, battery dispatch, EV/pool schedule, irrigation or equipment change.

Cost claims show source/date/period, evidence coverage and assumptions. No perfect bill reconstruction, invented equipment allocation, universal household financial model or numeric household-value score. Direct measurements, source reports, estimates and unknown stay distinct; runtime is not measured energy. Overlapping system allocations and intervention claims must not be counted twice.

Preserve comfort, health, accessibility, Asset-specific governed care, accepted HTBW Risk, security, resilience, Stewardship, preference/intent and manual control. Generic advice cannot override governed Asset care. RESILIENCE VALUE may justify behavior that is not MINIMUM-COST BEHAVIOR; retained battery reserve is not automatically a missed saving. Stored charge is not proof of usable backup or dispatchable capacity.

Person/source/purpose/namespace/audience scope, household-controlled locality and source-derived lifecycle remain independent requirements. Potential evidence is not admitted access; knowledge is not action or disclosure authority. Source reports and inference are not automatic Truth; unknown is valid and correlation is not causation.

Local means within the household-controlled network boundary, not necessarily one computer. Source sensitivity and derived correction, retention, expiry, deletion and revocation persist through summaries and comparisons; a report does not declassify private bills. Household membership, caretaker relationships and Identity context grant no source access. Occupancy/absence must be independently eligible; do not infer people or travel from device activity or add continuous recording/shadow history.

No optimization engine, forecasting system, connector, rate API, scheduler, control loop, report generator, Home Steward/HTBW hook, handler, proposal schema, MCP tool or Home Assistant service is designed or implemented. People and Pets are not Assets. No medical or life-safety path depends on Home Steward or an LLM; native safety and professional judgment remain independent.

### Comparison and lived-outcome requirements

Challenge simplistic before/after claims. Material confounders can include weather, occupancy, travel, billing-cycle length, tariffs/time-of-use mix, equipment replacement, setpoint changes, extreme events, season, delivered-service changes and overlapping interventions. Units, measurement boundaries, applicable dates and comparison coverage matter. No normalization or attribution algorithm is defined.

Increased water use is not a confirmed leak. Context may include irrigation, pool refill, independently known leaks, eligible occupancy, appliance use, seasonal conditions and service work; unexplained variation may warrant investigation without diagnosis. Irrigation review compares water with rainfall, temperature, season and governed landscape condition: LESS WATER WHILE MAINTAINING THE ACCEPTED LANDSCAPING OUTCOME, not merely LESS WATER.

Economizing candidates consider likely financial impact, household impact, maintenance burden, privacy, vendor dependence, reversibility, Technical Debt, reliability and evidence quality. Timing candidates also consider inconvenience, Asset needs, noise, deadlines, equipment constraints, safety, resilience and preferences. These are qualitative review considerations, not an optimizer or generated household-value hierarchy.

Insufficient evidence, no suitable opportunity and no action are valid. "No material resource change requiring attention" requires adequate authorized coverage; denied, stale or missing evidence is a gap, not proof of no change. KEEP / ADAPT / REMOVE remains governed candidate advice, not execution or acceptance.
