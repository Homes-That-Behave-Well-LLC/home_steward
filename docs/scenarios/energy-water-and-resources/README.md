# Energy, Water and Resources

Navigation and scope guidance only; not an architecture contract or roadmap. Browse the [scenario-family map](../README.md).

## Purpose

Understand resource use and trade-offs in the context of household outcomes rather than treating lower use as a universal goal.

## Relationship to HTBW

Truth owns supported measurements; Stewardship owns declared care; Continuity retains preferences and Operational Trust action authority. See the existing [HTBW boundary](../../architecture/htbw-boundary.md).

## Possible Home Steward extension

Compare authorized use evidence, applicable costs and contextual household requirements. Any new source admission, representation, handoff or implementation is an OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION.

## Boundaries

LOWER CONSUMPTION != BETTER OUTCOME. Preserve comfort, accessibility, care and manual control; no unapproved price optimization, control changes or generated universal limits.

Person/source/purpose/namespace/audience scope, household-controlled locality and source-derived lifecycle remain independent requirements. Potential evidence is not admitted access; knowledge is not action or disclosure authority. Source reports and inference are not automatic Truth; unknown is valid and correlation is not causation.

### Vision — Not Yet Architected

- [Why did electricity use increase this month?](why-did-electricity-use-increase-this-month.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A higher electricity total may reflect weather, billing coverage, changed equipment use or a different household pattern.
- [Which Home behaviors contributed most to peak usage?](which-home-behaviors-contributed-most-to-peak-usage.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Peak electricity use can be shaped by several activities happening together rather than one inefficient device.
- [Did the HVAC change reduce energy use without reducing comfort?](did-the-hvac-change-reduce-energy-use-without-reducing-comfort.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. An HVAC change is only beneficial if it preserves the household outcome, not merely if a meter total falls.
- [Are unused rooms being heated or cooled unnecessarily?](are-unused-rooms-being-heated-or-cooled-unnecessarily.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A room that appears unused may still need conditioning for stored objects, moisture management, care or an expected visitor.
- [Is water consumption different from the household's normal pattern?](is-water-consumption-different-from-the-household-s-normal-pattern.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Water use can vary with visitors, seasonal care, irrigation or the length of a measurement period.
- [Did the irrigation change actually help?](did-the-irrigation-change-actually-help.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. An irrigation change may save water while harming plant health or increasing later maintenance.
- [What equipment appears to run longer than it used to?](what-equipment-appears-to-run-longer-than-it-used-to.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Longer equipment runtime can signal a changed demand, different operating mode or a measurement artifact rather than deterioration.
- [What improvement could reduce cost without creating a worse outcome?](what-improvement-could-reduce-cost-without-creating-a-worse-outcome.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A cheaper household outcome is attractive only if it remains a good outcome.
- [Are solar production and battery behavior meeting the intended outcome?](are-solar-production-and-battery-behavior-meeting-the-intended-outcome.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Solar generation and battery operation can serve different goals, such as resilience, cost or reduced imports.
- [What happened during the utility peak-pricing window?](what-happened-during-the-utility-peak-pricing-window.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A utility pricing window can make the same electricity use cost more without making the household behavior inappropriate.
- [Is the apparent saving real, or was the Home simply unoccupied?](is-the-apparent-saving-real-or-was-the-home-simply-unoccupied.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A lower bill after a change may simply reflect fewer days at home rather than a more efficient capability.
