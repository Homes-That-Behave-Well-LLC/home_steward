# Did the HVAC change reduce energy use without reducing comfort?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

Did the HVAC change reduce energy use without reducing comfort?

## Why it matters

An HVAC change is only beneficial if it preserves the household outcome, not merely if a meter total falls. The household wants to compare energy use alongside declared comfort and relevant health, care and accessibility needs. Weather, occupancy and incomplete feedback can make an apparent improvement uncertain even when the numbers look persuasive.
