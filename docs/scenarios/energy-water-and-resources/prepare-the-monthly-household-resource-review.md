# Prepare the monthly Household Resource Review

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

What should we know about energy, water and household resource cost this month?

## Why it matters

Electricity, water and applicable gas evidence may be held separately from solar, battery, vehicle and pool context. A household review could bring authorized pieces together while showing missing coverage, rate changes and the outcomes those resources served.

The household should not be pressured to find a saving every month. No-action items, uncertainty and open questions belong alongside supported changes and candidate opportunities; an informative review is not authority to schedule a report, interrupt people or change the Home.

## Existing HTBW relationship

Foundation owns descriptions/relationships; Stewardship, declared significance/care/obligations; Identity, Person resolution, not authorization; Truth, current/historical Facts; Continuity, accepted preferences/session/experience; Operational Trust, request-specific permission/disclosure; Concierge, orchestration/Trace. Native Explainability remains HTBW-owned. Home Steward gains no ownership.

## Possible Home Steward extension

Home Steward could synthesize authorized evidence into a conceptual conversation about these eighteen potential topics, not a required output schema or implemented generator:

1. Electricity consumption
2. Electricity cost
3. Water consumption
4. Water cost
5. Gas consumption/cost where applicable
6. Solar production/offset
7. Battery behavior
8. EV charging
9. Pool/resource use
10. Major unusual changes
11. Rate/tariff changes
12. Context such as weather/occupancy
13. Candidate opportunities
14. Estimated benefit where evidence supports it
15. Household-outcome impact
16. Evidence quality/confidence
17. No-action items
18. Open questions

This complements the developed, release-oriented [Home Possibility Review](../possibility-and-improvement/prepare-the-monthly-home-possibility-review.md), not a duplicate or maturity promotion. The [monthly resource-change inquiry](what-changed-in-our-energy-and-water-use-this-month.md) remains the narrower question of what changed.

## Potential evidence

Eligible resource measurements and operational summaries; separately authorized electric/water/gas bills, tariffs, credits and service costs; solar/storage/EV/pool evidence where available; weather and authorized occupancy context; governed decisions/care; voluntary lived feedback; dated source coverage and confidence descriptions.

Categories only, not admitted sources. Person/source/purpose/namespace/audience authorization is independent. Household-controlled network locality and sensitivity persist; derivations inherit correction, retention, expiry, deletion and revocation. No new monitoring or shadow history.

## Important boundary

No scheduled watcher, report generator, automatic notification, financial model or opportunity database. No recommendation is required. "No material resource change requiring attention" requires adequate coverage; missing/denied/stale evidence is a gap. Preserve source confidence, provenance and estimate assumptions without converting HTBW ordinal confidence into arithmetic or probabilities.

HTBW Truth remains authoritative for governed Home Facts; retrieved utility bills are not automatically Truth. Financial/billing access is separately authorized; billing access grants neither banking access nor payment authority. Knowledge/recommendations grant no action or disclosure authority. No bill payment, utility-plan change, demand-response enrollment, procurement or autonomous HVAC, battery dispatch, EV/pool schedule, irrigation or equipment change.

Measurements, source reports, estimates and unknown remain distinct. Cost is not consumption; usage change, rate change, context change and unknown remain distinct. Show cost evidence and assumptions. Weather, occupancy/travel, billing-cycle length, tariffs, equipment/setpoint changes, season/extreme events and overlapping interventions may confound comparisons.

LOWER CONSUMPTION != BETTER HOUSEHOLD OUTCOME; LOWER COST != BETTER HOUSEHOLD OUTCOME. Preserve comfort, health, accessibility, Asset care, security, resilience, Stewardship, preference/intent and manual control. No medical or life-safety path depends on an LLM. Insufficient evidence and no action are valid.
