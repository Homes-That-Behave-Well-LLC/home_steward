# Are we buying electricity during expensive periods when stored energy might have been available?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

Are we buying electricity during expensive periods when stored energy might have been available?

## Why it matters

An expensive import period coinciding with a charged battery can invite an overly simple conclusion that the Home made a mistake. Reserve requirements, usable capacity, discharge limits, operating constraints or incomparable timestamps may explain why the stored energy was not an available alternative.

The household wants a retrospective, qualified explanation rather than an instruction to dispatch the battery. A legitimate resilience choice may intentionally leave a higher utility cost.
