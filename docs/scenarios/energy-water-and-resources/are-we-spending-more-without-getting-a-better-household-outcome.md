# Are we spending more without getting a better household outcome?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

Are we spending more without getting a better household outcome?

## Why it matters

Higher spending might buy more occupied comfort, maintained landscaping, resilient backup or a pool experience the household values. It might also accompany unchanged service, a higher rate or added obligations without a supported improvement.

The household wants a deliberately qualitative comparison based on its own declared outcomes. Human needs, Asset significance and resilience must not be converted into a universal numeric household-value score or judged by the price of serving them.

## Existing HTBW relationship

Foundation owns descriptions/relationships; Stewardship, declared significance/care/obligations; Identity, Person resolution, not authorization; Truth, current/historical Facts; Continuity, accepted preferences/session/experience; Operational Trust, request-specific permission/disclosure; Concierge, orchestration/Trace. Native Explainability remains HTBW-owned. Home Steward gains no ownership.

## Possible Home Steward extension

Home Steward could compare eligible cost changes with separately authorized evidence of occupied comfort, accepted landscaping, battery resilience or declared pool use. It could explain supported trade-offs and unknowns without creating an outcome unit, ranking people or deciding what the household should value.

## Potential evidence

Separately authorized bills/rates/service costs; household-declared outcome and comparison scope; voluntary experience feedback; eligible environmental and resource evidence; governed landscape/Asset care; resilience declarations; documented obligations and service limitations.

Categories only, not admitted sources. Person/source/purpose/namespace/audience authorization is independent. Household-controlled network locality and sensitivity persist; derivations inherit correction, retention, expiry, deletion and revocation. No new monitoring or shadow history.

## Important boundary

No universal financial model, cost-per-human-value metric or numeric household-value score. Unknown benefit is not zero benefit; People and Pets are not Assets. Generic advice does not override Asset-specific care or accepted HTBW Risk.

HTBW Truth remains authoritative for governed Home Facts; retrieved utility bills are not automatically Truth. Financial/billing access is separately authorized; billing access grants neither banking access nor payment authority. Knowledge/recommendations grant no action or disclosure authority. No bill payment, utility-plan change, demand-response enrollment, procurement or autonomous HVAC, battery dispatch, EV/pool schedule, irrigation or equipment change.

Measurements, source reports, estimates and unknown remain distinct. Cost is not consumption; usage change, rate change, context change and unknown remain distinct. Show cost evidence and assumptions. Weather, occupancy/travel, billing-cycle length, tariffs, equipment/setpoint changes, season/extreme events and overlapping interventions may confound comparisons.

LOWER CONSUMPTION != BETTER HOUSEHOLD OUTCOME; LOWER COST != BETTER HOUSEHOLD OUTCOME. Preserve comfort, health, accessibility, Asset care, security, resilience, Stewardship, preference/intent and manual control. No medical or life-safety path depends on an LLM. Insufficient evidence and no action are valid.
