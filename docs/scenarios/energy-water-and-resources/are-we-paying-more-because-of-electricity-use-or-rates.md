# Are we paying more because we're using more, or because electricity costs more?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

Are we paying more because we're using more, or because electricity costs more?

## Why it matters

Two comparable consumption totals can produce different bills, and the same tariff can produce a different effective unit cost when time-of-use mix changes. A bill-wide average can also be affected by fixed fees, credits or a correction rather than a changed energy price.

The household wants to distinguish these interpretations before treating a higher bill as evidence of inefficient Home behavior. More than one contributor may apply, and the available detail may not support a complete decomposition.
