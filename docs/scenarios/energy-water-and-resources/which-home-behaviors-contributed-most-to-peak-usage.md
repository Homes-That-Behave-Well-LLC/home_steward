# Which Home behaviors contributed most to peak usage?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

Which Home behaviors contributed most to peak usage?

## Why it matters

Peak electricity use can be shaped by several activities happening together rather than one inefficient device. The household wants to understand measured contributions and the coverage behind any ranking. A behavior that contributes to a peak may still be necessary for care, accessibility or maintenance and should not be labeled wasteful by its timing alone.
