# Over the last year, what did our efficiency decisions actually save?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

Over the last year, what did our efficiency decisions actually save?

## Why it matters

Several household efficiency decisions may occur during a year with changing rates, weather, occupancy and service needs. Adding separate before/after claims can count the same apparent saving more than once or imply attribution that the evidence does not support.

The household wants to review decisions against lived outcomes, not celebrate a portfolio total detached from accepted comfort, landscaping, Asset care or resilience. Some contributions may remain individually uncertain even when an aggregate resource change is measured.

## Possible Home Steward extension

Home Steward could compare governed decisions, implementation dates, eligible resource/cost periods and lived-outcome evidence across the authorized year. It could distinguish supported aggregate change, qualified per-decision estimates and unresolved overlapping attribution, drawing on [fix-review reasoning](../investigation-and-explainability/did-the-fix-actually-work.md).

## Potential evidence

Governed decision/reason/date records; implemented versions; annual and before/after resource coverage; separately authorized invoices/bills/rates; authorized occupancy and weather context; overlapping intervention descriptions; voluntary outcome feedback; declared comparison scope.

Categories only, not admitted sources. Person/source/purpose/namespace/audience authorization is independent. Household-controlled network locality and sensitivity persist; derivations inherit correction, retention, expiry, deletion and revocation. No new monitoring or shadow history.

## Important boundary

Do not fabricate individual attribution or add overlapping claims into a savings total. Operating benefit is not automatically net financial return; implementation/service costs need separate scope and authorization. Missing retained history remains unknown.

HTBW Truth remains authoritative for governed Home Facts; retrieved utility bills are not automatically Truth. Financial/billing access is separately authorized; billing access grants neither banking access nor payment authority. Knowledge/recommendations grant no action or disclosure authority. No bill payment, utility-plan change, demand-response enrollment, procurement or autonomous HVAC, battery dispatch, EV/pool schedule, irrigation or equipment change.

Measurements, source reports, estimates and unknown remain distinct. Cost is not consumption; usage change, rate change, context change and unknown remain distinct. Show cost evidence and assumptions. Weather, occupancy/travel, billing-cycle length, tariffs, equipment/setpoint changes, season/extreme events and overlapping interventions may confound comparisons.

LOWER CONSUMPTION != BETTER HOUSEHOLD OUTCOME; LOWER COST != BETTER HOUSEHOLD OUTCOME. Preserve comfort, health, accessibility, Asset care, security, resilience, Stewardship, preference/intent and manual control. No medical or life-safety path depends on an LLM. Insufficient evidence and no action are valid.
