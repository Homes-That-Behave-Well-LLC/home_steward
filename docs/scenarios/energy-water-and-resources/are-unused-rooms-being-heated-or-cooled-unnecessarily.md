# Are unused rooms being heated or cooled unnecessarily?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

Are unused rooms being heated or cooled unnecessarily?

## Why it matters

A room that appears unused may still need conditioning for stored objects, moisture management, care or an expected visitor. The household wants to understand documented purposes before calling the energy use unnecessary. Sparse occupancy evidence should not replace household declarations or create a surveillance-based definition of which rooms matter.

## Existing HTBW relationship

Foundation retains definitions/configuration; Truth, current/historical Facts; Identity, Person resolution, not authentication/authorization; Stewardship, household-declared care/obligations; Continuity, preference/experience eligibility; Operational Trust, request-specific permissions; Concierge, orchestration/Trace. Home Steward acquires no ownership.

## Important boundary

No observed occupancy does not establish an unused room or permission to stop conditioning it.

LOWER CONSUMPTION != BETTER OUTCOME: preserve comfort, health, care, accessibility and maintenance. No price-change or device-control authorization.

Knowledge/interpretation never grants action or disclosure authority. Unclear evidence stays unknown; correlation/timing is not causation. No life-safety or medical/emergency path depends on Home Steward/LLM.
