# Is the apparent saving real, or was the Home simply unoccupied?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

Is the apparent saving real, or was the Home simply unoccupied?

## Why it matters

A lower bill after a change may simply reflect fewer days at home rather than a more efficient capability. The household wants to compare equivalent service and occupancy context before celebrating savings. Voluntarily declared absence can help explain the denominator, but missing occupancy evidence must not prompt monitoring or invented assumptions about people's whereabouts.
