# Is the household battery actually saving us money?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

Is the household battery actually saving us money?

## Why it matters

Storage can shift purchases or reduce expensive imports, but charge source, discharge timing, tariffs and known round-trip loss can change the economic result. Charge or discharge activity alone does not demonstrate net monetary benefit.

RESILIENCE VALUE may justify behavior that is not MINIMUM-COST BEHAVIOR. The household may intentionally retain a backup reserve instead of using every available unit to reduce today's bill, and a cost comparison must respect that declared priority.
