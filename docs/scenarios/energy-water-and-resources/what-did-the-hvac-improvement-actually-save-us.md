# What did the HVAC improvement actually save us?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

What did the HVAC improvement actually save us?

## Why it matters

Lower energy use after an HVAC intervention does not by itself establish monetary savings. Weather, occupancy, billing periods, changed rates, equipment and setpoints can produce an apparent benefit while delivered comfort or care also changes.

The household wants an evidence-bounded financial interpretation alongside the existing [energy-and-comfort review](did-the-hvac-change-reduce-energy-use-without-reducing-comfort.md). A well-installed change may still have uncertain financial benefit, and a smaller bill obtained by losing required service is not the intended improvement.

## Possible Home Steward extension

Home Steward could compare eligible change/date/reason records, suitable before/after resource periods, applicable cost evidence and lived outcomes. It could qualify a supported savings estimate or say, "The available evidence does not support a confident savings estimate," without inventing a normalization model or causal attribution.

## Potential evidence

Governed HVAC decision and implementation dates; equipment/setpoint versions; before/after metering; weather and season; authorized occupancy context; billing-cycle coverage and rates; voluntarily supplied comfort feedback; governed environmental/Asset care evidence.

Categories only, not admitted sources. Person/source/purpose/namespace/audience authorization is independent. Household-controlled network locality and sensitivity persist; derivations inherit correction, retention, expiry, deletion and revocation. No new monitoring or shadow history.

## Important boundary

Energy saving is not financial saving. Unavailable or never-retained history is a gap, not a reconstructed baseline; simultaneous interventions prevent unsupported sole-cause claims.

HTBW Truth remains authoritative for governed Home Facts; retrieved utility bills are not automatically Truth. Financial/billing access is separately authorized; billing access grants neither banking access nor payment authority. Knowledge/recommendations grant no action or disclosure authority. No bill payment, utility-plan change, demand-response enrollment, procurement or autonomous HVAC, battery dispatch, EV/pool schedule, irrigation or equipment change.

Measurements, source reports, estimates and unknown remain distinct. Cost is not consumption; usage change, rate change, context change and unknown remain distinct. Show cost evidence and assumptions. Weather, occupancy/travel, billing-cycle length, tariffs, equipment/setpoint changes, season/extreme events and overlapping interventions may confound comparisons.

LOWER CONSUMPTION != BETTER HOUSEHOLD OUTCOME; LOWER COST != BETTER HOUSEHOLD OUTCOME. Preserve comfort, health, accessibility, Asset care, security, resilience, Stewardship, preference/intent and manual control. No medical or life-safety path depends on an LLM. Insufficient evidence and no action are valid.
