# What equipment appears to run longer than it used to?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

What equipment appears to run longer than it used to?

## Why it matters

Longer equipment runtime can signal a changed demand, different operating mode or a measurement artifact rather than deterioration. The household wants to know which comparisons are supported and what intended service the equipment delivered. An appliance working longer to meet a legitimate care need should not automatically be described as inefficient or failing.
