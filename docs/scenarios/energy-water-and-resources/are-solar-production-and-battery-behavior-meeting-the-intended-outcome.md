## Household question

Are solar production and battery behavior meeting the intended outcome?

## Why it matters

Solar generation and battery operation can serve different goals, such as resilience, cost or reduced imports. The household wants to compare evidence with its own declared priorities rather than a single production number. Seasonal conditions, unavailable capacity and competing needs can explain why apparently strong generation does not deliver the intended experience.
