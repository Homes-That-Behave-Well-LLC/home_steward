# What happened during the utility peak-pricing window?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

What happened during the utility peak-pricing window?

## Why it matters

A utility pricing window can make the same electricity use cost more without making the household behavior inappropriate. The household wants a bounded account of usage, applicable published pricing and necessary activities during that period. Understanding the bill should not imply that comfort, care or accessibility should have yielded to the tariff.
