# Which efficiency changes sounded good but did not produce the result we expected?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

Which efficiency changes sounded good but did not produce the result we expected?

## Why it matters

A plausible efficiency idea can fail to produce its intended financial or lived outcome, add maintenance burden or shift cost elsewhere. Successful installation and a quieter complaint channel are not evidence that the household received the promised benefit.

The household wants a fair review of implemented decisions, including conflicting evidence and uncertainty, rather than an automatic list of changes to remove. Keeping a change for comfort or resilience can still be justified when its anticipated bill saving did not materialize.

Native Explainability remains HTBW-owned. Home Steward gains no ownership.

## Possible Home Steward extension

Home Steward could apply the developed [Did the fix actually work?](../investigation-and-explainability/did-the-fix-actually-work.md) reasoning to eligible efficiency decisions: problem -> decision -> change -> lived outcome -> evidence -> review. KEEP / ADAPT / REMOVE remain candidate recommendations, with the declared original outcome, cost assumptions, actual dates, comparable exposure and new burdens visible.

## Potential evidence

Authorized original requirements/expected benefits; governed decisions and implemented versions/dates; resource/cost periods; separately authorized rates and invoices; maintenance/obligation descriptions; voluntary outcome feedback; comparable weather/service exposure; contradictory evidence and overlapping interventions.

Categories only, not admitted sources. Person/source/purpose/namespace/audience authorization is independent. Household-controlled network locality and sensitivity persist; derivations inherit correction, retention, expiry, deletion and revocation. No new monitoring or shadow history.

## Important boundary

Unsupported benefit is not proven failure. Candidate KEEP / ADAPT / REMOVE does not execute changes, close obligations/issues, accept a fix or create policy; silence is not satisfaction.

HTBW Truth remains authoritative for governed Home Facts; retrieved utility bills are not automatically Truth. Financial/billing access is separately authorized; billing access grants neither banking access nor payment authority. Knowledge/recommendations grant no action or disclosure authority. No bill payment, utility-plan change, demand-response enrollment, procurement or autonomous HVAC, battery dispatch, EV/pool schedule, irrigation or equipment change.

Measurements, source reports, estimates and unknown remain distinct. Cost is not consumption; usage change, rate change, context change and unknown remain distinct. Show cost evidence and assumptions. Weather, occupancy/travel, billing-cycle length, tariffs, equipment/setpoint changes, season/extreme events and overlapping interventions may confound comparisons.

LOWER CONSUMPTION != BETTER HOUSEHOLD OUTCOME; LOWER COST != BETTER HOUSEHOLD OUTCOME. Preserve comfort, health, accessibility, Asset care, security, resilience, Stewardship, preference/intent and manual control. No medical or life-safety path depends on an LLM. Insufficient evidence and no action are valid.
