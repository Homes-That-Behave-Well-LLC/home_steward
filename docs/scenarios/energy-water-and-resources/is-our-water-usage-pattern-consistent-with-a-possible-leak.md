# Is our water usage pattern consistent with a possible leak?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

Is our water usage pattern consistent with a possible leak?

## Why it matters

Unusual water use may warrant investigation, but irrigation, pool filling, service work, measurement gaps or changed household demand can offer other explanations. A pattern worth reviewing is not a confirmed leak or proof of which equipment is responsible.

The household wants a bounded statement of concern and uncertainty, not an LLM diagnosis or an unsafe delay while a reasoning service gathers evidence. Native detection and professionally appropriate investigation remain independent.

Eligible meter/flow periods and source quality; independently reported detector or professional observations; known service work; irrigation/pool-fill context; weather/season; voluntarily declared household demand; governed maintenance records; separately authorized bills.

## Important boundary

"Evidence warrants investigation" is allowed; "confirmed leak" is not inferred from unusual consumption. No universal leak threshold, diagnostic algorithm, automatic shutoff or service booking is defined or authorized.

HTBW Truth remains authoritative for governed Home Facts; retrieved utility bills are not automatically Truth. Financial/billing access is separately authorized; billing access grants neither banking access nor payment authority. Knowledge/recommendations grant no action or disclosure authority. No bill payment, utility-plan change, demand-response enrollment, procurement or autonomous HVAC, battery dispatch, EV/pool schedule, irrigation or equipment change.

Measurements, source reports, estimates and unknown remain distinct. Cost is not consumption; usage change, rate change, context change and unknown remain distinct. Show cost evidence and assumptions. Weather, occupancy/travel, billing-cycle length, tariffs, equipment/setpoint changes, season/extreme events and overlapping interventions may confound comparisons.
