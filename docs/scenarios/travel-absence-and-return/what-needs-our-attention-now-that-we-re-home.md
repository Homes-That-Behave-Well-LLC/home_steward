# What needs our attention now that we're home?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

What needs our attention now that we're home?

## Why it matters

Returning home creates a particular review context: existing due items may need attention, while newly reported changes may only need understanding. The household wants these distinguished rather than receiving a fabricated incident list or having every retained event become a priority.

## Existing HTBW relationship

HTBW owns configured Home behavior, Stewardship obligations, current and historical Truth, and governed Explainability. Declared travel plans or service records may provide context, but do not establish actual occupancy, permission or current physical state.

## Possible Home Steward extension

Bring native current attention items together with eligible absence-history context, preserving gaps and household-defined significance. Any new source admission, representation, handoff or implementation is an OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION; no mechanism is specified.

## Potential evidence

- Current Stewardship obligations
- Eligible absence-period Explainability and Truth
- Authorized service or delivery reports

These are possible categories only, not admitted sources. Each requires independent Person/source/purpose/audience scope; derived information inherits sensitivity, household-controlled locality and source lifecycle.

## Important boundary

Absence is not permission for surveillance, private-source aggregation or autonomous changes. An authorized source report is not automatically a Truth Fact; unknown and incomplete history remain visible. No trip details, credentials or private collection information are disclosed by default. Source reports and inference are not automatic Truth; unknown is a valid result. No silent cloud or source fallback.
