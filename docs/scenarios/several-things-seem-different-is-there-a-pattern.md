# Several things seem different; is there a pattern

> Aging in Place and Care Awareness: synthetic, person-scoped, non-diagnostic experience capture.

## 1. Household request

"Several things seem different. Is there a pattern?" Understand concurrent observations with restraint.

## 2. Existing HTBW knowledge

Truth current/historical Home Facts, Foundation Person context, eligible Identity, Stewardship care
and existing Explainability. Correlation and interpretation do not create new Truth.

## 3. Authorized private retrieval

Potential multiple Person-authorized wearable signals, activity, sleep, Home movement, routines and
environmental context. Separate source grants do not automatically permit every correlation/disclosure.

## 4. Optional public research

Source documentation may clarify units/limits; no public query contains Person/care data or substitutes
a generic medical pattern for evidence about this person.

## 5. Home Steward synthesis

Compare named personal baseline/recent periods, evidence quality and known context.
Several measures may have changed together; describe overlap, unchanged measures and gaps, not a
medical cause, disease prediction or inferred medication effect.

## 6. Provenance

Identify wearable/Home reports, household narrative, dates, units/coverage and inference separately.
Source estimates/confidence limitations are preserved, not converted into medical certainty.

## 7. Candidate relationships

Co-changing signals are tentative associations; correlation is not causation or a persisted Pattern record.

## 8. Household confirmation

Clarify Person/period/context; agreement is evidence, not permission to combine/disclose more sources.
Any new retention/review remains an OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION.

## 9. Governed durable relationship

OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION: no pattern/baseline model, care proposal object or diagnostic schema.

## 10. Proposed actions

The household may discuss observations or prepare independently authorized questions for a human.
Any disclosure, notification or handoff remains an OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION.

## 11. Prohibited autonomous actions

No diagnosis, disease/fall prediction, medical causation/medication-effect inference, treatment,
clinical monitoring, emergency decision, automatic caregiver notification or care/policy change.

## 12. HTBW authority

Truth current Home state/history; Stewardship care obligations; Identity context; Foundation definitions;
Operational Trust action/disclosure; Continuity where applicable; Concierge and existing Explainability/Trace.

## 13. Privacy and locality

Person-scoped signals remain individually source/purpose/audience-governed; ingestion is not disclosure.
Local combined narratives inherit the applicable source constraints/lifecycle, not blanket profiling rights.

## 14. Unavailable / degraded behavior

"I don't know whether these changes are related" is valid for gaps/incomparable sources.
Native HTBW/emergency mechanisms remain independent; no LLM critical path or source/cloud fallback.

## 15. Acceptance expectations

Six potential evidence groups, personal comparison and tentative overlap captured; no diagnostic/
correlation algorithm, medical cause, automatic care workflow or runtime acceptance.

## 16. Open questions

Authorized cross-source comparison, evidence/retention and human disclosure/handoff remain OPEN
ARCHITECTURAL QUESTIONS; no wearable ingestion, medical pattern detector or handler.
