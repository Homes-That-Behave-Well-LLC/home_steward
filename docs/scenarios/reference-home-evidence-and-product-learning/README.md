# Reference Home Evidence and Product Learning

Navigation and scenario-capture guidance only; not architecture, a roadmap or delivered functionality. Browse the [scenario-first map](../README.md).

## Purpose

Distinguish theoretical ideas, lived observations, educational stories, configuration-specific findings and unproven product claims without automatic architecture promotion.

## Relationship to HTBW

Foundation owns declared definitions and configured relationships; Stewardship owns household-declared significance, care, obligations and Custody; Identity supplies purpose-specific Person context, not authentication or authorization; Truth owns qualified current and historical Facts; Continuity owns accepted preferences and experience intent; Operational Trust determines permission and disclosure for this request, in this context; Concierge retains governed orchestration and authoritative Decision Trace composition. Native Explainability remains HTBW-owned. Home Assistant and devices retain execution. Home Steward does not require the standalone predecessor Asset Intelligence integration.

See the [HTBW boundary](../../architecture/htbw-boundary.md), [source authorization](../../architecture/source-authorization.md) and [derived lifecycle](../../architecture/derived-knowledge-lifecycle.md).

## Boundaries

Evidence categories are not source admission. Person/source/purpose/namespace/audience authorization remains independent before retrieval, combination and disclosure. One Person's grant is not another's; summaries retain the most restrictive applicable source constraints. USE != INGEST != DERIVE != RETAIN. Source dates, uncertainty, correction, retention, expiry, deletion and revocation remain binding within the household-controlled network boundary; ordinary retention may be zero/disabled. Confirmation does not grant authority to retain, disclose or act. No shadow history or silent cloud/source fallback.

Observation, report and inference are not automatic Truth. Correlation is not causation; unknown is valid. Issued command != observed physical result. People and Pets are not Assets. No medical, life-safety or emergency path depends on Home Steward or an LLM. Scenario capture only: no runtime, hook, interface, schema, handler, pipeline, scheduling, access decision or configuration change is specified or authorized.

## Primary scenarios

Primary files: **0 developed + 13 Vision = 13**. Existing coverage and aliases do not add primary files or change target maturity.

One Reference Home observation is not a universal requirement. Educational storytelling is not architecture acceptance. Exact configuration, context, contrary evidence, reproducibility and publishability remain separate. New primary files in this increment: **13**. Each is catalogued only in this primary family. All earlier bodies and maturity remain unchanged.

- [Which scenario has actually been observed in the lived Home?](which-scenario-has-actually-been-observed-in-the-lived-home.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A scenario can be plausible, demonstrated or actually observed in a lived Home, and those claims carry different evidence. The household and future implementers want attributable observation scope rather than a story presented as product proof. Observing a native HTBW experience does not prove the envisioned Home Steward extension exists.
- [What did the household learn from the lived outcome?](what-did-the-household-learn-from-the-lived-outcome.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Lived experience may reveal an unexpected limitation, useful manual alternative or overlooked household need beyond the original success metric. The household wants its voluntary learning and uncertainty preserved without silently turning a lesson into a requirement. One person's interpretation must not be promoted into shared preference or a product-wide rule.
- [Which idea remains theoretical?](which-idea-remains-theoretical.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A compelling idea can appear feasible without ever being exercised in the lived Home. The household wants theoretical portions distinguished from documented native capability and observed experience. A polished walkthrough or vendor demonstration must not erase untested assumptions or imply that Home Steward has been built.
- [What contradicts the original expectation?](what-contradicts-the-original-expectation.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A lived result can contradict the original expectation even while selected success metrics look favorable. The household wants eligible contrary accounts and conditions included rather than a demonstration curated into apparent agreement. Disagreement can justify a new question without proving a cause or revoking an accepted decision.
- [Which Reference Home finding should update or inform an HTBW issue?](which-reference-home-finding-should-update-or-inform-an-htbw-issue.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A Reference Home finding may be relevant to an HTBW issue while remaining private, configuration-specific or insufficiently qualified. The household wants its possible relevance and missing evidence understood before an authorized person decides what belongs in review. An AI interpretation must not silently edit the issue, create new work or rewrite accepted architecture.
- [Which finding belongs in public education but not product architecture?](which-finding-belongs-in-public-education-but-not-product-architecture.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A household story can help public education while depending on one configuration or private experience that does not justify product architecture. The household wants that educational value distinguished from validated requirements. Publishing a helpful narrative must not expose personal histories or imply owner acceptance of an envisioned capability.
- [Which finding requires broader household validation before promotion?](which-finding-requires-broader-household-validation-before-promotion.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A finding may come from only one household or depend on this Home's configuration, people and declared values. The household wants potentially generalizable findings distinguished from unsupported universal claims, including what might change in another context. Broader validation remains an open need rather than a claim that more households have already confirmed the result.
- [What evidence would another implementer need to reproduce this result?](what-evidence-would-another-implementer-need-to-reproduce-this-result.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Another implementer may need the declared outcome, exact conditions and evidence limitations to assess reproducibility, not the household's private inventory or credentials. The household wants that minimum independently shareable context distinguished from a copyable installation recipe. Reproducing a result elsewhere is not guaranteed by matching a vendor or device name.
- [Which finding should become a scenario rather than an architecture decision?](which-finding-should-become-a-scenario-rather-than-an-architecture-decision.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A lived finding can reveal a household question before enough evidence exists to assign architecture or implementation ownership. The household wants it captured at scenario maturity rather than promoted through a persuasive explanation. Repeated observations may inform later requirements, but repetition alone does not settle a mechanism or transfer HTBW responsibility.
- [Which new obligation appeared?](which-new-obligation-appeared.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. An improvement can reveal maintenance, training, credential or support work that was not part of the household's original expectation. The household wants that observed burden and uncertainty understood without an AI creating obligations or declaring the change unsuccessful solely because it needs care. Declared significance and acceptable burden remain household judgments.
- [Did the improvement remain valuable over time?](did-the-improvement-remain-valuable-over-time.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. An initially welcome improvement may become less useful as context, maintenance burden or household needs change. The household wants its value over eligible periods understood rather than initial enthusiasm treated as enduring success. Reduced use can reflect intentional choice or missing evidence, not automatically a failed capability or consent to remove it.
- [Which result depends on a preferred vendor rather than an architectural requirement?](which-result-depends-on-a-preferred-vendor-rather-than-an-architectural-requirement.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A successful result may depend on a vendor-specific feature while the architectural requirement is the household outcome it enabled. The household wants that distinction visible without turning a preferred brand into a universal standard. A supported vendor implementation can be useful evidence while alternatives and exact dependency remain unproven.
- [What remains unproven?](what-remains-unproven.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A mature-looking corpus or lived demonstration can leave mechanism, reproducibility, source eligibility and broader household value unproven. The household wants those separate limitations named rather than a general PASS concealing them. A scenario increment awaiting owner review cannot borrow prior corpus acceptance or imply runtime acceptance.
