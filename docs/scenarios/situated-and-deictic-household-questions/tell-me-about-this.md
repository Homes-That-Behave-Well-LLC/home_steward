# Tell me about this.

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

Tell me about this.

## Why it matters

The word 'this' may refer to an object, a control, a document or an ongoing experience. The household wants a useful conversation without a fluent explanation about the wrong thing. Room context may narrow the possibilities but does not establish the intended referent, Asset identity or permission to consult a nearby private source.

## Existing HTBW relationship

Foundation owns declared definitions and configured relationships; Stewardship owns household-declared significance, care, obligations and Custody; Identity supplies purpose-specific Person context, not authentication or authorization; Truth owns qualified current and historical Facts; Continuity owns accepted preferences and experience intent; Operational Trust determines permission and disclosure for this request, in this context; Concierge retains governed orchestration and authoritative Decision Trace composition. Native Explainability remains HTBW-owned. Home Assistant and devices retain execution. Home Steward does not require the standalone predecessor Asset Intelligence integration.

## Possible Home Steward extension

Home Steward could use independently authorized retrieval, synthesis, comparison and contextual interpretation to compare independently eligible contextual clues and present candidate meanings or a needed clarification. This is a prospective question, not a delivered capability or a mechanism. Any new source admission, representation or handoff remains an OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION.

## Important boundary

CONTEXT NARROWS CANDIDATES; IT DOES NOT ESTABLISH THE REFERENT. Unknown or clarification is preferable to confidently choosing the wrong object.

Evidence categories are not source admission. Person/source/purpose/namespace/audience authorization remains independent before retrieval, combination and disclosure. One Person's grant is not another's; summaries retain the most restrictive applicable source constraints. USE != INGEST != DERIVE != RETAIN. Source dates, uncertainty, correction, retention, expiry, deletion and revocation remain binding within the household-controlled network boundary; ordinary retention may be zero/disabled. Confirmation does not grant authority to retain, disclose or act. No shadow history or silent cloud/source fallback.

Any image or camera relationship remains separately authorized by purpose, source, eligible Rooms, private-space exclusions, requester and audience. LAST OBSERVED != CURRENT LOCATION; nonvisibility is not physical absence. Incidental Person identification is not required for an object question and creates no Identity assertion. Guests and service providers retain privacy; ephemeral use does not authorize durable images, derived location history or Person memory.

Observation, report and inference are not automatic Truth. Correlation is not causation; unknown is valid. Issued command != observed physical result. People and Pets are not Assets. No medical, life-safety or emergency path depends on Home Steward or an LLM. Scenario capture only: no runtime, hook, interface, schema, handler, pipeline, scheduling, access decision or configuration change is specified or authorized.
