# What can I use to control that television?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

What can I use to control that television?

## Why it matters

Several televisions or control surfaces may be nearby while only some have a configured relationship to the intended display. The household wants 'that television' and its eligible controls distinguished before trying something. Seeing a remote or speaker beside it does not establish which equipment it controls.
