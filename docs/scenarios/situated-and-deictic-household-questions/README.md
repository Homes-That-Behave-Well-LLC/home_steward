# Situated and Deictic Household Questions

Navigation and scenario-capture guidance only; not architecture, a roadmap or delivered functionality. Browse the [scenario-first map](../README.md).

## Purpose

Clarify this, that and here through independently eligible context; proximity narrows candidates but never establishes the intended referent.

## Relationship to HTBW

Foundation owns declared definitions and configured relationships; Stewardship owns household-declared significance, care, obligations and Custody; Identity supplies purpose-specific Person context, not authentication or authorization; Truth owns qualified current and historical Facts; Continuity owns accepted preferences and experience intent; Operational Trust determines permission and disclosure for this request, in this context; Concierge retains governed orchestration and authoritative Decision Trace composition. Native Explainability remains HTBW-owned. Home Assistant and devices retain execution. Home Steward does not require the standalone predecessor Asset Intelligence integration.

See the [HTBW boundary](../../architecture/htbw-boundary.md), [source authorization](../../architecture/source-authorization.md) and [derived lifecycle](../../architecture/derived-knowledge-lifecycle.md).

## Boundaries

Evidence categories are not source admission. Person/source/purpose/namespace/audience authorization remains independent before retrieval, combination and disclosure. One Person's grant is not another's; summaries retain the most restrictive applicable source constraints. USE != INGEST != DERIVE != RETAIN. Source dates, uncertainty, correction, retention, expiry, deletion and revocation remain binding within the household-controlled network boundary; ordinary retention may be zero/disabled. Confirmation does not grant authority to retain, disclose or act. No shadow history or silent cloud/source fallback.

Observation, report and inference are not automatic Truth. Correlation is not causation; unknown is valid. Issued command != observed physical result. People and Pets are not Assets. No medical, life-safety or emergency path depends on Home Steward or an LLM. Scenario capture only: no runtime, hook, interface, schema, handler, pipeline, scheduling, access decision or configuration change is specified or authorized.

## Primary scenarios

Primary files: **0 developed + 12 Vision = 12**. Existing coverage and aliases do not add primary files or change target maturity.

This/that/here can identify a requested scope only after adequate eligible clarification. Context and proximity narrow candidates; neither establishes identity, intended referent, current Truth or authority. Input origin and output audience remain separate. Linked existing coverage inherits these open referent and visual/privacy questions; no source is admitted by a cross-link.

Any image or camera relationship remains separately authorized by purpose, source, eligible Rooms, private-space exclusions, requester and audience. LAST OBSERVED != CURRENT LOCATION; nonvisibility is not physical absence. Incidental Person identification is not required for an object question and creates no Identity assertion. Guests and service providers retain privacy; ephemeral use does not authorize durable images, derived location history or Person memory.

New primary files in this increment: **12**. Each is catalogued only in this primary family. All earlier bodies and maturity remain unchanged.

- [Tell me about this.](tell-me-about-this.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. The word 'this' may refer to an object, a control, a document or an ongoing experience. The household wants a useful conversation without a fluent explanation about the wrong thing. Room context may narrow the possibilities but does not establish the intended referent, Asset identity or permission to consult a nearby private source.
- [What is that device on the wall?](what-is-that-device-on-the-wall.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A wall-mounted object may be a sensor, control, interface or unregistered device. The household wants its possible purpose understood without assuming every visible device belongs to HTBW or serves the current Room. Resemblance and proximity should not establish a configured function or permission to inspect it.
- [Why is it warm in here?](why-is-it-warm-in-here.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A person saying it is warm 'in here' may mean a physical spot, a configured Room or a broader comfort experience. The household wants the relevant scope and eligible evidence clarified before a cause is suggested. A Room reading, personal feeling and HVAC action can differ without any one of them proving the cause.
- [What can I use to control that television?](what-can-i-use-to-control-that-television.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Several televisions or control surfaces may be nearby while only some have a configured relationship to the intended display. The household wants 'that television' and its eligible controls distinguished before trying something. Seeing a remote or speaker beside it does not establish which equipment it controls.
- [Is this the Asset with the appraisal concern?](is-this-the-asset-with-the-appraisal-concern.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A household member may recognize an appraisal concern yet point at a different, similar Asset. The household wants the physical referent, authoritative description and dated appraisal issue compared before private financial information is disclosed. A visual match should not become an Asset association, valuation or insurance conclusion.
- [Does this room contain anything environmentally sensitive?](does-this-room-contain-anything-environmentally-sensitive.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A Room can contain objects with very different declared environmental care needs. The household wants eligible sensitive-Asset context without assuming market value, material appearance or a shared Room establishes vulnerability. The answer may itself reveal private collection information that a visitor is not permitted to receive.
- [Is that the filter we need to replace?](is-that-the-filter-we-need-to-replace.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A visible filter may look like the one mentioned by a due obligation while belonging to another appliance or model. The household wants the physical candidate, exact part reference and governed care requirement distinguished before any work. A reminder or a similar package is not proof that this is the correct component.
- [Which of these objects belongs somewhere else?](which-of-these-objects-belongs-somewhere-else.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Several visible objects may appear out of place, but an expected location can be temporary, privately remembered or never declared. The household wants those candidates distinguished without converting ordinary objects into Assets or claiming ownership from where they are seen. A proposed return location is not a current Truth Fact or an instruction to move anything.
- [Which appliance does this control belong to?](which-appliance-does-this-control-belong-to.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A control can be mounted near an appliance it does not operate, or operate more than one configured function. The household wants the actual declared relationship understood before assuming proximity implies control. A mistaken association could affect care or another person's experience even if the control itself is familiar.
- [What does “here” mean for this request?](what-does-here-mean-for-this-request.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. 'Here' may refer to where the person stands, the Room assigned to an input, the audience of an output or the space being discussed. The household wants these meanings clarified instead of silently choosing the microphone's Room. Separately configured input and output can legitimately provide different context.
- [Which nearby object is the strongest candidate for “this”?](which-nearby-object-is-the-strongest-candidate-for-this.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. The nearest object may be an obvious candidate for 'this' while still being the wrong referent. The household wants useful distinctions among eligible candidates rather than a strongest-looking guess presented as identity. Confidence must reflect missing clues and competing interpretations, not simply physical proximity.
- [What clarification is required before making a conclusion?](what-clarification-is-required-before-making-a-conclusion.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A useful clarification can prevent an explanation about the wrong Room, appliance, Asset or person. The household wants the missing distinction named without being asked to disclose unnecessary private details. A response that admits uncertainty is preferable to an authoritative-sounding answer built on an unconfirmed referent.
