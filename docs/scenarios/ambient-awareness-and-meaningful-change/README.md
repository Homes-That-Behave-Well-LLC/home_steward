# Ambient Awareness and Meaningful Change

Navigation and scenario-capture guidance only; not architecture, a roadmap or delivered functionality. Browse the [scenario-first map](../README.md).

## Purpose

Understand candidate meaningful change without requiring the household to know which question to ask first. Ordinary activity, materially different evidence and a candidate matter deserving attention are distinct; noticing is not significance, diagnosis, permission or a notification.

## Relationship to HTBW

Foundation describes structure, not current Truth; Stewardship owns household-declared significance, care, obligations and accepted Custody; Identity supplies Person/context, not authentication or authorization; Truth owns current/historical Facts; Continuity retains accepted preference/session/experience responsibilities; Operational Trust determines permission/disclosure for this request/context; Concierge retains governed orchestration and Trace, not centralized ownership or acceptance. Native Explainability already includes historical/time/cross-Room retrieval. Home Assistant/devices retain execution.

See the accepted-owner [HTBW boundary](../../architecture/htbw-boundary.md), [source authorization](../../architecture/source-authorization.md) and [derived lifecycle](../../architecture/derived-knowledge-lifecycle.md).

## Boundaries

NOTICE != DIAGNOSE; NOTICE != DISCLOSE; NOTICE != RETAIN; NOTICE != RECOMMEND; NOTICE != ACT. Noticing is not authority or automatic Truth, household significance, notification or action.

Potential evidence is not admitted access. Person/source/purpose/namespace/audience gates remain independent; one Person's grant is not everyone's access and cross-person grants do not merge. The most restrictive applicable source constraints remain binding. Summaries can disclose their sources; conclusions are not automatically safe to share. USE != INGEST != DERIVE != RETAIN. Derived information remains source-bound through correction, retention, expiry, deletion and revocation within the household-controlled network boundary. Household-configured ordinary retention may be zero/disabled; no numeric minimum or maximum is imposed. Confirmation alone does not grant retention, disclosure, policy or action authority. No shadow history or silent cloud/source fallback.

Observation, source report, comparison and inference are not automatic Truth. Correlation is not causation; unknown is valid. Issued command != observed physical result. People and Pets are not Assets; Pet/device location does not flow through human Identity. No diagnosis, prognosis, treatment or medical causation follows from changed observations. No medical, life-safety or emergency path depends on an LLM; native/deterministic/manual safety remains independent.

All future source eligibility, meaningful-change review, retention, correction, disclosure, notification and action questions remain OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTIONS. No runtime, scheduler, camera pipeline, learning system, hook, consent engine, platform qualification or acceptance mechanism is designed here.

"Don't require the household to know the question before useful intelligence can recognize that something may be worth understanding."

Direct-query and ambient origins do not require duplicate scenario files. Cross-links below capture candidate noticing contexts; they do not add watchers, automatic notifications, materiality thresholds or authorizations to the existing bodies.

## Primary scenarios

Primary files: **0 developed + 20 Vision = 20**. Existing coverage and aliases do not add primary files or change target maturity.

- [Tell me when something in the Home begins behaving materially differently.](tell-me-when-something-in-the-home-begins-behaving-materially-differently.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Familiar Home behavior can drift gradually enough that nobody forms a question. The household wants a bounded comparison with its accepted expectations, including ordinary variation and gaps, rather than every difference being treated as significant.
- [Notice recurring failures before they become something we repeatedly complain about.](notice-recurring-failures-before-they-become-something-we-repeatedly-complain-about.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Repeated unsatisfactory outcomes can become familiar before anybody starts complaining. Recognizing an eligible recurrence could help the household understand burden, but absent expected-state reports and missing complaints cannot establish repeated physical failures.
- [Tell us when a workaround appears to have become normal household behavior.](tell-us-when-a-workaround-appears-to-have-become-normal-household-behavior.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A person may repeatedly take an extra step to get an accepted outcome. The household wants to understand whether this is a growing workaround or an intentional preference, without treating its adaptation as consent to a defective experience.
- [Notice when we're repeatedly overriding the same Home decision.](notice-when-we-re-repeatedly-overriding-the-same-home-decision.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Repeated overrides may indicate a poor fit between a decision and lived context. They may also reflect legitimate exceptions, so the household needs the relevant decisions and reasons rather than a judgment about the person intervening.
- [Tell us when multiple small changes appear to form a larger pattern.](tell-us-when-multiple-small-changes-appear-to-form-a-larger-pattern.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Several small changes may be easier to understand together than individually. The household wants a cautious description of overlap and unchanged evidence, because a convincing combined story can conceal missing observations or unrelated causes.
- [Tell us when something changes enough that the previous household assumption may deserve review.](tell-us-when-something-changes-enough-that-the-previous-household-assumption-may-deserve-review.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. An earlier assumption may have been reasonable for a different context. The household wants changed evidence to make reconsideration possible without silently invalidating the original rationale or treating a newer observation as an accepted replacement decision.
- [Notice when the Home has started requiring more intervention than it used to.](notice-when-the-home-has-started-requiring-more-intervention-than-it-used-to.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A Home can still achieve its outcomes while asking people for increasing effort. Understanding that burden could expose a deteriorating experience, but counting interventions without context can misrepresent seasonal needs, exceptions or voluntary involvement.
- [Tell us when a previously rare event becomes common.](tell-us-when-a-previously-rare-event-becomes-common.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Something once exceptional may become familiar enough to escape notice. The household wants a source-bounded comparison of frequency and opportunity, not a claim of growth caused by better reporting or a longer observation window.
- [Tell us when an expected behavior quietly stops happening.](tell-us-when-an-expected-behavior-quietly-stops-happening.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. An expected outcome can stop occurring quietly, without a reported error. The household wants to distinguish an unmet expectation from intentional absence, changed demand or a source that stopped observing, before considering any intervention.
- [What changed that nobody explicitly asked about?](what-changed-that-nobody-explicitly-asked-about.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Useful household questions need not all be anticipated by a person. Exploring separately authorized changes could reveal a candidate concern, but a broad invitation must not become blanket access to everything or an obligation to find problems.
- [What appears newly important today?](what-appears-newly-important-today.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A previously ordinary detail may matter when household circumstances change. The household wants contextual relevance explained against its declared commitments, not urgency invented from novelty, model fluency or a desire to produce a daily alert.
- [What have you noticed that deserves understanding but not action?](what-have-you-noticed-that-deserves-understanding-but-not-action.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Sometimes the useful outcome is understanding rather than intervention. The household wants observations worth exploring without being pushed toward recommendations, because unfamiliar or inconvenient behavior can be intentional, tolerable or insufficiently understood.
- [Tell us when an Asset's environment begins changing materially.](tell-us-when-an-asset-s-environment-begins-changing-materially.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Conditions near an Asset can change while the Room still feels ordinary to people. The household wants context tied to that object's declared care and actual exposure, rather than generic environmental targets becoming a new risk authority.
- [Notice when upcoming Asset care requires preparation.](notice-when-upcoming-asset-care-requires-preparation.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Upcoming care may require documents, access or supplies before the due date. The household wants to understand those declared prerequisites early enough for human preparation, without Home Steward creating obligations or scheduling a professional visit.
- [Tell us when important Asset evidence appears incomplete.](tell-us-when-important-asset-evidence-appears-incomplete.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Important Asset knowledge can remain incomplete even when the catalog has an entry. The household wants gaps across provenance, care and documentation made intelligible against declared significance, not missing documents interpreted as proof of low value or neglect.
- [Tell us when a condition record may deserve professional review.](tell-us-when-a-condition-record-may-deserve-professional-review.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A condition record may contain an unresolved observation that matters to care. The household wants to understand why professional review could be useful, without a model diagnosing damage, appraising the Asset or manufacturing a new urgency threshold.
- [Notice when custody-related evidence is missing before return.](notice-when-custody-related-evidence-is-missing-before-return.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. An expected return can approach without the agreed condition or handling evidence. The household wants to understand that gap before accepting a complete narrative, while preserving native Custody ownership and avoiding accusations against the temporary holder.
- [Tell us when an off-site Asset lacks expected environmental or condition evidence.](tell-us-when-an-off-site-asset-lacks-expected-environmental-or-condition-evidence.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. An off-site Asset may have environmental reports but no condition record, or the reverse. The household wants combined coverage gaps understood against declared care and reporting expectations, not facility promises mistaken for actual exposure or placement.
- [Notice when a prior pattern appears to be becoming more frequent.](notice-when-a-prior-pattern-appears-to-be-becoming-more-frequent.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A familiar personal pattern may appear more often without proving a medical change. The household wants comparable observations from that person's own authorized history, including altered routine and observation coverage, before interpreting an apparent increase.
- [Notice when an important household system begins consuming materially more resources.](notice-when-an-important-household-system-begins-consuming-materially-more-resources.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A major household system can consume more while still delivering its expected service. The household wants supported changes in quantities distinguished from rates, longer operating opportunity or changed care, rather than runtime alone being treated as resource measurement.
