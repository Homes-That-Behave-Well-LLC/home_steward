# Has usual activity changed this week

> Aging in Place and Care Awareness: synthetic, person-scoped, non-diagnostic experience capture.

## 1. Household request

"Has Dad's usual activity changed this week?" Understand observations relative to his own prior pattern.

## 2. Existing HTBW knowledge

Foundation Person/Room definitions, eligible Identity associations, Truth current/historical Home Facts,
Stewardship declared care and existing Explainability. Room motion alone is not Dad's activity.

## 3. Authorized private retrieval

Potential Person-scoped presence/activity, Room movement, wearable activity, routine and historical
baseline, each separately authorized. Family/caretaker status is not access authority.

## 4. Optional public research

Device documentation may clarify what a measurement reports, not diagnose change.
No private Person/care evidence enters public queries or unauthorized external processing.

## 5. Home Steward synthesis

Compare an explicitly selected recent week with the person's prior observed personal pattern.
Describe activity/coverage differences, context and uncertainty, not generic population normality,
illness or inferred reasons. Person movement evidence retains existing historical-access governance.

## 6. Provenance

Distinguish wearable report, Home observation, household narrative and comparison/inference.
Name source, observation time, baseline/recent periods, evidence quality and missing coverage.

## 7. Candidate relationships

Possible change associations are tentative observations, not diagnosis, causal relationships or care records.

## 8. Household confirmation

Clarify person, dates and household observations; confirmation is not data/disclosure authority.
Any new retention or review mechanism is an OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION.

## 9. Governed durable relationship

OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION: no care/proposal object, baseline model or relationship schema.

## 10. Proposed actions

The household may review observations or consider an independently authorized conversation/check-in.
Any handoff, disclosure or notification mechanism remains an OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION.

## 11. Prohibited autonomous actions

No diagnosis, disease/fall prediction, medication-effect inference, treatment, clinical monitoring,
emergency decision, automatic caregiver notification, Person profiling or care/policy change.

## 12. HTBW authority

Truth current Home state/history; Stewardship care obligations; Identity context; Foundation definitions;
Operational Trust action/disclosure; Continuity where applicable; Concierge and existing Explainability/Trace.

## 13. Privacy and locality

Person-scoped care/activity/history requires separate ingestion and disclosure permissions.
Local derivatives inherit sensitivity/lifecycle; no unscoped movement archive or household-wide exposure.

## 14. Unavailable / degraded behavior

"I don't know whether activity changed" is valid for insufficient/denied evidence, not proof of inactivity.
Native HTBW and established emergency mechanisms remain independent; no LLM critical path or cloud fallback.

## 15. Acceptance expectations

Five potential sources, personal comparison and attribution limits captured; no medical normality,
notification/ingestion/diagnostic architecture or runtime acceptance.

## 16. Open questions

Source eligibility, comparison representation, retention, caregiver review and any handoff remain
OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTIONS; no wearable ingestion or clinical algorithm.
