# Play something like what we heard Saturday

> Entertainment and Media Discovery: synthetic experience capture, not implementation or architecture.

## 1. Household request

"Play something like what we listened to during dinner Saturday." Recover context, then explore similarity.

## 2. Existing HTBW knowledge

Foundation Room definitions, Truth retained Home Facts and authorized Continuity experience/history
references. Provider playback history stays source-owned; a dinner does not identify all listeners.

## 3. Authorized private retrieval

Separately approved listening history, household narrative, Room/event/time context and Person
preferences. No blanket access to everyone who may have attended dinner.

## 4. Optional public research

Public album/artist/style metadata may help after authorized context establishes possible content.
Private listening dates/people/history must not be transmitted to public research.

## 5. Home Steward synthesis

Clarify which Saturday/timezone/dinner/Room and who "we" includes. Distinguish recorded listening
from recollection, identify possible content and desired similarity, and acknowledge incomplete history.

## 6. Provenance

Separate provider/HA records, household report, public metadata and similarity inference; name dates,
coverage and uncertainty. Streaming availability is separately freshness/region-sensitive.

## 7. Candidate relationships

Past-experience-to-content/similarity matches remain candidates, not new shared preference or history.

## 8. Household confirmation

Clarify remembered content/similarity; one listener cannot authorize another's private history.
Confirmation retention is an OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION, not automatic memory learning.

## 9. Governed durable relationship

OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION: no dinner/event object, history store or preference/relationship schema.

## 10. Proposed actions

The household may request a candidate, or merely discuss past music. Any later governed execution/
handoff remains an OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION; no playback mechanism is chosen.

## 11. Prohibited autonomous actions

No automatic playback/purchase, listener inference/profile, unrestricted history retrieval, memory
learning, configuration/integration changes or Home Assistant service mapping.

## 12. HTBW authority

Foundation definitions; Identity context; Truth state/history; Continuity experience; Stewardship care;
Operational Trust action/disclosure; Concierge interaction and existing Explainability/Decision Trace.

## 13. Privacy and locality

Person-scoped listening/event evidence remains separately governed; read/ingestion is not disclosure.
Local derivatives inherit lifecycle; expired histories are not preserved in shadow narratives.

## 14. Unavailable / degraded behavior

"I don't know what played" is valid for missing/unauthorized history; it does not mean nothing played.
Ask a memory clue, not silently consult another account/cloud; native HTBW remains.

## 15. Acceptance expectations

Time/event/Room/Person scope, authorized history and similarity ambiguity captured; no universal shared
taste, event model, transfer mechanism or runtime acceptance.

## 16. Open questions

Historical context admission, similarity/clarification retention and governed execution/handoff
remain OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTIONS.
