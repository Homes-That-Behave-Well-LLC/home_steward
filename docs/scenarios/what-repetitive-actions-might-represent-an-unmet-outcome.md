# What repetitive actions might represent an unmet outcome

> Proposed synthetic scenario; no actual household sequence or automatic scene creation.
> Applies [the maturity model](../architecture/possibility-and-improvement-discovery.md#canonical-seven-level-possibility-maturity-model).

## 1. Household request

"What repetitive things do we keep doing manually that the Home might help with?" Agree technical
scope and outcome review, not permission for Person/movement monitoring.

## 2. Existing HTBW knowledge

Foundation configured rooms/device sets/participation; eligible Truth Historical Facts, native
Decision Traces, Continuity context and Stewardship declared outcomes.

## 3. Authorized private retrieval

Minimum eligible manual action sequences/overrides in agreed windows; history/logs and dashboard
interactions require separate source/Person grants. Unknown attribution remains unknown.

## 4. Optional public research

[Official scripts documentation](https://www.home-assistant.io/docs/script/) can verify a deliberately
invoked native mechanism; it does not authorize creating it or prove every media operation is supported.

## 5. Home Steward synthesis

Compare same room/device set, ordering and similar time/context with sample/coverage limits.
Synthetic sequence: pause Den music, lower shades, adjust lights, start video experience.
It is first **Observation**, then possibly **Candidate Opportunity**, never automatically a scene.

"Would you like to explore a deliberately invoked movie-preparation experience?" asks about an
outcome; it does not create the scene. Repetition may reflect preference for manual choice, testing,
exceptions or multiple unassociated people. Household-confirmed intent must precede proposal review.
Expected convenience benefit and new support/privacy obligation may not justify any automation.

## 6. Provenance

Source/version/time windows, sequence/sample/exposure, attribution and coverage; official effective/
review date, installed version and GA/Labs/preview status. Pattern/correlation is not Fact or intent.

## 7. Candidate relationships

Possible recurring-activity association is an inference, not a persisted Pattern, preference or
accepted experience. Concierge owns any authoritative Learned Suggestion formulation.

## 8. Household confirmation

"Yes, we intentionally do that and want it easier" is scoped maturity Level 3 evidence, **not**
DL-123 acceptance of preference/permission or implementation authorization.

## 9. Governed durable relationship

Responsible Foundation/Continuity/Operational Trust owners govern any later accepted records through
their existing paths. No Home Steward write or new learned-policy lifecycle.

## 10. Proposed actions

Offer an outcome/requirements draft via HTBW/Operational Trust; include manual invocation, exceptions,
failure behavior, new obligations, reversibility and acceptance/lived-outcome criteria.

## 11. Prohibited autonomous actions

No scene/automation/script generation or enablement, policy/preference/obligation edits, entity/
integration enablement, HA update/rollback, Labs enablement or upstream contribution.

## 12. HTBW authority

Foundation model; Truth Facts; Continuity intent; Stewardship care; Identity requester;
Operational Trust permission; Concierge formulation/coordination/Trace, not acceptance.

## 13. Privacy and locality

Minimum source/Person/audience scope before analysis/disclosure; no human profiles or movement-history
bypass. Local derived candidates inherit expiry; no indefinite raw-log/request memory.

## 14. Unavailable / degraded behavior

Insufficient/aged-out records or unattributed actions yield "I don't know whether this sequence is
intentional". Native manual control continues; no guessed intent or cloud fallback.

## 15. Acceptance expectations

Observation, Candidate Opportunity and confirmed intent remain distinct. Four synthetic steps are
not automatic automation; explicit control and no-action choice remain valid.

## 16. Open questions

Eligible recurrence/context evidence, feedback capture and governed proposal review remain future
design; no sequence detector, history ingestion or scene is implemented.
