# Multi-Surface Household Interaction

Navigation and scenario-capture guidance only; not architecture, a roadmap or delivered functionality. Browse the [scenario-first map](../README.md).

## Purpose

Capture voice, phone, wall-kiosk and equipment-side experiences with fresh context and audience boundaries. Interaction continuity is not automatic transfer of source grants or a routing architecture.

## Relationship to HTBW

Foundation describes structure, not current Truth; Stewardship owns household-declared significance, care, obligations and accepted Custody; Identity supplies Person/context, not authentication or authorization; Truth owns current/historical Facts; Continuity retains accepted preference/session/experience responsibilities; Operational Trust determines permission/disclosure for this request/context; Concierge retains governed orchestration and Trace, not centralized ownership or acceptance. Native Explainability already includes historical/time/cross-Room retrieval. Home Assistant/devices retain execution.

See the accepted-owner [HTBW boundary](../../architecture/htbw-boundary.md), [source authorization](../../architecture/source-authorization.md) and [derived lifecycle](../../architecture/derived-knowledge-lifecycle.md).

## Boundaries

NOTICE != DIAGNOSE; NOTICE != DISCLOSE; NOTICE != RETAIN; NOTICE != RECOMMEND; NOTICE != ACT. Noticing is not authority or automatic Truth, household significance, notification or action.

Potential evidence is not admitted access. Person/source/purpose/namespace/audience gates remain independent; one Person's grant is not everyone's access and cross-person grants do not merge. The most restrictive applicable source constraints remain binding. Summaries can disclose their sources; conclusions are not automatically safe to share. USE != INGEST != DERIVE != RETAIN. Derived information remains source-bound through correction, retention, expiry, deletion and revocation within the household-controlled network boundary. Household-configured ordinary retention may be zero/disabled; no numeric minimum or maximum is imposed. Confirmation alone does not grant retention, disclosure, policy or action authority. No shadow history or silent cloud/source fallback.

Observation, source report, comparison and inference are not automatic Truth. Correlation is not causation; unknown is valid. Issued command != observed physical result. People and Pets are not Assets; Pet/device location does not flow through human Identity. No diagnosis, prognosis, treatment or medical causation follows from changed observations. No medical, life-safety or emergency path depends on an LLM; native/deterministic/manual safety remains independent.

All future source eligibility, meaningful-change review, retention, correction, disclosure, notification and action questions remain OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTIONS. No runtime, scheduler, camera pipeline, learning system, hook, consent engine, platform qualification or acceptance mechanism is designed here.

## Primary scenarios

Primary files: **0 developed + 21 Vision = 21**. Existing coverage and aliases do not add primary files or change target maturity.

- [Ask Home Steward a question from a room voice assistant.](ask-home-steward-a-question-from-a-room-voice-assistant.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A Room voice assistant can be convenient while exposing an answer to everyone nearby. The household wants the specific requester, purpose and audience considered without assuming speaker presence authenticates a person or that a voice request grants every source needed for a private answer.
- [Continue a conversation on a personal phone.](continue-a-conversation-on-a-personal-phone.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Continuing on a personal phone may provide a different audience than a shared Room. The household wants conversation context preserved only where independently eligible, because possession of a phone or prior access on another surface does not authenticate the person or transfer source permissions.
- [Show supporting evidence on the wall kiosk.](show-supporting-evidence-on-the-wall-kiosk.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A wall kiosk may make supporting evidence easier to inspect but can reveal it to bystanders. The household wants separately eligible detail displayed only within its audience scope, rather than an answer authorized privately becoming a document disclosure on a shared surface.
- [This answer is private; do not speak it in the room.](this-answer-is-private-do-not-speak-it-in-the-room.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A private answer can become a disclosure simply by being spoken in a Room. The household wants that consequence respected before output, without sensitive content first being announced to explain why it should not be announced or a private label acting as a new permission rule.
- [Give the short answer by voice and supporting detail on screen.](give-the-short-answer-by-voice-and-supporting-detail-on-screen.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A concise spoken answer and detailed screen evidence can have different audiences and sensitivity. The household wants each part considered separately, because omitting detail from voice does not automatically make the summary safe or permit the full source on a shared display.
- [Continue a conversation after moving to another room.](continue-a-conversation-after-moving-to-another-room.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Starting in one Room and continuing elsewhere can change who is present and what context applies. The household wants eligible conversation continuity without transferring private source grants or assuming the new location preserves the prior audience, preference or permission decision.
- [Show media candidates on a suitable visual surface.](show-media-candidates-on-a-suitable-visual-surface.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Media candidates can be easier to distinguish visually than through a spoken list. The household wants eligible titles and availability context shown without revealing private viewing interests or treating discovery as permission to play media, authenticate a service or select a display automatically.
- [Show service instructions near the relevant equipment.](show-service-instructions-near-the-relevant-equipment.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Instructions near the equipment may reduce confusion during a household task. The household wants exact-model, eligible guidance and safe manual alternatives understood, rather than a convenient surface showing generic advice, changing device state or making safe operation depend on Home Steward availability.
- [Let a person choose among candidates on an appropriate interaction surface.](let-a-person-choose-among-candidates-on-an-appropriate-interaction-surface.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Selecting among candidates can clarify intent without authorizing the resulting action. The household wants that distinction preserved on an appropriate eligible surface, because a touch or spoken choice may identify an option but not grant disclosure, playback, purchase or authoritative relationship acceptance.
- [Do not display this information on a shared screen.](do-not-display-this-information-on-a-shared-screen.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A shared screen can expose source-bound information beyond the intended person. The household wants a non-display request respected without showing the private details to confirm it, and without assuming a nearby personal device or another room is automatically an eligible alternative audience.
- [Which interaction surface is appropriate for this information?](which-interaction-surface-is-appropriate-for-this-information.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. The appropriate interaction surface depends on content, requester and actual audience, not just convenience. The household wants these constraints understood before disclosure, rather than Home Steward inventing a surface-selection policy or treating a private device name as proof of authentication and eligible access.
- [What happens when speaker identity is unresolved?](what-happens-when-speaker-identity-is-unresolved.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A spoken request may arrive without a sufficiently resolved Person context. The household wants native uncertainty respected, so Home Steward does not guess identity from voice or nearby devices, disclose private answers or infer authority from the fact that someone can speak in the Room.
- [What happens when multiple people are in the room?](what-happens-when-multiple-people-are-in-the-room.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Several people in a Room can change the audience of a spoken or displayed answer. The household wants permitted shared context distinguished from each person's private grants, without identifying everyone unnecessarily or assuming the presence of one authorized person permits disclosure to all bystanders.

## Reference Home discovery increment

Native Room Help describes configured exposed capability and vocabulary, not raw inventory. Existence != Participation != Exposure != Authority. Input/microphone, output/speaker, interface and physical remote are separately configured relationships; HTBW exposure never bypasses native Assist safety exposure. A configured unresolved-Person fallback is an interaction default, not an Identity assertion. Physical movement, a personal device name or a remote choice transfers no source grant or execution authority. Continuity, scheduling, routing and surface selection remain unarchitected here.

New primary files in this increment: **8**. Each is catalogued only in this primary family. All earlier bodies and maturity remain unchanged.

- [What can I do in this room?](what-can-i-do-in-this-room.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A person in a Room may want the simplest permitted way to use it rather than a device inventory. The household wants configured, exposed capabilities and appropriate voice vocabulary explained in the current context. Knowing what can be asked does not permit every resulting action, and a short explanation should not reveal intentionally excluded capabilities.
- [What can this physical remote control here?](what-can-this-physical-remote-control-here.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A physical remote may control different things depending on its configured Room and current experience. The household wants its eligible controls and limits understood without guessing from the button labels or promising every nearby television can be controlled. Physical possession of the remote does not establish Person identity or authority.
- [Why is this capability available in the Den but not the Primary Bedroom?](why-is-this-capability-available-in-the-den-but-not-the-primary-bedroom.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A capability available in the Den may be intentionally absent from the Primary Bedroom because participation, exposure or current context differs. The household wants a permitted explanation, not an assumption that the Rooms should be identical. Even explaining the difference must not expose another person's private Room or policy details.
- [Which room capabilities are intentionally hidden from guests?](which-room-capabilities-are-intentionally-hidden-from-guests.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A guest-facing Room experience can be intentionally simpler than the household's full configuration. The household wants those exclusions understood by an eligible reviewer without showing the excluded functions to the guest. Hidden, unsupported and currently unavailable are different states; none should be inferred solely from an empty interface.
- [Which room capabilities require a sufficiently resolved person?](which-room-capabilities-require-a-sufficiently-resolved-person.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Some Room operations may require stronger Person resolution than others in the same conversation. The household wants existing requirements explained without equating recognition with authority or refusing every unresolved request. Native configured fallback defaults, if any, remain defaults rather than an Identity assertion about the speaker.
- [Pause this explanation and remind me when I reach the equipment room.](pause-this-explanation-and-remind-me-when-i-reach-the-equipment-room.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A person may pause an explanation because the relevant equipment is elsewhere and want to revisit the service inquiry when near it. The household wants that intention understood without a promise that anything is tracking movement or scheduling a reminder. Later proximity may change the Asset, audience and source eligibility, so continuation cannot simply replay the private earlier answer.
- [Let music follow the person across rooms, but stop when the context changes.](let-music-follow-the-person-across-rooms-but-stop-when-the-context-changes.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Music that follows a person can become inappropriate when another person is present, a Room purpose changes or the original intent expires. The household wants the accepted context limits understood without assuming physical movement transfers a session or authorizes sound everywhere. Stopping or continuing remains a native governed experience, not an inferred preference learned by Home Steward.
- [What context should follow a person?](what-context-should-follow-a-person.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Some context belongs to a Person's accepted experience while other context remains associated with a Room, its equipment or its audience. The household wants those distinctions understood without copying private source grants or carrying a Room assumption into another space. Person-associated and Room-associated context may coexist; neither is an automatic permission bundle.
