# Let a person choose among candidates on an appropriate interaction surface.

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

Let a person choose among candidates on an appropriate interaction surface.

## Why it matters

Selecting among candidates can clarify intent without authorizing the resulting action. The household wants that distinction preserved on an appropriate eligible surface, because a touch or spoken choice may identify an option but not grant disclosure, playback, purchase or authoritative relationship acceptance.
