# Pause this explanation and remind me when I reach the equipment room.

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

Pause this explanation and remind me when I reach the equipment room.

Related wording covered by this primary, without another file:

- Continue the service investigation when I am physically near the Asset.

## Why it matters

A person may pause an explanation because the relevant equipment is elsewhere and want to revisit the service inquiry when near it. The household wants that intention understood without a promise that anything is tracking movement or scheduling a reminder. Later proximity may change the Asset, audience and source eligibility, so continuation cannot simply replay the private earlier answer.
