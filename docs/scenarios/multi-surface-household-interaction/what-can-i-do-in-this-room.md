# What can I do in this room?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

What can I do in this room?

Related wording covered by this primary, without another file:

- What can I ask the voice assistant in this room?
- Show me the simplest way to use this room.

## Why it matters

A person in a Room may want the simplest permitted way to use it rather than a device inventory. The household wants configured, exposed capabilities and appropriate voice vocabulary explained in the current context. Knowing what can be asked does not permit every resulting action, and a short explanation should not reveal intentionally excluded capabilities.
