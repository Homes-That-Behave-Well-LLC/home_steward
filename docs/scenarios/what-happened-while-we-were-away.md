# What happened while we were away

> Proposed synthetic scenario; no actual absence dates, recordings or event log.

## 1. Household request

"What happened while we were away?"

## 2. Existing HTBW knowledge

Truth history and eligible Stewardship events; Continuity context; Foundation Asset/Room relationships.
An absence interval must be established, not inferred from private travel sources by default.

## 3. Authorized private retrieval

Approved service/event reports and eligible records within the specified interval; recordings only
with explicit capture, retention, beneficiary and retrieval scope.

## 4. Optional public research

Usually none. External weather/context is optional and separately authorized.

## 5. Home Steward synthesis

Build a bounded narrative from eligible dated evidence; label gaps. Correlated events do not prove
causation, occupant identity or an incident that Truth does not support.

## 6. Provenance

Event times/quality, source references, interval and missing observation periods.

## 7. Candidate relationships

Possible service-to-state change is inferred pending the responsible owner's evidence process.

## 8. Household confirmation

Confirm interval/desired detail; no consent to disclose third-party recordings or all occupant activity.

## 9. Governed durable relationship

No narrative-to-Truth write. Foundation/Truth govern eligible associations/Facts.

## 10. Proposed actions

Suggest investigating an eligible gap or review item through HTBW.

## 11. Prohibited autonomous actions

No surveillance expansion, inferred identity publication, alarm reset, incident conclusion or external contact.

## 12. HTBW authority

Truth history; Continuity context; Foundation identity; Stewardship care; Identity requester;
Operational Trust disclosure; Concierge orchestration.

## 13. Privacy and locality

Person/source/time/audience scope and recording lifecycle apply to local generated narratives.

## 14. Unavailable / degraded behavior

Present eligible native history; missing history stays missing, with no cloud/guess fallback.

## 15. Acceptance expectations

Bounded dates/gaps and source provenance; no unsupported causal/identity claims or expanded surveillance.

## 16. Open questions

Event eligibility, interval resolution, recording policy and narrative retention remain open.
