# What did the cameras not see?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

What did the cameras not see?

## Why it matters

Gaps between relevant camera observations can make a plausible movement story tempting. The household wants those gaps stated explicitly, because the absence of an object from a later view does not establish the intervening time, route, actor or physical absence.

Synthetic evidence: the object is visible at 09:01 and absent from the next relevant view at 11:15. No intervening movement, exact movement time, actor or cause is supplied.

## Important boundary

Do not invent intervening movement, exact time, actor or cause; frame nonvisibility is not physical absence.

"The object is visible in the 09:01 evidence and absent in the next relevant evidence at 11:15. The available evidence does not establish when or how it moved." A camera gap does not establish physical absence.
