# Visual Evidence and Household Observation

Navigation and scenario-capture guidance only; not architecture, a roadmap or delivered functionality. Browse the [scenario-first map](../README.md).

## Purpose

Understand bounded object, delivery, Asset, service, project and Pet observations. Visible evidence is partial and dated; LAST OBSERVED is not automatic CURRENT LOCATION or a continuous household history.

## Relationship to HTBW

Foundation describes structure, not current Truth; Stewardship owns household-declared significance, care, obligations and accepted Custody; Identity supplies Person/context, not authentication or authorization; Truth owns current/historical Facts; Continuity retains accepted preference/session/experience responsibilities; Operational Trust determines permission/disclosure for this request/context; Concierge retains governed orchestration and Trace, not centralized ownership or acceptance. Native Explainability already includes historical/time/cross-Room retrieval. Home Assistant/devices retain execution.

See the accepted-owner [HTBW boundary](../../architecture/htbw-boundary.md), [source authorization](../../architecture/source-authorization.md) and [derived lifecycle](../../architecture/derived-knowledge-lifecycle.md).

## Boundaries

NOTICE != DIAGNOSE; NOTICE != DISCLOSE; NOTICE != RETAIN; NOTICE != RECOMMEND; NOTICE != ACT. Noticing is not authority or automatic Truth, household significance, notification or action.

Potential evidence is not admitted access. Person/source/purpose/namespace/audience gates remain independent; one Person's grant is not everyone's access and cross-person grants do not merge. The most restrictive applicable source constraints remain binding. Summaries can disclose their sources; conclusions are not automatically safe to share. USE != INGEST != DERIVE != RETAIN. Derived information remains source-bound through correction, retention, expiry, deletion and revocation within the household-controlled network boundary. Household-configured ordinary retention may be zero/disabled; no numeric minimum or maximum is imposed. Confirmation alone does not grant retention, disclosure, policy or action authority. No shadow history or silent cloud/source fallback.

Observation, source report, comparison and inference are not automatic Truth. Correlation is not causation; unknown is valid. Issued command != observed physical result. People and Pets are not Assets; Pet/device location does not flow through human Identity. No diagnosis, prognosis, treatment or medical causation follows from changed observations. No medical, life-safety or emergency path depends on an LLM; native/deterministic/manual safety remains independent.

All future source eligibility, meaningful-change review, retention, correction, disclosure, notification and action questions remain OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTIONS. No runtime, scheduler, camera pipeline, learning system, hook, consent engine, platform qualification or acceptance mechanism is designed here.

LAST OBSERVED != automatic CURRENT LOCATION. Nonvisibility in a frame is not proof of physical absence or current location. Camera/image access is separately governed by purpose, eligible Rooms, private-space exclusions and eligible requester; identifying people is not necessary merely because they appear. Guests and service providers retain their own privacy boundaries. Incorrect Person/object guesses never become Identity assertions or access authority. Ephemeral analysis does not authorize a durable derived fact, another image/video copy or hidden person/location memory. Deletion/revocation of a source continues to constrain derived location knowledge.

Every relevant image scenario, including existing cross-linked coverage, inherits these unresolved questions:

- **OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION:** Which Rooms are eligible, and which private Rooms or spaces are excluded?
- **OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION:** Who is eligible to request and receive this observation?
- **OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION:** Is person identification necessary at all for this object/evidence question?
- **OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION:** What source, working-copy and derived retention is separately permitted, including zero/disabled retention?
- **OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION:** What happens to derived location knowledge when source evidence is deleted or revoked?
- **OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION:** How are guests and service providers appearing in frames protected?
- **OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION:** Could the evidence be used ephemerally without durable image/video or person memory?
- **OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION:** Could a separately permitted, scoped fact be retained without another copy of the image/video?
- **OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION:** What do framing, gaps, timestamps, uncertain identification and occlusion prevent the evidence from establishing?

## Primary scenarios

Primary files: **0 developed + 37 Vision = 37**. Existing coverage and aliases do not add primary files or change target maturity.

- [Where did I leave my glasses?](where-did-i-leave-my-glasses.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Misplaced glasses can be hard to search for without recalling their last appearance. The household wants help from specifically eligible views, while recognizing that similar objects, occlusion and missing intervals prevent a reliable assertion of present location.
- [Where was this object last observed?](where-was-this-object-last-observed.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A remembered object may appear in a dated view even when nobody knows where it is now. The household wants that limited observation and its confidence explained, not the latest available frame mistaken for a complete account of movement.
- [Was the package ever brought inside?](was-the-package-ever-brought-inside.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A package shown at the door may or may not have been brought inside. The household wants bounded visible evidence relevant to that question, without reconstructing unseen movement or deciding that a carrier notice establishes physical receipt.
- [Where was the pet's leash last observed?](where-was-the-pet-s-leash-last-observed.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A Pet's leash can be misplaced independently of the Pet's whereabouts. The household wants object recall using eligible evidence, not a location history of the animal or person and not an inferred relationship becoming a new Asset or Identity assertion.
- [Was this equipment returned to its normal location?](was-this-equipment-returned-to-its-normal-location.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Equipment may no longer be visible where the household usually stores it. The household wants dated evidence of return distinguished from a declared normal location, because an expected placement and a missing frame are not current Truth.
- [When was this Asset last observed?](when-was-this-asset-last-observed.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. An Asset's last visible appearance may help bound a question about its handling. The household wants the observation date, identification uncertainty and coverage gaps exposed, without treating a catalog relationship or newer unverified image as current placement.
- [Did the package arrive?](did-the-package-arrive.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A delivery notification and a package visible near the Home provide different evidence. The household wants those claims distinguished, with uncertainty about contents and actual receipt, so a confident answer does not invent what a camera or carrier could not establish.
- [Was it moved after delivery?](was-it-moved-after-delivery.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A package may appear in different places across eligible observations. The household wants visible changes distinguished from a reconstructed movement, because the interval between frames may contain unseen handling and people whose identity is not necessary to the question.
- [Was it still outside when the weather changed?](was-it-still-outside-when-the-weather-changed.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A weather change can make an outside package worth checking. The household wants the timing and view limits explained before associating weather with exposure or damage, rather than assuming the package remained outside throughout a missing interval.
- [When was this appliance delivered?](when-was-this-appliance-delivered.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. An appliance's delivery date may matter to warranty or service questions. The household wants a visible arrival distinguished from invoice dates, installation dates and household acceptance, because those events can happen separately and image evidence may not identify the exact unit.
- [How many items appear to have arrived?](how-many-items-appear-to-have-arrived.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Several visible packages can suggest a partial or complete delivery without proving contents. The household wants a qualified description of apparent items, including obscured views and possible repeated sightings, rather than a model certifying an inventory count from images.
- [When was this Asset visually observed leaving the Home?](when-was-this-asset-visually-observed-leaving-the-home.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A visible Asset near an exit may be relevant to a custody question. The household wants evidence of an observed departure distinguished from merely passing through a view, with actor and destination left unknown unless separately established.
- [What visual evidence exists from before departure?](what-visual-evidence-exists-from-before-departure.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Before-departure imagery may help describe documented appearance without certifying condition. The household wants the eligible views and their dates available for a bounded comparison, including lighting and incomplete coverage, rather than treating any photograph as a professional baseline.
- [What visual evidence exists after return?](what-visual-evidence-exists-after-return.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Images after return may show some features while leaving others unseen. The household wants those limits compared with prior evidence and native return records, so apparent resemblance does not establish unchanged condition or completed Check-In.
- [Was the Asset moved while the household was away?](was-the-asset-moved-while-the-household-was-away.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A household absence can leave uncertainty about an Asset's placement. The household wants source-bounded visible differences rather than surveillance of visitors or an invented movement story, because a changed frame does not establish when, why or by whom something moved.
- [What visibly changed during the service visit?](what-visibly-changed-during-the-service-visit.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A service visit may leave visible changes that the household wants to understand. The useful outcome is a dated comparison with provider accounts and coverage limits, not an assertion that a technician caused every difference or completed all reported work.
- [Was this damage visible beforehand?](was-this-damage-visible-beforehand.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A concern discovered after service may have been visible earlier or may lack a usable baseline. The household wants that uncertainty preserved, because images can show marks without establishing physical damage, onset or who was responsible.
- [Which equipment did the technician appear to access?](which-equipment-did-the-technician-appear-to-access.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. The household may want context about equipment near a technician during a visit. A partial view can show proximity without proving access, work or permission, and identifying the person is not automatically necessary to understand the equipment evidence.
- [What happened immediately before this event?](what-happened-immediately-before-this-event.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Evidence before an event may help frame an investigation without revealing its cause. The household wants the nearest eligible observations and their gaps, because temporal adjacency is often more persuasive than the source coverage can justify.
- [What happened afterward?](what-happened-afterward.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Evidence after an event may show an outcome while leaving the intervening action unknown. The household wants to understand what became visible and when, rather than assuming that a later appearance verifies a command or explains how the outcome occurred.
- [When was the pet last observed in this area?](when-was-the-pet-last-observed-in-this-area.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A Pet's last appearance in an area may be useful without establishing its present whereabouts. The household wants a bounded observation that respects eligible Rooms and source scope, rather than continuous tracking or an emergency judgment about the animal.
- [Has an observed room-to-room pattern changed?](has-an-observed-room-to-room-pattern-changed.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A Pet's apparent movement between Rooms may change when access, routine or camera coverage changes. The household wants its own eligible observed pattern compared fairly, without reconstructing unseen routes or interpreting a changed pattern as illness or distress.
- [What visual evidence exists around a pet-monitor event?](what-visual-evidence-exists-around-a-pet-monitor-event.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A Pet monitor's event report may have nearby image evidence or none at all. The household wants reports and visible observations kept distinct, with timing and coverage limits made clear, rather than a sensor event becoming proof of a particular behavior or medical condition.
- [Is this the same object?](is-this-the-same-object.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Two objects may look alike while differing in ownership, model or identity. The household wants candidate similarities and discriminating evidence explained, because a fluent visual guess can otherwise become a mistaken Asset relationship or private attribution to a person.
- [Does this image show the same condition feature?](does-this-image-show-the-same-condition-feature.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A visible feature may recur across today's photo and last year's written record without proving the same physical condition. The household wants capture differences and description ambiguity explained, so a matched feature is not treated as diagnosis or an unchanged-condition certificate.
- [Does this serial number match the Asset record?](does-this-serial-number-match-the-asset-record.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A photographed serial number can help distinguish similar appliances or equipment. The household wants readable characters compared with the eligible Asset record, including ambiguous digits and record errors, rather than automatic catalog correction or another retained copy of the image.
- [Which Asset appears in this historical photograph?](which-asset-appears-in-this-historical-photograph.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A painting or other Asset may appear in a family photograph without an authoritative caption. The household wants candidate matching against eligible descriptions while protecting people in the image, not a visual guess becoming provenance, ownership or a new family-memory assertion.
- [Which room appears in this older photograph?](which-room-appears-in-this-older-photograph.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. An older photograph may show only a corner of a Room whose appearance later changed. The household wants a candidate Room association with dated configuration and framing limits, without reconstructing unseen private spaces or treating current decoration as historical proof.
- [What did the cameras not see?](what-did-the-cameras-not-see.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Gaps between relevant camera observations can make a plausible movement story tempting. The household wants those gaps stated explicitly, because the absence of an object from a later view does not establish the intervening time, route, actor or physical absence.
- [Use this camera view only to locate an object.](use-this-camera-view-only-to-locate-an-object.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A live view may answer a narrow object question without belonging in durable household knowledge. The household wants the eligible view used only for that purpose, so incidental people, private spaces and inferred location histories do not become retained memories.
- [Read this equipment label without retaining the entire image.](read-this-equipment-label-without-retaining-the-entire-image.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Reading a label may require only a small detail from an image. The household wants that distinction maintained without retaining the full frame or treating extracted text as automatically durable knowledge, particularly when the image also shows people or private surroundings.
- [Compare these condition photographs and then remove the working copies.](compare-these-condition-photographs-and-then-remove-the-working-copies.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A condition comparison may be useful once while its working copies need not persist. The household wants original sources, temporary copies and any separately permitted conclusion distinguished, so removing working images does not falsely imply original deletion or unrestricted retention of derived condition knowledge.
- [Use this temporary recording only for this investigation.](use-this-temporary-recording-only-for-this-investigation.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A temporary recording may support one investigation without authorizing later household reuse. The household wants its exact purpose, expiry and bystander sensitivity preserved, rather than a useful recording silently becoming general memory, a training example or permanent event history.
- [Do not add people visible in the frame to household memory.](do-not-add-people-visible-in-the-frame-to-household-memory.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. People can appear incidentally while the household asks about an object or event. The household wants their presence kept outside household memory, because seeing a face does not establish identity, participation, consent or a reason to retain a personal association.
- [Retain a household-confirmed serial number without retaining another copy of the source image.](retain-a-household-confirmed-serial-number-without-retaining-another-copy-of-the-source-image.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A household-confirmed serial number may be useful without another stored image. The household wants confirmation, source use and durable fact authority kept separate, so an accurate extraction does not automatically authorize retention, catalog editing or a permanent copy of the surrounding scene.
- [Retain the conclusion only if the household confirms it.](retain-the-conclusion-only-if-the-household-confirms-it.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A household may want a conclusion retained only after reviewing it. The useful distinction is between confirmation of meaning and independent authority to keep or share the derivation, because a confirmed answer can still contain restricted source information or an uncertain factual claim.
- [Use this document once without adding it to the durable knowledge corpus.](use-this-document-once-without-adding-it-to-the-durable-knowledge-corpus.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A document can answer one question without joining a durable corpus. The household wants the narrow use and any separately permitted derivation distinguished, so local reading does not become silent ingestion, an indefinite summary or a future source for unrelated questions.
