# Why is this object important to us?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

Why is this object important to us?

## Why it matters

An object's importance may come from different memories, relationships or occasions rather than a single documented origin. The household wants to hear the attributed perspectives it has chosen to share while preserving disagreement and uncertainty. Emotional significance should not be invented from appearance, price or ownership, nor treated as a settled fact about every family member.

## Existing HTBW relationship

Foundation retains definitions/configuration; Truth, current/historical Facts; Identity, Person resolution, not authentication/authorization; Stewardship, household-declared care/obligations; Continuity, preference/experience eligibility; Operational Trust, request-specific permissions; Concierge, orchestration/Trace. Home Steward acquires no ownership.

## Important boundary

Do not infer emotions, relationships, ownership or consent from an object's presence or appearance.

Not continuous recording or a new accepted memory/history responsibility. Preserve dates, scope, version and retention; corrected/disputed recollection is distinct from Facts. Derived-source revocation/expiry prohibit shadow memory.

Knowledge/interpretation never grants action or disclosure authority. Unclear evidence stays unknown; correlation/timing is not causation. No life-safety or medical/emergency path depends on Home Steward/LLM.
