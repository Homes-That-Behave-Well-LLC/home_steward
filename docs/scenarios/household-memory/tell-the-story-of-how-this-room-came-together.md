# Tell the story of how this room came together.

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

Tell the story of how this room came together.

## Why it matters

A room's present appearance can hide years of choices, gifts and practical compromises. The household wants an authorized, attributed account of how it came together without pretending that every choice has a recorded motive. Different participants may remember the sequence differently, and a smooth narrative should not conceal those disagreements or private details.
