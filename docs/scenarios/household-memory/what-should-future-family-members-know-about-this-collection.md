# What should future family members know about this collection?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

What should future family members know about this collection?

## Why it matters

Future family members may value the context behind a collection as much as its inventory. The household wants to understand which authorized stories, provenance claims and care descriptions could be meaningful under an explicitly agreed audience and purpose. A wish to preserve context does not establish inheritance rights or permission to retain every contributor's personal account indefinitely.

## Existing HTBW relationship

Foundation retains definitions/configuration; Truth, current/historical Facts; Identity, Person resolution, not authentication/authorization; Stewardship, household-declared care/obligations; Continuity, preference/experience eligibility; Operational Trust, request-specific permissions; Concierge, orchestration/Trace. Home Steward acquires no ownership.
