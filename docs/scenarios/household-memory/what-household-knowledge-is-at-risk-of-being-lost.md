# What household knowledge is at risk of being lost?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

What household knowledge is at risk of being lost?

## Why it matters

Knowledge can become difficult to find when documents are scattered, terms change or a contributor no longer wishes to share a recollection. The household wants to identify voluntarily acknowledged gaps and fragile source coverage without treating every detail as something that must be saved. Privacy choices and lawful expiry are legitimate limits, not failures of household memory.

## Existing HTBW relationship

Foundation retains definitions/configuration; Truth, current/historical Facts; Identity, Person resolution, not authentication/authorization; Stewardship, household-declared care/obligations; Continuity, preference/experience eligibility; Operational Trust, request-specific permissions; Concierge, orchestration/Trace. Home Steward acquires no ownership.

## Potential evidence

Approved topic inventories; source availability categories; consented recollections; version dates; retention/revocation limits.

Categories only, each independently authorized for purpose, Person, source, audience and namespace. Local processing and derived retention, correction, expiry and revocation remain source-bound.

## Important boundary

No compulsory capture, account harvesting or preservation of revoked knowledge under a loss-prevention rationale.

Not continuous recording or a new accepted memory/history responsibility. Preserve dates, scope, version and retention; corrected/disputed recollection is distinct from Facts. Derived-source revocation/expiry prohibit shadow memory.

Knowledge/interpretation never grants action or disclosure authority. Unclear evidence stays unknown; correlation/timing is not causation. No life-safety or medical/emergency path depends on Home Steward/LLM.
