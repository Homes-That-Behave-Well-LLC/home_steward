# Do we have all expected documents after return?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

Do we have all expected documents after return?

## Why it matters

An Asset may physically return while expected condition documentation, receipts or agreement-related papers remain incomplete. A household needs to understand those documentary gaps without confusing possession with recorded Check-In. The scope of expected documents also comes from accepted obligations and agreements, not from an AI's generic idea of what every loan should produce.

## Existing HTBW relationship

HTBW Foundation owns Assets and descriptive relationships; Truth owns current/historical Facts. Stewardship owns Custody Period lifecycle, Check-Out/Check-In, expected-return and due/overdue obligations, reminders, history and insurance-responsibility state, together with governed care.

## Possible Home Steward extension

Compare accessible return-document categories with the agreement and existing governed obligations. Explain absent, restricted, outdated and conflicting evidence separately, leaving document acceptance and all custody closure decisions to HTBW.

## Potential evidence

Return condition reports; agreement-document categories; governed obligations; delivery-document categories; Custody Period history; authorized correspondence categories.
These are categories only, not admitted sources.

## Important boundary

Not retrieved does not mean nonexistent; documentary completeness does not itself record Check-In.

Physical return is not recorded Check-In; declared custody location is not current Truth. Missing chain legs stay missing. Insurance, ownership, caretaking and custody are distinct. No shadow lifecycle or new Counterparty/role construct is envisioned.

Retrieval, derivation and disclosure each require independent Person/source/purpose/namespace/audience authorization. Unknown is a valid result. Sensitivity, locality and derived retention/revocation/deletion constraints persist. Family membership or caretaker identity grants no access.
