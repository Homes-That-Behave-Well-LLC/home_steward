# Which exhibitions changed provenance?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

Which exhibitions changed provenance?

## Why it matters

A household may hear that an exhibition changed an artwork's provenance and wonder what that claim actually means. Display history may add documented context without changing ownership or custody authority, and evidence quality can vary. Understanding which claims rest on catalogs, agreements or professional opinions would help the household ask better questions without accepting an AI-created provenance conclusion.
