# Where has this artwork been exhibited since acquisition?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

Where has this artwork been exhibited since acquisition?

## Why it matters

A household may want to understand an artwork's exhibition history after acquisition rather than its custody chain alone. An exhibition announcement, catalog entry and recorded loan can describe different things: intended display, documented participation and accountability. Comparing their scope could produce a useful explanation while keeping uncertain attendance, dates and venues visibly unresolved instead of turning publicity into a confirmed exhibition history.
