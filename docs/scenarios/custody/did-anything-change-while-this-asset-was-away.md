# Did anything change while this Asset was away?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

Did anything change while this Asset was away?

## Why it matters

A household may wonder whether condition, treatment, display context or agreement terms changed while an Asset was away. A dated report and a private message may offer different levels of support, and an undocumented interval remains unknown. A coherent comparison could aid review after return without treating every reported change as current Truth or assuming that silence means the Asset was unchanged.

## Existing HTBW relationship

HTBW Foundation owns Assets and descriptive relationships; Truth owns current/historical Facts. Stewardship owns Custody Period lifecycle, Check-Out/Check-In, expected-return and due/overdue obligations, reminders, history and insurance-responsibility state, together with governed care.

## Possible Home Steward extension

Compare authorized departure and later documentation with the recorded custody history. Explain reported changes, professional assessments and uncertain intervals without updating condition, provenance, custody or insurance responsibility.

## Potential evidence

Condition reports; departure and later photographs; treatment-document categories; agreement amendments; Custody Period history; authorized correspondence categories.
These are categories only, not admitted sources.

## Important boundary

Documented or reported change is not automatically a current Fact, accepted treatment history update or causation finding.

Physical return is not recorded Check-In; declared custody location is not current Truth. Missing chain legs stay missing. Insurance, ownership, caretaking and custody are distinct. No shadow lifecycle or new Counterparty/role construct is envisioned.

Retrieval, derivation and disclosure each require independent Person/source/purpose/namespace/audience authorization. Unknown is a valid result. Sensitivity, locality and derived retention/revocation/deletion constraints persist. Family membership or caretaker identity grants no access.
