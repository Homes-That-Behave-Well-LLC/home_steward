# What is currently away from the Home?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

What is currently away from the Home?

## Why it matters

A household may ask what is away when some Assets are recorded on loan, some are stored elsewhere and others have uncertain current location. Understanding the difference matters for a truthful overview. Ownership or the absence of an open Custody Period cannot establish that an object is physically at Home, and a declared off-site location is not a current observation.

## Existing HTBW relationship

HTBW Foundation owns Assets and descriptive relationships; Truth owns current/historical Facts. Stewardship owns Custody Period lifecycle, Check-Out/Check-In, expected-return and due/overdue obligations, reminders, history and insurance-responsibility state, together with governed care.

## Possible Home Steward extension

Explain the existing HTBW custody and location context, comparing declared expectations, current Location Facts and relevant authorized documents. Identify contradictions and unknowns without creating a new inventory state or changing a Custody Period.

## Potential evidence

Custody Period history; Location Facts; declared locations; loan-document categories; storage-document categories; authorized condition documentation.
These are categories only, not admitted sources.

## Important boundary

An overview is not a new custody state, location assertion or permission to contact a custodian.

Physical return is not recorded Check-In; declared custody location is not current Truth. Missing chain legs stay missing. Insurance, ownership, caretaking and custody are distinct. No shadow lifecycle or new Counterparty/role construct is envisioned.

Retrieval, derivation and disclosure each require independent Person/source/purpose/namespace/audience authorization. Unknown is a valid result. Sensitivity, locality and derived retention/revocation/deletion constraints persist. Family membership or caretaker identity grants no access.
