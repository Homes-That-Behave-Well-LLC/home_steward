# Who carries insurance responsibility while the Asset is away?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

Who carries insurance responsibility while the Asset is away?

## Why it matters

A household may be uncertain who bears insurance responsibility during a loan or storage period, especially when an agreement and a certificate use different language. Stewardship already owns the period's insurance-responsibility state. Authorized interpretation could expose inconsistencies for professional review, while keeping responsibility for coverage, actual policy protection, ownership and accountability for a claim from being collapsed into one assumption.

## Important boundary

Physical return is not recorded Check-In; declared custody location is not current Truth. Missing chain legs stay missing. Insurance, ownership, caretaking and custody are distinct. No shadow lifecycle or new Counterparty/role construct is envisioned.

Retrieval, derivation and disclosure each require independent Person/source/purpose/namespace/audience authorization. Unknown is a valid result. Sensitivity, locality and derived retention/revocation/deletion constraints persist. Family membership or caretaker identity grants no access.
