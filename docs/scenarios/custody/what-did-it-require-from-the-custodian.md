# What did it require from the custodian?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

What did it require from the custodian?

## Why it matters

A household may want to understand what a loan agreement asked of the custodian, particularly when later reports are incomplete or differently worded. Explicit requirements and inferred expectations should remain distinguishable. An explanation could support informed review without declaring breach, treating a reported action as verified performance or granting the household access to the custodian's private systems and correspondence.

## Existing HTBW relationship

HTBW Foundation owns Assets and descriptive relationships; Truth owns current/historical Facts. Stewardship owns Custody Period lifecycle, Check-Out/Check-In, expected-return and due/overdue obligations, reminders, history and insurance-responsibility state, together with governed care.

## Possible Home Steward extension

Compare authorized custodian-facing agreement clauses with available documentary evidence and existing HTBW obligations. Explain uncertain performance claims and missing evidence without enforcing terms, issuing demands or changing the Custody Period.

## Potential evidence

Loan agreements; amendments; facility-document categories; condition reports; Custody Periods; governed obligations; authorized correspondence categories.
These are categories only, not admitted sources.

## Important boundary

Missing evidence is not proven noncompliance; no legal conclusion or communication to the custodian is made.

Physical return is not recorded Check-In; declared custody location is not current Truth. Missing chain legs stay missing. Insurance, ownership, caretaking and custody are distinct. No shadow lifecycle or new Counterparty/role construct is envisioned.

Retrieval, derivation and disclosure each require independent Person/source/purpose/namespace/audience authorization. Unknown is a valid result. Sensitivity, locality and derived retention/revocation/deletion constraints persist. Family membership or caretaker identity grants no access.
