# Who has had custody since acquisition?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

Who has had custody since acquisition?

## Why it matters

A household may want a coherent account of who has been accountable for an Asset since acquisition. Some households record every handoff; others record only the primary custodian. Those choices define what history can honestly show. Authorized supporting documents can contextualize the recorded chain, but plausible shipping or storage legs must never be inserted to make the narrative appear complete.

## Existing HTBW relationship

HTBW Foundation owns Assets and descriptive relationships; Truth owns current/historical Facts. Stewardship owns Custody Period lifecycle, Check-Out/Check-In, expected-return and due/overdue obligations, reminders, history and insurance-responsibility state, together with governed care.

## Possible Home Steward extension

Explain recorded Custody Period history alongside authorized acquisition, loan and transport documentation. Distinguish recorded custodians from document mentions and unknown intervals, without reconciling them into new periods or relationships.

## Potential evidence

Custody Period history; acquisition-document categories; loan agreements; transport-document categories; storage-document categories; authorized correspondence categories.
These are categories only, not admitted sources.

## Important boundary

Document mentions do not create custodians; missing chain legs remain missing rather than inferred.

Physical return is not recorded Check-In; declared custody location is not current Truth. Missing chain legs stay missing. Insurance, ownership, caretaking and custody are distinct. No shadow lifecycle or new Counterparty/role construct is envisioned.

Retrieval, derivation and disclosure each require independent Person/source/purpose/namespace/audience authorization. Unknown is a valid result. Sensitivity, locality and derived retention/revocation/deletion constraints persist. Family membership or caretaker identity grants no access.
