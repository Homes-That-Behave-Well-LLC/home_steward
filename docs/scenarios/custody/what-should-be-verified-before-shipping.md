# What should be verified before shipping?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

What should be verified before shipping?

## Why it matters

A household preparing for an Asset's departure may want to understand unresolved documentary questions before shipping. Agreement language, condition evidence, insurance context and professional handling restrictions can be scattered. A focused explanation could help qualified participants review what is already documented, without turning a synthesis into shipping clearance, a packing guide or a decision that the household can safely handle the object.

## Existing HTBW relationship

HTBW Foundation owns Assets and descriptive relationships; Truth owns current/historical Facts. Stewardship owns Custody Period lifecycle, Check-Out/Check-In, expected-return and due/overdue obligations, reminders, history and insurance-responsibility state, together with governed care.

## Possible Home Steward extension

Compare authorized shipping-related documents with governed care, existing obligations and custody context. Explain conflicting or missing documentation and professional-review questions without arranging transport, choosing a carrier or recording Check-Out.

## Potential evidence

Transport-document categories; loan agreements; condition reports; professional handling restrictions; governed obligations; insurance-document categories; Custody Period context.
These are categories only, not admitted sources.

## Important boundary

No packing, lifting, carrier-selection or shipping instruction is supplied; readiness requires responsible human and professional judgment.

Physical return is not recorded Check-In; declared custody location is not current Truth. Missing chain legs stay missing. Insurance, ownership, caretaking and custody are distinct. No shadow lifecycle or new Counterparty/role construct is envisioned.

Retrieval, derivation and disclosure each require independent Person/source/purpose/namespace/audience authorization. Unknown is a valid result. Sensitivity, locality and derived retention/revocation/deletion constraints persist. Family membership or caretaker identity grants no access.
