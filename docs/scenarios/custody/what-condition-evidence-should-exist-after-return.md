# What condition evidence should exist after return?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

What condition evidence should exist after return?

## Why it matters

A household may see an Asset back at Home yet lack a comparable condition account after return. Different photographs or report scopes may make departure-to-return comparison uncertain. Understanding what the agreement expected and what evidence exists could support qualified review without equating physical return with recorded Check-In, diagnosing transit damage or declaring all return obligations satisfied.

## Existing HTBW relationship

HTBW Foundation owns Assets and descriptive relationships; Truth owns current/historical Facts. Stewardship owns Custody Period lifecycle, Check-Out/Check-In, expected-return and due/overdue obligations, reminders, history and insurance-responsibility state, together with governed care.

## Possible Home Steward extension

Compare authorized departure and return condition-document categories with agreement expectations and governed care. Explain capture differences, report scope, missing evidence and uncertain timing without updating condition or closing the Custody Period.

## Potential evidence

Departure and return condition reports; photographs; capture-context categories; loan agreements; governed obligations; treatment-document categories; Custody Period history.
These are categories only, not admitted sources.

## Important boundary

Image differences do not prove transit damage or causation; document synthesis does not record Check-In.

Physical return is not recorded Check-In; declared custody location is not current Truth. Missing chain legs stay missing. Insurance, ownership, caretaking and custody are distinct. No shadow lifecycle or new Counterparty/role construct is envisioned.

Retrieval, derivation and disclosure each require independent Person/source/purpose/namespace/audience authorization. Unknown is a valid result. Sensitivity, locality and derived retention/revocation/deletion constraints persist. Family membership or caretaker identity grants no access.
