# What correspondence should be reconciled when the Asset returns?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

What correspondence should be reconciled when the Asset returns?

## Why it matters

A household may have several messages about an Asset's return that disagree about dates, documents or reported condition. Some messages may include amendments; others may be informal expectations. Understanding those differences could help review the existing custody record and obligations, but readable correspondence is not permission to contact anyone, disclose personal details or treat a message as a recorded lifecycle change.

## Existing HTBW relationship

HTBW Foundation owns Assets and descriptive relationships; Truth owns current/historical Facts. Stewardship owns Custody Period lifecycle, Check-Out/Check-In, expected-return and due/overdue obligations, reminders, history and insurance-responsibility state, together with governed care.

## Possible Home Steward extension

Synthesize authorized correspondence claims against existing Custody Period history, agreement documents and obligations. Explain unresolved discrepancies and documentary context without replying, accepting amendments, updating insurance responsibility or recording Check-In.

## Potential evidence

Authorized correspondence categories; agreement documents; amendments; condition reports; Custody Period history; governed obligations; return-document categories.
These are categories only, not admitted sources.

## Important boundary

Private correspondence remains person-scoped; interpretation neither authorizes communications nor changes accepted records.

Physical return is not recorded Check-In; declared custody location is not current Truth. Missing chain legs stay missing. Insurance, ownership, caretaking and custody are distinct. No shadow lifecycle or new Counterparty/role construct is envisioned.

Retrieval, derivation and disclosure each require independent Person/source/purpose/namespace/audience authorization. Unknown is a valid result. Sensitivity, locality and derived retention/revocation/deletion constraints persist. Family membership or caretaker identity grants no access.
