# Which off-site Assets lack environmental evidence?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

Which off-site Assets lack environmental evidence?

## Why it matters

A household may have a facility description for an off-site Asset but no environmental observations for the relevant period. A statement of intended conditions differs from evidence that those conditions occurred near the object. Understanding the gap could improve a professional conversation without interpreting absent evidence as a breach or assuming a declared custody location establishes current placement.

## Existing HTBW relationship

HTBW Foundation owns Assets and descriptive relationships; Truth owns current/historical Facts. Stewardship owns Custody Period lifecycle, Check-Out/Check-In, expected-return and due/overdue obligations, reminders, history and insurance-responsibility state, together with governed care.

## Possible Home Steward extension

Compare accessible facility and environmental-document categories with Asset-specific governed care and the recorded Custody Period. Explain promised conditions, reported measurements, missing periods and enclosure uncertainty without commissioning monitoring or changing custody.

## Potential evidence

Facility-document categories; environmental reports; sampling-quality information; governed care; enclosure descriptions; Custody Periods; professional assessments.
These are categories only, not admitted sources.

## Important boundary

Environmental reports are not automatically Truth; missing measurements neither prove safe conditions nor establish violation.

Physical return is not recorded Check-In; declared custody location is not current Truth. Missing chain legs stay missing. Insurance, ownership, caretaking and custody are distinct. No shadow lifecycle or new Counterparty/role construct is envisioned.

Retrieval, derivation and disclosure each require independent Person/source/purpose/namespace/audience authorization. Unknown is a valid result. Sensitivity, locality and derived retention/revocation/deletion constraints persist. Family membership or caretaker identity grants no access.
