# Is anything showing early signs of trouble

Status: CANONICAL SCENARIO

Developed-content maturity only; not Owner, runtime or architecture acceptance.

> Proposed synthetic scenario; no predictive-maintenance result or actual Home metrics.
> Applies the [investigation evidence rules](../../architecture/knowledge-architecture.md#evidence-and-epistemic-rules).

## 1. Household request

"Is anything in the Home showing early signs of trouble?" This is requested evidence-based
operational trend review, not a promise to predict equipment failure.

## 2. Existing HTBW knowledge

Foundation component/source definitions; Truth Historical Facts with qualified freshness/
confidence; authoritative Decision Traces; Stewardship care/service history and eligible manual links.

## 3. Authorized private retrieval

Minimum eligible unavailable periods, retries, battery observations, temperature drift, storage
growth, reconnects, action outcomes/interventions and latency over comparable use. HA logs/history
require a separate future source grant; no blanket sensor/log/network surveillance.

## 4. Optional public research

Permitted exact-model/manual/revision guidance may connect a supported observation to documented
troubleshooting. A generic symptom list is not an authoritative diagnosis or unsafe repair procedure.

## 5. Home Steward synthesis

Identify "evidence of changing behavior requiring attention" with units, exposure and alternatives.
Distinguish genuine battery/temperature measurements from provider estimates; incomplete outcome
confirmation remains unknown. No "this component will fail" prediction unless a separately
authoritative diagnostic source actually establishes that conclusion, cited at its scope.
Increasing retries/storage/latency are patterns, not magical forecasts or physical causes.

## 6. Provenance

Cite trace, Historical Fact, operational log, technical/service documentation and any external
diagnostic evidence separately. State window, baseline, coverage, source/measurement changes and
uncertainty; statistical correlation is not causation or a model diagnosis.

## 7. Candidate relationships

Possible trend-to-care relevance is an interpretive hypothesis and candidate for review; no new Fact, failure forecast or
automatic Stewardship obligation.

## 8. Household confirmation

Confirm relevant operating/service context; confirmation does not prove predicted failure or
authorize a replacement, new monitoring or service appointment.

## 9. Governed durable relationship

Foundation governs document associations; Stewardship evaluates care proposals. Historical Facts
and traces remain owner records, never a Home Steward monitoring/Fact-history store.

## 10. Proposed actions

Offer review, safe documented guidance or authorized diagnostic evidence through HTBW/Operational
Trust. Certified/native diagnostic authorities remain authoritative for their domain.

## 11. Prohibited autonomous actions

No preference/policy change, integration disablement, automation removal, obligation modification,
rollback/restart, replacement, service scheduling or vendor contact without HTBW authority.

## 12. HTBW authority

Truth Facts; Stewardship care/significance; Foundation structure; Identity requester; Continuity
context; Operational Trust action/disclosure; Concierge Trace/explanation and coordination.

## 13. Privacy and locality

Minimum separately scoped HA logs/measurements before retrieval/disclosure; no people ranking.
Local derivations inherit source lifecycle; no automatic indefinite raw-log memory or expired-content shadow.

## 14. Unavailable / degraded behavior

Aged-out or never-retained baseline, missing battery semantics or changed monitoring yields
"I don't know whether this is deteriorating". Native care and certified alarms remain independent.

## 15. Acceptance expectations

Only evidenced changing behavior is identified; uncertainty, measurement semantics and alternatives
are visible. No unsupported failure prediction, diagnosis, care mutation or safety-claim inflation.

## 16. Open questions

Source/diagnostic authority, comparable trend definitions, audience scope and future query/
retention/redaction remain open; no monitoring, ingestion or predictive-maintenance runtime.
