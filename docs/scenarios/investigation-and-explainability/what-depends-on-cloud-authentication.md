## Household question

What depends on cloud authentication?

Related wording covered by this primary, without another file:

## Why it matters

A locally installed capability can still require vendor authentication at startup, renewal or use. The household wants those conditions distinguished from ordinary Internet access and local inference claims. A local-looking interface may conceal an expiring external dependency, while documented offline support may not cover the current authorization state.

Evidence categories are not source admission. Person/source/purpose/namespace/audience authorization remains independent before retrieval, combination and disclosure. Source dates, uncertainty, correction, retention, expiry, deletion and revocation remain binding within the household-controlled network boundary; ordinary retention may be zero/disabled. Confirmation does not grant authority to retain, disclose or act. No shadow history or silent cloud/source fallback.
