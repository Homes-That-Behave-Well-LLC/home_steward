# What keeps making us intervene

Status: CANONICAL SCENARIO

Developed-content maturity only; not Owner, runtime or architecture acceptance.

> Proposed synthetic scenario; no actual household behavior/performance profile.
> Applies the [investigation evidence rules](../../architecture/knowledge-architecture.md#evidence-and-epistemic-rules).

## 1. Household request

"What in this Home keeps making us intervene?" Examine recurring technical experiences creating
household friction, not whether people are difficult, impatient or inconsistent.

## 2. Existing HTBW knowledge

Foundation capabilities/dependencies; authoritative Decision Traces; Truth Historical Facts;
Stewardship service/intervention records; eligible Continuity context and explicit feedback.

## 3. Authorized private retrieval

Minimum technical override/reversal/retry outcomes, acknowledged recurring notifications, Repairs,
service interventions, eligible abandoned-capability evidence and explicit corrections. HA log/
history access is a separately governed future source; no Person performance or movement-history scan.

## 4. Optional public research

Normally unnecessary. Permitted exact-version documentation may contextualize a recorded recurring
technical problem without sending household feedback, private logs or Person details.

## 5. Home Steward synthesis

Group evidenced technical friction by experience, frequency, comparable opportunity, unavailable
duration and repeated need for intervention. A deliberate manual choice or acknowledged notification
does not prove frustration; use explicit eligible feedback and preserve alternatives.
Focus on "this lighting experience repeatedly needed correction", not human temperament or
individual performance. Distinguish normal decisions from recurring system mismatch/failure.

## 6. Provenance

Cite exact traces, Historical Facts, operational logs, Repairs/service records and scoped feedback.
State windows, deduplication, denominators, missing coverage and statistical pattern versus hypothesis.
Overrides/failures occurring together are correlation, not causation or psychological diagnosis.

## 7. Candidate relationships

Possible technical mismatch or KEEP / ADAPT / REMOVE review is a candidate, not accepted intent,
Truth, household significance or a Person profile.

## 8. Household confirmation

Ask which technical experience merits review. Confirming friction does not authorize policy changes,
disablement or judgments about who caused the difficulty.

## 9. Governed durable relationship

Foundation/Continuity/Stewardship retain their records and eligible owner changes. No person-ranking,
new friction-history authority, rewritten trace or automatically learned preference.

## 10. Proposed actions

Offer a bounded technical experience review via HTBW and Operational Trust; Concierge coordinates
only separately authorized owner/executor work.

## 11. Prohibited autonomous actions

No preference/policy changes, integration disablement, automation removal, obligation changes,
rollback/restart, device replacement, service scheduling or vendor contact without HTBW authority.

## 12. HTBW authority

Foundation model; Truth Facts/history; Stewardship care; Continuity context/preferences; Identity
requester; Operational Trust privacy/action; Concierge Trace/explanation and coordination.

## 13. Privacy and locality

Minimum separately scoped technical logs/feedback before assembly/disclosure; no people ranking or
profiles. Local derived patterns inherit lifecycle; no automatic indefinite raw-log memory or expired-trace copy.

## 14. Unavailable / degraded behavior

Aged-out/never-retained intervention records or absent explicit feedback limit the conclusion.
Answer "I don't know which experience creates the most friction" without a fabricated profile.

## 15. Acceptance expectations

Technical/system focus, fair opportunity counts, deduplicated evidence and explicit feedback/gaps.
No people evaluation, inferred personality, silent preference change or automatic removal.

## 16. Open questions

Eligible intervention/feedback definitions, privacy-safe grouping and future query/redaction/
retention rules remain open; no behavioral profiling or log-ingestion runtime is created.
