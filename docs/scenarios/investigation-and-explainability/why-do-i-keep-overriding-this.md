# Why do I keep overriding this

Status: CANONICAL SCENARIO

Developed-content maturity only; not Owner, runtime or architecture acceptance.

> Proposed synthetic scenario; no actual Person preferences or behavior history.
> Applies the [investigation evidence rules](../../architecture/knowledge-architecture.md#evidence-and-epistemic-rules).

## 1. Household request

"Why do I keep overriding the bedroom temperature?" This reviews Home preference fit, not the
requester's personality, consistency or performance.

## 2. Existing HTBW knowledge

Continuity owns eligible preferences/context; Foundation supplies configured capability/Room;
Truth owns current/Historical temperature, occupancy and HVAC Facts. Authoritative Decision Traces
explain each evaluation and the preference/policy versions actually used.

## 3. Authorized private retrieval

Minimum relevant preference/Change Records, traces, overrides, eligible temperature/HVAC/occupancy
history and time context. HA log/history access is separately governed; person/movement reconstruction
requires its dedicated HTBW path, not generic Room Help or General Questions.

## 4. Optional public research

Authorized weather may help compare conditions. Keep household location/schedule/person information
out of external queries unless separately permitted; public comfort guidance cannot dictate preference.

## 5. Home Steward synthesis

Compare overrides with configured preference, conditions/time and eligible feedback. An unattributed
HA override does not prove this Person performed it. A recurring authorized pattern may suggest the
configured evening preference no longer fits current intent, but overrides can be deliberate,
temporary or unrelated to comfort. Propose "Would you like to review the evening temperature
preference?" Never silently learn/apply a replacement.

## 6. Provenance

Distinguish exact preferences/Change Records, Historical Facts, trace decisions, operational logs,
attributed feedback, statistical pattern and hypothesis. State denominators, coverage and gaps;
weather/override correlation is not causation or psychological diagnosis.

## 7. Candidate relationships

Time/condition-to-preference mismatch remains a Learned Suggestion/candidate, not accepted intent,
Truth or a durable cross-visit identity for an Unknown Person.

## 8. Household confirmation

Current explicit intent must come from an eligible Person through the accepted owner process.
Agreement with a pattern is not permission to rewrite preference/policy.

## 9. Governed durable relationship

Continuity/appropriate configuration owner governs accepted preference changes with provenance.
Home Steward does not write them or create competing Person memory.

## 10. Proposed actions

Offer review through HTBW/Operational Trust, with required contextual authorization/confirmation;
Concierge orchestrates an approved owner interaction rather than owning acceptance.

## 11. Prohibited autonomous actions

No preference/policy edits, integration disablement, automation removal, obligation changes,
rollback/restart, replacement, service scheduling or vendor contact without separate HTBW authority.

## 12. HTBW authority

Continuity preferences; Truth Facts; Foundation definitions; Identity requester/consent lifecycle;
Stewardship care; Operational Trust privacy/actions; Concierge recorded decisions and interaction.

## 13. Privacy and locality

Person/actor scope is evaluated before minimal retrieval and audience disclosure. Separate HA log
grants do not authorize movement profiling. Local derivations inherit lifecycle; no automatic indefinite raw-log memory.

## 14. Unavailable / degraded behavior

Aged-out/never-retained or unattributed overrides are gaps. Say "I don't know why these overrides
occurred" where evidence is insufficient; keep existing configured preferences and native behavior.

## 15. Acceptance expectations

No human evaluation/profiling; unattributed actions stay unattributed; configured and current intent
stay distinct; the review proposal cannot silently change anything.

## 16. Open questions

Eligible attribution/context, minimum comparison scope, preference-review interaction and future
log/query/retention controls remain open.
