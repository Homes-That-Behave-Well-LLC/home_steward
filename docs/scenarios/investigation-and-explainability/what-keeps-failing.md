# What keeps failing

Status: CANONICAL SCENARIO

Developed-content maturity only; not Owner, runtime or architecture acceptance.

> Proposed synthetic scenario; no actual failure ranking or household performance assessment.
> Applies the [investigation evidence rules](../../architecture/knowledge-architecture.md#evidence-and-epistemic-rules).

## 1. Household request

"What keeps failing in the Home?" Aggregate technical evidence only; do not evaluate or rank people.

## 2. Existing HTBW knowledge

Foundation integration/device/service/automation/script identities and dependencies; authoritative
Decision Traces, Truth Historical Facts and Stewardship service evidence.
HTBW explains its own evaluations, not decisions native automations made without HTBW participation.

## 3. Authorized private retrieval

Minimum eligible failure/outcome/availability/retry records for the requested interval and components.
HA logs/history are a separate future source relationship; no unrestricted log or Person-history scan.

## 4. Optional public research

Permitted component/version documentation can explain specific error semantics; do not upload
private logs/configuration or substitute web guesses for local evidence.

## 5. Home Steward synthesis

Compare technical components by evidenced failure frequency, unavailable duration, retries and
failed action counts with windows/exposure. Deduplicate retries/repeated log lines belonging to one
episode. Successful issuance with unconfirmed physical outcome is unknown, not success or failure.
Intentional non-actions are not failures. Raw counts with different command opportunities cannot
support an unqualified reliability ranking; no people-performance inference follows.

## 6. Provenance

Cite source/class/version, denominators, interval, deduplication, definitions and gaps. Distinguish
trace decision, Historical Fact, operational log/error evidence, statistical pattern and diagnosis hypothesis.
Correlation among failures or temporal proximity never establishes causation.

## 7. Candidate relationships

Possible common dependency or recurring fault is an investigation hypothesis, not Truth or a
physical diagnosis. Preserve alternative explanations.

## 8. Household confirmation

Confirm which technical experience needs review and relevant known context; no assessment of a
Person's competence and no implied permission to disable components.

## 9. Governed durable relationship

No new failure-history authority or trace overwrite. Foundation owns approved dependency changes;
Stewardship owns eligible service/care records; Truth remains the Fact owner.

## 10. Proposed actions

Propose reviewing an evidenced component/coverage gap via HTBW and Operational Trust; Concierge
coordinates permitted investigation with responsible owners.

## 11. Prohibited autonomous actions

No policy/preference changes, integration disablement, automation removal, obligation change,
rollback/restart, replacement, service scheduling or vendor contact without HTBW authority.

## 12. HTBW authority

Concierge Trace/explanation; Truth Facts/history; Foundation components; Stewardship care;
Identity requester; Continuity context; Operational Trust source/audience/action decisions.

## 13. Privacy and locality

Separately governed HA logs are minimally retrieved and Person-safe before assembly/disclosure.
Local derived metrics inherit source lifecycle; no automatic indefinite raw-log memory or people profiles.

## 14. Unavailable / degraded behavior

Aged-out, never-retained or unequally observed components are incomplete evidence. Answer "I don't
know which is less reliable" where no fair comparison exists; preserve native operation.

## 15. Acceptance expectations

Technical-only output, deduplicated evidence, unknown versus failed outcomes, comparable exposure
and explicit gaps; no model diagnosis or autonomous remediation.

## 16. Open questions

Failure-episode definitions, exposure/availability semantics, eligible cross-component scope and
future log-query/redaction/retention controls remain open.
