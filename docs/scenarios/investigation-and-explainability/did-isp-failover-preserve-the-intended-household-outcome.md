## Why it matters

A second ISP becoming active can restore connectivity while leaving an important household outcome unavailable or degraded. The household wants dual-provider failover reviewed against what actually mattered, not interface status alone. Changed authentication, delayed information or lost remote visibility can matter even when some local functions continue.
