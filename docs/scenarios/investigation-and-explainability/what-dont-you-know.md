# What don't you know

Status: CANONICAL SCENARIO

Developed-content maturity only; not Owner, runtime or architecture acceptance.

> Proposed synthetic trust-boundary scenario; no actual failed light or diagnostic evidence.
> Applies the [investigation evidence rules](../../architecture/knowledge-architecture.md#evidence-and-epistemic-rules).

## 1. Household request

"Are you sure that's why the lights failed?", "What don't you know?", "What evidence are you missing?"
Challenge both the inferred cause and whether a physical failure has actually been established.

## 2. Existing HTBW knowledge

Authoritative Decision Trace establishes the governed decision/action and recorded observed or
unknown outcome. Truth owns qualified current/Historical Facts; Foundation supplies eligible
configuration/dependencies. Native HTBW explanation/reconstruction remains its accepted capability.

## 3. Authorized private retrieval

Only the exact relevant trace, historical versions/Facts and eligible HA timeout/availability
evidence through the separately governed future log source. Device/network/power diagnostics require
their own authorization; no wholesale ingestion to compensate for missing evidence.

## 4. Optional public research

Permitted exact-model/version documentation may list possibilities, labelled external. It cannot
establish which cause occurred here; no private evidence uploaded.

## 5. Home Steward synthesis

Hypothetical answer: "I know the command was issued, the light did not report the expected state,
and Home Assistant logged a timeout. I don't know whether the physical light failed or where the
problem occurred. I lack device-level evidence distinguishing the bulb, network, integration and
power path."

Separate known trace/log observations from missing physical evidence and competing unranked
hypotheses. No expected-state report is not proof the light stayed off. Name what cannot be
reconstructed: an expired original trace, missing historical configuration, unavailable device
telemetry or timing finer than the source supports. A current diagnostic explanation must not
masquerade as the missing contemporaneous record.

Do not invent a "most likely" root cause to provide satisfaction. Confidence follows evidence scope,
freshness, coverage and contradiction, not model fluency or arithmetic on Truth/Identity bands.

## 6. Provenance

Cite trace/Exact Version references, observation times/uncertainty, Historical Fact scope, operational
log source, configuration/document evidence and unavailable references. Distinguish observation,
statistical pattern, correlation, inference, hypothesis and diagnosis. Temporal association is not causation.

## 7. Candidate relationships

Bulb/network/integration/power explanations are hypotheses, not Truth, accepted causal links or
automatic diagnoses. Preserve competing evidence and unresolved ambiguity.

## 8. Household confirmation

A scoped report of visible behavior is attributable evidence at its own quality, not automatic
diagnostic proof or permission to alter power/network/device configuration.

## 9. Governed durable relationship

Truth evaluates any eligible new Fact; responsible owners govern corrections. Home Steward does
not backdate or overwrite a trace, reconstruct expired data by invention or create a shadow history.

## 10. Proposed actions

Identify the minimum discriminating evidence that could help, such as an authorized device-level
observation, through the source owner, HTBW and Operational Trust. Explain limits without leaking
protected source existence; Concierge may coordinate a separately permitted request.

## 11. Prohibited autonomous actions

No preference/policy edit, integration disablement, automation removal, obligation change,
rollback/restart, device replacement, service scheduling or vendor contact without HTBW authority.

## 12. HTBW authority

Concierge Trace/explanation; Truth current/historical Facts; Foundation definitions; Stewardship
care; Continuity context; Identity requester; Operational Trust evidence/disclosure/action policy.

## 13. Privacy and locality

Minimum separately scoped HA/device/network evidence before reasoning and audience disclosure.
Local derived answers inherit lifecycle; no automatic indefinite raw-log memory or shadow of expired explanation.

## 14. Unavailable / degraded behavior

Explicitly distinguish never retained, aged out, redacted, unavailable and unknown. "I don't know"
is acceptable. Preserve native operation, and never replace missing evidence with plausible narrative
or an unauthorized/cloud source.

## 15. Acceptance expectations

Known evidence, missing evidence, competing explanations, unreconstructable history, helpful next
evidence and confidence limits are explicit. A command/timeout is not proof of physical cause;
unsupported root cause is refused even when the requester wants certainty.

## 16. Open questions

Eligible diagnostic sources, evidence-quality/confirmation scope, historical gaps and future
query/redaction/derived retention controls remain open; no diagnostic or log-ingestion runtime.
