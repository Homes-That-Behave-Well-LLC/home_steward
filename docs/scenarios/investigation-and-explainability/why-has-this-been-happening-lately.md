# Why has this been happening lately

Status: CANONICAL SCENARIO

Developed-content maturity only; not Owner, runtime or architecture acceptance.

> Proposed synthetic scenario; hypothetical evidence only, not Reference Home data.
> Applies the [investigation evidence rules](../../architecture/knowledge-architecture.md#evidence-and-epistemic-rules).

## 1. Household request

"Why have the Den shades been opening late lately?" Resolve the relevant capability and requested
interval; do not assume every non-action or manual override was a failure.

## 2. Existing HTBW knowledge

HTBW Explainability answers each governed evaluation from its authoritative Decision Trace and
exact historical policy/configuration. Truth owns Historical Facts; Foundation identifies the shades/
dependencies; Continuity supplies eligible intent/preferences. Native historical retrieval remains native.

## 3. Authorized private retrieval

Minimum eligible Den shade traces and Historical Facts for the agreed three-week window, relevant
Change Records and, only through the separately governed future source, applicable HA availability/
log evidence. Do not ingest complete logs or retrieve unrelated Person movement history.

## 4. Optional public research

Normally unnecessary. Exact-model integration documentation may clarify a reported condition if
permitted; no private trace, household schedule or identifier enters an external query.

## 5. Home Steward synthesis

Hypothetical fixture: eleven relevant retained evaluations; seven normal and four delayed. Three
delays have traces naming unavailable environmental evidence; one records a manual override.
Report "four of these eleven retained evaluations", not a Home-wide reliability rate. Separate
recorded decision reasons from merely coincident HA warnings. The recurring reason dominates this
sample, not necessarily every occurrence. A deliberate override is not an equipment fault.

## 6. Provenance

Cite each trace/record version, exact window, delay definition, event-time precision, inclusion/
exclusion and missing coverage. Classify Historical Fact, trace reason, operational log, computed
frequency, correlation and hypothesis separately; no temporal association establishes root cause.

## 7. Candidate relationships

A recurring dependency issue is an investigation hypothesis, not a diagnosis or authoritative
relationship. A warning near an evaluation does not automatically cause that evaluation.

## 8. Household confirmation

Confirm whether "late" means outside the configured expectation and whether the override was
intentional. This contributes evidence, not permission to change schedules or policy.

## 9. Governed durable relationship

No analysis writes Truth, rewrites traces or creates a new history store. Any eligible dependency
association returns to Foundation; historical/configuration records remain with their owners.

## 10. Proposed actions

Propose reviewing the evidenced environmental dependency or missing coverage through HTBW and
Operational Trust. Concierge coordinates only separately authorized investigation/execution.

## 11. Prohibited autonomous actions

No policy/preference change, integration disablement, automation removal, obligation change,
rollback, restart, device replacement, service scheduling or vendor contact without HTBW authorization.

## 12. HTBW authority

Concierge composes governed explanations; Truth owns Facts/history; Stewardship care; Continuity
intent; Foundation structure; Identity requester; Operational Trust source/audience/action decisions.

## 13. Privacy and locality

Separate HA log access, Person scope and audience eligibility apply before minimal retrieval.
Local derived summaries inherit source governance; no automatic indefinite raw-log memory or expired-trace shadow copy.

## 14. Unavailable / degraded behavior

Aged-out or never-retained records are gaps, not normal evaluations. Answer "I don't know how often
this happened outside the retained sample." Preserve native HTBW operation; no guessed/cloud fallback.

## 15. Acceptance expectations

The hypothetical counts reconcile: 7 + 4 = 11 and 3 + 1 = 4. Native per-event explanation remains
authoritative; rates name coverage, normal decisions stay distinct from anomalies, and no inference
becomes Truth or authorization.

## 16. Open questions

Delay semantics, eligible comparison coverage, future query/redaction/retention controls and
dependency-review evidence remain open; no log-ingestion implementation is implied.
