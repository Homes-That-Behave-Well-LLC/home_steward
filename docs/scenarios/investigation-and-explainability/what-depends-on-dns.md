# What depends on DNS?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

What depends on DNS?

## Why it matters

DNS loss may interrupt a service even when its device is reachable by another means, while some outcomes may not require current name resolution. The household wants documented name-resolution dependencies and uncertainty explained without a claim that every simultaneous failure has the same cause. A DNS error alone does not identify the full household impact.
