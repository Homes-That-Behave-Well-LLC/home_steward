# What changed in the Home

Status: CANONICAL SCENARIO

Developed-content maturity only; not Owner, runtime or architecture acceptance.

> Proposed synthetic scenario; no actual weekly configuration or activity log.
> Applies the [investigation evidence rules](../../architecture/knowledge-architecture.md#evidence-and-epistemic-rules).

## 1. Household request

"What changed in the Home this week?" Agree the interval and meaningful-change scope, not a feed
of every raw HA state transition.

## 2. Existing HTBW knowledge

Foundation/configuration owners hold device/relationship/policy version changes; Stewardship owns
new/changed obligations; authoritative Decision Traces explain governed decisions. HTBW already
supports native historical configuration/reconstruction queries.

## 3. Authorized private retrieval

Minimum eligible Change Records, device additions/removals, integration/version records, Repairs,
significant availability changes and recurring warning/error summaries. HA logs/history require
their separate future governed relationship; no wholesale stream ingestion.

## 4. Optional public research

Normally none. Permitted release documentation may explain a recorded version change without sending
private configuration or household records.

## 5. Home Steward synthesis

Group meaningful governed changes: configuration/policy or authorized relationship changes,
device/integration additions/removals/updates, new obligations, Repairs and new recurring technical
patterns. Ordinary light toggles, occupancy fluctuations and successful routine executions are
activity, not automatically meaningful change. Significance follows the household's declared scope,
not a universal model hierarchy. Separate recorded changes from inferred pattern shifts.

## 6. Provenance

Cite owner, subject, prior/new version, Effective and Recorded Time, coverage and exclusions.
Native operational events/logs are not Change Records or automatically Truth; timing association
between a change and a warning is correlation, not causation.

## 7. Candidate relationships

Possible change-to-behavior connections remain hypotheses; do not manufacture governed changes
from ordinary activity or create causation links from timestamps.

## 8. Household confirmation

Confirm relevance or missing context as scoped feedback, not approval of rollback or new obligations.

## 9. Governed durable relationship

The summary is a projection/derived interpretation, not a replacement change ledger. Original
configuration, policy, obligation and relationship records remain owner-governed.

## 10. Proposed actions

Propose reviewing a significant eligible change via HTBW and Operational Trust; Concierge handles
authorized next steps, not automatic remediation.

## 11. Prohibited autonomous actions

No policy/preference changes, integration disablement, automation removal, obligation modification,
rollback, restart, replacement, booking or vendor contact without separate HTBW authority.

## 12. HTBW authority

Foundation model/configuration; Stewardship obligations; Truth Facts; Continuity context; Identity
requester; Operational Trust privacy/action; Concierge native explanation/orchestration.

## 13. Privacy and locality

Filter protected actor/Person/account/change details before assembly and audience disclosure.
Separately scoped logs and local derived summaries inherit lifecycle; no automatic indefinite raw-log memory.

## 14. Unavailable / degraded behavior

Aged-out or never-recorded intervals are missing coverage, not "nothing changed". Answer "I don't
know about changes outside these retained records"; do not reconstruct erased content by guessing.

## 15. Acceptance expectations

Meaningful changes are distinguished from activity; exact versions/times/gaps remain visible;
new obligations are Stewardship-owned and no summary creates state or authority.

## 16. Open questions

Household significance/filter scope, source availability, future log query/redaction controls and
retention of derived weekly summaries remain open.
