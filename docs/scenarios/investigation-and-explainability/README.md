# Investigation and Explainability

Navigation and scope guidance only; not an architecture contract or roadmap. Browse the [scenario-family map](../README.md).

## Purpose

Preserve developed inquiries about recurring behavior, changes, reliability, friction, consequences and uncertainty. Investigation is evidence-bounded understanding, not an invented diagnosis.

## Relationship to HTBW

Native Explainability already supports time/history and cross-Room retrieval; Truth and Decision Trace retain their accepted scope. Home Steward is not necessary for straightforward native reconstruction. See the existing [HTBW boundary](../../architecture/htbw-boundary.md).

## Possible Home Steward extension

Interpret separately authorized events, logs, changes and documents to explore patterns, hypotheses and gaps. Any new source admission, representation, handoff or implementation is an OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION.

## Boundaries

Observation, source report, historical Fact, correlation and inference remain distinct. Correlation is not causation; command issuance is not physical success. Retention is household-configured; no expired-history reconstruction, shadow history or human performance ranking.

Person/source/purpose/namespace/audience scope, household-controlled locality and source-derived lifecycle remain independent requirements. Potential evidence is not admitted access; knowledge is not action or disclosure authority. Source reports and inference are not automatic Truth; unknown is valid and correlation is not causation.

## Reference Home discovery increment

Shared timing != shared dependency != causal root cause. Declared, observed, inferred and unknown paths remain distinct; availability and command issuance are not observed household outcomes. Device/network/integration/service/policy/execution stages include legitimate non-actions. Network evidence requires separately governed source/account/field/time eligibility; no graph, capture, discovery, network change or remediation is introduced.

New primary files in this increment: **17**. Each is catalogued only in this primary family. All earlier bodies and maturity remain unchanged.

- [What exact dependency path supports this household capability?](what-exact-dependency-path-supports-this-household-capability.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A household capability can depend on more than the device visible in the Room. The household wants the exact path that is actually documented or evidenced, including missing segments, without a fabricated complete topology. A declared dependency explains intended structure but does not by itself prove the path currently succeeds.
- [If this VLAN is unavailable, which household experiences are affected?](if-this-vlan-is-unavailable-which-household-experiences-are-affected.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A VLAN becoming unavailable may affect only some household experiences, and devices in another segment may depend on services within it. The household wants evidenced consequences and independent alternatives distinguished from assumptions based on network membership. Loss of visibility into a segment is not proof every device there failed.
- [Which capabilities depend on the Home Assistant or server VLAN?](which-capabilities-depend-on-the-home-assistant-or-server-vlan.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Home Assistant or server-segment availability may matter to different experiences through different services. The household wants those declared relationships understood without treating a segment label as proof that every capability requires it. Independently operating equipment and optional knowledge enrichment must remain distinct from controller-dependent outcomes.
- [Which kiosk capabilities still work if the kiosk VLAN cannot reach an external service?](which-kiosk-capabilities-still-work-if-the-kiosk-vlan-cannot-reach-an-external-service.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A kiosk can remain powered and reachable locally while an external capability is unavailable. The household wants its native local help, provider-dependent information and unknown outcomes distinguished rather than a single online/offline label. A display remaining visible is not proof that its controls or external data still work.
- [What depends on DNS?](what-depends-on-dns.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. DNS loss may interrupt a service even when its device is reachable by another means, while some outcomes may not require current name resolution. The household wants documented name-resolution dependencies and uncertainty explained without a claim that every simultaneous failure has the same cause. A DNS error alone does not identify the full household impact.
- [What depends on cloud authentication?](what-depends-on-cloud-authentication.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A locally installed capability can still require vendor authentication at startup, renewal or use. The household wants those conditions distinguished from ordinary Internet access and local inference claims. A local-looking interface may conceal an expiring external dependency, while documented offline support may not cover the current authorization state.
- [What depends on local discovery?](what-depends-on-local-discovery.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A device can function physically while its integration cannot discover it, and a known configured relationship may remain usable without fresh discovery. The household wants discovery, identity, reachability and successful outcome kept separate. An absent discovery result should not silently retire or replace a device.
- [Which capabilities depend on one Home Assistant integration config entry?](which-capabilities-depend-on-one-home-assistant-integration-config-entry.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. One Home Assistant integration config entry may contribute several entities and household experiences. The household wants its actual declared contribution understood without assuming every related entity or similarly named capability shares the same execution path. An entry being loaded is not proof its outcomes succeed.
- [What depends on the Windows domain controller?](what-depends-on-the-windows-domain-controller.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A Windows domain controller can provide services relevant to household operation without every server or device depending on it. The household wants explicitly evidenced authentication, naming or other service dependencies distinguished from assumptions about the operating system. Private account relationships and infrastructure details must not become public explanatory material.
- [Which experiences currently use Spectrum?](which-experiences-currently-use-spectrum.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. With Spectrum and AT&T as contextual provider examples, configuration may describe preferred routing while actual traffic uses a different path during failover. The household wants provider use for specific experiences and times distinguished from the existence of two subscriptions. A sampled route does not establish every connection's path or future provider use.
- [Did ISP failover preserve the intended household outcome?](did-isp-failover-preserve-the-intended-household-outcome.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A second ISP becoming active can restore connectivity while leaving an important household outcome unavailable or degraded. The household wants dual-provider failover reviewed against what actually mattered, not interface status alone. Changed authentication, delayed information or lost remote visibility can matter even when some local functions continue.
- [Which capabilities appear local but fail without an external service?](which-capabilities-appear-local-but-fail-without-an-external-service.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A familiar local control surface can depend on an external service for a particular operation. The household wants that actual dependency distinguished from local branding, cached information or a separate optional enrichment. Missing external access must not be hidden by a plausible answer or an unauthorized replacement source.
- [What is the smallest known failed dependency common to these symptoms?](what-is-the-smallest-known-failed-dependency-common-to-these-symptoms.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Several symptoms may have one known failed dependency, unrelated failures or only shared timing. The household wants the narrowest actually supported common failure distinguished from a plausible root-cause story. If the relevant path is incomplete, 'smallest known' may itself be unknown rather than an invitation to infer missing topology.
- [Which dependency is known, inferred or still unknown?](which-dependency-is-known-inferred-or-still-unknown.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A dependency may be explicitly configured, inferred from behavior or not established at all. The household wants the basis of each claim visible because repeating an inference can make it sound like accepted architecture. Documentation of intended dependence and evidence of actual runtime contribution are also different.
- [Did the failure occur in the device, network, integration, service, policy or execution path?](did-the-failure-occur-in-the-device-network-integration-service-policy-or-execution-path.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. An unsuccessful experience may arise in a device, network, integration, service, policy or execution path. The household wants the independently evidenced stage distinguished from the reported symptom, including intentional governed non-actions. A policy refusal is not a broken device, and a successful service call is not proof of physical effect.
- [Which household outcomes were affected even though individual devices still appeared available?](which-household-outcomes-were-affected-even-though-individual-devices-still-appeared-available.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Individual devices can report available while the requested household experience fails or remains unverified. The household wants availability, issued actions and observed results separated before concluding that an end-to-end outcome succeeded. It may also need to distinguish a valid suppression from a failed execution without assuming physical evidence exists.
- [Which technical failures had no visible household effect?](which-technical-failures-had-no-visible-household-effect.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A technical failure can be contained by an existing fallback or affect a function nobody used during the interval. The household wants known failures without a visible household effect distinguished from failures whose effect was simply unobserved. Quiet operation should not erase technical evidence or create false reassurance about resilience.
