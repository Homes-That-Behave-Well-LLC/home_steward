# Has this happened before? (Care)

Status: CANONICAL SCENARIO

Developed-content maturity only; not Owner, runtime or architecture acceptance.

> Aging in Place and Care Awareness: synthetic historical comparison, not causal or diagnostic recurrence.

## 1. Household request

"Has this happened before?" Compare authorized Person-scoped evidence, clarifying what "this" means.

## 2. Existing HTBW knowledge

Truth current/historical Home Facts, Foundation Person context, eligible Identity, Stewardship care
and existing Explainability. Historical availability is separately governed, not assumed complete.

## 3. Authorized private retrieval

Potential separately approved Person measurement/activity/sleep/alert/Home history and household
context. Person movement reconstruction retains its existing dedicated authorization boundary.

## 4. Optional public research

Source definitions may clarify comparable observations; public pattern examples do not prove personal
or medical recurrence. Private Person/history data do not enter public queries.

## 5. Home Steward synthesis

Distinguish similar measurement, similar multi-signal pattern, similar household context and actual
causal recurrence. The last is a different claim requiring authoritative evidence, not derived from
resemblance; no medical cause is inferred. State chosen personal periods, coverage and differences.

## 6. Provenance

Preserve original wearable/Home/household sources and dates, personal baseline/recent/history coverage,
quality and inference limits. Similar prior alerts are not evidence the present condition is harmless.

## 7. Candidate relationships

Similarity remains a candidate, not medical causation, actual recurrence or a durable care relationship.

## 8. Household confirmation

Clarify referent/period/context; agreement is not broader history access/disclosure authority.
Any new clarification/retention mechanism is an OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION.

## 9. Governed durable relationship

OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION: no recurrence/pattern model, care proposal object or history store.

## 10. Proposed actions

The household may review comparisons or prepare independently authorized human questions.
Any disclosure, notification or handoff remains an OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION.

## 11. Prohibited autonomous actions

No diagnosis, medical-causation/medication-effect inference, disease/fall prediction, treatment,
clinical monitoring, emergency decision, alert downgrade, automatic caregiver notification or care/policy change.

## 12. HTBW authority

Truth current Home state/history; Stewardship care obligations; Identity context; Foundation definitions;
Operational Trust action/disclosure; Continuity where applicable; Concierge and existing Explainability/Trace.

## 13. Privacy and locality

Person-scoped history remains independently authorized; ingestion is not disclosure permission.
Local derivations inherit sensitivity/lifecycle; expired history is not restored as a shadow narrative.

## 14. Unavailable / degraded behavior

"I don't know whether this happened before" is valid; absence from retained history is not never happened.
Native HTBW/emergency mechanisms stay independent; no LLM critical path or source/cloud fallback.

## 15. Acceptance expectations

Four comparison classes and evidence limits captured; no causal recurrence inferred from similarity,
clinical reassurance, historical store or runtime acceptance.

## 16. Open questions

Eligible historical comparison, representation/retention and human disclosure/handoff remain OPEN
ARCHITECTURAL QUESTIONS; no diagnostic matching or care handler.
