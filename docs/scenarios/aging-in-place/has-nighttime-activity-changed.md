# Has nighttime activity changed

Status: CANONICAL SCENARIO

Developed-content maturity only; not Owner, runtime or architecture acceptance.

> Aging in Place and Care Awareness: synthetic, person-scoped, non-diagnostic experience capture.

## 1. Household request

"Has nighttime activity changed?" Understand the authorized person's observations over a chosen period.

## 2. Existing HTBW knowledge

Foundation Person/Room context, eligible Identity associations, Truth current/historical Facts and
Stewardship care; generic Room motion does not establish who moved or got out of bed.

## 3. Authorized private retrieval

Potential nighttime Room activity, time out of bed, wearable and sleep evidence, individually
authorized for this Person/question. Existing Person movement-history restrictions remain binding.

## 4. Optional public research

Source/device documentation may describe measurements, not explain a medical condition.
Private nighttime/activity/care information is not public query context.

## 5. Home Steward synthesis

Clarify "nighttime", personal baseline and recent dates; compare comparable observed periods,
coverage and known context. Increased nighttime movement is not evidence of a specific medical condition.
Unattributed Room evidence must not be silently assigned to the Person.

## 6. Provenance

Separate Home/wearable/sleep reports and household narrative, with source/time/quality, selected
personal periods, missing evidence and inference limits.

## 7. Candidate relationships

Nighttime differences or overlaps remain candidates, not medical causation or new care relationships.

## 8. Household confirmation

Clarify Person/time/context, not permissions to observe/disclose or automatically notify.
New confirmation retention is an OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION.

## 9. Governed durable relationship

OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION: no sleep/baseline model, care proposal object or relationship schema.

## 10. Proposed actions

The household may review the observations or consider an independently authorized conversation.
Any disclosure/notification/handoff mechanism remains an OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION.

## 11. Prohibited autonomous actions

No diagnosis, disease/fall prediction, medication-effect inference, treatment, clinical monitoring,
emergency decision, automatic caregiver notification, wake-up action or policy/care change.

## 12. HTBW authority

Truth current Home state/history; Stewardship care obligations; Identity context; Foundation definitions;
Operational Trust action/disclosure; Continuity where applicable; Concierge and existing Explainability/Trace.

## 13. Privacy and locality

Person-scoped nighttime/sleep data needs separate ingestion and disclosure permissions.
Local derivatives inherit sensitivity/lifecycle; no unapproved movement archive or shared-speaker exposure.

## 14. Unavailable / degraded behavior

"I don't know whether nights changed" is valid; missing observation is not sleep or inactivity.
Native HTBW/emergency mechanisms stay independent; no LLM critical path or cloud fallback.

## 15. Acceptance expectations

Four potential evidence sources, Person attribution and chosen comparison captured.
No medical condition, sleep-monitoring algorithm or notification architecture established.

## 16. Open questions

Source eligibility, personal comparison/retention and any caregiver disclosure/handoff remain OPEN
ARCHITECTURAL QUESTIONS; no wearable ingestion or sleep algorithm.
