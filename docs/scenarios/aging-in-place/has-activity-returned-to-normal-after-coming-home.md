# Has activity returned to normal after coming home

Status: CANONICAL SCENARIO

Developed-content maturity only; not Owner, runtime or architecture acceptance.

> Aging in Place and Care Awareness: synthetic, person-scoped, non-diagnostic experience capture.

## 1. Household request

"Has activity returned to normal since coming home from the hospital?"
Understand comparison with a prior observed personal pattern, not medical recovery.

## 2. Existing HTBW knowledge

Foundation Person context, eligible Identity, Truth current/historical Home Facts, Stewardship care
and existing Explainability. HTBW has no authority to define medically "normal" recovery.

## 3. Authorized private retrieval

Potential independently approved Person activity/wearable/history and household-provided return date/
context. Hospital/care documents require their own scope; hospitalization is not a blanket grant.

## 4. Optional public research

Device measurement definitions may be relevant; no generic recovery schedule or clinical normality.
Private hospital/Person circumstances are not public query inputs.

## 5. Home Steward synthesis

Use explicitly selected before/after periods and the prior observed personal pattern.
Describe activity progression, uncertainty and an incomplete recovery/comparison period without
declaring recovered, unhealthy or medically normal; known context changes may limit comparability.

## 6. Provenance

Separate device/Home observations, household-reported event/date and inference, with personal
baseline/recent periods, source/time/quality, gaps and comparison limits.

## 7. Candidate relationships

Before/after timing is contextual association, not clinical recovery, causal relationship or new care record.

## 8. Household confirmation

Clarify return date/period/context; confirmation is evidence, not processing/disclosure authority.
Future record/retention mechanics remain an OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION.

## 9. Governed durable relationship

OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION: no recovery/baseline model, care proposal object or schema.

## 10. Proposed actions

The household may review observations or prepare separately authorized questions for a human.
Any disclosure, caregiver communication or handoff remains an OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION.

## 11. Prohibited autonomous actions

No diagnosis, medical recovery verdict, disease/fall prediction, medication-effect inference, treatment,
clinical monitoring, emergency decision, automatic caregiver notification or care/policy change.

## 12. HTBW authority

Truth current Home state/history; Stewardship care obligations; Identity context; Foundation definitions;
Operational Trust action/disclosure; Continuity where applicable; Concierge and existing Explainability/Trace.

## 13. Privacy and locality

Person-scoped activity and hospital context remain separately source/purpose/audience-authorized.
Ingestion is not disclosure; local derivatives inherit sensitivity/lifecycle, not unrestricted retention.

## 14. Unavailable / degraded behavior

"I don't know whether the prior pattern resumed" is valid; incomplete observations do not establish
recovery. Native HTBW/emergency mechanisms stay independent; no LLM critical path or cloud fallback.

## 15. Acceptance expectations

Selected before/after, personal baseline, progression and incomplete period captured; "normal" is not
defined medically. No recovery algorithm, ingestion/notification architecture or runtime acceptance.

## 16. Open questions

Eligible event/history context, comparison/retention and any human disclosure/handoff remain OPEN
ARCHITECTURAL QUESTIONS; no hospital/medical connector or recovery logic.
