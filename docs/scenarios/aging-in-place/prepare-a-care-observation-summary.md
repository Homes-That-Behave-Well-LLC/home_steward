# Prepare a care observation summary

Status: CANONICAL SCENARIO

Developed-content maturity only; not Owner, runtime or architecture acceptance.

> Aging in Place and Care Awareness: synthetic human-review narrative, not diagnosis or transmission design.

## 1. Household request

"Prepare a summary of what we have actually observed." Produce a careful narrative for human review.

## 2. Existing HTBW knowledge

Truth current/historical Home Facts, Foundation Person context, eligible Identity, Stewardship care
and existing Explainability. Home Steward does not become the care, Fact or medical record owner.

## 3. Authorized private retrieval

Potential independently approved Person wearable/Home observations, household reports and history.
Source/Person/purpose/audience scope is separate from any later caregiver/clinician disclosure.

## 4. Optional public research

Device/source documentation may clarify labels and limitations; it is not diagnosis or missing evidence.
Private Person/care information must not enter public queries.

## 5. Home Steward synthesis

Distinguish wearable observation, Home observation, household report, historical comparison, inference
and unknown. State actual sources/dates, selected personal comparison periods, quality and gaps.
The narrative is for human review, not a diagnosis, medical causal claim or automatic communication.

## 6. Provenance

Preserve device-reported values/alerts as reported, Home Truth references and attributed household
statements; name source/time/coverage and limit any interpretation to supported evidence.

## 7. Candidate relationships

Possible temporal/context associations stay tentative, not medical causation or accepted care records.

## 8. Household confirmation

An eligible human may correct observations/questions; review does not authorize disclosure/delivery.
Future confirmation/retention remains an OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION.

## 9. Governed durable relationship

OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION: no Care Observation Proposal, summary object, schema or new history store.

## 10. Proposed actions

The household may review the narrative and separately decide whether authorized sharing is desired.
How it is transmitted, notified or handed off remains an OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION.

## 11. Prohibited autonomous actions

No diagnosis, disease/fall prediction, medication-effect/medical-causation inference, treatment,
clinical monitoring, emergency decision, automatic caregiver notification/disclosure or care/policy change.

## 12. HTBW authority

Truth current Home state/history; Stewardship care obligations; Identity context; Foundation definitions;
Operational Trust action/disclosure; Continuity where applicable; Concierge and existing Explainability/Trace.

## 13. Privacy and locality

Person-scoped source sensitivity remains in the narrative; ingestion is not disclosure permission.
Local derivatives inherit lifecycle; no indefinite summary shadow or broad shared-household publication.

## 14. Unavailable / degraded behavior

"I don't know" and explicit gaps are valid; no reconstructed missing history or clinical reassurance.
Native HTBW/emergency mechanisms remain independent; no LLM critical path or cloud fallback.

## 15. Acceptance expectations

All six narrative classes and human review captured. No diagnosis, notification/transport,
handoff/proposal architecture, clinical monitoring or runtime acceptance established.

## 16. Open questions

Source/narrative eligibility, representation/retention, human review and transmission/handoff remain
OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTIONS; no care handler or wearable-ingestion design.
