# What are you seeing that is different

Status: CANONICAL SCENARIO

Developed-content maturity only; not Owner, runtime or architecture acceptance.

> Aging in Place and Care Awareness: synthetic, person-scoped, non-diagnostic experience capture.

## 1. Household request

"Mom doesn't seem to be moving around as much. What are you seeing?" Compare concern with actual evidence.

## 2. Existing HTBW knowledge

Truth current/historical Home Facts, Foundation Person/Room definitions, eligible Identity context,
Stewardship care and existing Explainability; a household impression is not automatically Truth.

## 3. Authorized private retrieval

Potential independently approved activity/wearable/movement/history and household observations.
Person attribution and cross-source use require scope; family/caregiver relationship does not grant access.

## 4. Optional public research

Device/source documentation may clarify meanings or limits, not a medical explanation.
No protected Person data or care concern sent to public queries.

## 5. Home Steward synthesis

Compare multiple eligible observations with the person's prior observed pattern and named recent period.
Separate household concern, recorded changes, unchanged evidence, gaps and competing non-diagnostic
interpretations. Less recorded motion does not prove reduced personal activity or illness.

## 6. Provenance

Source/Person/time, personal comparison periods, coverage/quality and conflicting observations remain
visible; distinguish wearable, Home, household report and inference.

## 7. Candidate relationships

Possible concurrent changes remain tentative; correlation is not medical causation or a durable care finding.

## 8. Household confirmation

Clarify dates/person/context and the household report; clarification does not grant disclosure or care action.
Future retention/review is an OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION.

## 9. Governed durable relationship

OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION: no care observation object, diagnostic record or proposal schema.

## 10. Proposed actions

The household may review evidence or request an independently authorized conversation.
Any caregiver disclosure, notification or handoff remains an OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION.

## 11. Prohibited autonomous actions

No diagnosis, disease/fall prediction, medication-effect inference, treatment, clinical monitoring,
emergency decision, automatic caregiver notification, Person ranking or policy/care change.

## 12. HTBW authority

Truth current Home state/history; Stewardship care obligations; Identity context; Foundation definitions;
Operational Trust action/disclosure; Continuity where applicable; Concierge and existing Explainability/Trace.

## 13. Privacy and locality

Person-scoped observations stay independently source/purpose/audience-authorized; ingestion does not
grant disclosure. Local derivatives inherit lifecycle; no broad surveillance or private query leakage.

## 14. Unavailable / degraded behavior

"I don't know what differs" remains valid for poor coverage or unavailable/unauthorized history.
Native HTBW/emergency mechanisms remain independent; no LLM critical path or silent cloud fallback.

## 15. Acceptance expectations

Concern versus observations, personal scope, provenance and uncertainty captured; no guessed medical
cause, wearer association or diagnostic/notification architecture.

## 16. Open questions

Evidence eligibility, comparison/retention, human review and any disclosure/handoff remain OPEN
ARCHITECTURAL QUESTIONS; no diagnostic or caregiver workflow.
