# Asset Intelligence

Navigation and scope guidance only; not an architecture contract or roadmap. Browse the [scenario-family map](../README.md).

## Purpose

Understand household possessions through their governed identity, documentation, history and care context. This is an outcome family, not the independently released predecessor product.

## Relationship to HTBW

Foundation owns descriptive Asset knowledge and relationships; Truth owns supported current locations and Facts; Stewardship owns significance and obligations. HTBW does not require the standalone Asset Intelligence integration. See the existing [HTBW boundary](../../architecture/htbw-boundary.md).

## Possible Home Steward extension

Explore authorized unstructured Asset evidence, comparisons, provenance and candidate relationships beyond straightforward native answers. Any new source admission, representation, handoff or implementation is an OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION.

## Boundaries

HTBW owns the Asset and Stewardship obligation; Home Steward may enrich understanding. Interpretation does not bind coverage, appraise value, mark care complete, or authorize contact, handling or procurement.

Person/source/purpose/namespace/audience scope, household-controlled locality and source-derived lifecycle remain independent requirements. Potential evidence is not admitted access; knowledge is not action or disclosure authority. Source reports and inference are not automatic Truth; unknown is valid and correlation is not causation.

## Reference Home discovery increment

Readiness enriches understanding of existing prerequisites; HTBW owns obligation condition, lifecycle and accountability. Requirements, actual stock, ordered parts, delivery, installation and completed work remain distinct. No inventory engine, purchase, work procedure, professional judgment, warranty assurance or care mutation is introduced.

New primary files in this increment: **7**. Each is catalogued only in this primary family. All earlier bodies and maturity remain unchanged.

- [What upcoming Asset care requires parts or supplies we do not appear to have?](what-upcoming-asset-care-requires-parts-or-supplies-we-do-not-appear-to-have.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Knowing which supplies are required does not establish whether the household currently has them. The household wants upcoming care compared with separately eligible stock and expected-delivery evidence, including what should arrive before the visit. An old receipt, missing inventory entry or delivery estimate should not become certainty about possession or readiness.
- [Which replacement part did we use last time?](which-replacement-part-did-we-use-last-time.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A prior part number may be useful while an invoice, service report and household recollection disagree about what was actually used. The household wants those dated claims and exact-model applicability distinguished, not a past purchase mistaken for installation. Even an accurately identified past part may no longer be appropriate.
- [Did we order the correct part for the exact Asset model?](did-we-order-the-correct-part-for-the-exact-asset-model.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. An order can list a plausible part while the Asset's revision or prior treatment requires something different. The household wants the actual order claim compared with the exact governed model and applicable documentation before relying on it. Confirmation of the order does not establish receipt, compatibility or permission to install.
- [Which work appears to require a qualified professional?](which-work-appears-to-require-a-qualified-professional.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Some work may be appropriate for an authorized household participant while other work requires qualified professional judgment or a documented restriction. The household wants those existing limits explained without an AI assessing someone's competence or declaring a hazardous procedure safe. Lack of a recorded restriction is not permission to proceed.
- [What should be prepared before taking this system offline?](what-should-be-prepared-before-taking-this-system-offline.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Taking a system offline for care may affect other equipment, access to instructions or an existing household obligation. The household wants approved prerequisites and documented consequences understood before the work begins. Preparation is not a shutdown plan, and missing dependencies must not be filled with generic maintenance advice.
- [Did a substitution introduce a different maintenance or warranty implication?](did-a-substitution-introduce-a-different-maintenance-or-warranty-implication.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A substitution already made may change care requirements, warranty applicability or service expectations in ways not reflected in older documents. The household wants the actual substituted item and effective terms compared rather than assuming equivalence from appearance. An apparent implication remains a review question, not a binding warranty decision or automatic schedule change.
- [What documentation should accompany the work?](what-documentation-should-accompany-the-work.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Household or professional work may need applicable instructions, approved scope and later completion evidence, not an indiscriminate document bundle. The household wants the right references and unresolved gaps understood while protecting financial, personal and unrelated Asset records. A useful document association is not permission to transmit it or retain a new copy.
