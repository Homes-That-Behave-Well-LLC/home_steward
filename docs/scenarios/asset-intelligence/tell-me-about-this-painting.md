# Tell me about this painting

Status: CANONICAL SCENARIO

Developed-content maturity only; not Owner, runtime or architecture acceptance.

> Proposed synthetic acceptance scenario; no household inventory or runtime result.
> Painting A / Artist X / Sculpture B / Textile C are **fictional**.

## 1. Household request

A resolved, eligible Person asks about a painting. Conversation follows ten turns: identify it;
learn about the artist; assess career period; ask about a Santa Fe trip; find other purchases;
confirm the trip association; locate items; inspect insurance; inspect appraisal; ask about care.

## 2. Existing HTBW knowledge

Foundation supplies Asset identity/Type, recorded artist/provenance, ownership, Room relationships,
owner/guest/insurance-facing descriptions and associated documents. Truth supplies eligible
current/historical Facts. Stewardship supplies care obligations. These stay native, not dependent on AI.

## 3. Authorized private retrieval

Approved Asset documents, receipts, travel/renovation records, appraisal, insurance rider and service/
conservation records. A personal calendar/email is queried only when specifically authorized for
this Person, purpose and fields; no household-wide financial or travel-history sweep.

## 4. Optional public research

Permitted artist biography, exhibition/career chronology and general conservation reference.
Use an established artist identity and public terms; never send purchase price, home address,
location, insurance or private travel details. Public claims remain separate from household evidence.

## 5. Home Steward synthesis

### Turn 1 - "Tell me about this painting."

HTBW resolves Painting A, its description and artist association. If that native answer is enough,
answer without Home Steward. Otherwise retrieve the authorized provenance documents and clearly
distinguish their text from interpretation; do not invent artist/title/value from visual resemblance.

### Turn 2 - "Tell me more about the artist."

Use the governed Artist X association, permitted public research and any authorized catalog.
Cite the public source/date and identify uncertain identification. Do not blend general artist
biography into authoritative ownership/provenance.

### Turn 3 - "Was this painted during an important period in the artist's career?"

Compare an evidenced creation date with cited career chronology. If the date/attribution is unknown,
say the conclusion cannot be established. "Important" is an interpretation, not a Fact or appraisal.

### Turn 4 - "Did we buy this during our Santa Fe trip?"

HTBW supplies any existing trip relationship. Otherwise compare approved receipt/date/travel records;
report a possible association with evidence/gaps. Similar dates alone do not establish household history.

### Turn 5 - "What else did we buy on that trip?"

Search only approved associated purchase records. A **fictional** example response identifies
Painting A - Dining Room; Sculpture B - Den; Textile C - Primary Bedroom as possible co-acquisitions.
Declared Rooms are Foundation relationships, not independently observed current locations.
Label each candidate and do not claim these are real household Assets.

### Turn 6 - "Yes, those were all from that trip."

Keep the sequence explicit: source evidence -> inferred candidate relationship -> attributable
household confirmation -> responsible HTBW review -> governed durable relationship, if permitted.
This statement confirms only the specified proposed trip association. Verify effective Person,
scope and conflicts; it is not permission to send records, change insurance or link other sources.
The final confirmation/promotion mechanism remains open rather than fabricated.

### Turn 7 - "Where are those items now?"

Read Foundation's declared locations and eligible Truth current-location evidence separately.
If only declared Rooms exist, say "recorded in" rather than claiming a fresh observation.
If Truth disagrees, present eligible uncertainty/conflict; do not silently update either authority.

### Turn 8 - "Are they all insured?"

Show governed policy/document associations and dates; compare an approved dated rider with the living
inventory. Missing rider entries, exclusions or stale documents mean uncertainty, not guaranteed
lack/presence of coverage. Home Steward cannot bind coverage, amend a policy or contact an insurer.

### Turn 9 - "When were they last appraised?"

Find associated, authorized appraisal dates. Distinguish last documented appraisal from an inferred
date or current valuation. Do not fabricate a value or automatically schedule a new appraisal.

### Turn 10 - "What care do they need?"

Stewardship supplies current governed obligations. Home Steward may explain authorized conservation/
manual/service guidance and propose review of a gap. It cannot create/remove/satisfy an obligation
or treat public advice as an exact procedure for the Asset.

## 6. Provenance

Preserve Asset/source/version/date, public-versus-private class, candidate basis and uncertainties.
Confirmation is separate evidence with attributable scope. Concierge composes the existing Trace
with owners' contributions; do not retain raw financial documents/prompts indefinitely for it.

## 7. Candidate relationships

Possible trip, artist, co-acquisition and document associations remain labelled inferred.
They cannot overwrite established Asset identity/provenance or become Truth because the model is confident.

## 8. Household confirmation

Use resolved, appropriately authorized Person confirmation for the particular candidates.
"Yes" does not confer universal source access, guest disclosure, action permission or every inferred link.

## 9. Governed durable relationship

Foundation evaluates eligible relationship promotion with relevant owners, evidence, conflicts and
authorization. Durable trip/co-acquisition relationships must preserve provenance and correction.
No final schema, API, identity key or automatic write mechanism is chosen in this scenario.

## 10. Proposed actions

Suggest reviewing missing appraisal/insurance evidence or care needs. An authorized claim-support
package/service contact is a proposal returning to HTBW; no external contact or scheduling by default.

## 11. Prohibited autonomous actions

No authoritative relationship/Truth writes, policy binding/amendment, insurer contact, appraisal
valuation, payment, hidden retention, unapproved photo/document upload, care-cycle alteration or
obligation completion.

## 12. HTBW authority

Foundation owns identity/relationships; Truth owns observations; Stewardship owns care; Identity
owns effective Person/consent lifecycle; Continuity owns context; Operational Trust governs source/
audience/permission; Concierge orchestrates/Trace composition. Home Steward owns none of these.

## 13. Privacy and locality

Person-scoped records remain scoped; guest/spoken versus private-screen responses may differ.
Local retrieval/inference/derived memory uses admitted sources and inherits their lifecycle.
No private trip, value or household location context goes to public research without separate permission.

## 14. Unavailable / degraded behavior

Native Asset descriptions/relationships/documents/obligations remain available by HTBW scope.
Say private retrieval or public research is unavailable; no fabricated provenance/cloud fallback.

## 15. Acceptance expectations

Ten turns preserve knowledge/authority separation. Native identity answers avoid needless AI;
scope-denied records never enter retrieval/answer; trip candidates stay inferred until governed
promotion; declared/current location stays distinct; insurance/care claims remain bounded.
Source removal/revocation reaches derived summaries; no prohibited action is executed.

## 16. Open questions

Artist/Asset disambiguation, explicit source/question category policy, confirmation strength,
durable relationship schema/promotion/correction, policy/appraisal interpretation bounds,
private output audience and deletion/Trace evidence mechanics remain unsettled.
