# What Asset knowledge exists only in correspondence?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

What Asset knowledge exists only in correspondence?

## Why it matters

A household may discover important Asset context in correspondence rather than in its existing documentation. A message might explain a repair, a provenance claim or the origin of care advice, but may also contain private details unrelated to stewardship. Understanding what authorized correspondence can clarify is useful only if interpretation remains distinct from accepted knowledge and private messages are not published as household examples.

## Existing HTBW relationship

HTBW Foundation owns Assets and descriptive relationships; Truth owns current/historical Facts; Stewardship owns governed care and obligations. Standalone Asset Intelligence is independently released; HTBW does not depend on its installation or consume it at runtime. The canonical Asset model permits it only as a predecessor-product migration source.

## Possible Home Steward extension

Compare authorized correspondence claims with existing Asset documentation and governed care. Explain possible documentary gaps, conflicts and uncertain attribution without importing messages wholesale, updating relationships, creating obligations or communicating with their authors.

## Potential evidence

Authorized correspondence categories; Asset documentation; treatment reports; acquisition-document categories; governed care; professional statement categories.
These are categories only, not admitted sources.

## Important boundary

Readable correspondence is neither disclosure permission nor accepted Truth; private personal wording is not published.

Document interpretation does not update HTBW knowledge or obligations. Observations, reports and inference are not Truth; correlation is not causation. Home Steward does not replace HTBW Risk.

Retrieval, derivation and disclosure each require independent Person/source/purpose/namespace/audience authorization. Unknown is a valid result. Sensitivity, locality and derived retention/revocation/deletion constraints persist. Family membership or caretaker identity grants no access.
