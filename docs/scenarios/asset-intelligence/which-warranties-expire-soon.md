# Which warranties expire soon?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

Which warranties expire soon?

## Why it matters

A household may want to understand upcoming warranty dates when receipts, registrations and terms describe different starting events. A date printed on a document does not necessarily settle whether protection is active or what it covers. Comparing the authorized paperwork could clarify uncertainty for human review without creating a second expiry schedule, initiating a claim or treating warranty expiry as an Asset failure.
