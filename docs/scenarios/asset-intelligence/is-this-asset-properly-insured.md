# Is this Asset properly insured

Status: CANONICAL SCENARIO

Developed-content maturity only; not Owner, runtime or architecture acceptance.

> Proposed synthetic scenario; no actual policy, coverage decision or claim.

## 1. Household request

"Is this Asset covered?", "Is the rider current?", "Help me prepare the evidence."

## 2. Existing HTBW knowledge

Foundation holds Asset/ownership/location, insurer-facing description and approved policy/appraisal/
document relationships. A living inventory can remain current through governed relationships;
an insurer rider remains a dated snapshot.

## 3. Authorized private retrieval

Specific eligible policy/rider, receipt, appraisal, inventory and service documents.
No household-wide finance/bank extraction or unrestricted personal mailbox.

## 4. Optional public research

Permitted general policy terminology, never a remote coverage determination. No protected values,
policy/account numbers, photos or locations submitted without separate disclosure authorization.

## 5. Home Steward synthesis

Compare associated Assets with dated rider entries; surface missing/stale evidence, exclusions and
interpretation uncertainty. Generate an authorized draft claim-support package if requested, but
do not guarantee or bind coverage. "Not found in this rider" is not "uninsured".

## 6. Provenance

Exact policy/rider version/date, Asset relationship and appraisal evidence; distinguish interpretation
from insurer confirmation and current authoritative relationships.

## 7. Candidate relationships

Possible Asset-to-rider/appraisal association is a candidate; names/values alone may be ambiguous.

## 8. Household confirmation

Confirm which Asset/documents and who may receive a draft. That is not policy change or insurer
contact authority; disclosures/actions need independent evaluation.

## 9. Governed durable relationship

Foundation owns approved Asset/document associations. No new insurance-state authority or
automatic rider/coverage write is created.

## 10. Proposed actions

Suggest Owner review, authorized appraisal or insurer confirmation and a minimized claim-support
package. Contacts, sending, policy modification and payments return through HTBW governance.

## 11. Prohibited autonomous actions

No binding coverage, submitting claims, contacting insurer, amending policy, changing premium,
inventing valuations or sharing private inventory merely because documents are accessible.

## 12. HTBW authority

Foundation model/document relationships; Truth eligible observations; Stewardship care;
Identity/Continuity requester/context; Operational Trust disclosure/action; Concierge workflow.

## 13. Privacy and locality

Insurance/financial sources and derived drafts retain person/purpose/audience restrictions.
Keep authorized inference local; disclose only minimized approved package fields.
The living inventory and claim-support package remain household-controlled; an implementer does
not need continuing possession of household records or a retained private copy to support the product.

## 14. Unavailable / degraded behavior

Show permitted native document relationships and dates. Missing retrieval/policy freshness means
bounded uncertainty, not invented coverage or silent remote upload.

## 15. Acceptance expectations

Dated snapshot versus living inventory stays explicit. Every coverage interpretation is bounded
and cited; prohibited contacts/transactions do not occur; denied sources never enter draft output.

## 16. Open questions

Policy interpretation limits, explicit package audience/scope, appraisal freshness and governed
relationship promotion remain open; no legal/insurance certification is claimed.
