Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

Which condition reports deserve follow-up?

## Why it matters

A household may have several condition reports whose recommendations, qualifications and dates are difficult to compare. A report mentioning follow-up may reflect a professional request, while an older report may simply have limited scope. Understanding those distinctions could support consultation without ranking intervention urgency, diagnosing deterioration or treating a document's age as a new care obligation.
