# Which obligations are blocked by another dependency?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

Which obligations are blocked by another dependency?

## Why it matters

A household may see maintenance approaching without understanding why an existing obligation cannot yet be fulfilled. A documented service dependency, an uncertain prerequisite and a merely suggested sequence are different things. Explaining the evidence behind a blockage could support an informed review without inventing dependencies, reordering care or treating an AI interpretation as a change to the obligation's governed condition.

## Existing HTBW relationship

HTBW Foundation owns Assets and descriptive relationships; Truth owns current/historical Facts; Stewardship owns governed care and obligations. Standalone Asset Intelligence is independently released; HTBW does not depend on its installation or consume it at runtime. The canonical Asset model permits it only as a predecessor-product migration source.

## Possible Home Steward extension

Compare existing Stewardship obligations and documented dependency context with authorized manuals and service records. Explain accepted versus inferred prerequisites, conflicting descriptions and missing evidence without altering schedules, dependency relationships or obligation state.

## Potential evidence

Governed obligations; existing dependency relationships; service-document categories; manuals; care requirements; authorized correspondence categories.
These are categories only, not admitted sources.

## Important boundary

Inferred prerequisites do not become accepted dependencies; no workflow, service booking or reminder mechanism is created.

Document interpretation does not update HTBW knowledge or obligations. Observations, reports and inference are not Truth; correlation is not causation. Home Steward does not replace HTBW Risk.

Retrieval, derivation and disclosure each require independent Person/source/purpose/namespace/audience authorization. Unknown is a valid result. Sensitivity, locality and derived retention/revocation/deletion constraints persist. Family membership or caretaker identity grants no access.
