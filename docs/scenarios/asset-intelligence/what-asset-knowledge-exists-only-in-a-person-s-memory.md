# What Asset knowledge exists only in a person's memory?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

What Asset knowledge exists only in a person's memory?

## Why it matters

A household may rely on a person's memory for an Asset's origin, past care or significance. That knowledge can be meaningful even when its documentary basis is uncertain, but personal memory also carries privacy and continuity concerns. A useful explanation would distinguish voluntary recollection from accepted relationships and professional care, without assuming family membership entitles others to hear it or turning uncertainty into a confident history.

## Existing HTBW relationship

HTBW Foundation owns Assets and descriptive relationships; Truth owns current/historical Facts; Stewardship owns governed care and obligations. Standalone Asset Intelligence is independently released; HTBW does not depend on its installation or consume it at runtime. The canonical Asset model permits it only as a predecessor-product migration source.

## Possible Home Steward extension

Compare voluntarily shared, authorized recollection with existing Asset documentation and governed care. Explain remembered claims, conflicts and missing corroboration without creating durable memory, accepting provenance, revising obligations or asserting current Facts.

## Potential evidence

Voluntary recollection categories; Asset descriptions; acquisition documentation; treatment reports; governed care; existing provenance relationships.
These are categories only, not admitted sources.

## Important boundary

Recollection is not consent, professional advice or Truth; no private biography or identifiable household example is published.

Document interpretation does not update HTBW knowledge or obligations. Observations, reports and inference are not Truth; correlation is not causation. Home Steward does not replace HTBW Risk.

Retrieval, derivation and disclosure each require independent Person/source/purpose/namespace/audience authorization. Unknown is a valid result. Sensitivity, locality and derived retention/revocation/deletion constraints persist. Family membership or caretaker identity grants no access.
