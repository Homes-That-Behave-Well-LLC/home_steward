## Why it matters

A household may have acquisition papers and some later history but still lack a complete provenance account. Gaps matter differently from contradictory claims, and restricted documents may simply be unavailable to the requester. Understanding the limits of the existing evidence could support professional review without making a plausible ownership chain appear recorded, authenticated or complete.
