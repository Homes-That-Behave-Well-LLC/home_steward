## Household question

What supplies or parts are needed before maintenance?

## Why it matters

A household preparing for maintenance may not know which documented parts or supplies apply to its exact Asset and treatment history. A generic manual, a later service note and an uncertain model match can point in different directions. Understanding those differences could reduce confusion before a qualified service discussion, without letting an AI select substitutes, authorize procurement or provide potentially unsafe maintenance instructions.
