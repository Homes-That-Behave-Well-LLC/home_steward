## Household question

Did a substitution introduce a different maintenance or warranty implication?

## Why it matters

A substitution already made may change care requirements, warranty applicability or service expectations in ways not reflected in older documents. The household wants the actual substituted item and effective terms compared rather than assuming equivalence from appearance. An apparent implication remains a review question, not a binding warranty decision or automatic schedule change.
