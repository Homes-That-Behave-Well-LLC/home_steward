# Which replacement part did we use last time?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

Which replacement part did we use last time?

## Why it matters

A prior part number may be useful while an invoice, service report and household recollection disagree about what was actually used. The household wants those dated claims and exact-model applicability distinguished, not a past purchase mistaken for installation. Even an accurately identified past part may no longer be appropriate.
