# Did the fix actually work

> Proposed synthetic scenario; all illustrative counts are hypothetical, not Reference Home evidence.
> Applies the [investigation evidence rules](../architecture/knowledge-architecture.md#evidence-and-epistemic-rules).

## 1. Household request

"We changed the Den presence sensor last month. Did that actually fix the lighting problem?"
Review the lived outcome against the declared original problem, not just whether the new device responds.

## 2. Existing HTBW knowledge

Foundation declared sensor/configuration/dependency versions; authoritative Decision Traces and
Truth Historical Facts; Continuity relevant context; Stewardship service history. HTBW's native
historical reconstruction/recorded event explanations remain unchanged.

## 3. Authorized private retrieval

Minimum problem/decision records, exact sensor/configuration Change Record, reason and Effective/
Recorded date; comparable pre/post false-event evidence, manual corrections, confidence failures
and failure/non-action outcomes. HA logs/history require a separate future grant; Person/movement
scope is classified through its dedicated HTBW path before protected assembly.

## 4. Optional public research

Permitted exact-model technical documentation may clarify limitations. No private occupancy/
movement history, topology or raw traces disclosed to external sources.

## 5. Home Steward synthesis

### Outcome-review cycle

**Problem -> decision -> change -> lived outcome -> evidence -> review ->
KEEP / ADAPT / REMOVE candidate recommendation.**

Identify the recorded lighting problem and the decision's desired outcome. Confirm the actual
change, date and reason from governed evidence. Compare the same metrics before and after over
comparable periods/exposure; successful installation alone is not the lived outcome.

Hypothetical fixture: two matched fourteen-day windows, forty eligible lighting evaluations each:

| Evidence metric | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Independently evidenced false events | 6 | 1 |
| Attributed/authorized manual corrections | 5 | 1 |
| Traces with recorded confidence/unknown-outcome limitations | 4 | 1 |

These metrics can overlap; they are not a partition or invented failure rate. "False" requires
eligible corroborating/attested evidence, not absence of a report. Distinguish intentional
non-actions from failures. Consider changed use, environmental conditions, privacy scope,
sample size, evidence age and other simultaneous changes.

Where the hypothetical evidence supports it: "The evidence suggests the change substantially
improved the behavior." Recommend KEEP as a review candidate, ADAPT for remaining evidenced gaps,
or REMOVE for household review where outcomes do not meet declared needs. None is an action.
This supports closing the governance feedback loop with lived-outcome review, not merely diagnosis,
and does not itself close an issue, obligation or governed acceptance.

## 6. Provenance

Cite exact old/new versions, recorded reason/date, comparable windows, exposure, metric definitions,
sample/coverage and alternatives. Separate Historical Facts, traces, operational logs, service/
configuration evidence, statistical improvement and inference. Before/after correlation alone
does not establish that this sensor was the sole cause.

## 7. Candidate relationships

Possible change-to-improvement association and KEEP / ADAPT / REMOVE disposition remain candidates.
No model-generated diagnosis, causal reference or accepted household preference is created.

## 8. Household confirmation

Ask whether observed lived experience meets the original declared need. This is scoped feedback,
not authority to accept a fix, change configuration, replace another device or erase history.

## 9. Governed durable relationship

Responsible owners govern accepted configuration/relationship/obligation records and acceptance.
Home Steward contributes evidence, never overwrites the original decision or promotes its review
into Truth, completion or a competing history.

## 10. Proposed actions

Offer the candidate outcome review through HTBW and Operational Trust. Concierge can coordinate
the authoritative owner interaction and separately authorized investigation/change.

## 11. Prohibited autonomous actions

No preference/policy edits, integration disablement, automation removal, obligation closure/change,
rollback/restart, device replacement, service scheduling or vendor contact without HTBW authority.

## 12. HTBW authority

Foundation configuration; Truth Facts/history; Stewardship care; Continuity context; Identity
requester; Operational Trust permission; Concierge recorded explanation/coordination, not acceptance ownership.

## 13. Privacy and locality

Separately governed logs and Person-sensitive evidence are minimally retrieved for the eligible
audience. Local derived comparisons inherit source lifecycle; no automatic indefinite raw-log memory or shadow trace.

## 14. Unavailable / degraded behavior

Aged-out, never-retained or incomparable before/after evidence requires "I don't know whether the
fix worked". State what can be observed now without inventing history; native behavior continues.

## 15. Acceptance expectations

Every cycle stage is represented; change/date/reason precedes review; matched exposure, false-event
evidence, overlapping metrics, confidence limits and alternatives are explicit.
Candidate KEEP / ADAPT / REMOVE never performs an action or closes anything.

## 16. Open questions

Comparable windows, declared success criteria, eligible false-event/attestation evidence and owner
review process remain open; no query, ingestion, benchmark or automatic fix acceptance is implemented.
