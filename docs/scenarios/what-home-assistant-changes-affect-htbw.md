# What Home Assistant changes affect HTBW

> Proposed synthetic architecture release review; no actual HTBW regression or deployment.
> Applies [HTBW Architecture Stewardship](../architecture/htbw-architecture-stewardship.md).

## 1. Household request

"What changed in Home Assistant this month that affects HTBW architecture or implementation?"
Resolve authorized engineering scope/version; this is not the household Home Possibility Review.

## 2. Existing HTBW knowledge

Accepted architecture, responsibility contracts, dependency/configuration/implementation and tests;
current scoped issues/status, Foundation model and authoritative Decision Traces.

## 3. Authorized private retrieval

Minimum approved repository/version/decision/contract/dependency and acceptance references.
Owner/private issue/code/log sources need independent grants; no credential access assumed.

## 4. Optional public research

[Official Core releases](https://www.home-assistant.io/blog/categories/core/), [developer blog](https://contributors.home-assistant.io/blog/),
integration/architecture/deprecation and Labs documentation; no community-only proof or private uploads.

## 5. Home Steward synthesis

Distinguish household-facing capability, developer/API change, deprecation, breaking change,
new native abstraction, Labs/preview feature, implementation opportunity, implementation risk and
no-action item. Match actual HTBW assumptions/dependencies; do not equate published date with effect.

For example, a November announcement effective in 2026.12 is future review evidence, not a 2026.11
failure. Native logs/traces or targets do not automatically replace HTBW governance/Decision Trace.
Every review candidate gives expected outcome, benefit/new obligation, affected contracts/tests/docs
and gaps. No meaningful applicable change is a valid result with adequate coverage.

## 6. Provenance

Source URL/repository location/version/publication/commit date, review date, effective/installed HA
version, GA/Labs/preview/deprecated/removal and scope. Interpretation/pattern is not current Truth.

## 7. Candidate relationships

Release-to-HTBW impact is a candidate, not a reopened decision, current failure or accepted native
replacement. Incomplete official evidence cannot support migration advice.

## 8. Household confirmation

Eligible reviewer confirms scope/outcome concerns, not permission to amend architecture, update HA
or accept changed behavior.

## 9. Governed durable relationship

Original issue/decision/contract/history owners remain authoritative; no Ledger/ADR/issue mutation
or new architecture acceptance store by Home Steward.

## 10. Proposed actions

Offer scoped reconsideration/testing drafts through HTBW/Operational Trust with equivalence/privacy,
rollback, HA-DEV and lived validation, and Owner acceptance before any retirement.

## 11. Prohibited autonomous actions

No issue creation/closure, decision reopening, Ledger/ADR edit, code removal, migration, automation/
policy/configuration changes, HA update/Labs enablement or autonomous upstream contribution.

## 12. HTBW authority

Accepted architecture controls contracts; Foundation model; Truth Facts; Identity reviewer;
Stewardship care; Continuity context; Operational Trust permission; Concierge formulation/Trace.

## 13. Privacy and locality

Minimum approved engineering source/Person/audience evidence; no sensitive code/log queries.
Local derived impact summaries inherit lifecycle and source freshness, not blanket indexing permission.

## 14. Unavailable / degraded behavior

Unknown dependency/version or stale official evidence yields "I don't know what affects HTBW".
Preserve accepted architecture/native behavior; no unqualified impact or cloud fallback.

## 15. Acceptance expectations

All nine categories; distinct from household monthly review, with official effective-version evidence.
No decisions, code, update or retirement changed by a release summary.

## 16. Open questions

Future scope/admission, impact criteria and review presentation remain open; no release watcher,
repository/log ingestion or architecture-change runtime.
