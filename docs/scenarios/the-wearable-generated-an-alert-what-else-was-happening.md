# The wearable generated an alert; what else was happening

> Aging in Place and Care Awareness: synthetic context review, not alerting or medical judgment.

## 1. Household request

"The wearable generated an alert. What else was happening around that time?"
Understand context without changing the device's report.

## 2. Existing HTBW knowledge

Truth current/historical Home Facts, Foundation Person/Room context, eligible Identity, Stewardship
care and existing Explainability. The wearable is authoritative for its own device-originated alert.

## 3. Authorized private retrieval

Potential Person-authorized original alert, activity, Room context, environmental conditions, recent
Home events and prior similar device-originated alerts; movement history remains separately governed.

## 4. Optional public research

Device documentation may clarify its exact reported alert; no reinterpretation or diagnosis.
Private alert/Person/care context does not enter public queries.

## 5. Home Steward synthesis

Preserve original alert wording/provenance and event time. Describe eligible contemporaneous
observations and timing/coverage limits, not intensify, downgrade or reinterpret the alert as a diagnosis.
Coincidence/correlation is not cause; contextual review does not evaluate emergency severity.

## 6. Provenance

Keep original device/source/version/time distinct from wearable/Home observations, household report,
historical comparison and inference. Prior alert similarity does not prove equivalent cause or safety.

## 7. Candidate relationships

Possible time/context overlap remains tentative, not medical causation or a new alert/relationship record.

## 8. Household confirmation

Clarify which alert/time/Person is meant; context confirmation does not authorize changing the alert
or disclosure. Future retention/review is an OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION.

## 9. Governed durable relationship

OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION: no alert/proposal object, medical event model or notification schema.

## 10. Proposed actions

The household may review context or separately request a human-reviewed summary.
Any new disclosure/notification/handoff mechanism remains an OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION.

## 11. Prohibited autonomous actions

No alert intensification/downgrade/reinterpretation, diagnosis, disease/fall prediction, medication-effect
inference, treatment, clinical monitoring, emergency decision, automatic caregiver notification or policy/care change.

## 12. HTBW authority

Truth current Home state/history; Stewardship care obligations; Identity context; Foundation definitions;
Operational Trust action/disclosure; Continuity where applicable; Concierge and existing Explainability/Trace.

## 13. Privacy and locality

Person-scoped alerts/context require separate ingestion and disclosure permissions.
Local derivatives inherit source sensitivity/lifecycle; native alert scope is not expanded by contextual data.

## 14. Unavailable / degraded behavior

"I don't know what else was happening" is valid; the original alert is not suppressed, delayed or
changed. Established emergency mechanisms/native HTBW stay independent; no LLM critical path or cloud fallback.

## 15. Acceptance expectations

Five contextual evidence groups, original provenance and no stronger/weaker alert captured.
No medical judgment, alert/notification architecture or runtime acceptance.

## 16. Open questions

Alert/context admission, retention, human review and any authorized disclosure/handoff remain OPEN
ARCHITECTURAL QUESTIONS; no wearable ingestion or emergency workflow.
