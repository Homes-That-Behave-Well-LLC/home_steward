# What information belongs to the people leaving?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

What information belongs to the people leaving?

## Why it matters

A move can leave personal documents, preferences and stories mixed with shared household records. The household wants to understand whose stated interests and documented rights are involved without reducing ownership to file location. Shared storage or past availability should not erase a departing person's privacy, nor automatically assign every mixed record to one party.

## Existing HTBW relationship

Foundation retains definitions/configuration; Truth, current/historical Facts; Identity, Person resolution, not authentication/authorization; Stewardship, household-declared care/obligations; Continuity, preference/experience eligibility; Operational Trust, request-specific permissions; Concierge, orchestration/Trace. Home Steward acquires no ownership.

## Possible Home Steward extension

## Potential evidence

Source ownership categories; approved transition declarations; Person scope records; mixed-document descriptions; retention/hold terms.

Categories only, each independently authorized for purpose, Person, source, audience and namespace. Local processing and derived retention, correction, expiry and revocation remain source-bound.

## Important boundary

No automatic export, disclosure, deletion or ownership reassignment; shared storage does not resolve personal rights.

No inferred incapacity, death, delegation, credential transfer or legal authority. Humans/professionals make legal/estate decisions. No credential store or permission-inheritance architecture.

Knowledge/interpretation never grants action or disclosure authority. Unclear evidence stays unknown; correlation/timing is not causation. No life-safety or medical/emergency path depends on Home Steward/LLM.
