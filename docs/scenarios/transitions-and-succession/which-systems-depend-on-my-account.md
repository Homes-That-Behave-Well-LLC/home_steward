# Which systems depend on my account?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

Which systems depend on my account?

## Why it matters

A household can become dependent on one person's account even when several people use the same equipment. The household wants to understand documented dependencies before considering a voluntary transition. An account dependency is not evidence of incapacity, permission to share credentials or authority for someone else to assume ownership.

## Existing HTBW relationship

Foundation retains definitions/configuration; Truth, current/historical Facts; Identity, Person resolution, not authentication/authorization; Stewardship, household-declared care/obligations; Continuity, preference/experience eligibility; Operational Trust, request-specific permissions; Concierge, orchestration/Trace.
