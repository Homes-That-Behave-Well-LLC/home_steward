# What household history belongs with the property?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

What household history belongs with the property?

## Why it matters

Maintenance and renovation records can help a future owner understand the property, while household stories and personal details may belong elsewhere. The household wants to compare record purposes, ownership and documented sharing scope. The useful history of a building should not become a blanket justification for transferring the lives of the people who occupied it.

## Existing HTBW relationship

Foundation retains definitions/configuration; Truth, current/historical Facts; Identity, Person resolution, not authentication/authorization; Stewardship, household-declared care/obligations; Continuity, preference/experience eligibility; Operational Trust, request-specific permissions; Concierge, orchestration/Trace. Home Steward acquires no ownership.
