# Which licenses, subscriptions, credentials, and warranties transfer?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

Which licenses, subscriptions, credentials, and warranties transfer?

## Why it matters

Licenses, subscriptions, warranties and credentials do not all follow the same transfer rules. The household wants to understand the terms and dates that apply to each category before asking a qualified person or provider. A product being left in the house should not be treated as proof that its account, benefits or permissions transfer with it.
