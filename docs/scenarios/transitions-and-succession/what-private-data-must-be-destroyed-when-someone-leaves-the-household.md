# What private data must be destroyed when someone leaves the household?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

What private data must be destroyed when someone leaves the household?

## Why it matters

A departing member may have privacy expectations alongside shared records and retention obligations. The household wants to understand the documented categories and unresolved constraints rather than assume that departure authorizes wholesale erasure. Removal of source access must also remain binding on derived material; an old summary is not an acceptable way to keep revoked personal information.

## Existing HTBW relationship

Foundation retains definitions/configuration; Truth, current/historical Facts; Identity, Person resolution, not authentication/authorization; Stewardship, household-declared care/obligations; Continuity, preference/experience eligibility; Operational Trust, request-specific permissions; Concierge, orchestration/Trace. Home Steward acquires no ownership.

## Important boundary

No autonomous destruction; preserve binding revocation/expiry and refer legal retention conflicts to qualified humans.

No inferred incapacity, death, delegation, credential transfer or legal authority. Humans/professionals make legal/estate decisions. No credential store or permission-inheritance architecture.

Knowledge/interpretation never grants action or disclosure authority. Unclear evidence stays unknown; correlation/timing is not causation. No life-safety or medical/emergency path depends on Home Steward/LLM.
