# If a household member becomes unavailable, what responsibilities need reassignment?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

If a household member becomes unavailable, what responsibilities need reassignment?

## Why it matters

A household may voluntarily plan for a member being unavailable while still relying on them for specific care obligations. The desired outcome is understanding documented responsibilities and dependencies, not inferring why the person is absent or who should take over. A contingency conversation does not create delegation, consent or authority for a substitute.

## Existing HTBW relationship

Foundation retains definitions/configuration; Truth, current/historical Facts; Identity, Person resolution, not authentication/authorization; Stewardship, household-declared care/obligations; Continuity, preference/experience eligibility; Operational Trust, request-specific permissions; Concierge, orchestration/Trace. Home Steward acquires no ownership.
