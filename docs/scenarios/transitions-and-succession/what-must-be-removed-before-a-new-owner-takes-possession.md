# What must be removed before a new owner takes possession?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

What must be removed before a new owner takes possession?

## Why it matters

Before possession changes, personal information and property-related knowledge may need different treatment. The household wants to understand documented removal expectations and unresolved dependencies without assuming everything should be erased. Useful care records, another person's privacy rights and applicable retention obligations can conflict and require separately authorized human or professional decisions.
