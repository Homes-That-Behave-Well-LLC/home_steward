# Where can we watch this

> Entertainment and Media Discovery: synthetic experience capture, not implementation or architecture.

## 1. Household request

"Where can we watch this?" Understand available options without knowing providers, URLs or library paths.

## 2. Existing HTBW knowledge

Foundation Room/device definitions, Truth current Home state and eligible Continuity context.
A configured player or installed app does not establish content entitlement.

## 3. Authorized private retrieval

Potential private NAS, household-authorized Plex/Jellyfin/Channels or equivalent library, and approved
streaming/account information. Each source/Person scope is independent.

## 4. Optional public research

Dated regional streaming, purchase or rental listings; title/edition availability can change.
Public listing presence is not proof of subscription, household access or playback compatibility.

## 5. Home Steward synthesis

Distinguish private holdings, authorized streaming, purchase/rental, unavailable and unknown.
Clarify title/edition, region, requester entitlement and intended Room; say what was actually checked.

## 6. Provenance

Source, listing/review time, region, edition and entitlement evidence with coverage/freshness limits.
Stale or undated availability is not current assurance.

## 7. Candidate relationships

Content-to-source options remain candidates, not a new entitlement, selection rule or durable record.

## 8. Household confirmation

Clarify which content/option the requester means; choosing rental does not authorize spending.
Future clarification retention is an OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION.

## 9. Governed durable relationship

OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION: no catalog/entitlement object, source-selection policy or schema.

## 10. Proposed actions

Answer availability or compare options. A later watch/purchase request requires separate authority;
the future governed handoff/execution is an OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION.

## 11. Prohibited autonomous actions

No purchase/rental/subscription/playback, credential use, private-library scan, access bypass,
configuration/integration change or Home Assistant service mapping.

## 12. HTBW authority

Foundation definitions; Identity context; Truth state; Continuity experience; Stewardship care;
Operational Trust action/disclosure/financial authority; Concierge and existing Explainability/Trace.

## 13. Privacy and locality

Source availability is not access or disclosure permission. Private titles/accounts/history remain
Person/purpose/audience-scoped; local derivatives inherit lifecycle, with no public query leakage.

## 14. Unavailable / degraded behavior

Unknown region, stale evidence or denied library access yields "I don't know where you can watch".
Unavailable and unchecked differ; preserve native HTBW without unauthorized source/cloud substitution.

## 15. Acceptance expectations

All source classes, entitlement/compatibility limits and time-sensitive availability captured.
No universal access, purchase authority or provider-selection/execution architecture.

## 16. Open questions

Availability admission/review, entitlement proof, source selection and execution/handoff remain
OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTIONS; no provider adapter or deep-link resolver.
