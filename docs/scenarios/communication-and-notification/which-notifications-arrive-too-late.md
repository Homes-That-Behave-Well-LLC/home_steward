# Which notifications arrive too late?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

Which notifications arrive too late?

## Why it matters

A message can arrive after its intended usefulness because of a source delay, eligibility constraint or unavailable surface. The household wants a supported timing explanation rather than blame, an assumption of receipt, or an automatic reduction in interruption or disclosure protections.

## Existing HTBW relationship

HTBW already distinguishes Communication from delivery. Originating responsibilities retain their content; Operational Trust governs audience and interruption, and Concierge retains delivery orchestration and Decision Trace. Existing disclosure and retention rules are unchanged.

## Possible Home Steward extension

Compare eligible relevance, decision and delivery timing with authorized expectations and known uncertainty. Any new source admission, representation, handoff or implementation is an OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION; no mechanism is specified.

## Potential evidence

- Eligible Communication and delivery evidence
- Applicable urgency and audience decisions
- Authorized usefulness-window feedback

These are possible categories only, not admitted sources. Each requires independent Person/source/purpose/audience scope; derived information inherits sensitivity, household-controlled locality and source lifecycle.

## Important boundary

Action permission is not announcement or disclosure permission. A delivery attempt does not establish receipt, attention or understanding. No new delivery owner, notification engine, recipient inference or suppression policy is created; ordinary retention remains household-configured, including zero or disabled. Source reports and inference are not automatic Truth; unknown is a valid result. No silent cloud or source fallback.
