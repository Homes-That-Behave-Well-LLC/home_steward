# Communication and Notification

Navigation and scope guidance only; not an architecture contract or roadmap. Browse the [scenario-family map](../README.md).

## Purpose

Understand communication experience, repetition, usefulness and disclosure without designing delivery mechanisms.

## Relationship to HTBW

Communication is distinct from delivery; originating responsibilities own content, Operational Trust governs audience/interruption and Concierge retains delivery orchestration and Trace. See the existing [HTBW boundary](../../architecture/htbw-boundary.md).

## Possible Home Steward extension

Explain eligible delivery and interaction evidence with explicit household feedback. Any new source admission, representation, handoff or implementation is an OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION.

## Boundaries

Action permission != announcement/disclosure permission. Delivery attempt != receipt or understanding. No new delivery owner, recipient inference, suppression engine or shadow history; household-configured retention, including zero/disabled, stays authoritative.

Person/source/purpose/namespace/audience scope, household-controlled locality and source-derived lifecycle remain independent requirements. Potential evidence is not admitted access; knowledge is not action or disclosure authority. Source reports and inference are not automatic Truth; unknown is valid and correlation is not causation.

### Vision — Not Yet Architected

- [What notifications do we keep ignoring?](what-notifications-do-we-keep-ignoring.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A missing acknowledgement does not prove that a person ignored a message.
- [Which announcements cause intervention anyway?](which-announcements-cause-intervention-anyway.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. An announcement may convey information while people still need to intervene manually for legitimate reasons.
- [Are we receiving the same concern through too many channels?](are-we-receiving-the-same-concern-through-too-many-channels.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Several messages may concern the same underlying obligation, or merely look similar while conveying different information.
- [Which notifications arrive too late?](which-notifications-arrive-too-late.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A message can arrive after its intended usefulness because of a source delay, eligibility constraint or unavailable surface.
- [What important information has no appropriate recipient?](what-important-information-has-no-appropriate-recipient.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Information can matter to the household without an authorized audience being configured to receive it.
- [Which messages disclose too much in shared spaces?](which-messages-disclose-too-much-in-shared-spaces.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A shared-space announcement may reveal private content even when the underlying action was permitted.
- [What should be summarized rather than announced immediately?](what-should-be-summarized-rather-than-announced-immediately.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A household may prefer a later summary for some information while other concerns retain their existing urgency.
- [Did reducing notifications cause anything important to be missed?](did-reducing-notifications-cause-anything-important-to-be-missed.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Fewer notifications may reduce friction while also reducing awareness, but missing acknowledgements cannot prove what people knew.
