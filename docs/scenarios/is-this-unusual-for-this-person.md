# Is this unusual for this person

> Aging in Place and Care Awareness: synthetic, person-scoped, non-diagnostic experience capture.

## 1. Household request

"Are these wearable readings unusual for this person, or just unusual generally?"
Understand personal observed comparison, not generic medical normality.

## 2. Existing HTBW knowledge

Foundation Person/source definitions, eligible Identity, Truth current/historical Home Facts and
Stewardship care. Device ownership alone does not establish wearer identity or medical interpretation.

## 3. Authorized private retrieval

Potential independently approved Person-specific wearable reports, historical baseline and household
context. No comparison against other household members without their separate authorization.

## 4. Optional public research

Device definitions or properly governed authoritative classifications may be attributed as source
claims. Do not invent population-level medical thresholds or treat them as this person's clinical state.

## 5. Home Steward synthesis

Favor the person's own authorized prior observed baseline, with chosen recent/baseline periods,
quality and known context. Distinguish personal difference from a provider's generic classification;
neither establishes illness, medical severity or treatment need.

## 6. Provenance

Preserve wearable/source-qualified readings/classifications, dates/units/coverage, personal periods
and inference limits. A provider classification is not a Home Steward medical judgment.

## 7. Candidate relationships

Possible deviation remains descriptive, not a medical threshold, causal relationship or new care record.

## 8. Household confirmation

Clarify whose readings/period/context; confirmation is not wearer identification or disclosure permission.
Any new review/retention mechanism is an OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION.

## 9. Governed durable relationship

OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION: no normality/baseline model, threshold algorithm or care proposal schema.

## 10. Proposed actions

The household may review evidence or prepare separately authorized questions for a human.
Any disclosure, notification or handoff remains an OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION.

## 11. Prohibited autonomous actions

No diagnosis, disease/fall prediction, medication-effect inference, treatment, clinical monitoring,
emergency decision, medical ranking/threshold creation, automatic caregiver notification or care/policy change.

## 12. HTBW authority

Truth current Home state/history; Stewardship care obligations; Identity context; Foundation definitions;
Operational Trust action/disclosure; Continuity where applicable; Concierge and existing Explainability/Trace.

## 13. Privacy and locality

Person-scoped readings/baseline remain separately governed; ingestion is not disclosure.
Local derivatives inherit sensitivity/lifecycle, not population profiling or cross-Person access.

## 14. Unavailable / degraded behavior

"I don't know what is usual for this person" is valid with insufficient baseline; do not substitute a
generic normal person. Native HTBW/emergency paths stay independent; no LLM critical path/cloud fallback.

## 15. Acceptance expectations

Personal versus generic/provider comparison and source/wearer limits captured; no clinical norm,
diagnostic capability, baseline/threshold architecture or runtime acceptance.

## 16. Open questions

Eligible baseline/context sources, comparison/retention and any human review/disclosure/handoff
remain OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTIONS; no medical algorithm.
