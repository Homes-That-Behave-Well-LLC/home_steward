> Entertainment and Media Discovery: synthetic experience capture, not implementation or architecture.

"What was that British detective show we watched last winter?" Recover a shared reference without
assuming complete or universally available viewing history.

## 2. Existing HTBW knowledge

existing Explainability. Provider histories remain source-owned, not a new Home Steward memory.

## 3. Authorized private retrieval

Potential household media history, private-library history, streaming history, native HA media history,
household narrative and Person-scoped preferences/history, each separately authorized.

## 4. Optional public research

Public program/cast/country/era metadata may narrow candidates; no private dates or Person history
sent in public queries. A catalog match does not prove household viewing or entitlement.

## 5. Home Steward synthesis

Clarify who "we" includes, which winter, remembered setting/cast and authorized history gaps.

## 6. Provenance

Label household report, provider/HA history and public metadata separately, with dates, coverage and
uncertainty. Time-sensitive streaming availability needs source/review time and current regional
evidence; a stale listing is not a current availability claim.

## 7. Candidate relationships

Show-to-past-experience matches remain candidates, not new Person associations or durable history.

Do not equate missing history with never watched; preserve native HTBW without source/cloud fallback.

retain all viewing history or implement a retrieval/learning mechanism.
