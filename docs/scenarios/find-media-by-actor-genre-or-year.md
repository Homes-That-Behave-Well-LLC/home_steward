> Entertainment and Media Discovery: synthetic experience capture, not implementation or architecture.

"Show me movies with Tilda Swinton from the 1990s."
"What good science-fiction movies from the last five years can we watch?"
"Find comedies with this actor."

## 2. Existing HTBW knowledge

Eligible Person/Room context, declared Continuity preferences and Truth current Home state.
"Good" is not a universal household preference or an authority to decide taste.

## 3. Authorized private retrieval

Separately approved private holdings, subscriptions and Person preferences/history; library access
does not authorize every household member's interests or accounts.

## 4. Optional public research

Public actor/director/artist credits, genre, year/decade and rating/classification metadata.
Availability needs dated regional evidence, not a title's presence in a public catalog.

## 5. Home Steward synthesis

Combine remembered actor/director/artist, genre, year/decade, appropriate classification and household
availability. Clarify "this actor", rolling date ranges and subjective "good"; present uncertain options.

## 6. Provenance

Separate external metadata/classification, declared preference and private entitlement/holdings.
Name source/review date and freshness/region limits for streaming results.

## 7. Candidate relationships

Compound matches are candidate options, not new household taste, ranking or durable relationships.

## 11. Prohibited autonomous actions

source-lifecycle-bound. Public criteria do not authorize leaking private viewing or subscriptions.

complete catalog or guaranteed entitlement. Preserve native HTBW without silent fallback.
