# Has anything changed since the medication changed

> Aging in Place and Care Awareness: synthetic timing review, not medication-effect inference.

## 1. Household request

"Has anything changed since the medication changed?" Compare observed periods without attributing cause.

## 2. Existing HTBW knowledge

Truth current/historical Home Facts, Foundation Person context, eligible Identity, Stewardship care
and existing Explainability. Home Steward does not know or prescribe medication effects.

## 3. Authorized private retrieval

Potential Person-authorized medication-change date/report, activity/sleep/device observations and
history. Prescription/care sources each need separate scope; family/caregiver role is not permission.

## 4. Optional public research

Device terminology or separately authoritative professional statements may supply attributed context.
No private prescription/care data enters public queries; no medication-effect research is treated as diagnosis.

## 5. Home Steward synthesis

Identify timing, observed changes, unchanged measures and evidence gaps over explicitly selected
personal periods. Temporal relationship is not causation. Do not say "the medication caused this":
that would require separately authoritative medical evidence, not an inference from these observations.
No independent causal/clinical assessment or advice to change medication is supplied.

## 6. Provenance

Medication-change timing remains a sourced household/professional report; wearable/Home measurements,
baseline/recent coverage, dates/quality and inference limits remain separately identified.

## 7. Candidate relationships

Before/after associations remain tentative, not medical causation, accepted effects or new care records.

## 8. Household confirmation

Clarify Person/dates/context; confirming timing does not authorize prescribing, additional sources
or disclosure. Future confirmation/retention is an OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION.

## 9. Governed durable relationship

OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION: no medication/effect model, care proposal object or diagnostic schema.

## 10. Proposed actions

The household may prepare observations/questions for independently authorized professional review.
Transmission, caregiver notification and handoff remain OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTIONS.

## 11. Prohibited autonomous actions

No diagnosis, medication-effect/medical-causation inference, treatment/prescription change, disease/fall
prediction, clinical monitoring, emergency decision, automatic caregiver notification or care/policy change.

## 12. HTBW authority

Truth current Home state/history; Stewardship care obligations; Identity context; Foundation definitions;
Operational Trust action/disclosure; Continuity where applicable; Concierge and existing Explainability/Trace.

## 13. Privacy and locality

Person-scoped prescription/care evidence remains separately governed; ingestion is not disclosure.
Local derivatives inherit sensitivity/lifecycle; no private medication details exposed through public/shared output.

## 14. Unavailable / degraded behavior

"I don't know whether observations changed" is valid; missing data is not proof of medication safety/
effect. Native HTBW/emergency paths stay independent; no LLM critical path or cloud fallback.

## 15. Acceptance expectations

Timing/changes/unchanged measures/gaps captured, with strict no-causal-inference boundary.
No medical algorithm, caregiver/clinical workflow architecture or runtime acceptance.

## 16. Open questions

Authorized timing/comparison sources, retention, human review and disclosure/handoff remain OPEN
ARCHITECTURAL QUESTIONS; no medication or wearable ingestion/diagnostic mechanism.
