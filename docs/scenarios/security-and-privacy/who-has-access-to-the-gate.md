# Who has access to the gate?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

Who has access to the gate?

## Why it matters

Gate access can involve different people, services and temporary purposes. The household wants to understand the currently recorded scope of access without confusing a familiar name, credential possession or prior entry with present permission. A historical successful entry may say nothing about the person's current eligibility.
