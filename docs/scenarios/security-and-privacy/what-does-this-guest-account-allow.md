Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

What does this guest account allow?

## Why it matters

A guest account can mean different things across rooms, information sources and controls. The household wants to understand its recorded purpose, audience and time limits without relying on the reassuring word guest. Account existence alone should not imply access to private knowledge, camera recordings or unrelated household capabilities.
