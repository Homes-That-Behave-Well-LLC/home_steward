# What household information could this integration correlate?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

What household information could this integration correlate?

## Why it matters

An integration may receive several ordinary data categories that become revealing when combined. The household wants to understand plausible linkage risks without building the very personal profile it is concerned about. A theoretical possibility should be labeled as such, distinct from evidence that a service actually made or disclosed the correlation.
