# Evidence Review and Challenge

Navigation and scenario-capture guidance only; not architecture, a roadmap or delivered functionality. Browse the [scenario-first map](../README.md).

## Purpose

Inspect attributable evidence, alternatives, uncertainty and correction scope. Challenging a derived association differs from amending authoritative HTBW Truth; inspectability does not require an evidence-graph interface.

## Relationship to HTBW

Foundation describes structure, not current Truth; Stewardship owns household-declared significance, care, obligations and accepted Custody; Identity supplies Person/context, not authentication or authorization; Truth owns current/historical Facts; Continuity retains accepted preference/session/experience responsibilities; Operational Trust determines permission/disclosure for this request/context; Concierge retains governed orchestration and Trace, not centralized ownership or acceptance. Native Explainability already includes historical/time/cross-Room retrieval. Home Assistant/devices retain execution.

See the accepted-owner [HTBW boundary](../../architecture/htbw-boundary.md), [source authorization](../../architecture/source-authorization.md) and [derived lifecycle](../../architecture/derived-knowledge-lifecycle.md).

## Boundaries

NOTICE != DIAGNOSE; NOTICE != DISCLOSE; NOTICE != RETAIN; NOTICE != RECOMMEND; NOTICE != ACT. Noticing is not authority or automatic Truth, household significance, notification or action.

Potential evidence is not admitted access. Person/source/purpose/namespace/audience gates remain independent; one Person's grant is not everyone's access and cross-person grants do not merge. The most restrictive applicable source constraints remain binding. Summaries can disclose their sources; conclusions are not automatically safe to share. USE != INGEST != DERIVE != RETAIN. Derived information remains source-bound through correction, retention, expiry, deletion and revocation within the household-controlled network boundary. Household-configured ordinary retention may be zero/disabled; no numeric minimum or maximum is imposed. Confirmation alone does not grant retention, disclosure, policy or action authority. No shadow history or silent cloud/source fallback.

Observation, source report, comparison and inference are not automatic Truth. Correlation is not causation; unknown is valid. Issued command != observed physical result. People and Pets are not Assets; Pet/device location does not flow through human Identity. No diagnosis, prognosis, treatment or medical causation follows from changed observations. No medical, life-safety or emergency path depends on an LLM; native/deterministic/manual safety remains independent.

All future source eligibility, meaningful-change review, retention, correction, disclosure, notification and action questions remain OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTIONS. No runtime, scheduler, camera pipeline, learning system, hook, consent engine, platform qualification or acceptance mechanism is designed here.

## Primary scenarios

Primary files: **0 developed + 23 Vision = 23**. Existing coverage and aliases do not add primary files or change target maturity.

- [Show me the evidence behind that conclusion.](show-me-the-evidence-behind-that-conclusion.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A convincing conclusion is difficult to trust without knowing its attributable support. The household wants inspectable eligible evidence and stated limits, not an invented model rationale or disclosure of protected documents merely because they helped produce a previously permitted answer.
- [Which sources contributed?](which-sources-contributed.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Several sources may be mentioned in an answer without each supporting its main claim. The household wants actual attributable contributions distinguished from background context and unused material, so a long citation list does not create an illusion of independent corroboration.
- [Which source contributed most?](which-source-contributed-most.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A household may ask which source mattered most when evidence conflicts. The useful outcome is a bounded explanation of directly attributable support and limitations, not invented numerical influence, model confidence arithmetic or a false claim that a particular document caused an answer.
- [What evidence did you reject?](what-evidence-did-you-reject.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A conclusion may omit evidence because it was inapplicable, contradictory or unauthorized for the purpose. The household wants eligible reasons distinguished without revealing protected source existence, so rejected evidence is not silently discarded or portrayed as false merely because it was not used.
- [Which evidence is stale?](which-evidence-is-stale.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. An answer can rely on evidence whose effective period has ended or whose freshness is unknown. The household wants those limits attached to the relevant claims, rather than treating retrieval time as source currency or using a newer copy to imply verified accuracy.
- [What was observed?](what-was-observed.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A household may need to separate visible or reported events from interpretation in an answer. The useful outcome is an attributable account of what the permitted evidence actually observed, with timestamps and gaps, rather than a fluent narrative presenting inferred outcomes as physical Facts.
- [What was inferred?](what-was-inferred.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Interpretation can help connect evidence while remaining uncertain. The household wants those inferred claims explicitly labeled and contrasted with observations, so an explanatory story does not become a diagnosis, accepted relationship or historical Fact simply because it sounds internally consistent.
- [Which person authorized each source?](which-person-authorized-each-source.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Evidence can be present locally without the requester knowing whose grant applies. The household wants permitted authorization context understood at the right scope, but identifying source owners can itself be sensitive and must not leak protected source existence or private relationships.
- [What changes if this source is removed?](what-changes-if-this-source-is-removed.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Removing a source may leave a conclusion weaker, different or unsupported. The household wants that dependency understood without continuing to quote revoked evidence or inventing a replacement justification, because a cached narrative cannot legitimately substitute for lost source eligibility.
- [Show me competing interpretations.](show-me-competing-interpretations.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Competing interpretations may fit the same incomplete evidence. The household wants them presented as bounded possibilities with contradictory support, rather than a model choosing a satisfying winner or ranking causes numerically when the source cannot discriminate among them.
- [Correct this conclusion.](correct-this-conclusion.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A household may dispute an answer without knowing the exact corrected version. The useful outcome is an attributable, scoped correction question that preserves original source claims and affected audiences, rather than silently replacing the conclusion or treating disagreement as authority to edit Truth.
- [Do not use this source for this purpose again.](do-not-use-this-source-for-this-purpose-again.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A source may remain authorized generally while no longer being acceptable for a particular purpose. The household wants that distinction respected, without a model treating a refusal as permission to delete the source, change another person's grant or find a substitute route to the same private information.
- [No, those are the other person's AirPods.](no-those-are-the-other-person-s-airpods.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Similar AirPods can belong to different people even when a visual match looks convincing. The household wants its scoped correction treated as attributable evidence, not a new ownership Fact, disclosure of another person's records or automatic learning that spreads beyond this event.
- [That isn't the person you inferred.](that-isn-t-the-person-you-inferred.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A mistaken Person inference can expose information or create a false attribution. The household wants the guess challenged without another guess becoming an identity assertion, enrollment or permission change, because naming the correct person may itself require independent disclosure authority.
- [Do not generalize this correction beyond this event.](do-not-generalize-this-correction-beyond-this-event.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A correction may be valid only for one observation or event. The household wants that narrow intent preserved so Home Steward does not turn it into a general preference, future object rule or cross-person assumption that the source and requester never authorized.
- [Correct this candidate relationship.](correct-this-candidate-relationship.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A candidate association may be wrong while its source evidence remains accurate. The household wants those layers distinguished before review, rather than correction silently deleting the source, changing an authoritative Asset relationship or implying that every related conclusion is now invalid.
- [Show me which conclusions were affected by this correction.](show-me-which-conclusions-were-affected-by-this-correction.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A correction can affect several derived conclusions without touching all their sources. The household wants eligible dependencies and uncertainty visible, so a model does not claim complete propagation, conceal affected private audiences or manufacture an explanation of influence it cannot attribute.
- [Forget the incorrect derived association.](forget-the-incorrect-derived-association.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. An incorrect association should not survive as a hidden substitute after it is rejected. The household wants the scope and source implications understood, while distinguishing forgetting a derived candidate from deleting authoritative originals or revoking another person's independently valid records.
- [Was the source evidence deleted or only the conclusion corrected?](was-the-source-evidence-deleted-or-only-the-conclusion-corrected.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Correcting a conclusion and deleting its source are different lifecycle events. The household wants that distinction inspectable, because an improved answer can coexist with the original evidence and deletion may affect other eligible conclusions that the correction did not address.
- [Who is allowed to correct a shared household fact?](who-is-allowed-to-correct-a-shared-household-fact.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A shared Fact may be disputed by someone who can read it but cannot change it. The household wants native ownership and eligible correction scope understood, so household membership, source access or agreement does not become authority to alter authoritative Truth.
- [Does this correction change authoritative HTBW Truth, or only Home Steward's derived knowledge?](does-this-correction-change-authoritative-htbw-truth-or-only-home-steward-s-derived-knowledge.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A corrected interpretation may leave authoritative Home state unchanged. The household wants that separation explicit, because derived knowledge, source records and HTBW Truth have different owners and a conversational correction must not silently update native Facts or accepted relationships.

## Reference Home discovery increment

Corrections to Office/Den input, speaker/output, Asset placement, manual model or a visual candidate do not silently alter Foundation or Truth. One event, media choice or artwork-related override does not become a preference. Recognized guests can retain guest-level authority under native consent; no encounter creates enrollment. Report scope is not authority to edit or retain; no propagation graph, learning or memory-mutation mechanism is introduced.

New primary files in this increment: **2**. Each is catalogued only in this primary family. All earlier bodies and maturity remain unchanged.

- [This guest should not become a recognized household person.](this-guest-should-not-become-a-recognized-household-person.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A temporary guest can interact successfully without consenting to durable recognition or becoming a household participant. The household wants that stated limit respected rather than an encounter silently creating Identity enrollment, residence or future authority. Conversely, legitimately consented recognition does not require resident privileges; recognition and guest-level permissions can coexist under native governance.
- [Should this correction apply only to this event, this room, this person, or the household?](should-this-correction-apply-only-to-this-event-this-room-this-person-or-the-household.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A correction may describe one event, a Room relationship, a Person's intent or a genuinely household-scoped claim. The household wants the contributor's intended scope and authority distinguished before the report influences another answer. A broad wording choice cannot authorize changes to other people's sources, native preferences or shared Truth.
