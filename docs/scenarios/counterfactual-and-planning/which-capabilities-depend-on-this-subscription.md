# Which capabilities depend on this subscription?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

Which capabilities depend on this subscription?

## Why it matters

A subscription may support one outcome directly and merely enrich another. The household wants to understand actual recorded dependence before considering cancellation, rather than having a subscription name stand in for a complete capability map or authorization to end a service.

## Existing HTBW relationship

HTBW owns actual declared capabilities, dependencies, Asset relationships, governed configuration, obligations and Truth. A hypothetical state is not current Truth, an approved configuration, or acceptance of a change. Home Assistant and specialists retain their existing roles.

## Possible Home Steward extension

Explain known subscription-to-capability relationships, current applicable terms and missing dependence evidence. Any new source admission, representation, handoff or implementation is an OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION; no mechanism is specified.

## Potential evidence

- Declared subscription and service references
- Permitted applicable service terms
- Authorized capability requirements

These are possible categories only, not admitted sources. Each requires independent Person/source/purpose/audience scope; derived information inherits sensitivity, household-controlled locality and source lifecycle.

## Important boundary

Reason only from known dependencies and authorized evidence; do not claim perfect prediction or invent missing relationships. No hypothetical question authorizes removal, relocation, revocation, purchase or configuration change. Manual alternatives and independent life-safety paths must remain distinct. Source reports and inference are not automatic Truth; unknown is a valid result. No silent cloud or source fallback.
