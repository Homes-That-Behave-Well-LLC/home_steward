# Did the arriving person go only where expected?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

Did the arriving person go only where expected?

## Why it matters

An authorized household member may ask whether a visit remained within its expected areas. A few sightings cannot establish every place someone went, and a permitted route is not proof of an observed route. The useful outcome is bounded evidence and explicit unseen intervals, not general visitor tracking or a judgment about the person's trustworthiness.
