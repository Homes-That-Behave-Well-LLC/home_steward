## Why it matters

A person claiming the HVAC appointment may know a real event without being its authorized participant. The household wants the claim, scheduled purpose and expected time compared as separate evidence, not a reassuring story that converts a matching appointment into authentication. The same comparison applies when a visitor's timing or stated purpose differs from an eligible household event.
