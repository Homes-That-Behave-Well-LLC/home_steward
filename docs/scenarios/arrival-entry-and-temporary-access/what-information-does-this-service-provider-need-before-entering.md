## Why it matters

Someone arriving for work may need a narrow explanation of the task, equipment or permitted route before admission is even considered. The household wants useful information separated from private occupancy, travel, financial and unrelated Asset details. Both what may be explained and what must remain undisclosed depend on the specific visit and audience, not professional appearance or a plausible purpose.
