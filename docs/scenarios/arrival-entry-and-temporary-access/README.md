# Arrival, Entry and Temporary Access

Navigation and scenario-capture guidance only; not architecture, a roadmap or delivered functionality. Browse the [scenario-first map](../README.md).

## Purpose

Understand expected events, arrival claims, purpose-limited access questions, visit activity, completion and expiry evidence without independently admitting anyone.

## Relationship to HTBW

Foundation owns declared definitions and configured relationships; Stewardship owns household-declared significance, care, obligations and Custody; Identity supplies purpose-specific Person context, not authentication or authorization; Truth owns qualified current and historical Facts; Continuity owns accepted preferences and experience intent; Operational Trust determines permission and disclosure for this request, in this context; Concierge retains governed orchestration and authoritative Decision Trace composition. Native Explainability remains HTBW-owned. Home Assistant and devices retain execution. Home Steward does not require the standalone predecessor Asset Intelligence integration.

See the [HTBW boundary](../../architecture/htbw-boundary.md), [source authorization](../../architecture/source-authorization.md) and [derived lifecycle](../../architecture/derived-knowledge-lifecycle.md).

## Boundaries

Evidence categories are not source admission. Person/source/purpose/namespace/audience authorization remains independent before retrieval, combination and disclosure. One Person's grant is not another's; summaries retain the most restrictive applicable source constraints. USE != INGEST != DERIVE != RETAIN. Source dates, uncertainty, correction, retention, expiry, deletion and revocation remain binding within the household-controlled network boundary; ordinary retention may be zero/disabled. Confirmation does not grant authority to retain, disclose or act. No shadow history or silent cloud/source fallback.

Observation, report and inference are not automatic Truth. Correlation is not causation; unknown is valid. Issued command != observed physical result. People and Pets are not Assets. No medical, life-safety or emergency path depends on Home Steward or an LLM. Scenario capture only: no runtime, hook, interface, schema, handler, pipeline, scheduling, access decision or configuration change is specified or authorized.

## Primary scenarios

Primary files: **0 developed + 8 Vision = 8**. Existing coverage and aliases do not add primary files or change target maturity.

EXPECTED EVENT -> ARRIVAL -> CONTEXT -> PURPOSE-LIMITED ACCESS -> ACTIVITY -> PURPOSE COMPLETION -> ACCESS EXPIRATION -> EVIDENCE REVIEW is a discovery lens, not an event/schema or access workflow. Expected visitor, authenticated visitor and permitted visit remain distinct. Person movement/visit history needs its separately governed native historical scope; a visit authorizes no general camera history. Home Steward never independently admits, unlocks, opens a gate, issues or extends access.

New primary files in this increment: **8**. Each is catalogued only in this primary family. All earlier bodies and maturity remain unchanged.

- [Who or what is expected to arrive today?](who-or-what-is-expected-to-arrive-today.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. An expected arrival may be a Person, delivery or service event, each recorded for a different purpose. The household wants a bounded account of today's eligible commitments, including changes and uncertain timing, without exposing everyone's calendar or treating an expectation as proof that someone will arrive.
- [The person at the gate says they are here for the HVAC appointment. What do we know?](the-person-at-the-gate-says-they-are-here-for-the-hvac-appointment-what-do-we-know.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A person claiming the HVAC appointment may know a real event without being its authorized participant. The household wants the claim, scheduled purpose and expected time compared as separate evidence, not a reassuring story that converts a matching appointment into authentication. The same comparison applies when a visitor's timing or stated purpose differs from an eligible household event.
- [What information does this service provider need before entering?](what-information-does-this-service-provider-need-before-entering.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Someone arriving for work may need a narrow explanation of the task, equipment or permitted route before admission is even considered. The household wants useful information separated from private occupancy, travel, financial and unrelated Asset details. Both what may be explained and what must remain undisclosed depend on the specific visit and audience, not professional appearance or a plausible purpose.
- [What temporary access may be appropriate for this visit?](what-temporary-access-may-be-appropriate-for-this-visit.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A visit can require access to one task or area without access to the entire Home. The household wants to understand how declared purpose, duration and exclusions relate to existing permissions, while distinguishing a possible mismatch from an approved grant. A visit lasting longer than expected must not silently extend its authority.
- [Did the arriving person go only where expected?](did-the-arriving-person-go-only-where-expected.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. An authorized household member may ask whether a visit remained within its expected areas. A few sightings cannot establish every place someone went, and a permitted route is not proof of an observed route. The useful outcome is bounded evidence and explicit unseen intervals, not general visitor tracking or a judgment about the person's trustworthiness.
- [What should happen differently when a household member returns after an extended absence?](what-should-happen-differently-when-a-household-member-returns-after-an-extended-absence.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Returning after an extended absence can make the familiar entry experience inappropriate: care questions, changed Room context or a private briefing may matter more than routine announcements. The household wants existing accepted return intent and current evidence understood, without assuming travel dates prove presence or that an old preference still authorizes today's behavior.
- [What evidence exists that the purpose of the visit was completed?](what-evidence-exists-that-the-purpose-of-the-visit-was-completed.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A visit ending does not prove its purpose was fulfilled. A technician may report work, an invoice may list it and a device may respond, yet the agreed result or required care evidence can remain unverified. The household wants these claims and missing confirmation distinguished before anyone treats work as completed.
- [What remains unknown about the visit?](what-remains-unknown-about-the-visit.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Arrival records can establish different fragments of a visit while leaving identity, route, purpose fulfillment or effective access expiry unknown. The household wants those gaps named at the relevant lifecycle stage rather than filled from a coherent narrative. Missing coverage is especially important when the explanation could disclose another person's activity.
