# What evidence exists that the purpose of the visit was completed?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

What evidence exists that the purpose of the visit was completed?

Related wording covered by this primary, without another file:

- What evidence would confirm that the work was completed?

## Why it matters

A visit ending does not prove its purpose was fulfilled. A technician may report work, an invoice may list it and a device may respond, yet the agreed result or required care evidence can remain unverified. The household wants these claims and missing confirmation distinguished before anyone treats work as completed.
