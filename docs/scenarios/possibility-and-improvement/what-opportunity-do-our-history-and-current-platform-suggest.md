# What opportunity do our history and current platform suggest

Status: CANONICAL SCENARIO

Developed-content maturity only; not Owner, runtime or architecture acceptance.

> Proposed synthetic broad inquiry; no household profile or actual improvement recommendation.
> Applies [Possibility and Improvement Discovery](../../architecture/possibility-and-improvement-discovery.md).

## 1. Household request

"You know our Home. What automation, capability, or benefit are we not exploring that we should?"
Broad wording does not broaden source, disclosure, Person or action authorization.

## 2. Existing HTBW knowledge

Foundation actual capabilities/participation; declared outcomes, Stewardship care, Continuity eligible
preferences/context, Truth/Historical Facts and authoritative Decision Traces.

## 3. Authorized private retrieval

Minimum explicitly authorized outcomes, capabilities/configuration, traces, logs/history, voice
requests, manual intervention, preferences, Stewardship and debt evidence. Admit each source
independently; no complete behavioral/Person history or unauthorized association.

## 4. Optional public research

Current [official HA capabilities](https://contributors.home-assistant.io/core/) and relevant release/
integration evidence; community popularity is not support. No private Home context in external queries.

## 5. Home Steward synthesis

For **every** recommendation provide:

- **EVIDENCE:** observed eligible household evidence and its limits.
- **OUTCOME:** declared human/household outcome improved, not more automation.
- **CURRENT CAPABILITY:** verified current HA/HTBW support, installed applicability and gaps.
- **NEW OBLIGATION:** maintenance, privacy, vendor/security, learning and support burden.
- **AUTHORITY:** responsible reviewer/owner and required approvals.
- **UNCERTAINTY:** missing/contradictory/stale evidence and hypotheses.
- **REVERSIBILITY:** safe rollback/removal, data/configuration recovery and limits.
- **ACCEPTANCE:** measurable intended outcome, tests and later lived review.

Require the strongest minimization and evidence controls. Do not invent household goals, silently
learn preferences or assume all supported devices should participate. "Nothing worth doing" is valid;
inadequate coverage is "I don't know", not an invented comprehensive recommendation.

## 6. Provenance

Exact authorized references/window/denominator, official version/publication/review/installed match,
GA/Labs/preview/deprecation/removal and evidence quality. Pattern/correlation/inference is not Truth.

## 7. Candidate relationships

Possible outcome/capability fit stays a candidate, not consent, accepted intent, autonomous policy
or new Fact. No persistent Pattern/opportunity store.

## 8. Household confirmation

An eligible participant confirms current outcome and constraints; this is maturity evidence only.
It does not authorize implementation or bypass the stronger learned-policy acceptance path.

## 9. Governed durable relationship

Responsible owner interfaces govern later approved requirements/preferences/permissions/obligations.
Home Steward's draft is consultation; Concierge formulates authoritative suggestions.

## 10. Proposed actions

Return a complete outcome/requirements or experimental proposal through HTBW/Operational Trust with
manual control, exceptions, new obligations, reversibility and exact-version acceptance.

## 11. Prohibited autonomous actions

No automation/scripts, policy/preference/configuration/obligation changes, entity/integration/Labs
enablement, HA update/rollback, procurement, code generation or upstream contribution.

## 12. HTBW authority

Foundation definitions; Stewardship declared value; Truth Facts; Identity requester; Continuity
context; Operational Trust permission; Concierge governed formulation/orchestration/Trace.

## 13. Privacy and locality

Separate source/Person/purpose/audience authorization before reasoning; dedicated historical
reconstruction for Person/actor movement. Local derivations inherit lifecycle, not profiling permission.

## 14. Unavailable / degraded behavior

Missing/aged-out/denied evidence or unverifiable current capability limits advice. State uncertainty,
preserve native Home behavior and never fall back to unapproved sources/cloud reasoning.

## 15. Acceptance expectations

All eight safeguards per recommendation; scope never broadens from conversational wording.
No-action valid; no hypothesis, manual pattern or household interest becomes authority or implementation.

## 16. Open questions

Source/grant scope, benefit evidence, attribution and governed proposal review remain future design;
no recommendation engine, repository/log/voice ingestion or autonomous change path.
