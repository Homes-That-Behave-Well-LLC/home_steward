# What voice requests reveal a missing capability

Status: CANONICAL SCENARIO

Developed-content maturity only; not Owner, runtime or architecture acceptance.

> Proposed synthetic scenario; no recorded voice history, transcript corpus or provider diagnosis.
> Applies [Possibility and Improvement Discovery](../../architecture/possibility-and-improvement-discovery.md).

## 1. Household request

"What are we repeatedly asking the Home to do that it cannot currently do?" Resolve Person/purpose,
eligible request evidence and what failure actually means.

## 2. Existing HTBW knowledge

Foundation vocabulary/participation/function assignments; Identity effective requester; Continuity
session/transfer intent; Truth availability; authoritative Decision Traces/OT refusal evidence.

## 3. Authorized private retrieval

Only separately authorized minimized voice-request text/category, eligible outcomes and context.
Capture, retention, attribution and retrieval are independent; no raw audio/biometrics by default,
no assumption an installed microphone grants voice-history access.

## 4. Optional public research

Use [official Conversation documentation](https://www.home-assistant.io/integrations/conversation/)
and exact provider/integration documentation. It proves neither universal transfer nor source retention.
No private utterances, identities or household context sent outward.

## 5. Home Steward synthesis

Distinguish speech recognition, vocabulary, room-context resolution, person-context resolution,
missing capability, unavailable capability, Operational Trust refusal, ambiguous request,
unsupported provider, temporary failure and deliberate prohibition.

"Take this to the Den" might identify an unsupported configured media-session transfer, not phrase
recognition. But HTBW already owns transfer/session governance: first check the accepted native
Continuity path, referent, configuration, provider support, context and permission. Do not falsely
declare all media transfer missing. Repeated requests need not be supported where intentionally
prohibited or unsuitable. Give expected outcome, evidence/uncertainty and new obligation.

## 6. Provenance

Eligible request/outcome/context references, counts/denominator/windows and attribution limits;
official source/version/publication/review, installed applicability and GA/Labs/preview/deprecation.
Failure hypotheses/patterns are not Truth, identity or permission.

## 7. Candidate relationships

Possible request-to-unmet-outcome mapping is a candidate, not accepted intent, Fact or provider
selection. A refusal never justifies lowering policy or inventing a capability.

## 8. Household confirmation

Confirm the actual requested outcome and present interest with an eligible participant.
Confirmation is evidence, not permission to expose entities, relax OT or change provider.

## 9. Governed durable relationship

Foundation vocabulary/capability, Continuity intent and OT permission remain separately owned.
No request corpus, persistent Pattern, new Person profile or silent preference write.

## 10. Proposed actions

Propose scoped context/support review or a complete draft through HTBW/Operational Trust, including
manual control, exceptions, obligation, reversibility and acceptance evidence.

## 11. Prohibited autonomous actions

No automation/scripts, vocabulary/configuration/preference/policy/obligation changes, provider
substitution, HA update/rollback, Labs/integration/entity enablement or autonomous upstream work.

## 12. HTBW authority

Foundation definitions; Identity evidence; Truth Facts; Continuity sessions; Stewardship care;
Operational Trust request-specific authority; Concierge interpretation/formulation/Trace.

## 13. Privacy and locality

Separate Person/source/purpose/audience scope; minimize sensitive request text before local reasoning.
Biometric locality/consent stays unchanged; no indefinite raw-request memory or movement profiling.

## 14. Unavailable / degraded behavior

No eligible history, missing outcomes or ambiguous attribution yields "I don't know why these requests
were unfulfilled". Native interaction continues; no invented counts or unauthorized fallback.

## 15. Acceptance expectations

All eleven failure classes distinguish capability gaps from recognition/context/authorization.
The transfer example is conditional, refusals stay respected and no request becomes automatic permission.

## 16. Open questions

Future voice-evidence capture/minimization, outcome semantics, retention and source/provider proof
remain open; no voice/log ingestion, diagnosis engine or new runtime capability.
