# Which device capabilities are not participating

Status: CANONICAL SCENARIO

Developed-content maturity only; not Owner, runtime or architecture acceptance.

> Proposed synthetic scenario; no actual device inventory/exposure or enabled capability.
> Applies [Possibility and Improvement Discovery](../../architecture/possibility-and-improvement-discovery.md).

## 1. Household request

"What can our existing devices do that we are not currently using?" Review eligible capabilities
against declared participation and household outcomes, not a count of unused features.

## 2. Existing HTBW knowledge

Foundation device/capability/Participation/Deliberate Exclusion and Interaction Function Assignment;
Truth qualified availability; Stewardship declared care; native Decision Traces.

## 3. Authorized private retrieval

Minimum approved device/model/entity semantics, participation/assignment records and declared goals.
No automatic network discovery, diagnostics exposure or private activity history.

## 4. Optional public research

Official exact-model/integration documentation verifies supported sensors, buttons, media,
diagnostics, energy data or notification features. [HA developer documentation](https://contributors.home-assistant.io/core/)
is first authority for platform mechanics; no household identifiers in queries.

## 5. Home Steward synthesis

Preserve **Capability != Participation != Interaction Function Assignment != Household Intent**.
A speaker's technical function does not assign music/spoken response, nor does an unused sensor
authorize contribution. Native hidden/disabled/exposure flags alone do not establish HTBW intent.

For every recommendation ask "What household outcome would this improve?" Consider privacy,
designated-use exclusions, availability, maintenance and credential/support burden. A supported
feature without an evidenced valued outcome receives "No action recommended", not default enablement.

## 6. Provenance

Exact model/integration/configuration references, source/publication/review/effective/installed version,
GA/Labs/preview/deprecation and missing support evidence. Capability claims are not current Truth Facts.

## 7. Candidate relationships

Possible outcome-to-capability fit is a candidate, not participation, an endpoint assignment,
current availability or consent. Do not infer intent from absence of use.

## 8. Household confirmation

An eligible participant confirms desired outcome and exclusions; this is evidence, not authority
to expose an entity, change function assignment or activate diagnostics.

## 9. Governed durable relationship

Foundation governs approved participation/configuration; Truth owns current evidence. Existing
owner paths decide changes; no autonomous relationship/Pattern store.

## 10. Proposed actions

Offer scoped review/draft through HTBW/Operational Trust with expected benefit, obligation, exceptions,
manual control, failure handling, reversibility and acceptance tests.

## 11. Prohibited autonomous actions

No participation/assignment/exposure edits, automation/scripts, configuration/policy/preference/
obligation changes, integration/Labs enablement, HA update/rollback or upstream contribution.

## 12. HTBW authority

Foundation definitions; Truth availability/Facts; Stewardship care; Identity requester; Continuity
intent; Operational Trust permission; Concierge formulation/coordination/Trace.

## 13. Privacy and locality

Minimum source/Person/purpose/audience scopes before local synthesis; diagnostic usefulness is not
consent. Derived results inherit lifecycle and never create people-performance profiles.

## 14. Unavailable / degraded behavior

Unknown model/support or missing participation rationale yields "I don't know whether this should
participate". Preserve existing functions and exclusions; no guessed enablement.

## 15. Acceptance expectations

All four concepts distinct; valued outcome required; exclusions/unknown availability respected.
No-action valid and no capability automatically participates or gains authority.

## 16. Open questions

Capability evidence, outcome relevance and governed assignment review remain open; no device scan,
ingestion, entity activation or runtime data model.
