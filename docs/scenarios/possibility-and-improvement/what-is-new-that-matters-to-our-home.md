# What is new that matters to our Home

Status: CANONICAL SCENARIO

Developed-content maturity only; not Owner, runtime or architecture acceptance.

> Proposed synthetic scenario; no actual release-to-Reference-Home applicability result.
> Applies [Possibility and Improvement Discovery](../../architecture/possibility-and-improvement-discovery.md).

## 1. Household request

"What did Home Assistant add this month that matters to our Home?" Resolve eligible requester,
actual installed version and proposed release rather than equating the latest website with deployment.

## 2. Existing HTBW knowledge

Foundation configured capabilities/participation/exclusions; Truth qualified current/Historical Facts;
Stewardship declared outcomes/obligations; Continuity eligible context; native Decision Traces.

## 3. Authorized private retrieval

Minimum governed integration/device/version, outcome, requirement and change references. Logs/history
or voice requests require separate grants; release relevance does not authorize a whole-Home scan.

## 4. Optional public research

Technical claims require [official release evidence](https://www.home-assistant.io/blog/2026/11/04/release-202611/)
and relevant official integration/developer documentation, live or an eligible reviewed copy.
No private configuration, identifiers or logs enter public queries.

## 5. Home Steward synthesis

Ignore irrelevant release-note volume. Classify materially relevant findings as newly possible,
newly relevant, newly risky, deprecated, preview/Labs or no action required.
If four findings are supported, say "Four appear relevant", not that every release needs four.

Each item contains capability/change, why it applies, affected household capability, evidence source,
whether action is necessary and whether investigation is recommended. For example, documented
2026.11 target exclusions might support a scoped simplification inquiry only where an authorized
requirement matches; no actual Home match is asserted. Expected household outcome, benefit and new
obligation accompany any candidate. "Nothing in this release creates a meaningful improvement or required action" is valid
only with adequate eligible coverage; missing evidence instead yields uncertainty.

## 6. Provenance

Cite official source/version/publication and review date, installed-version applicability, GA/Labs/
preview/deprecation/removal status, governed references and coverage. Pattern/inference is not Truth.

## 7. Candidate relationships

Release-to-household relevance is an evidenced candidate, not a new Fact, acceptance or authority.
Similar feature names do not establish behavioral fit.

## 8. Household confirmation

Confirm the stated outcome and relevance with an eligible participant; confirmation is maturity
Level 3 evidence, not authorization to implement or an accepted preference.

## 9. Governed durable relationship

Responsible HTBW owners govern any accepted requirement/configuration/obligation relationship.
Home Steward supplies a draft, not an authoritative change or new opportunity store.

## 10. Proposed actions

Offer scoped investigation or a complete proposal through HTBW and Operational Trust. Include
benefit, obligations, manual control, exceptions, reversibility and acceptance evidence.

## 11. Prohibited autonomous actions

No automation/script creation or enablement, configuration/policy/preference/obligation edits,
integration installation, entity exposure, HA update/rollback, Labs enablement or upstream contribution.

## 12. HTBW authority

Foundation model; Truth Facts; Stewardship care; Identity requester; Continuity context;
Operational Trust request-specific permission; Concierge formulation/orchestration/Trace.

## 13. Privacy and locality

Minimized source/Person/purpose/audience scope before reasoning and disclosure. Local derived findings
inherit lifecycle; no indefinite raw-log/request memory, profiling or unauthorized source fallback.

## 14. Unavailable / degraded behavior

Unknown installed version, stale official evidence or denied sources prevents an applicability claim.
Say "I don't know what applies"; preserve native HTBW, not guessed recommendations.

## 15. Acceptance expectations

Six classifications and six per-item fields; official citations, actual applicability and no forced
recommendation. Candidates never implement, change policy or record household acceptance.

## 16. Open questions

Future source admission/review, relevance thresholds, comparison windows and proposal UX remain open;
no release watcher, feed, crawling or ingestion is implemented.
