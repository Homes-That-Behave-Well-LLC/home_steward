# What public idea is worth testing here

Status: CANONICAL SCENARIO

Developed-content maturity only; not Owner, runtime or architecture acceptance.

> Proposed synthetic inspiration review; no adopted community idea or live experiment.
> Applies [Possibility and Improvement Discovery](../../architecture/possibility-and-improvement-discovery.md).

## 1. Household request

"Are there ideas other Home Assistant users are using that might actually help us?" Resolve declared
outcome and eligible inspiration scope, not popularity-driven adoption.

## 2. Existing HTBW knowledge

Foundation actual capabilities/exclusions; Stewardship declared goals/obligations; Truth Facts;
Continuity context; native Decision Traces and accepted requirements.

## 3. Authorized private retrieval

Minimum outcome/configuration/support/acceptance evidence. Logs/history, requests and feedback are
separately governed; no private Home profile is sent to community sources.

## 4. Optional public research

Approved public/community material supplies **INSPIRATION**. Technical support requires
**AUTHORITATIVE PLATFORM EVIDENCE** from official HA release/developer/integration documentation.
The [2026.11 Marketplace announcement](https://www.home-assistant.io/blog/2026/11/04/release-202611/)
does not make community projects HA-audited/supported merely by listing them.

## 5. Home Steward synthesis

Extract a candidate idea, then require official technical verification, household-outcome fit,
privacy review, Operational Trust review, support obligation and Reference Home experimental
classification. Similar API names, stars, videos and a successful demo do not establish support here.

Define intended outcome, expected benefit/new burden, safety/degradation, manual control, exceptions,
rollback/removal and acceptance criteria before a separately authorized experiment. Incomplete
official evidence means unresolved and no migration recommendation. "No action recommended" is valid;
novelty or community popularity is not a household value.

## 6. Provenance

Community origin/date versus official source/version/publication/review/installed match and
GA/Labs/preview/deprecation/removal status. Label inference, gaps and experiment classification.
An idea/pattern is not Truth or acceptance.

## 7. Candidate relationships

Possible idea-to-outcome fit remains a candidate; it does not create a household preference,
participation, capability authority or persisted Pattern.

## 8. Household confirmation

Confirm interest/outcome with an eligible participant; this is evidence, not authorization to
install community code, enable Labs or run an experiment.

## 9. Governed durable relationship

Responsible owners evaluate requirements/configuration/care/permission records. No automatic
adoption, trusted-source status, authoritative write or new acceptance store.

## 10. Proposed actions

Offer an officially verified, scoped experimental review through HTBW/Operational Trust, with
maintainer/new obligation, reversibility, acceptance tests and later lived-outcome review.

## 11. Prohibited autonomous actions

No community code/automation/script generation or install, configuration/policy/preference/obligation
change, integration/entity/Labs enablement, HA update/rollback or autonomous upstream contribution.

## 12. HTBW authority

Foundation model; Truth Facts; Stewardship care; Identity requester; Continuity context;
Operational Trust review/permission; Concierge formulation/coordination/Trace.

## 13. Privacy and locality

Public queries use approved nonsensitive terms, never private requests/logs/configuration.
Local derived interpretations inherit source/lifecycle/audience limits; no broad profiling.

## 14. Unavailable / degraded behavior

Unavailable official proof, unknown installed support or denied context yields "I don't know whether
this belongs here". Preserve native operation; no popularity-based or cloud fallback.

## 15. Acceptance expectations

Inspiration versus authoritative proof explicit; all six reviews plus experimental classification.
No unsupported adoption or executed experiment; no-action and evidence-insufficient outcomes valid.

## 16. Open questions

Approved inspiration/source scope, support evidence and experiment governance remain future work;
no web crawler/feed, code ingestion, recommendation runtime or testing process.
