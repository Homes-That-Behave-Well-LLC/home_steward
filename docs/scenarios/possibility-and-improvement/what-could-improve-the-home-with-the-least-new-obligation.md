# What could improve the Home with the least new obligation

Status: CANONICAL SCENARIO

Developed-content maturity only; not Owner, runtime or architecture acceptance.

> Proposed synthetic comparison; no actual cost/benefit score or household value ranking.
> Applies [obligation-aware discovery](../../architecture/possibility-and-improvement-discovery.md#obligation-aware-evaluation-and-technical-debt).

## 1. Household request

"What could make the biggest improvement without adding a lot more complexity?" Resolve declared
outcome/beneficiary and what additional obligation the household is willing to review.

## 2. Existing HTBW knowledge

Foundation capabilities/configuration; Stewardship declared significance/care; Truth qualified
Facts; Continuity context; native Decision Traces and eligible existing requirement evidence.

## 3. Authorized private retrieval

Minimum eligible outcomes/friction, maintenance/support evidence and configuration/dependency records.
Logs, requests, finances or Person feedback each require separate authorization.

## 4. Optional public research

Official feature/integration documentation, beginning with [HA documentation](https://www.home-assistant.io/docs/automation/),
verifies actual support/dependencies. No private costs, schedules or records sent outward.

## 5. Home Steward synthesis

Apply Behaves Well governance: compare household outcome impact, implementation complexity, privacy,
vendor/cloud dependence, maintenance, credential burden, support obligation, failure modes,
recoverability, reversibility, Technical Debt, household learning and measurable benefit.

Prefer meaningful improvement with the lowest justified additional obligation, not automation count,
AI usage, novelty or device count. Benefits are bounded estimates until lived evidence supports them;
unknown burden is not zero. Stewardship represents household values, not a model-authored hierarchy.
Technical Debt is an assessment lens, not a new canonical framework/store. Doing nothing or preserving
a simple manual/native path may be preferable to a new integration.

## 6. Provenance

Declared outcome and authorized evidence/version/window, official publication/review/effective/
installed version and GA/Labs/preview/deprecation; assumptions, uncertainty and nonnumeric comparisons.
Projected benefit/pattern is not Truth or acceptance.

## 7. Candidate relationships

Possible improvement-to-outcome association stays a candidate; no universal model score ranks
people, assigns significance or changes obligations.

## 8. Household confirmation

Confirm outcome and acceptable burden through eligible review; this is maturity evidence, not
permission to install, pay, change policy or accept an implementation.

## 9. Governed durable relationship

Responsible owners govern approved configuration, significance, care and permissions. No separate
Technical Debt/benefit registry, persisted Pattern or silent preference.

## 10. Proposed actions

Offer a complete HTBW/Operational Trust proposal with expected outcome, new obligation/maintainer,
manual control, exceptions, rollback/removal and measurable acceptance/lived review.

## 11. Prohibited autonomous actions

No automation/scripts, configuration/policy/preference/obligation edits, procurement/credential
activation, HA update/rollback, Labs/entity/integration enablement or autonomous upstream work.

## 12. HTBW authority

Stewardship declared value/care; Foundation structure; Truth Facts; Identity requester; Continuity
context; Operational Trust permission; Concierge formulation/orchestration/Trace.

## 13. Privacy and locality

Minimum authorized source/Person/beneficiary/audience scope; local derivations inherit sensitivity/
lifecycle. No broad behavioral profiling or private data in public comparison queries.

## 14. Unavailable / degraded behavior

Missing benefit/support evidence yields "I don't know which option is worth the obligation".
Preserve native operation; no forced score, recommendation or cloud fallback.

## 15. Acceptance expectations

All thirteen evaluation factors visible, benefit measurable, unknown burden explicit and no
optimization for automation/AI/novelty/device count. Review never performs implementation.

## 16. Open questions

Eligible benefit metrics, burden evidence and household review criteria remain future design;
no recommendation engine, TCO implementation, crawler or ingestion.
