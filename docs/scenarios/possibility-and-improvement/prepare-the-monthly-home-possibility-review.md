# Prepare the monthly Home Possibility Review

Status: CANONICAL SCENARIO

Developed-content maturity only; not Owner, runtime or architecture acceptance.

> Proposed synthetic report scenario; no scheduled watcher, actual monthly findings or recommendation.
> Applies [the future Home Possibility Review](../../architecture/possibility-and-improvement-discovery.md#future-home-possibility-review--release-watch).

## 1. Household request

"What should we know this month?" Establish eligible requester, reviewed release interval and
authorized sources; monthly cadence is not permission to interrupt or nag.

## 2. Existing HTBW knowledge

Foundation actual configuration/capabilities; Stewardship declared outcomes/obligations; eligible
Truth/Historical Facts, Continuity context, native Decision Traces and deferred requirement references.

## 3. Authorized private retrieval

Minimum relevant outcome/friction/wish/requirement/configuration evidence. Logs/history, voice requests
and useful dashboard interactions each need separate grants, not a standing whole-Home capture.

## 4. Optional public research

Reviewed [official releases](https://www.home-assistant.io/blog/categories/core/), developer/integration/
Labs documentation and approved related-platform sources; every technical claim has authority.
No private household history, requests or identifiers sent outward.

## 5. Home Steward synthesis

Prepare the conceptual **Home Possibility Review**:

1. Newly possible
2. Newly relevant
3. Newly risky
4. Deprecated or changing
5. Repeated household friction
6. Previously deferred ideas now worth reconsidering
7. Candidate improvements
8. Potential simplification / native replacement
9. Suggested experiments
10. No-action items
11. Evidence gaps
12. Questions for the household

Each externally grounded capability has source/version/date/applicability/status. Each recommendation
has intended outcome, expected benefit/new obligation, reviewer and reversible acceptance plan.
No recommendation is required every month. "No material change" is valid with adequate coverage;
unknown/denied/stale evidence is instead a disclosed gap. Suggested experiments are not executed.

## 6. Provenance

Official publication/review/effective and installed version, GA/Labs/preview/deprecation/removal,
governed references/windows/coverage and inference versus Fact. No uncited capability summaries.

## 7. Candidate relationships

Report candidates are not approved requirements, preferences, permission or household significance.
Recurring friction/patterns are interpretation, not a persisted Pattern or Truth.

## 8. Household confirmation

Confirm selected outcome/relevance through eligible review; accepting receipt or dismissing a report
is not implementation authorization, consent or acceptance.

## 9. Governed durable relationship

Existing owners govern any resulting accepted records. The report is not a central approval queue,
acceptance store or opportunity database; original source histories remain owner-governed.

## 10. Proposed actions

Offer bounded requirements, experiment or native-path review through HTBW/Operational Trust with
manual control, exceptions, obligations, rollback and measurable acceptance/lived review.

## 11. Prohibited autonomous actions

No scheduled report/nudge runtime, automation/scripts, configuration/policy/preference/obligation
changes, HA update/rollback, Labs/entity/integration enablement or upstream contribution.

## 12. HTBW authority

Foundation model; Truth Facts; Stewardship care; Identity requester; Continuity context;
Operational Trust audience/permission; Concierge formulation/communication/Trace, not acceptance.

## 13. Privacy and locality

Minimize approved Person/source/purpose/audience fields before local synthesis/disclosure.
Derived report inherits lifecycle; no indefinite raw-log/voice memory or human profiling.

## 14. Unavailable / degraded behavior

Insufficient authorized coverage yields "I don't know what materially changed". State evidence gaps,
preserve native operation and do not fabricate a monthly recommendation.

## 15. Acceptance expectations

All twelve report sections and capability citations; opportunity/risk/deprecation/preview separate.
No-action and no-material-change legitimate; report neither implements nor records acceptance.

## 16. Open questions

Future admission/review cadence, output audience, retention and experiments remain open; no feed,
crawler, release watcher, voice/log ingestion or report scheduler.
