# What can the Home do now that it could not before

Status: CANONICAL SCENARIO

Developed-content maturity only; not Owner, runtime or architecture acceptance.

> Proposed synthetic scenario; no asserted original design gap or newly enabled capability.
> Applies [Possibility and Improvement Discovery](../../architecture/possibility-and-improvement-discovery.md).

## 1. Household request

"What can the Home do now that it could not do when we first designed this?" Establish the prior
design/version and authorized outcome, not a universal technology-upgrade wish.

## 2. Existing HTBW knowledge

Foundation current and historical configuration/capabilities; owner-held prior requirements/gaps;
Truth Historical Facts; Stewardship declared needs; authoritative Decision Traces.

## 3. Authorized private retrieval

Minimum exact prior/current requirement, design, configuration and version evidence. Logs/history and
Person requests need separate permission; do not infer a historical blocker from missing data.

## 4. Optional public research

Verify current functionality with [official HA documentation](https://www.home-assistant.io/docs/automation/)
and versioned release/integration sources. No private design, schedule or household details in public queries.

## 5. Home Steward synthesis

Compare prior requirements, known gaps, previously unavailable capability, current authoritative platform
functionality and current Home configuration. Identify what changed and whether the actual blocker
is removed, partly removed or unknown. A native target-exclusion mechanism, for example, does not prove
the Home's governed participation/exception requirements are satisfied.

Distinguish **technically possible** from **appropriate for this household**. A supported feature can
still violate privacy, create unacceptable maintenance or provide no valued outcome. Give benefit,
new obligation, constraints and a bounded review candidate; "No action recommended" is valid.

## 6. Provenance

Exact prior/current references, official version/publication/review, installed applicability and
GA/Labs/preview/deprecation/removal status. Separate evidence, pattern and inference from Truth.

## 7. Candidate relationships

Possible requirement-to-capability fit remains a candidate, not an accepted design, preference or Fact.
Technical similarity does not establish household intent or causation.

## 8. Household confirmation

Confirm present outcome relevance through the eligible requester; old interest is not current consent
and current interest is not implementation authorization.

## 9. Governed durable relationship

Foundation/responsible owners govern approved requirement/configuration relationships. No new durable
pattern/possibility registry, rewritten history or automatic adoption.

## 10. Proposed actions

Propose investigation or a governed draft through HTBW/Operational Trust, with manual control,
exceptions, new obligations, rollback/removal and acceptance criteria.

## 11. Prohibited autonomous actions

No automation/script generation/enablement, configuration/policy/preference/obligation change,
integration/entity enablement, HA update/rollback, Labs enablement or autonomous upstream contribution.

## 12. HTBW authority

Foundation definitions; Truth Facts/history; Stewardship care; Identity requester; Continuity context;
Operational Trust permission; Concierge authoritative formulation/coordination/Trace.

## 13. Privacy and locality

Minimum approved source/Person/purpose/audience evidence, local scoped derivations and inherited
retention/deletion. No private query leakage or broad behavioral profiling.

## 14. Unavailable / degraded behavior

Missing/aged-out original requirements or unverified current version yields "I don't know what became
possible". Native capability remains independent of the extension.

## 15. Acceptance expectations

All five comparison inputs present; technical possibility and household suitability stay distinct.
Unknown blockers, rejected governance choices and no-action outcomes remain visible; no implementation.

## 16. Open questions

Original requirement capture, current relevance and version-specific capability comparison remain
future design; no model, ingestion or change executor is introduced.
