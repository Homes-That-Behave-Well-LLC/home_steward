# Local Intelligence Personalization

Navigation and scenario-capture guidance only; not architecture, a roadmap or delivered functionality. Browse the [scenario-first map](../README.md).

## Purpose

Explore inspectable Person-scoped or household-scoped vocabulary, naming, context and improvement expectations while preserving native Continuity. GOVERNED HOUSEHOLD KNOWLEDGE SHOULD NOT BE TRAPPED IN MODEL WEIGHTS.

## Relationship to HTBW

Foundation describes structure, not current Truth; Stewardship owns household-declared significance, care, obligations and accepted Custody; Identity supplies Person/context, not authentication or authorization; Truth owns current/historical Facts; Continuity retains accepted preference/session/experience responsibilities; Operational Trust determines permission/disclosure for this request/context; Concierge retains governed orchestration and Trace, not centralized ownership or acceptance. Native Explainability already includes historical/time/cross-Room retrieval. Home Assistant/devices retain execution.

See the accepted-owner [HTBW boundary](../../architecture/htbw-boundary.md), [source authorization](../../architecture/source-authorization.md) and [derived lifecycle](../../architecture/derived-knowledge-lifecycle.md).

## Boundaries

NOTICE != DIAGNOSE; NOTICE != DISCLOSE; NOTICE != RETAIN; NOTICE != RECOMMEND; NOTICE != ACT. Noticing is not authority or automatic Truth, household significance, notification or action.

Potential evidence is not admitted access. Person/source/purpose/namespace/audience gates remain independent; one Person's grant is not everyone's access and cross-person grants do not merge. The most restrictive applicable source constraints remain binding. Summaries can disclose their sources; conclusions are not automatically safe to share. USE != INGEST != DERIVE != RETAIN. Derived information remains source-bound through correction, retention, expiry, deletion and revocation within the household-controlled network boundary. Household-configured ordinary retention may be zero/disabled; no numeric minimum or maximum is imposed. Confirmation alone does not grant retention, disclosure, policy or action authority. No shadow history or silent cloud/source fallback.

Observation, source report, comparison and inference are not automatic Truth. Correlation is not causation; unknown is valid. Issued command != observed physical result. People and Pets are not Assets; Pet/device location does not flow through human Identity. No diagnosis, prognosis, treatment or medical causation follows from changed observations. No medical, life-safety or emergency path depends on an LLM; native/deterministic/manual safety remains independent.

All future source eligibility, meaningful-change review, retention, correction, disclosure, notification and action questions remain OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTIONS. No runtime, scheduler, camera pipeline, learning system, hook, consent engine, platform qualification or acceptance mechanism is designed here.

GOVERNED HOUSEHOLD KNOWLEDGE SHOULD NOT BE TRAPPED IN MODEL WEIGHTS. Inspectable, reconstructable and exportable knowledge remains governed separately from a replaceable base model. No personalization, fine-tuning, training, model-routing or memory-mutation mechanism is designed.

## Primary scenarios

Primary files: **0 developed + 10 Vision = 10**. Existing coverage and aliases do not add primary files or change target maturity.

- [Does Home Steward understand this household's vocabulary?](does-home-steward-understand-this-household-s-vocabulary.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Household vocabulary may differ from generic labels or from one Person's preferred phrasing. The household wants naming context understood without a model inventing shared definitions, exposing private vocabulary or replacing Foundation's contextual descriptions and Continuity's accepted preference responsibilities.
- [Does it understand Asset naming conventions?](does-it-understand-asset-naming-conventions.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Asset names may reflect family language, formal catalog identifiers or insurer-facing descriptions. The household wants these eligible conventions distinguished, rather than personalization merging Assets, disclosing private nicknames or making an informal name authoritative for ownership, provenance or current location.
- [Can it become better at the types of questions this household asks?](can-it-become-better-at-the-types-of-questions-this-household-asks.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A household may want more useful answers to the questions it actually asks. The useful aspiration is inspectable improvement under eligible source and purpose limits, not silent retention of private prompts, model training or a promise that repeated interaction automatically improves reasoning.
- [What personalization belongs to a person?](what-personalization-belongs-to-a-person.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. One Person's vocabulary, preferred explanation style or question history may be private. The household wants that context kept separate from household adaptation, so a useful personal preference does not become shared behavioral profiling or authority to reuse personal evidence for another requester.
- [What personalization belongs to the household?](what-personalization-belongs-to-the-household.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Household adaptation should reflect explicitly eligible shared context rather than a dominant person's repeated requests. The household wants shared vocabulary and expectations distinguished from personal preferences, because repeated use does not establish consensus, native preference acceptance or permission to expose private contributions.
- [Can personalization be inspected?](can-personalization-be-inspected.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. An adaptation can influence answers without the household knowing its source or scope. The household wants eligible personalized context inspectable alongside correction and expiry limits, not hidden in model weights or revealed to an audience that cannot access the underlying private contribution.
- [Can personalization be corrected?](can-personalization-be-corrected.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Personalized context can be mistaken or become inappropriate when circumstances change. The household wants an attributable correction distinguished from a native preference change or model training, so a conversational repair does not silently reshape future answers beyond its declared scope.
- [Can personalization be reset?](can-personalization-be-reset.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A Person or household may want to stop using some personalized context. The household wants that reset scope distinguished from source deletion, revoked grants and preserved authoritative knowledge, so a reset neither erases valid records nor leaves an undisclosed shadow profile in continued use.
- [Can personalization be exported?](can-personalization-be-exported.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Exportability can make personalized context less dependent on one model or appliance. The household wants the eligible scope, provenance and restrictions preserved, rather than an export request granting access to every person's contributions or making a private context package safe for any destination.
- [What happens when the underlying model changes?](what-happens-when-the-underlying-model-changes.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A different base model may reason differently without owning the household's knowledge. The household wants governed vocabulary, scoped corrections and source lineage to remain inspectable and reconstructable, so replacing or improving a model does not silently erase authority, copy private data or rewrite accepted history.
