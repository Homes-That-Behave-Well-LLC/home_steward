# How do I use this appliance

> Proposed synthetic scenario; no real device/model/manual or executed procedure.

## 1. Household request

"How do I reset the filter reminder?", "What does this red light mean?", "Have we had this problem
before?", "What did the service company say?", and "Can you schedule service?"

## 2. Existing HTBW knowledge

Foundation resolves the exact Asset, manufacturer/model, Room, associated manual and service records.
Truth supplies eligible fault/current-state evidence; Stewardship supplies obligations/schedule.
Native display of the associated manual is possible without Home Steward.

## 3. Authorized private retrieval

Only the exact model/revision's approved manual, service invoice/report and applicable correspondence.
No unrelated mailbox or complete NAS scan. A missing model/manual association must be surfaced.

## 4. Optional public research

Approved official manufacturer manual/service bulletin for the verified model. Do not send account,
address, private fault log or service records. Generic web instructions are not an exact procedure.

## 5. Home Steward synthesis

Cite the model-specific filter-reminder/reset steps **only if actually present in approved evidence**.
Interpret red-light meaning from exact documentation and applicable observed state; ask for missing
information rather than guessing. Distinguish a prior similar symptom from diagnosed recurrence.
Compare a service company's recommendations without marking work completed or changing a cycle.
Service scheduling is a separate proposal.

## 6. Provenance

Manual model/revision/page, service date/source, current-state age and uncertainty.
Label inferred cause versus recorded diagnosis. No invented reset buttons, timings or codes.

## 7. Candidate relationships

A possible fault-to-prior-service association is a candidate, not a new Truth diagnosis.

## 8. Household confirmation

Confirm the exact Asset/model and intended request before guidance where ambiguity matters.
Confirming a symptom or receipt is not payment/contact/scheduling permission.

## 9. Governed durable relationship

Foundation owns any proposed manual/service association; Truth owns eligible verified state;
Stewardship evaluates care change proposals. No silent write or invented completion mechanism.

## 10. Proposed actions

Suggest approved safe inspection, a qualified service review or a separately authorized appointment.
Return details to HTBW for disclosure/confirmation/execution.

## 11. Prohibited autonomous actions

No speculative electrical/mechanical procedure, safety interlock bypass, service booking/contact,
payment, cycle change, alarm reset or obligation completion based on AI interpretation.

## 12. HTBW authority

Foundation identity/documents; Truth fault evidence; Stewardship care; Identity requester;
Continuity context; Operational Trust privacy/permission; Concierge workflow/native execution.

## 13. Privacy and locality

Source/person scope applies to manuals and private service correspondence. Redact protected account/
contact data on shared outputs. Local processing/derived representations inherit source constraints.

## 14. Unavailable / degraded behavior

Show native approved documents and known state where permitted. Missing manual/model/retrieval means
no claimed procedure; offer safe manufacturer/professional escalation, not cloud guessing.

## 15. Acceptance expectations

Exact model/page evidence for procedural guidance; unknown red light stays uncertain; no obligation
or current-state mutation; service scheduling never follows merely from read access.

## 16. Open questions

Evidence quality for exact procedures, safe-guidance thresholds, native document UX, service-action
authorization and care-review workflow require separate design/acceptance.
