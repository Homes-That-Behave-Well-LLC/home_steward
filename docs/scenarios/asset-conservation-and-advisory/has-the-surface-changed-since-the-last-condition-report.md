## Household question

Has the surface changed since the last condition report?

## Why it matters

A household may want to understand a surface difference since the last professional condition report. The report may describe only selected areas or use terms that are easy to misread. Comparing its actual scope with later observations could clarify which changes are evidenced, which are impressions and which cannot be assessed without another qualified examination.
