# Could moving it introduce another risk?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

Could moving it introduce another risk?

## Why it matters

A household considering relocation may focus on reduced sunlight while overlooking handling, enclosure changes or a different environmental history. An improvement in one respect need not improve the overall situation. Identifying overlooked questions could help a conservator assess the proposed change without the household mistaking a seemingly cooler or darker room for a proven safer destination.
