## Household question

Are candles relevant?

## Why it matters

A household may regard occasional candle use as too minor to affect an artwork or textile. Duration, placement, enclosure and the actual condition evidence may complicate that assumption. An explanation of what is known could make the conversation more precise without asserting that candles caused a surface change, certifying their use or suggesting household cleaning of possible deposits.
