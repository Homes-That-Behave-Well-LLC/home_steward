Has exposure accumulated enough to justify professional review?

## Why it matters

A household may notice modest but repeated environmental excursions and wonder whether they now warrant a conservator's attention. The question is about the quality and significance of accumulated evidence, not simply the latest reading. Missing periods, changed measurement locations and uncertain starting condition can make an apparent long-term pattern less conclusive than it looks.
