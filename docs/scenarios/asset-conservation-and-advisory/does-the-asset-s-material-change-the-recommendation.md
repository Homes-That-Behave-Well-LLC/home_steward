# Does the Asset's material change the recommendation?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

Does the Asset's material change the recommendation?

## Why it matters

Two similarly displayed objects may respond differently because one is a photograph and the other an oil painting. A household benefits from understanding why a generic recommendation may not fit both. Mixed construction, coatings and repairs can make even a familiar material label incomplete, so the explanation should reveal rather than conceal uncertainty about what the Asset contains.

## Important boundary

Asset-specific governed care, informed by material, age, treatment and enclosure, outranks generic published guidance. Observations, reports and inference are not Truth; correlation is not causation.
