## Household question

Which collection-level risks affect several Assets?

## Why it matters

A household may discover that several Assets share an environmental concern, but not every member of the collection has the same vulnerability or documentation. Explaining the common context could help a professional see the breadth of the question while preserving object-specific differences. A collection-level narrative should not hide gaps behind an aggregate reassurance or equate shared exposure with shared damage.
