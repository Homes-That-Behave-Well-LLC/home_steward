# Is the artwork receiving too much light over time?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

Is the artwork receiving too much light over time?

## Why it matters

A household may judge display light by how bright the artwork looks at one moment, while the concern may involve months of exposure. Photographs, textiles and treated surfaces can raise different questions from other materials. Understanding duration, seasonal variation and gaps in measurement would help explain the concern without claiming that visible fading has or has not occurred.
