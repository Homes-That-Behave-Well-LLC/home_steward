# Is the recommendation based on household evidence, published guidance, or inference?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

Is the recommendation based on household evidence, published guidance, or inference?

## Why it matters

A household may hear an apparently unified recommendation even though it combines measured conditions, generic publications and interpretation. Distinguishing those ingredients matters because they carry different authority and limitations. A persuasive explanation should not allow published advice or an AI inference to appear as a household Fact, especially when Asset-specific treatment and accepted care point in a different direction.

## Existing HTBW relationship

HTBW Foundation owns Assets and descriptive relationships; Truth owns current/historical Facts; Stewardship owns governed care and obligations. Conservation advisory never replaces HTBW Risk.

## Possible Home Steward extension

Explain the basis of each conservation-related statement as household evidence, professional assessment, published guidance or inference. Compare applicability against Asset-specific governed care and identify unresolved conflicts without producing new care instructions.

## Potential evidence

Household observation categories; environmental Facts; professional reports; governed care; guidance-provenance categories; stated inference assumptions.
These are categories only, not admitted sources.

## Important boundary

Synthesis does not elevate guidance or inference into Truth, accepted care or professional judgment.

Asset-specific governed care, informed by material, age, treatment and enclosure, outranks generic published guidance. Observations, reports and inference are not Truth; correlation is not causation. AI is not a conservator, appraiser, authenticator or emergency authority; professionals make professional judgments.

Retrieval, derivation and disclosure each require independent Person/source/purpose/namespace/audience authorization. Unknown is a valid result. Sensitivity, locality and derived retention/revocation/deletion constraints persist. Family membership or caretaker identity grants no access.
