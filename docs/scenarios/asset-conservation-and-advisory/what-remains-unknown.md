# What remains unknown?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

What remains unknown?

## Why it matters

A household may feel that extensive paperwork means an Asset is well understood, yet still lack a reliable baseline, treatment details or measurements near the object. Making those gaps explicit would support an honest conservation conversation. Unknowns differ from negative findings: no accessible report of damage is not evidence that no damage exists, and an unmeasured exposure cannot be called absent.

## Existing HTBW relationship

HTBW Foundation owns Assets and descriptive relationships; Truth owns current/historical Facts; Stewardship owns governed care and obligations. Conservation advisory never replaces HTBW Risk.

## Possible Home Steward extension

Synthesize the limits of available material, age, treatment, enclosure, care and exposure context. Explain inaccessible, missing, conflicting and unmeasured evidence separately, keeping current conditions, accumulation and fluctuation distinct.

## Potential evidence

Documentation-gap descriptions; condition reports; measurement coverage; treatment histories; Asset descriptions; governed care; access-scope categories.
These are categories only, not admitted sources.

## Important boundary

Unknown is a valid outcome; do not infer condition, compose missing history or prescribe evidence-gathering procedures.

Asset-specific governed care, informed by material, age, treatment and enclosure, outranks generic published guidance. Observations, reports and inference are not Truth; correlation is not causation. AI is not a conservator, appraiser, authenticator or emergency authority; professionals make professional judgments.

Retrieval, derivation and disclosure each require independent Person/source/purpose/namespace/audience authorization. Unknown is a valid result. Sensitivity, locality and derived retention/revocation/deletion constraints persist. Family membership or caretaker identity grants no access.
