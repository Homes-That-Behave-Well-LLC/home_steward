# Is the concern the current extreme, accumulated exposure, or repeated fluctuation?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

Is the concern the current extreme, accumulated exposure, or repeated fluctuation?

## Why it matters

A household may be concerned by today's unusual reading while overlooking months of less conspicuous exposure. Conversely, a brief excursion may look dramatic without revealing the object's accumulated experience. Separating magnitude, duration and repeated changes would help explain what the evidence actually supports and why a conservator might ask different questions about each pattern.
