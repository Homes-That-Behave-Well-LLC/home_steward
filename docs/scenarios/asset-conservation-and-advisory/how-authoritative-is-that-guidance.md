# How authoritative is that guidance?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

How authoritative is that guidance?

## Why it matters

A household may receive similar advice from a museum publication, a product description and an undated personal note. Similar wording does not make their authority equivalent. Understanding authorship, intended audience, revision history and Asset-specific professional context would help the household ask better questions without treating publication prestige or an AI's confidence as accepted household authority.

## Existing HTBW relationship

## Possible Home Steward extension

## Potential evidence

Authorship categories; publication scope; revision histories; professional assessments; governed care; treatment documentation.

## Important boundary

Source reputation does not override governed care or turn generic guidance into a professional judgment.

Asset-specific governed care, informed by material, age, treatment and enclosure, outranks generic published guidance. Observations, reports and inference are not Truth; correlation is not causation. AI is not a conservator, appraiser, authenticator or emergency authority; professionals make professional judgments.

Retrieval, derivation and disclosure each require independent Person/source/purpose/namespace/audience authorization. Unknown is a valid result. Sensitivity, locality and derived retention/revocation/deletion constraints persist. Family membership or caretaker identity grants no access.
