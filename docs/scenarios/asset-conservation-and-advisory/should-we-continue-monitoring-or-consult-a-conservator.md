Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

Should we continue monitoring or consult a conservator?

## Why it matters

A household may be uncertain whether a documented concern merits professional consultation or whether additional observation would answer it. The uncertainty is itself important when baseline quality, exposure coverage or material composition is weak. A useful explanation could articulate what present evidence supports and what only a qualified examination could determine, without reassuring the household that waiting is safe.
