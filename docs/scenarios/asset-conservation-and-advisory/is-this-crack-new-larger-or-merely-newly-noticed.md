## Why it matters

A household may discover a crack and not know whether it appeared recently or simply escaped notice before. Earlier photographs may lack the detail needed to answer, and report wording may be ambiguous. A careful comparison could distinguish what was documented from what is newly reported, without converting discovery time into onset time or encouraging manipulation to check the crack.
