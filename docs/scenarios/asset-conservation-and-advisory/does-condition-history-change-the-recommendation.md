# Does condition history change the recommendation?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

Does condition history change the recommendation?

## Why it matters

A room condition that appears ordinary may prompt concern when an Asset previously showed instability. Another Asset may have a long, well-documented history without a comparable change. The household needs a coherent account of those differences, including gaps and changes in reporting quality, rather than assuming that an old problem has either disappeared or inevitably returned.

## Possible Home Steward extension

Synthesize dated condition observations and professional reports alongside governed care and environmental history. Distinguish recurring descriptions from confirmed change, relating current conditions to accumulated exposure and fluctuation without asserting a cause.

## Potential evidence

Condition reports; baseline photographs; treatment histories; environmental Fact histories; care requirements; examination-quality information.
These are categories only, not admitted sources.

## Important boundary

Repeated wording in reports does not establish recurrence; undocumented periods remain unknown.

Asset-specific governed care, informed by material, age, treatment and enclosure, outranks generic published guidance. Observations, reports and inference are not Truth; correlation is not causation. AI is not a conservator, appraiser, authenticator or emergency authority; professionals make professional judgments.

Retrieval, derivation and disclosure each require independent Person/source/purpose/namespace/audience authorization. Unknown is a valid result. Sensitivity, locality and derived retention/revocation/deletion constraints persist. Family membership or caretaker identity grants no access.
