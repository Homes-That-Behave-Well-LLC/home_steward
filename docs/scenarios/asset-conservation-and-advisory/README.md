# Asset Conservation and Advisory

Navigation and scope guidance only; not an architecture contract or roadmap. Browse the [scenario-family map](../README.md).

## Purpose

Preserve questions about Asset-specific condition, exposure, care evidence and professional consultation. Current extremes, cumulative exposure, repeated fluctuation and an uncertain baseline are different concerns.

## Relationship to HTBW

Foundation holds Asset-specific governed requirements and documentation; Truth supplies supported measurements; Stewardship owns care and obligations. Conservation advisory does not replace HTBW Risk or its accepted native care evaluation. See the existing [HTBW boundary](../../architecture/htbw-boundary.md).

## Possible Home Steward extension

Contextualize separately authorized material, age, treatment, enclosure, condition history and published guidance to explain concerns and evidence gaps. Any new source admission, representation, handoff or implementation is an OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION.

## Boundaries

Generic guidance never overwrites Asset-specific governed care. Home Steward is not a conservator, appraiser or authenticator; no cleaning algorithm, handling instruction, relocation prescription or rescue ranking is established. Professional judgments stay professional.

Person/source/purpose/namespace/audience scope, household-controlled locality and source-derived lifecycle remain independent requirements. Potential evidence is not admitted access; knowledge is not action or disclosure authority. Source reports and inference are not automatic Truth; unknown is valid and correlation is not causation.
