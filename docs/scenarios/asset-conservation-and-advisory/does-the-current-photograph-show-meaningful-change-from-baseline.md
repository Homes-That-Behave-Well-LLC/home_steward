# Does the current photograph show meaningful change from baseline?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

Does the current photograph show meaningful change from baseline?

## Why it matters

A household may see a difference between photographs and wonder whether the Asset changed. Lighting, angle, camera processing and image quality can produce apparent differences even when condition is unchanged. A comparison should help identify those limitations and useful professional questions, particularly when the baseline is undated or too uneven to support a confident condition assessment.
