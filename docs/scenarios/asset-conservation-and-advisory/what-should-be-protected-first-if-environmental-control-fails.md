# What should be protected first if environmental control fails?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

What should be protected first if environmental control fails?

## Why it matters

A household facing failed environmental control may want a simple answer about what to protect first. That request can conceal competing human-safety, handling and conservation judgments that an AI must not resolve. Understanding existing professional instructions and missing preparedness evidence could support advance consultation, but monetary value, material labels or incomplete exposure histories cannot establish a rescue order.

## Existing HTBW relationship

HTBW Foundation owns Assets and descriptive relationships; Truth owns current/historical Facts; Stewardship owns governed care and obligations. Conservation advisory never replaces HTBW Risk.

## Possible Home Steward extension

Explain existing governed care, professional emergency restrictions and evidence gaps relevant to the scenario. Distinguish current Facts from hypothetical consequences and prepare questions about priorities for qualified authorities without choosing an order of action.

## Potential evidence

Governed care; professional emergency documentation; condition reports; material and enclosure descriptions; current environmental Facts; handling assessments.
These are categories only, not admitted sources.

## Important boundary

No ranked rescue algorithm or emergency handling advice; human safety and existing emergency authority are not displaced.

Asset-specific governed care, informed by material, age, treatment and enclosure, outranks generic published guidance. Observations, reports and inference are not Truth; correlation is not causation. AI is not a conservator, appraiser, authenticator or emergency authority; professionals make professional judgments.

Retrieval, derivation and disclosure each require independent Person/source/purpose/namespace/audience authorization. Unknown is a valid result. Sensitivity, locality and derived retention/revocation/deletion constraints persist. Family membership or caretaker identity grants no access.
