## Household question

Which displayed Assets should be rotated or rested?

## Why it matters

A household may have heard that sensitive works benefit from time out of display and wonder how that relates to its own collection. Materials, prior exposure, condition and storage context differ, so a generic rotation idea may not fit. Understanding the documented basis for discussion would be useful without creating an AI-selected rotation list or encouraging unqualified movement.
