## Household question

Do we have emergency handling instructions?

## Why it matters

A household may believe emergency instructions exist somewhere among collection papers but not know their scope or whether they still apply. Locating and explaining that documentation could support advance consultation, particularly when condition or enclosure has changed. A document's title alone cannot establish completeness, current professional suitability or authorization for household members to act during an emergency.
