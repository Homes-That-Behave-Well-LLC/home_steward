# Which Assets cannot safely be moved by household members?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

Which Assets cannot safely be moved by household members?

## Why it matters

A household may assume an object can be moved because it looks manageable or has been moved before. Weight, unstable condition, mounts and past treatment can make that assumption unsafe for both people and the Asset. Understanding documented professional-only restrictions and missing assessments could support consultation without implying that the absence of a warning establishes household handling competence.

## Existing HTBW relationship

## Possible Home Steward extension

Retrieve and explain governed handling restrictions, condition context and professional assessments. Identify where safe movement is explicitly constrained or remains unknown; do not infer permission from size, familiar appearance or previous household handling.

## Potential evidence

Professional handling assessments; governed care; condition reports; mount descriptions; treatment histories; dimensional-description categories.
These are categories only, not admitted sources.

## Important boundary

No lifting, packing, dismantling or relocation instructions are supplied; unknown suitability is not permission.

Asset-specific governed care, informed by material, age, treatment and enclosure, outranks generic published guidance. Observations, reports and inference are not Truth; correlation is not causation. AI is not a conservator, appraiser, authenticator or emergency authority; professionals make professional judgments.

Retrieval, derivation and disclosure each require independent Person/source/purpose/namespace/audience authorization. Unknown is a valid result. Sensitivity, locality and derived retention/revocation/deletion constraints persist. Family membership or caretaker identity grants no access.
