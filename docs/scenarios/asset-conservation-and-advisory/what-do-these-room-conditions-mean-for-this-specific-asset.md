## Household question

What do these room conditions mean for this specific Asset?

## Why it matters

A household may see a room humidity reading without knowing what it means for a framed photograph beside a wooden instrument. The useful outcome is an Asset-specific explanation, not a room-wide verdict. Material, age, previous treatment and enclosure can change which uncertainties deserve a conservator's attention, even when the room reading is identical.
