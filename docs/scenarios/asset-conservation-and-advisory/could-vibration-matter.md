## Household question

Could vibration matter?

## Why it matters

A household may notice vibration near an instrument, sculpture or framed work and wonder whether it is significant. Perceived vibration, measured vibration and documented structural vulnerability are different forms of evidence. A focused explanation could help identify the uncertainty around source, duration and mounting context without declaring damage or encouraging a household test that might disturb the Asset.
