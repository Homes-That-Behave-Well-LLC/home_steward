# Could this cleaning method damage the Asset?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

Could this cleaning method damage the Asset?

## Why it matters

A household may consider a familiar cleaning product harmless because it works on everyday surfaces. An Asset's coating, aged material or prior treatment may make that assumption unsafe. The useful outcome is to recognize uncertainty and formulate a conservator question before action, not to obtain a recipe, a compatibility assurance or a less visibly aggressive substitute.
