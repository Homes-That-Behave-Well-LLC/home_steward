Are cooking aerosols relevant?

## Why it matters

A household may wonder whether nearby cooking contributes to a film or change on a displayed object. Odor, visible residue and measured air conditions do not establish the same thing, and several sources may be plausible. Understanding material, treatment, enclosure and observation history could improve a conservator question without turning a suspicion into a diagnosis or a cleaning recommendation.
