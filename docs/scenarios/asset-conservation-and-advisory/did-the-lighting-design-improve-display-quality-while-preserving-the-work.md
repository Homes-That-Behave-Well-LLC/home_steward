# Did the lighting design improve display quality while preserving the work?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

Did the lighting design improve display quality while preserving the work?

## Why it matters

A household may enjoy a lighting redesign while wondering whether it also preserved the displayed work. Better visual presentation and conservation suitability are separate outcomes. Comparing available evidence could clarify what changed in illumination, duration and environmental context, without treating aesthetic satisfaction or an absence of obvious fading as proof of preservation success.
