# Could cleaning products matter?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

Could cleaning products matter?

## Why it matters

A household may wonder whether ordinary room cleaning affects an Asset that is not itself being cleaned. Product descriptions, application context and material vulnerabilities may be incomplete, especially for treated or enclosed objects. Understanding documented cautions could support consultation while avoiding a false reassurance based on familiar branding or a harmful suggestion to test a product near the collection.
