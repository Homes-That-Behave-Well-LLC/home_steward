# What should never be done without a conservator?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

What should never be done without a conservator?

## Why it matters

A household may want to distinguish routine stewardship from an intervention that demands specialist judgment. Opening an enclosure, addressing a stain or adjusting a damaged mount may seem minor while concealing serious uncertainty. Understanding documented professional-only constraints could help the household recognize the limits of its knowledge, without implying that everything outside a short caution list is safe.

## Existing HTBW relationship

HTBW Foundation owns Assets and descriptive relationships; Truth owns current/historical Facts; Stewardship owns governed care and obligations. Conservation advisory never replaces HTBW Risk.

## Possible Home Steward extension

Retrieve and explain professional restrictions in Asset-specific governed care and treatment documentation. Synthesize questions about uncertain interventions for consultation rather than inventing a universal list of permissible household actions.

## Potential evidence

Governed care requirements; treatment reports; professional restrictions; enclosure documentation; condition assessments.
These are categories only, not admitted sources.

## Important boundary

An absent prohibition is not permission; no handling, cleaning or treatment procedure is supplied.

Asset-specific governed care, informed by material, age, treatment and enclosure, outranks generic published guidance. Observations, reports and inference are not Truth; correlation is not causation. AI is not a conservator, appraiser, authenticator or emergency authority; professionals make professional judgments.

Retrieval, derivation and disclosure each require independent Person/source/purpose/namespace/audience authorization. Unknown is a valid result. Sensitivity, locality and derived retention/revocation/deletion constraints persist. Family membership or caretaker identity grants no access.
