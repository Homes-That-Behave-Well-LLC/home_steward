## Household question

Which Assets are vulnerable to a plumbing leak?

## Why it matters

A household may want to understand which collection objects have a documented relationship to a possible plumbing leak. Declared locations, current location Facts and unknown placement are not interchangeable. A useful explanation would show relevant care and professional handling constraints without turning a location comparison into a predicted flood path, a guarantee of safety or an instruction to move objects.

## Important boundary
