# What kind of specialist might be appropriate?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

What kind of specialist might be appropriate?

## Why it matters

A household may not know which professional discipline fits an object with mixed materials, earlier repairs or an uncertain condition concern. Understanding the documented question could make initial consultation more effective. Material names alone cannot establish the expertise required, and a conservation concern is different from appraisal, authentication, insurance or emergency response even when several professionals may be involved.

## Important boundary

No professional credential qualification, provider ranking or claim that AI can perform specialist judgment is envisioned.

Asset-specific governed care, informed by material, age, treatment and enclosure, outranks generic published guidance. Observations, reports and inference are not Truth; correlation is not causation. AI is not a conservator, appraiser, authenticator or emergency authority; professionals make professional judgments.

Retrieval, derivation and disclosure each require independent Person/source/purpose/namespace/audience authorization. Unknown is a valid result. Sensitivity, locality and derived retention/revocation/deletion constraints persist. Family membership or caretaker identity grants no access.
