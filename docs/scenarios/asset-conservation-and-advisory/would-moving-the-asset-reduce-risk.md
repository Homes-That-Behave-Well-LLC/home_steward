# Would moving the Asset reduce risk?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

Would moving the Asset reduce risk?

## Why it matters

A household may associate a display location with uncomfortable light or humidity and wonder whether another room would be better. The proposed destination may have its own unknowns, and moving can introduce handling and transition concerns. A useful explanation would make those trade-offs visible for professional discussion rather than treating relocation as an obvious or harmless remedy.
