# Would shade behavior meaningfully reduce exposure?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

Would shade behavior meaningfully reduce exposure?

## Why it matters

A household may believe shade use protects an artwork because the room looks darker afterward. That impression does not establish the change in exposure near the object or the effect across a full day. Comparing available before-and-after evidence could clarify the question, including seasonal differences, while leaving preservation significance and any actual control decision with the responsible owners.
