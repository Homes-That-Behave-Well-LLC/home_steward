# What is the least invasive reasonable next step?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

What is the least invasive reasonable next step?

## Why it matters

A household concerned about an Asset may seek the least invasive next step and assume a gentle-looking action is harmless. Even minor cleaning, opening or handling can be inappropriate when materials or treatments are uncertain. The useful outcome is to articulate the concern and professional consultation questions, not to have AI choose an intervention or convert caution into a household procedure.

## Possible Home Steward extension

Compare existing professional restrictions and governed care with the stated concern and evidence gaps. Explain why apparently minimal actions still require context, framing clarification and consultation questions rather than selecting an action.

## Potential evidence

Governed care; professional restrictions; treatment reports; condition documentation; enclosure descriptions; consultation-concern categories.
These are categories only, not admitted sources.

## Important boundary

No cleaning, handling, sampling, relocation or intervention prescription; least invasive is not an AI safety certification.

Asset-specific governed care, informed by material, age, treatment and enclosure, outranks generic published guidance. Observations, reports and inference are not Truth; correlation is not causation. AI is not a conservator, appraiser, authenticator or emergency authority; professionals make professional judgments.

Retrieval, derivation and disclosure each require independent Person/source/purpose/namespace/audience authorization. Unknown is a valid result. Sensitivity, locality and derived retention/revocation/deletion constraints persist. Family membership or caretaker identity grants no access.
