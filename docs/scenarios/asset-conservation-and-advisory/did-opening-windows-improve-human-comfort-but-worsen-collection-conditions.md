## Household question

Did opening windows improve human comfort but worsen collection conditions?

## Why it matters

A household may feel more comfortable after opening windows while its collection experiences a different humidity or pollutant context. Human comfort and Asset care need not move together. Understanding the available timing and conditions would help the household discuss the trade-off without assuming that window opening caused a condition change or that collection concerns override human safety.
