# Does the room recover adequately after a door is left open?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

Does the room recover adequately after a door is left open?

## Why it matters

A household may see room conditions return near earlier readings after a door was left open and call the room recovered. The Asset's enclosure or immediate surroundings may not follow that same pattern. Comparing evidence could clarify what returned, what remained unknown and whether the baseline was well characterized, rather than equating a familiar room reading with a restored conservation condition.
