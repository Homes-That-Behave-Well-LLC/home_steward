# Did the intervention stabilize conditions?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

Did the intervention stabilize conditions?

## Why it matters

A household may consider an intervention successful because subsequent readings look quieter. Different weather, missing samples or a shorter comparison period may explain part of that appearance. Understanding what changed in the measured environment would help separate apparent stabilization from proven preservation benefit, particularly when the Asset's original condition and immediate enclosure environment were not well documented.
