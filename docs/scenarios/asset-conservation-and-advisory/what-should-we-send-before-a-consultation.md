# What should we send before a consultation?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

What should we send before a consultation?

## Why it matters

A household may want to prepare a concise account before a conservation consultation rather than overwhelm a professional with unrelated papers. Relevant treatment, condition and environmental context could help, but documents may include financial or personal details unnecessary to the question. Understanding relevance and uncertainty would support preparation without assuming that permission to read a document permits sending it.

## Existing HTBW relationship

HTBW Foundation owns Assets and descriptive relationships; Truth owns current/historical Facts; Stewardship owns governed care and obligations. Conservation advisory never replaces HTBW Risk.

## Possible Home Steward extension

Synthesize a consultation-oriented explanation of relevant evidence categories, dates and gaps. Separate direct household observations, professional reports, published guidance and inference, leaving selection, disclosure and any actual transmission outside this vision.

## Potential evidence

Condition reports; treatment histories; Asset and enclosure descriptions; environmental histories; governed care; professional-request categories.
These are categories only, not admitted sources.

## Important boundary

No transmission or disclosure authorization is inferred; insurance values and private correspondence remain separately scoped.

Asset-specific governed care, informed by material, age, treatment and enclosure, outranks generic published guidance. Observations, reports and inference are not Truth; correlation is not causation. AI is not a conservator, appraiser, authenticator or emergency authority; professionals make professional judgments.

Retrieval, derivation and disclosure each require independent Person/source/purpose/namespace/audience authorization. Unknown is a valid result. Sensitivity, locality and derived retention/revocation/deletion constraints persist. Family membership or caretaker identity grants no access.
