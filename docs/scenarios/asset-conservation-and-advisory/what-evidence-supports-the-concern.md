# What evidence supports the concern?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

What evidence supports the concern?

## Why it matters

A household may receive a plausible concern without knowing whether it rests on a measured condition, a photograph, a reported impression or general guidance. Seeing the actual basis could help decide what to discuss with a conservator. Supporting evidence and contradictory evidence both matter, while missing documentation should remain a limitation rather than be replaced by a confident narrative.

## Existing HTBW relationship

HTBW Foundation owns Assets and descriptive relationships; Truth owns current/historical Facts; Stewardship owns governed care and obligations. Conservation advisory never replaces HTBW Risk.

## Possible Home Steward extension

Explain the provenance, date, scope and limitations of evidence relevant to the concern. Compare household observations and professional reports with governed care and published guidance, distinguishing current Facts, exposure history and inference.

## Potential evidence

Environmental Fact histories; condition reports; photographs; observation categories; governed care; guidance-provenance categories; contradictory documentation.
These are categories only, not admitted sources.

## Important boundary

Evidence supporting concern does not establish damage, causation or professional necessity of an intervention.

Asset-specific governed care, informed by material, age, treatment and enclosure, outranks generic published guidance. Observations, reports and inference are not Truth; correlation is not causation. AI is not a conservator, appraiser, authenticator or emergency authority; professionals make professional judgments.

Retrieval, derivation and disclosure each require independent Person/source/purpose/namespace/audience authorization. Unknown is a valid result. Sensitivity, locality and derived retention/revocation/deletion constraints persist. Family membership or caretaker identity grants no access.
