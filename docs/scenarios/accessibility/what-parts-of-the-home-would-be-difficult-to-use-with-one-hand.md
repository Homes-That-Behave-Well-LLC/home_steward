# What parts of the Home would be difficult to use with one hand?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

What parts of the Home would be difficult to use with one hand?

## Why it matters

Using one hand can be a permanent, temporary or situational need, including carrying something while opening a door. The household wants to understand interaction demands without requiring anyone to disclose a condition. Difficulty should be described in relation to the task and environment, not as a deficiency in the person trying to use the Home.
