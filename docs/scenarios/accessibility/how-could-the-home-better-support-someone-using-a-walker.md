# How could the Home better support someone using a walker?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

How could the Home better support someone using a walker?

## Why it matters

Someone using a walker may need a clear approach, reachable controls and time to move without juggling tasks. The household wants to explore these stated needs without inferring a diagnosis or observing movement continuously. Suitability depends on the person, room and situation, so generic suggestions should not be presented as verified accessibility.

## Existing HTBW relationship

Foundation retains definitions/configuration; Truth, current/historical Facts; Identity, Person resolution, not authentication/authorization; Stewardship, household-declared care/obligations; Continuity, preference/experience eligibility; Operational Trust, request-specific permissions; Concierge, orchestration/Trace. Home Steward acquires no ownership.
