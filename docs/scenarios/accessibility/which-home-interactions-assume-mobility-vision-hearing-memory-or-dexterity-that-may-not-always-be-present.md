# Which Home interactions assume mobility, vision, hearing, memory, or dexterity that may not always be present?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

Which Home interactions assume mobility, vision, hearing, memory, or dexterity that may not always be present?

## Why it matters

Home interactions can silently assume steady mobility, sight, hearing, recall or dexterity. Those assumptions may fail permanently, temporarily, situationally or for a visitor even when the technology works as designed. The household wants to explore task demands and alternatives without turning the inquiry into ability surveillance or a judgment about who can live independently.

## Existing HTBW relationship

Foundation retains definitions/configuration; Truth, current/historical Facts; Identity, Person resolution, not authentication/authorization; Stewardship, household-declared care/obligations; Continuity, preference/experience eligibility; Operational Trust, request-specific permissions; Concierge, orchestration/Trace. Home Steward acquires no ownership.

## Potential evidence

Task-demand descriptions; voluntary needs/preferences; native alternative documentation; visitor/recovery use categories; usability feedback.

Categories only, each independently authorized for purpose, Person, source, audience and namespace. Local processing and derived retention, correction, expiry and revocation remain source-bound.

## Important boundary

No inferred cognitive or physical impairment, independence assessment or ranking of people by ability.

Consent is required; preserve manual/native alternatives. No diagnosis, judgment of human worth, impairment inference or surveillance. Accessibility is not a permission expansion.

Knowledge/interpretation never grants action or disclosure authority. Unclear evidence stays unknown; correlation/timing is not causation. No life-safety or medical/emergency path depends on Home Steward/LLM.
