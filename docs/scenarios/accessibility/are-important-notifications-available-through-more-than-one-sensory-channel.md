# Are important notifications available through more than one sensory channel?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

Are important notifications available through more than one sensory channel?

## Why it matters

Information delivered only as sound or only as text may be missed in everyday situations without any permanent impairment. The household wants to understand the documented sensory options for important information and whether people consider them usable. Multiple channels should support comprehension without creating louder interruptions, private disclosures or a new notification design.
