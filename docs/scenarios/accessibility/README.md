# Accessibility

Navigation and scope guidance only; not an architecture contract or roadmap. Browse the [scenario-family map](../README.md).

## Purpose

Preserve household questions about usable, dignified interaction and independent participation without assuming impairment from behavior.

## Relationship to HTBW

People are not Assets; Identity, consent, Continuity and Operational Trust keep their accepted scope. Manual and native alternatives remain important. See the existing [HTBW boundary](../../architecture/htbw-boundary.md).

## Possible Home Steward extension

Explore authorized preferences, interface experiences and evidence of practical barriers. Any new source admission, representation, handoff or implementation is an OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION.

## Boundaries

No diagnosis, impairment inference, human-worth assessment, surveillance or permission lowering. Assistance is not authority transfer or a guarantee of emergency delivery.

Person/source/purpose/namespace/audience scope, household-controlled locality and source-derived lifecycle remain independent requirements. Potential evidence is not admitted access; knowledge is not action or disclosure authority. Source reports and inference are not automatic Truth; unknown is valid and correlation is not causation.

### Vision — Not Yet Architected

- [What parts of the Home would be difficult to use with one hand?](what-parts-of-the-home-would-be-difficult-to-use-with-one-hand.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Using one hand can be a permanent, temporary or situational need, including carrying something while opening a door.
- [How could the Home better support someone using a walker?](how-could-the-home-better-support-someone-using-a-walker.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Someone using a walker may need a clear approach, reachable controls and time to move without juggling tasks.
- [Which interactions depend on hearing?](which-interactions-depend-on-hearing.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. An audible confirmation may be useful but unavailable to someone who cannot hear it in that moment.
- [Which interactions depend on seeing a screen?](which-interactions-depend-on-seeing-a-screen.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A screen can be inaccessible because of vision, glare, distance, occupied hands or a device being unavailable.
- [What happens when someone cannot use voice?](what-happens-when-someone-cannot-use-voice.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Voice may be unavailable because of speech differences, temporary recovery, noise, privacy or a preference not to speak.
- [Can a guest understand what this room can do?](can-a-guest-understand-what-this-room-can-do.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A visitor should not need prior household knowledge to understand basic room functions.
- [What should change temporarily during recovery from surgery?](what-should-change-temporarily-during-recovery-from-surgery.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Recovery from surgery can temporarily change how someone wishes to move, rest or interact with the Home.
- [Which capabilities have no usable fallback?](which-capabilities-have-no-usable-fallback.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A capability can be convenient under ideal conditions yet unusable when its preferred interaction is unavailable.
- [Are important notifications available through more than one sensory channel?](are-important-notifications-available-through-more-than-one-sensory-channel.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Information delivered only as sound or only as text may be missed in everyday situations without any permanent impairment.
- [Which Home interactions assume mobility, vision, hearing, memory, or dexterity that may not always be present?](which-home-interactions-assume-mobility-vision-hearing-memory-or-dexterity-that-may-not-always-be-present.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Home interactions can silently assume steady mobility, sight, hearing, recall or dexterity.
