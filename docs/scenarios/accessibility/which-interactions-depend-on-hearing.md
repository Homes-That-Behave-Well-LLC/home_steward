# Which interactions depend on hearing?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

Which interactions depend on hearing?

## Why it matters

An audible confirmation may be useful but unavailable to someone who cannot hear it in that moment. The household wants to understand which interactions depend on hearing, including noisy rooms and temporary conditions. The goal is equivalent understanding and choice, not a classification of residents by hearing ability or an assumption that louder sound solves the problem.

## Important boundary

Do not infer hearing loss or change sound levels; availability of another channel does not prove usability.

Consent is required; preserve manual/native alternatives. No diagnosis, judgment of human worth, impairment inference or surveillance. Accessibility is not a permission expansion.

Knowledge/interpretation never grants action or disclosure authority. Unclear evidence stays unknown; correlation/timing is not causation. No life-safety or medical/emergency path depends on Home Steward/LLM.
