# Can a guest understand what this room can do?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

Can a guest understand what this room can do?

## Why it matters

A visitor should not need prior household knowledge to understand basic room functions. The household wants to explore whether permitted room help is understandable across differing abilities, languages and familiarity. Clear explanations must not become a back door into private preferences, household routines or capabilities the guest is not authorized to use.
