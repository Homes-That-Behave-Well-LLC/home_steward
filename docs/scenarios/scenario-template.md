# Scenario template

> Proposed acceptance template; awaiting Owner review. Use fictional/synthetic records, not real
> household data. Scenarios are architectural tests, not executed runtime tests or authorization.

## 1. Household request

State the question, effective Person/context assumptions and possible follow-up conversation.
Resolve identity through HTBW rather than guessing from voice, room or available credentials.

## 2. Existing HTBW knowledge

Identify native Asset/Room/Person definitions, relationships, descriptions, documents, obligations,
Truth/context and governance. Do not require Home Steward for a native answer.

## 3. Authorized private retrieval

Specify only eligible source/namespace/document/fields and beneficiary/purpose scope.
Original documents remain source-owned.

## 4. Optional public research

Explicitly separate external knowledge and permitted outbound query contents.
No private context or household-sensitive data leaks into search.

## 5. Home Steward synthesis

Describe authorized comparison/retrieval/reasoning and uncertainty, not state mutation.

## 6. Provenance

Source class/reference/version/date, freshness/gaps, inference versus Fact and minimal explanation.
Do not require indefinite raw prompt/source retention.

## 7. Candidate relationships

Distinguish possible associations from existing governed relationships and Truth.

## 8. Household confirmation

Define what attributable confirmation would mean and what it would not authorize.

## 9. Governed durable relationship

Describe responsible HTBW owner and promotion boundary without inventing a final schema/mechanism.

## 10. Proposed actions

Return proposed care, service, scheduling, disclosure or document updates to HTBW governance.

## 11. Prohibited autonomous actions

Explicitly prohibit relevant write/disclosure/transaction/Truth/obligation shortcuts.

## 12. HTBW authority

Name Foundation, Stewardship, Identity, Truth, Continuity, Operational Trust, Concierge as applicable.
No owner is transferred; native HA/other authorized capabilities execute.

## 13. Privacy and locality

Effective Person, beneficiary/output audience, source scope, local processing and derived lifecycle.
Read access is not disclosure or transaction authority.

## 14. Unavailable / degraded behavior

Preserve authoritative/native HTBW behavior; explain unavailable extensions and uncertainty.
No cloud or unauthorized-source fallback.

## 15. Acceptance expectations

Observable future tests for boundary/correctness/privacy and no unnecessary invocation.
No runtime PASS is claimed by writing expectations.

## 16. Open questions

List unsettled schema/policy/confirmation/connector/evidence questions without silently deciding them.
