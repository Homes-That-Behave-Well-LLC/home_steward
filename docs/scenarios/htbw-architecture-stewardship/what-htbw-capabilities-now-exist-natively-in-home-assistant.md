# What HTBW capabilities now exist natively in Home Assistant

Status: CANONICAL SCENARIO

Developed-content maturity only; not Owner, runtime or architecture acceptance.

> Proposed synthetic architecture-review scenario; no actual obsolete capability or migration finding.
> Applies [HTBW Architecture Stewardship](../../architecture/htbw-architecture-stewardship.md).

## 1. Household request

"What are we still doing in HTBW that Home Assistant can now do natively?" Resolve an authorized
household/developer review context; do not assume feature names establish equivalence.

## 2. Existing HTBW knowledge

Accepted behavior, decision identifier/rationale, historical platform limitation, responsibility
contracts and acceptance references; Foundation configuration, Truth Facts and Decision Traces.

## 3. Authorized private retrieval

Minimum approved exact repository/commit/decision/contract/test and implementation references.
Private code, credentials, Owner reviews and logs/history require their own source permissions.

## 4. Optional public research

Current official [HA release evidence](https://www.home-assistant.io/blog/2026/11/04/release-202611/)
and [native target documentation](https://www.home-assistant.io/docs/automation/targets/) may ground
an exclusion-mechanism comparison. Community material is inspiration only; no sensitive code upload.

## 5. Home Steward synthesis

Identify accepted HTBW behavior, original rationale, original HA limitation, current official evidence,
behavioral scope/gaps and whether governed reconsideration is justified. A hypothetical custom
exclusion mechanism may merit comparison with documented native targets, not replacement by default.

Compare participation, exceptions, dynamic membership, permission, outcomes, degradation and
Explainability/Decision Trace. A similar name/API is insufficient. State expected household benefit,
maintenance/new obligation and unknowns; no review/no action may be the correct result.

## 6. Provenance

Exact source location/commit/decision, publication/commit date, review date, effective/installed HA
version and GA/Labs/preview/deprecation/removal status. Inferred fit is not Truth or accepted equivalence.

## 7. Candidate relationships

Possible behavior-to-native-mechanism replacement remains a candidate, not an obsolete decision,
new Fact, reopened issue or transferred governance responsibility.

## 8. Household confirmation

Eligible human review confirms intended outcome/scope; confirmation is evidence, not authorization
to migrate or accept native behavior.

## 9. Governed durable relationship

Existing owners retain decisions/configuration/acceptance/history. Home Steward drafts consultation,
not Ledger/ADR changes, replacement records or a central acceptance store.

## 10. Proposed actions

Propose review through HTBW/Operational Trust, with equivalence, privacy, new obligation, rollback,
tests, HA-DEV and Reference Home lived validation plus Owner acceptance before retirement.

## 11. Prohibited autonomous actions

No decision reopening, Decision Ledger/ADR edit, issue creation/closure, custom-code removal,
migration, automation/script/configuration/policy change, HA update/Labs enablement or upstream work.

## 12. HTBW authority

Foundation definitions; Truth Facts; Identity requester; Stewardship care; Continuity intent;
Operational Trust permission; Concierge formulation/orchestration/Trace, not acceptance.

## 13. Privacy and locality

Minimum independently governed repository/source/Person/audience evidence before local reasoning.
No secrets, private correspondence or raw histories admitted by utility; derivations inherit lifecycle.

## 14. Unavailable / degraded behavior

Missing original rationale or incomplete/stale official evidence yields "I don't know whether native
replacement is equivalent"; no migration recommendation. Native/custom operation remains unchanged.

## 15. Acceptance expectations

All seven comparison requirements, exact source/version and behavioral proof; governance distinct
from mechanism. Native availability is not authorization; no decision/migration/retirement executed.

## 16. Open questions

Source admission, comparison tests and contextual migration review remain future design; no crawler,
repository/log ingestion, code generator or migration runtime.
