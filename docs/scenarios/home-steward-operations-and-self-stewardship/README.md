# Home Steward Operations and Self-Stewardship

Navigation and scenario-capture guidance only; not architecture, a roadmap or delivered functionality. Browse the [scenario-first map](../README.md).

## Purpose

Explore how an eventual Home Steward deployment could remain understandable, maintainable, replaceable and recoverable. These are prospective household questions, not delivered health checks, installed models, operational reports or platform requirements.

## Relationship to HTBW

Foundation describes structure, not current Truth; Stewardship owns household-declared significance, care, obligations and accepted Custody; Identity supplies Person/context, not authentication or authorization; Truth owns current/historical Facts; Continuity retains accepted preference/session/experience responsibilities; Operational Trust determines permission/disclosure for this request/context; Concierge retains governed orchestration and Trace, not centralized ownership or acceptance. Native Explainability already includes historical/time/cross-Room retrieval. Home Assistant/devices retain execution.

See the accepted-owner [HTBW boundary](../../architecture/htbw-boundary.md), [source authorization](../../architecture/source-authorization.md) and [derived lifecycle](../../architecture/derived-knowledge-lifecycle.md).

## Boundaries

NOTICE != DIAGNOSE; NOTICE != DISCLOSE; NOTICE != RETAIN; NOTICE != RECOMMEND; NOTICE != ACT. Noticing is not authority or automatic Truth, household significance, notification or action.

Potential evidence is not admitted access. Person/source/purpose/namespace/audience gates remain independent; one Person's grant is not everyone's access and cross-person grants do not merge. The most restrictive applicable source constraints remain binding. Summaries can disclose their sources; conclusions are not automatically safe to share. USE != INGEST != DERIVE != RETAIN. Derived information remains source-bound through correction, retention, expiry, deletion and revocation within the household-controlled network boundary. Household-configured ordinary retention may be zero/disabled; no numeric minimum or maximum is imposed. Confirmation alone does not grant retention, disclosure, policy or action authority. No shadow history or silent cloud/source fallback.

Observation, source report, comparison and inference are not automatic Truth. Correlation is not causation; unknown is valid. Issued command != observed physical result. People and Pets are not Assets; Pet/device location does not flow through human Identity. No diagnosis, prognosis, treatment or medical causation follows from changed observations. No medical, life-safety or emergency path depends on an LLM; native/deterministic/manual safety remains independent.

All future source eligibility, meaningful-change review, retention, correction, disclosure, notification and action questions remain OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTIONS. No runtime, scheduler, camera pipeline, learning system, hook, consent engine, platform qualification or acceptance mechanism is designed here.

## Primary scenarios

Primary files: **0 developed + 13 Vision = 13**. Existing coverage and aliases do not add primary files or change target maturity.

- [Is Home Steward healthy?](is-home-steward-healthy.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. An eventual Home Steward deployment could appear responsive while some knowledge or dependencies are unavailable. The household wants attributable status and uncertainty understood rather than a generic healthy label, without Home Steward becoming responsible for determining the safety of native HTBW operation.
- [Is the household knowledge index current?](is-the-household-knowledge-index-current.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A future knowledge representation may lag behind its permitted sources or contain revoked material. The household wants currency understood at the source and purpose level, rather than a global current label masking stale documents, missing updates or expired derived support.
- [Which sources failed to refresh?](which-sources-failed-to-refresh.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A source may become unavailable while other knowledge remains accessible. The household wants failed or unverified refresh claims distinguished from deleted, denied or unchanged evidence, so an eventual Home Steward answer does not silently substitute another source or replay revoked information.
- [Is storage approaching capacity?](is-storage-approaching-capacity.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. An eventual household appliance may have limited storage shared by permitted knowledge and other functions. The household wants capacity claims and uncertainty explained without automatically deleting sources, imposing retention quotas or treating storage pressure as permission to discard governed evidence.
- [Are backups current?](are-backups-current.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A future backup may be dated recently yet omit the sources or configuration needed for recovery. The household wants current scope and known gaps distinguished from a successful backup report, before assuming that source restrictions or replacement-hardware needs are preserved.
- [Which models are installed?](which-models-are-installed.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. An eventual platform may contain several models with different intended uses or availability. The household wants a source-attributable inventory distinguished from advertised support, without a scenario document implying models are installed, eligible or selected for Home Steward.
- [Did a model update materially change answer quality?](did-a-model-update-materially-change-answer-quality.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A model update can alter answer behavior while the household's governed knowledge stays separate. The household wants fair comparison against its own eligible questions and source constraints, not novelty or fluency treated as improved quality or an update automatically accepted as beneficial.
- [What changed after the last Home Steward platform update?](what-changed-after-the-last-home-steward-platform-update.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A future platform update may change knowledge access, availability or visible behavior. The household wants exact versions and attributable differences understood while preserving prior source constraints, rather than interpreting every later change as caused by the update or authorizing an automatic rollback.
- [Can Home Steward operate without the vendor?](can-home-steward-operate-without-the-vendor.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Vendor loss may affect an eventual Home Steward deployment differently from an Internet outage. The household wants documented boot, authentication, knowledge and recovery dependencies understood, rather than a local label promising vendor independence or this scenario becoming a platform qualification decision.
- [Which household capabilities currently depend on Home Steward?](which-household-capabilities-currently-depend-on-home-steward.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A future household could value Home Steward enrichment without needing it for native Home outcomes. The household wants actual declared dependencies distinguished from convenient usage, so reliance on a knowledge answer does not transfer deterministic care, permission or safety responsibilities to an LLM.
- [Can another household steward recover the system without vendor assistance?](can-another-household-steward-recover-the-system-without-vendor-assistance.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Recovery knowledge may be concentrated with one operator even when sources remain household-controlled. The household wants another eligible steward to understand prospective recovery scope without receiving unnecessary secrets, new authority or a promise that vendor-independent restoration already works.
- [What information must be preserved before replacing the appliance?](what-information-must-be-preserved-before-replacing-the-appliance.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Replacing an eventual appliance could lose configuration or eligible derived context if their ownership is unclear. The household wants originals, recoverable knowledge and revoked or expired information distinguished, without a preservation question becoming permission to copy every private record or export secrets.
- [Could a replacement private-AI platform reconstruct Home Steward from governed sources and configuration?](could-a-replacement-private-ai-platform-reconstruct-home-steward-from-governed-sources-and-configuration.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A replacement platform should not depend on a departing vendor's hidden knowledge state. The household wants reconstructable governed sources, configuration and correction lineage distinguished from unsupported portability claims, while recognizing that replacement can change answer quality without changing source authority.
