# Guests and Temporary Authority

Navigation and scope guidance only; not an architecture contract or roadmap. Browse the [scenario-family map](../README.md).

## Purpose

Understand temporary participation, care information and bounded guest/service experiences.

## Relationship to HTBW

Operational Trust retains actual access and disclosure decisions; Identity context alone grants neither. Stewardship responsibilities do not grant source access. See the existing [HTBW boundary](../../architecture/htbw-boundary.md).

## Possible Home Steward extension

Explain already-governed temporary scope and synthesize separately permitted visit information. Any new source admission, representation, handoff or implementation is an OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION.

## Boundaries

Guest, family, caretaker or professional status is not authorization. Welcome speech does not grant private data; no automatic credential provisioning, consent inference or permanent permission inheritance.

Person/source/purpose/namespace/audience scope, household-controlled locality and source-derived lifecycle remain independent requirements. Potential evidence is not admitted access; knowledge is not action or disclosure authority. Source reports and inference are not automatic Truth; unknown is valid and correlation is not causation.

- [What should this guest know about the room?](what-should-this-guest-know-about-the-room.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A guest may need a small amount of room information to feel comfortable without learning private household routines.

- [What should the cleaner know, and what should remain private?](what-should-the-cleaner-know-and-what-should-remain-private.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A cleaner may need practical information about surfaces, exclusions or household-declared care requirements without receiving personal routines or confidential documents.
- [What does the HVAC technician need to know?](what-does-the-hvac-technician-need-to-know.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. An HVAC technician can benefit from documented equipment and service context without needing unrelated household information.
- [Which areas/capabilities should be unavailable to a contractor?](which-areas-capabilities-should-be-unavailable-to-a-contractor.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A contractor may need access to one work area while other rooms, information and capabilities remain private.
- [What changed while the service provider was here?](what-changed-while-the-service-provider-was-here.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. The household may notice a difference after a service visit and want to understand the documented sequence.
- [Did temporary access expire as intended?](did-temporary-access-expire-as-intended.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. The household wants to know whether temporary permission ended as intended, not merely whether a guest stopped appearing in activity records.
- [What should an overnight guest know about security and emergency procedures?](what-should-an-overnight-guest-know-about-security-and-emergency-procedures.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. - [How can someone use entertainment without understanding the entire Home?](how-can-someone-use-entertainment-without-understanding-the-entire-home.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A visitor may want to enjoy entertainment without learning the Home's entire control vocabulary.
- [What should never appear on a guest kiosk?](what-should-never-appear-on-a-guest-kiosk.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A guest kiosk can unintentionally combine room help with sensitive household information.
