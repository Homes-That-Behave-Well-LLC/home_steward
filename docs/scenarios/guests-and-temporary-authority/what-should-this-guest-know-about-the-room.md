# What should this guest know about the room?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

What should this guest know about the room?

## Why it matters

A guest may need a small amount of room information to feel comfortable without learning private household routines. The household wants to distinguish practical, permitted guidance from information that belongs to residents. An understandable introduction can support welcome and independence while leaving private preferences, schedules and protected capabilities outside the guest's scope.
