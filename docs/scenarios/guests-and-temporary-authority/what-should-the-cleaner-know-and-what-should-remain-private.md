# What should the cleaner know, and what should remain private?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

What should the cleaner know, and what should remain private?

## Why it matters

A cleaner may need practical information about surfaces, exclusions or household-declared care requirements without receiving personal routines or confidential documents. The household wants purpose-limited guidance that supports the work and respects privacy. A service relationship should not turn an ordinary care explanation into unrestricted room, camera or knowledge access.

## Existing HTBW relationship

Foundation retains definitions/configuration; Truth, current/historical Facts; Identity, Person resolution, not authentication/authorization; Stewardship, household-declared care/obligations; Continuity, preference/experience eligibility; Operational Trust, request-specific permissions; Concierge, orchestration/Trace. Home Steward acquires no ownership.

## Potential evidence

Approved cleaning scope; surface/material guidance; declared care exclusions; audience restrictions; temporary access descriptions.

Categories only, each independently authorized for purpose, Person, source, audience and namespace. Local processing and derived retention, correction, expiry and revocation remain source-bound.

## Important boundary

Readable cleaning guidance does not authorize entering excluded areas, disclosing personal routines or changing care obligations.

Temporary audience, scope and time limits are distinct from identity. Guest, family, caretaker or service-professional status grants no access; a welcome voice grants no private data.

Knowledge/interpretation never grants action or disclosure authority. Unclear evidence stays unknown; correlation/timing is not causation. No life-safety or medical/emergency path depends on Home Steward/LLM.
