# Did temporary access expire as intended?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

Did temporary access expire as intended?

## Why it matters

The household wants to know whether temporary permission ended as intended, not merely whether a guest stopped appearing in activity records. Intended expiry, recorded status and effective permissions may be different evidence categories. An unexplained mismatch should remain visible without attempting to test access, extend the grant or silently remediate it.

## Important boundary

No recent use does not prove expiry; this comparison authorizes no grant extension, revocation or access test.

Temporary audience, scope and time limits are distinct from identity. Guest, family, caretaker or service-professional status grants no access; a welcome voice grants no private data.

Knowledge/interpretation never grants action or disclosure authority. Unclear evidence stays unknown; correlation/timing is not causation. No life-safety or medical/emergency path depends on Home Steward/LLM.
