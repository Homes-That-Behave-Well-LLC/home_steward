# What does the HVAC technician need to know?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

What does the HVAC technician need to know?

## Why it matters

An HVAC technician can benefit from documented equipment and service context without needing unrelated household information. The household wants to understand which authorized observations, manuals and recorded concerns are relevant to the approved visit. A concise account should preserve uncertainty and leave technical diagnosis and professional recommendations to the qualified person.
