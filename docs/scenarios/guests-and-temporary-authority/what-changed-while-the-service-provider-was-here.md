Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

What changed while the service provider was here?

## Why it matters

The household may notice a difference after a service visit and want to understand the documented sequence. A bounded comparison can separate recorded work, other household changes and unexplained observations. The visitor's presence is not proof of responsibility, and successful-looking operation is not proof that contracted work was completed.
