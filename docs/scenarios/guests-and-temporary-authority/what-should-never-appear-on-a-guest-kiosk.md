# What should never appear on a guest kiosk?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

What should never appear on a guest kiosk?

## Why it matters

A guest kiosk can unintentionally combine room help with sensitive household information. The household wants to understand which information categories fall outside the explicitly permitted audience and purpose. The desired outcome is a privacy-aware account of existing descriptions, not a new kiosk design or confidence that a friendly display automatically keeps backing sources private.

## Important boundary

Do not retrieve protected content to illustrate exclusions; a visible screen grants neither source access nor disclosure.

Temporary audience, scope and time limits are distinct from identity. Guest, family, caretaker or service-professional status grants no access; a welcome voice grants no private data.

Knowledge/interpretation never grants action or disclosure authority. Unclear evidence stays unknown; correlation/timing is not causation. No life-safety or medical/emergency path depends on Home Steward/LLM.
