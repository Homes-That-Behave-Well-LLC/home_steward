# How can someone use entertainment without understanding the entire Home?

Status: VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED

## Household question

How can someone use entertainment without understanding the entire Home?

## Why it matters

A visitor may want to enjoy entertainment without learning the Home's entire control vocabulary. The household wants to understand the minimum guest-eligible information needed for a simple task and the documented native alternatives. Ease of use must not expose resident viewing history, private libraries or permissions unrelated to the guest's entertainment scope.

Home Steward acquires no ownership.
