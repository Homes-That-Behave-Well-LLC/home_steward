# Entertainment discovery without execution

Status: CANONICAL SCENARIO

Developed-content maturity only; not Owner, runtime or architecture acceptance.

> Entertainment and Media Discovery: synthetic experience capture, not implementation or architecture.

## 1. Household request

"What movies do we own by this director?" "What episodes feature this actor?"
"Which of these movies are already in our library?" Learn without requesting playback.

## 2. Existing HTBW knowledge

Foundation governed definitions/source associations, eligible Continuity context and Truth Home state.
No complete media catalog or automatic ownership knowledge is assumed.

## 3. Authorized private retrieval

Separately approved private library/ownership/context information, with Person/namespace limits.
An informational question still requires source and audience authorization.

## 4. Optional public research

Public film/program/episode/cast/director metadata, distinct from private holdings.
Any optional streaming result remains freshness/region-sensitive and separate from entitlement.

## 5. Home Steward synthesis

Answer informational questions, distinguish catalog metadata from actual authorized holdings,
clarify actor/director/title/edition ambiguity and state coverage. Not every media question implies playback.

## 6. Provenance

Private holdings, ownership evidence and public metadata carry source/date/version/coverage.
Inferences and incomplete catalogs are not current Truth or proof the household owns every result.

## 7. Candidate relationships

Content/credit/library matches may be candidate answers, not durable ownership or Person preferences.

## 8. Household confirmation

Clarify referents or scope when needed; no requirement to select a playback action.
Any new clarification retention remains an OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION.

## 9. Governed durable relationship

OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION if a later durable association is desired; no new catalog/object/schema.

## 10. Proposed actions

The desired outcome is an answer/list/comparison only. Playback is not implied; any later governed
execution/handoff is an OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION, not designed here.

## 11. Prohibited autonomous actions

No playback/purchase/rental, private-library scan, account access, preference learning, configuration/
integration change or Home Assistant service mapping from an informational question.

## 12. HTBW authority

Foundation definitions; Identity context; Truth state; Continuity context; Stewardship care;
Operational Trust action/disclosure; Concierge interaction and existing Explainability/Decision Trace.

## 13. Privacy and locality

Library/history remain independently Person/purpose/audience-authorized and locally governed.
Public information does not declassify holdings; derivatives inherit source lifecycle.

## 14. Unavailable / degraded behavior

Missing/denied sources yield qualified coverage or "I don't know", not "none owned".
No source/cloud substitution or unsolicited playback; native HTBW remains.

## 15. Acceptance expectations

Informational-only success, separate public/private evidence and unresolved ambiguity captured.
No forced action, new catalog/handoff architecture or runtime acceptance.

## 16. Open questions

Knowledge-source admission, answer/clarification retention and any distinct future execution
mechanism remain OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTIONS.
