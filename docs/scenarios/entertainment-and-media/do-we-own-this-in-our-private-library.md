# Do we own this in our private library

Status: CANONICAL SCENARIO

Developed-content maturity only; not Owner, runtime or architecture acceptance.

> Entertainment and Media Discovery: synthetic experience capture, not implementation or architecture.

## 1. Household request

"Do we already own this movie?" Identify an authorized household copy, not merely an online listing.

## 2. Existing HTBW knowledge

Foundation governed source/Asset associations if present, Truth current Home state and eligible
Continuity context. No existing complete media inventory is assumed.

## 3. Authorized private retrieval

Separately approved household private library/local NAS and owned digital media; ownership/account
records where authorized. Access to one folder or Person's account does not admit the whole library.

## 4. Optional public research

Public title/edition/release metadata can distinguish versions; it cannot prove household ownership.
Any optional streaming comparison needs current dated regional evidence.

## 5. Home Steward synthesis

Identify possible copies and alternate editions, resolution/quality, audio and subtitle/accessibility
differences. File presence, licensed access and ownership evidence differ; ambiguity remains explicit.

## 6. Provenance

Private source/version/review time, rights/ownership evidence and external edition metadata remain
separate. A file name or search hit alone does not establish the claimed rights or current accessibility.

## 7. Candidate relationships

Title-to-file/edition matches are candidates, not accepted ownership or durable catalog relationships.

## 8. Household confirmation

Clarify title/edition or household-held evidence; confirmation does not grant broader library access
or playback. Future retention is an OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION.

## 9. Governed durable relationship

OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION: no ownership/copy object, catalog schema or new storage model.

## 10. Proposed actions

Answer or compare the copies. Watching one is a separate possible request; any governed execution
handoff remains an OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION.

## 11. Prohibited autonomous actions

No file scanning/copying/deleting, rights bypass, playback/purchase, preference learning,
configuration/integration change or Home Assistant service mapping.

## 12. HTBW authority

Foundation definitions; Identity context; Truth state; Continuity preferences; Stewardship care;
Operational Trust action/disclosure; Concierge interaction and existing Explainability/Decision Trace.

## 13. Privacy and locality

Private library access remains separately Person/purpose/namespace/audience-authorized within the
governed local boundary. Derived holdings narratives inherit source lifecycle and disclosure limits.

## 14. Unavailable / degraded behavior

Unchecked/unavailable sources yield "I don't know whether we own it", not "we do not own it".
No unauthorized source/cloud fallback; native HTBW remains available.

## 15. Acceptance expectations

Private holdings, alternate editions, quality/accessibility and ownership uncertainty captured.
Discovery alone does not imply playback or a new catalog/rights architecture.

## 16. Open questions

Authorized holdings/rights evidence, version knowledge and any playback/handoff remain OPEN
ARCHITECTURAL QUESTIONS; no private-library ingestion or content abstraction.
