# Find the movie from a plot description

Status: CANONICAL SCENARIO

Developed-content maturity only; not Owner, runtime or architecture acceptance.

> Entertainment and Media Discovery: synthetic experience capture, not implementation or architecture.

## 1. Household request

"Find the movie where someone is being chased through a cornfield."
"Find the movie with the train crash and Harrison Ford."

## 2. Existing HTBW knowledge

Eligible Room/Person context and Continuity preferences; Truth current Home state is distinct from
movie knowledge. Neither a remembered scene nor a provider recommendation is a Fact about this Home.

## 3. Authorized private retrieval

Separately approved library/history or household narrative might help; do not search another
Person's viewing history because an incomplete description is difficult.

## 4. Optional public research

Permitted film metadata, actor credits and concise scene descriptions, without private query context.
Public identification evidence is not proof the household owns or may watch a film.

## 5. Home Steward synthesis

Use partial plot, scene, actors and approximate era as uncertain clues. Present alternatives and ask
conversational clarification: "I found several possible matches." Do not force a confident selection.

## 6. Provenance

Cite external metadata versus household memory/private evidence; mark uncertain associations,
source date and any separate freshness-sensitive streaming-availability finding.

## 7. Candidate relationships

Clue-to-title associations remain candidates, not accepted memories or durable viewing-history records.

## 8. Household confirmation

The requester may recognize/reject a candidate or add a clue; clarification is not playback authority.
Its future retention is an OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION.

## 9. Governed durable relationship

OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION: no new title-resolution object, relationship schema or learning store.

## 10. Proposed actions

Identify or compare films; playback is a separate possible request, not implied by "find".
Any future governed execution handoff remains an OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION.

## 11. Prohibited autonomous actions

No automatic playback/purchase, fabricated certainty, private-history access, learned preference,
configuration/integration change or Home Assistant service mapping.

## 12. HTBW authority

Foundation definitions; Identity context; Truth state; Continuity preferences; Stewardship care;
Operational Trust action/disclosure; Concierge interaction and existing Explainability/Decision Trace.

## 13. Privacy and locality

Person-scoped media/history and derived interpretations inherit source authorization/locality/
lifecycle. Public film research is not permission to expose private viewing habits.

## 14. Unavailable / degraded behavior

"I don't know" or several candidates remains valid with missing/conflicting clues. Do not invent
a remembered title or silently use another source/account; native HTBW behavior remains available.

## 15. Acceptance expectations

Incomplete human memory, multiple candidates and correction captured; identification can finish
without playback. No resolver algorithm, architecture or runtime acceptance.

## 16. Open questions

Knowledge-source admission, ambiguity interaction/retention and any later execution/handoff remain
OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTIONS; no plot resolver or content-provider abstraction.
