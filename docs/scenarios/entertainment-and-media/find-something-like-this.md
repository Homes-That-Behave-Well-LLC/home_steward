# Find something like this

Status: CANONICAL SCENARIO

Developed-content maturity only; not Owner, runtime or architecture acceptance.

> Entertainment and Media Discovery: synthetic experience capture, not implementation or architecture.

## 1. Household request

"Find something like this movie." "Play something like this album."
Explore similarity without needing catalog identifiers or an exact artist/director name.

## 2. Existing HTBW knowledge

Truth current media/Home state where supported, Foundation Room context and eligible Continuity
preferences/experience. One Person's taste is not every household member's preference.

## 3. Authorized private retrieval

Separately approved current-content context, library, Person preference/listening/viewing history.
Shared playback does not authorize all participants' private histories or infer agreement.

## 4. Optional public research

Public genre/style/artist/director/era metadata and permitted similarity research.
[Official Music Assistant documentation](https://www.home-assistant.io/integrations/music_assistant/)
describes native track/artist/album/provider URI or URL playback possibilities, not this scenario's design.
Undated reference reviewed 2026-11-07; no installed-version or household compatibility result.

## 5. Home Steward synthesis

Clarify the referent and desired genre, style, artist/director, era, mood or similarity dimension.
Distinguish declared household/Person preference from inferred resemblance; offer alternatives,
not a universal taste or guaranteed enjoyment.

## 6. Provenance

Separate public metadata, household statement and authorized private context, with dates/uncertainty.
Any current streaming-availability claim needs source/review time and regional applicability.

## 7. Candidate relationships

Similarity associations are candidates, not accepted preference, shared taste or durable relationships.

## 8. Household confirmation

Ask what "like" means and who is choosing; a preference clue is not permission to play or learn a
profile. Future clarification retention is an OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION.

## 9. Governed durable relationship

OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION: no similarity/preference object, algorithm or proposal schema.

## 10. Proposed actions

Present recommendations or consider a separately authorized playback request; informational discovery
can stand alone. Any future governed handoff/execution is an OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION.

## 11. Prohibited autonomous actions

No automatic playback/purchase, Person profiling, universal preference, unrestricted history access,
configuration/integration change or Home Assistant service mapping.

## 12. HTBW authority

Foundation definitions; Identity context; Truth state; Continuity preferences/experience; Stewardship
care; Operational Trust action/disclosure; Concierge and existing Explainability/Decision Trace.

## 13. Privacy and locality

Person-scoped media/history remain independently authorized; ingestion is not disclosure permission.
Local derivations inherit lifecycle; public similarity queries must not leak household listening habits.

## 14. Unavailable / degraded behavior

Unknown referent/taste or unavailable evidence yields clarification or "I don't know what would fit",
not invented personalization. Preserve native HTBW without silent source/cloud fallback.

## 15. Acceptance expectations

Seven similarity dimensions and individual/shared preference limits captured. No similarity engine,
provider abstraction, playback mechanism or runtime acceptance.

## 16. Open questions

Similarity knowledge admission, clarification retention and any execution/handoff remain OPEN
ARCHITECTURAL QUESTIONS; no recommendation/learning architecture.
