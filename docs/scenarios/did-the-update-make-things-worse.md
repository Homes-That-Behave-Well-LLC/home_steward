# Did the update make things worse

> Proposed synthetic scenario; no actual HA update, benchmark or regression finding.
> Applies the [investigation evidence rules](../architecture/knowledge-architecture.md#evidence-and-epistemic-rules).

## 1. Household request

"Did the latest Home Assistant update cause any problems?" Establish the actual version/update
timestamp and what before/after observations can legitimately support.

## 2. Existing HTBW knowledge

Foundation/configuration version relationships; authoritative Decision Traces and Truth Historical
Facts; existing Repairs and Stewardship service evidence. Native retained-record explanations and
historical reconstruction remain HTBW-owned.

## 3. Authorized private retrieval

Minimum verified update/deployment record, comparable pre/post traces, eligible integration errors,
entity availability, latency, failed automation outcomes, Repairs and warning patterns. HA log/
history access is a separate future relationship; do not invent an HTBW Change Record for a raw log.

## 4. Optional public research

Permitted exact-version release notes/known-issue documentation, dated and clearly external.
No private configuration, topology or raw log upload; published reports do not prove local causation.

## 5. Home Steward synthesis

Require update timestamp, comparison windows, affected systems, unaffected systems, evidence
strength and alternatives. Compare equivalent operation/coverage and distinguish restart transients
from sustained changes, retaining disclosed exclusions. Hypothetically, one integration has more
errors after the update while comparable others do not: that supports a localized temporal
association, not "the update caused it". Consider simultaneous configuration/network/service
changes, workload, season/use and monitoring changes; preserve contrary evidence.

## 6. Provenance

Cite exact version/Effective or recorded update time, windows, samples/denominators, exclusions,
coverage and error semantics. Separate trace/Historical Fact/operational log/configuration/external
evidence, statistical pattern and hypothesis. Correlation is not causation.

## 7. Candidate relationships

A possible update regression remains a hypothesis until discriminating evidence supports it;
unaffected systems and alternative causes limit scope, not guarantee no other problem exists.

## 8. Household confirmation

Confirm relevant symptoms/interventions as scoped evidence; neither dissatisfaction nor a date
match authorizes rollback or permanent policy changes.

## 9. Governed durable relationship

No Truth diagnosis, changed Fact history or overwritten trace. Original version/Change Records
remain with their owners; a candidate finding is not an authoritative cause.

## 10. Proposed actions

Propose targeted review or a separately authorized, reversible validation plan through HTBW and
Operational Trust. Concierge coordinates permitted owner/executor work.

## 11. Prohibited autonomous actions

No software rollback/restart, integration disablement, automation removal, preference/policy or
obligation change, device replacement, service booking or vendor contact without HTBW authorization.

## 12. HTBW authority

Truth Facts/history; Foundation configuration; Concierge Trace/explanation; Stewardship care;
Identity requester; Continuity context; Operational Trust source/disclosure/action decisions.

## 13. Privacy and locality

Separately governed logs, minimum evidence and Person/audience-safe fields apply before retrieval.
Local derived comparisons inherit source lifecycle; no automatic indefinite raw-log memory or shadow history.

## 14. Unavailable / degraded behavior

Missing/aged-out pre-update data or an unverified timestamp prevents a causal comparison. Say
"I don't know whether the update caused this"; no invented baseline or hidden cloud fallback.

## 15. Acceptance expectations

All six comparison requirements appear; affected/unaffected groups, alternatives, sample limits
and unknown outcomes remain visible. First appearance after update is not enough for causation.

## 16. Open questions

Update record authority, matched windows, regression evidence strength and future query/redaction/
retention controls remain open. No update, rollback, test harness or ingestion is implemented.
