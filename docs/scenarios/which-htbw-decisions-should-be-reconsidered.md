# Which HTBW decisions should be reconsidered

> Proposed synthetic review scenario; no accepted decision is reopened or declared obsolete.
> Applies [HTBW Architecture Stewardship](../architecture/htbw-architecture-stewardship.md).

## 1. Household request

"Which accepted HTBW decisions were based on Home Assistant limitations that may no longer exist?"
Use a separately authorized human review context, not permission to rewrite accepted architecture.

## 2. Existing HTBW knowledge

Accepted ADR/Decision Ledger identifiers, rationale, exact historical versions, contracts and
acceptance evidence; current Foundation configuration and authoritative Decision Traces.

## 3. Authorized private retrieval

Minimum scoped decision/history/implementation/contract references and eligible Owner review records.
Each repository/source is separately governed; no blanket private history or credential access.

## 4. Optional public research

Verify changed boundaries through [official HA developer documentation](https://contributors.home-assistant.io/core/)
and versioned release/integration sources. Community claims cannot prove a removed limitation.

## 5. Home Steward synthesis

For each candidate provide decision identifier, accepted rationale, original platform limitation,
new authoritative evidence, fully removed/partially removed/unchanged limitation, affected
responsibilities/contracts and recommendation. Unresolved support remains evidence insufficient,
not a fabricated removed limitation.

Recommendations: **no review required**, **monitor**, **governed reconsideration warranted** or
**evidence insufficient**. State expected outcome/benefit, new obligation and uncertainties.
Future Native-Capability Dependency/Reconsideration Condition/Retirement Preference conventions
may inform a draft; they are not retrofitted into the original decisions.

## 6. Provenance

Decision/source location/commit/version/publication or commit date, review date, installed/effective
HA match, GA/Labs/preview/deprecated/removal status and gaps. Hypothesis is not Truth or obsolete status.

## 7. Candidate relationships

Possible stale-assumption association is an evidence-backed candidate, not a reopened decision,
accepted replacement or automatic native-capability dependency record.

## 8. Household confirmation

An eligible human confirms scope/outcome relevance; agreement to review does not amend the decision,
grant migration authority or satisfy Owner acceptance.

## 9. Governed durable relationship

Accepted decision/history remains with its governing architecture. Only the separately authorized
Owner/governance process can change it; no Home Steward Ledger/ADR write or new decision store.

## 10. Proposed actions

Return reconsideration drafts through HTBW/Operational Trust and responsible human review with
equivalence, privacy, rollback, tests, HA-DEV/lived validation and acceptance before any retirement.

## 11. Prohibited autonomous actions

No reopening or obsolescence declaration, Ledger/ADR edits, issue creation/closure, code removal,
data/configuration migration, automation/policy changes, HA update/Labs enablement or upstream work.

## 12. HTBW authority

Accepted governance controls architecture; Foundation records; Truth Facts; Identity requester;
Stewardship care; Continuity context; Operational Trust permission; Concierge formulation/Trace.

## 13. Privacy and locality

Approved minimum repository/review/Person/audience scope before local synthesis. Retained histories/
derivations keep source lifecycle; private acceptance data are not public query inputs.

## 14. Unavailable / degraded behavior

Stale or incomplete official evidence or missing accepted rationale yields "I don't know whether
reconsideration is warranted"; do not recommend migration. Preserve original decisions/native behavior.

## 15. Acceptance expectations

All candidate fields, three evidenced limitation outcomes plus unresolved, and four recommendations.
No existing decision is reopened, conventions retrofitted, or superseded code retired before acceptance.

## 16. Open questions

Future convention placement, source completeness and comparison/Owner review remain unsettled;
no watcher, ingestion, decision-amendment or migration mechanism.
