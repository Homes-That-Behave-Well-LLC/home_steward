# Procurement and Replacement

Navigation and scope guidance only; not an architecture contract or roadmap. Browse the [scenario-family map](../README.md).

## Purpose

Understand repair/replacement, maintainability, vendor dependence and real household benefit before a separately governed decision.

## Relationship to HTBW

HTBW owns declared requirements, significance, care and existing architecture. A chosen preference differs from a purchase history or vendor claim. See the existing [HTBW boundary](../../architecture/htbw-boundary.md).

## Possible Home Steward extension

Compare permitted evidence and stated outcomes, including native, professional, nontechnical and no-action alternatives. Any new source admission, representation, handoff or implementation is an OPEN ARCHITECTURAL QUESTION.

## Boundaries

No purchase, endorsement, platform selection or Issues #2-#5 qualification work. Price does not declare significance; no private inventory disclosure or automatic replacement.

Person/source/purpose/namespace/audience scope, household-controlled locality and source-derived lifecycle remain independent requirements. Potential evidence is not admitted access; knowledge is not action or disclosure authority. Source reports and inference are not automatic Truth; unknown is valid and correlation is not causation.

### Vision — Not Yet Architected

- [Should we repair or replace this?](should-we-repair-or-replace-this.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Repair and replacement can differ in cost, disruption, maintainability and household fit.
- [What did we dislike about the current product?](what-did-we-dislike-about-the-current-product.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A replacement may repeat the same frustration if the household cannot distinguish documented friction from a temporary fault or one person's preference.
- [Which replacements fit the existing architecture?](which-replacements-fit-the-existing-architecture.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A promising replacement may create new dependencies or undermine accepted behavior despite superficially matching a product category.
- [What subscriptions would this product create?](what-subscriptions-would-this-product-create.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A product's ongoing obligations may matter more than its initial price.
- [What cloud dependencies would this product create?](what-cloud-dependencies-would-this-product-create.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A new product can introduce cloud authentication, data transfer or operation dependencies that are not obvious from a local-looking interface.
- [Will this work if the vendor disappears?](will-this-work-if-the-vendor-disappears.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Vendor disappearance can affect updates, authentication, content or basic operation in different ways.
- [What else must change if this component is replaced?](what-else-must-change-if-this-component-is-replaced.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Replacing one component may affect accessories, training, configuration, documentation or care obligations elsewhere.
- [Are replacement parts still available?](are-replacement-parts-still-available.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A repair decision may depend on the availability of the correct part, not simply a similar item offered online.
- [Which preferred vendor fits this architectural lane?](which-preferred-vendor-fits-this-architectural-lane.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. An explicitly preferred vendor may fit one architectural lane but not another.
- [What Reference Home evidence supports the recommendation?](what-reference-home-evidence-supports-the-recommendation.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. A recommendation should not borrow confidence from a demonstration or a different Home version.
- [Are we solving the real problem, or merely buying technology?](are-we-solving-the-real-problem-or-merely-buying-technology.md) — **VISION — NOT YET ARCHITECTED**. Buying technology can obscure an unresolved household problem or add maintenance without improving experience.
