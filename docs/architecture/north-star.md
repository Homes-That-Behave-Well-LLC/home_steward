# BehavesWell Extended Intelligence North Star

> Proposed canonical baseline; awaiting Owner acceptance. Accepted HTBW DL-131/DL-81/DL-82/DL-93
> boundaries are inherited, not re-decided. [Authority](../governance/authority-order.md).
> Product name per HTBW DL-153: **BehavesWell Extended Intelligence**, formerly Home Steward — one
> product, renamed. "Extended" means broader *authorized reasoning context*, never broader authority.
> "HTBW" below refers to BehavesWell Core, Home Assistant Edition. See
> [product identity](product-identity-and-compatibility-boundary.md).

BehavesWell Core gives the Home governed knowledge of itself. BehavesWell Extended Intelligence lets
the household explore, extend, retrieve, synthesize, compare and reason over that knowledge and
explicitly authorized additional sources. It helps people understand, not surrender authority.

## Principles

1. HTBW gives the Home a governed model of itself and the household.
2. BehavesWell Extended Intelligence extends knowledge exploration rather than replacing HTBW.
3. Knowledge is not authority.
4. Access is not disclosure.
5. Reasoning is not consent.
6. Recommendation is not authorization.
7. An AI result is not permission to act.
8. External/model knowledge is not automatically HTBW Truth.
9. Missing information and candidate relationships may be identified openly.
10. Candidates remain inferred until confirmed or otherwise promoted through governance.
11. The household governs sources, beneficiaries, permitted questions, retention, derived knowledge
    and any authority created through a separately accepted process.
12. Platforms, inference providers, models, retrieval services and hardware are replaceable.
13. No vendor/runtime is inseparable from the product.
14. BehavesWell Extended Intelligence failure must not destroy HTBW knowledge, relationships, obligations, Identity,
    Truth, Continuity, Operational Trust or deterministic behavior that does not require it.

Assist remains the household interaction surface; Concierge orchestrates; BehavesWell Extended Intelligence contributes
governed knowledge and uncertainty behind those boundaries. The effective Person's independent
Allow Home Steward gate applies; Room context is never its authorization owner.

Use [the extension test](home-steward-extension-model.md) before invoking AI.
Public Framework alignment is [reconciled by DL-153](behaves-well-framework-relationship.md): six
Framework stages over Layer 0 Foundation; this product is independent of both.
