# Relationship Discovery

> Proposed canonical baseline; awaiting Owner acceptance. Foundation remains durable-relationship owner.

Home Steward may discover candidate connections among authorized Assets, trips, renovations, family
provenance, documents and events. Discovery is inference, not Truth, consent or a silent write.

## Distinct stages

1. **Candidate:** a possible relationship, labelled inferred, with competing explanations/gaps.
2. **Supporting evidence:** references to authorized originals, evidence dates and inference basis.
3. **Household confirmation:** attributable confirmation from a sufficiently resolved, authorized
   Person for the specific relationship, not a generic conversational "yes".
4. **Governed durable relationship:** responsible HTBW owner evaluates scope, identity, schema,
   conflict/provenance and any required governance before promotion.

This sequence is a boundary requirement, not an invented persistence API/state machine. The exact
record shape, promotion transaction, authorization strength and correction/rollback remain open.

Example: receipts and dates suggest that Painting A, Sculpture B and Textile C were acquired on a
Santa Fe trip. That is not yet the household's confirmed history. "Yes, those were all from that
trip" can contribute evidence of confirmation; it must not silently authorize disclosure, insurance
changes, source correlation beyond scope, or other unrelated actions.

Present disputed/incomplete evidence honestly. A candidate must never overwrite Asset identity,
current Truth location or an existing authoritative provenance relationship.
