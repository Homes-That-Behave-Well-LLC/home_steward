# Community expectations

Be respectful, precise and evidence-led. Challenge claims and designs without attacking people.
Welcome different household needs and levels of technical experience.

Do not harass, discriminate, expose private information, pressure anyone to disclose personal data,
or imply a vendor affiliation that does not exist. Respect consent, confidentiality and copyright.

Maintainers may remove harmful material and restrict participation. Raise concerns privately through
a route agreed with the maintainers; do not reproduce the harmful or sensitive content publicly.

These are repository participation expectations, not a new HTBW responsibility or legal compliance claim.
